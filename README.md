# The Pony In The High Castle MOD

"We had friendship, and magic, but now, we've lost'em all."
What if...They've won?
In this fictional world, Cozy Glow, Queen Chrysalis and Lord Tirek won the Ultimate Battle (S09E24, S09E25), instead of M6 in the official episodes. In that Ultimate Battle of Equestria on year 999, other creatures did not come to help, leading to the defeat and imprison of M6. Rapidly, after two years of consequent wars, on year 1001, the "Big Three" signed the Canterlot PACT to divide and conquer Equestria: Cozy Glow, now the "Friendship Empress", is the recognized ruler of ponies in Equestria. She uses the magic of an alicorn and the trickery talent to reign the country, and built a medieval feudalism Equestria. After coquering Changeling Empire, Queen Chrysalis took back her power and reverted all Changelings back to the original form. Lord Tirek is interested in magic only, instead of political power. He took away half of Equestrian magic, returned to his centaur hometown and enslaved creatures around. M6 and the sisters are now separately locked deeply instead Tartarus, the place that used to hold the "Big Three". Discord and Starlight lost most of their magic, but managed to escape during the chaos in the Ultimate Battle. While some have followed their path of revolution, most had accepted the failure and moved on to embrace the next era...

## MOD Features

- **Story-oriented pony world**: story aligns with the storyline of My Little Pony, no OCs and OOCs (perhaps)

- **Dark humor**: political ironies and classical references all over the place

- **Brand-new technologies**: from the wooden melees to magical giant robots

- **Mirror portals**: sending your troops faster than light

- **Gambler's party**: a lot of random events and decisions

- **Snowy march**: fight with all kinds of monsters in the cold global climate

- **Magical fight**：your special forces have skills

- **Original music**: The Pony In The High Castle album and radio music

- **Inventory system**: Play HoI4 like an RPG game

- **Active update**: loads and loads of contents to be consistently updated

- **Autodev tool**: this mod is generated via an auto development tool: HOI4DEV (details about the tool are below)

### Windows

(sus: could randomly crash)

Move the mod folder and description file to `"C:/Users/<YOUR_USER_NAME>/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod"`. File structure:
```
Hearts of Iron IV/
  mod/
    PIHC_dev/
    PIHC_dev.mod
```

Open `PIHC_dev.mod` with text editor, replace `path` to the last line:

```
...
path = "C:/Users/<YOUR_USER_NAME>/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/PIHC_dev"
```

Then launch the HOI4 launcher to reload installed mods.

## About HOI4DEV

[url=https://github.com/Magolor/hoi4dev]Github Link[/url]

HOI4DEV is a python library created by Talirian the creator of this mod. HOI4DEV is a package for automated development of HOI4 MODs, which allow MOD developers to build a MOD much more quickly than ever before.

Useful features include CCL (Clausewitz scripting language) to JSON converter, JSON to CCL converter, DDS image editing, automatic national focus positioning, fast characters creation, fast events creation, fast technology creation, automatic multi-language localisation, etc.

Currently, there is no GUI for the HOI4DEV pacakge. If you are interested in using HOI4DEV as a python programmer or an ordinary MOD developer, helping develop HOI4DEV or HOI4DEV GUI, please feel free to contact Talirian.
