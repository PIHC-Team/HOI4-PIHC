[zh.@NAME]
雨晴

[zh.@DESC]
在“静默”中，麒麟族找到了生存的契机，而雨晴则为此付出了自己的声音。一名麒麟族的歌者，因为其美妙动听的嗓音而被选举为领袖。她曾经以为，自己的歌声能够带来和平，但她错了。
她亲眼看着昔日的家园化作焦土，她亲眼看着自己的子民，或者说昔日的朋友，反目成仇互相攻伐。虽然这在麒麟历史上已经无数次发生，但她还是无法接受，将这一切怪罪于自己，并最终提出了“静默”。她强制在全国范围内推广了“静默”，消除了麒麟们的绝大部分情感，以及她自己的声音。
随后，是压抑的数十年，也是麒麟族前所未有的和平的数十年：如果没有谁能说话，那么就没有谁能争吵。雨晴比任何其他麒麟都清楚“静默”的危害，但她从没有后悔过，因为这是麒麟族的唯一选择，因为她见过更糟糕的景象。
[en.@DESC]
In the “Silence”, the Kirins found an opportunity to survive, and Rain Shine paid for it with her voice. A Kirin singer was elected leader because of her beautiful voice. She once thought that her singing could bring peace, but she was wrong.
She watched her former home turn to ashes, and she watched her subjects, or, former friends, turn against each other. Although this has happened countless times in Kirin history, she still could not accept it, blamed herself for everything, and eventually proposed “Silence”. She forcibly promoted “Silence” nationwide, eliminating most of the Kirin’s emotions, as well as her own voice.
Then came decades of repression, and at the same time decades of unprecedented peace for the Kirins: if no one can speak, then no one can argue. Rain Shine is more aware of the dangers of “Silence” than any other Kirin, but she has never regretted it, because it is the only choice for the Kirin commune, because she has seen worse.
