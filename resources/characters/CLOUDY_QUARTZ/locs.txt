[en.@DESC]
In the eyes of the Pie sisters, Cloudy Quartz, their mother, always remained kind and approachable. As a deeply conservative pony, she quickly married Igneous Rock Pie under the arrangement of a “marriage stone”, and from then on, she tirelessly cared for the family, equally loving each of her children, which was one of her few hobbies. To her, Pinkie Pie, the Element of Laughter, was not the legendary pony who had saved Equestria multiple times, but simply her third child who lacked maturity. After Pinkie Pie disappeared, Cloudy Quartz wept daily, her health deteriorating, but she refused to retire completely. Instead, she continued to devote all her energy to caring for her bedridden husband, doing as much as she could for the rock farm to distract herself from worrying about the safety of her dear little Pinkamena.
