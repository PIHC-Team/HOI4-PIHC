Sable Spirit
[zh.@NAME]
紫影朱灵

[zh.@DESC]
许久以前，当她还年轻但样貌衰老之时，她曾犯下大错，尝试将世界的美丽之物抢夺一空。
是她所背叛的挚友，帮助她幡然醒悟，并帮她找回了她自己的美丽。从那以后，她便开始给予而不是索取。
如今，不知过去几百几千年，她已年老，但她的样貌依然年轻，她灵魂的美丽也将趋向永恒。
[en.@DESC]
Long ago, when she was young but her appearance was old, she made a great mistake and attempted to take away all the beauty of the world.
It was the friend she betrayed who helped her wake up and restored her own beauty. Since then, she began to give instead of take.
Now, after hundreds or thousands of years, she is old, but her appearance is still young, and her soul will be beautiful for eternity.
