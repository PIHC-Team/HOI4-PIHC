[en.@DESC]
Cheese Sandwich, the president of Joyful Enterprises and once the premier party expert in Equestria, treated everyone equally and dedicated himself to spreading smiles to every kind soul on the continent. As a young pony, Cheese Sandwich was timid and lost, but he ventured out to find his purpose, until he encountered Pinkie Pie and her parties in Ponyville. From that moment on, Cheese Sandwich not only found himself but also formed an inseparable bond with the pink mare.
As a close friend of the Elements of Harmony, especially Pinkie Pie, Cheese Sandwich, in order to spread joy more effectively, founded Joyful Enterprises and factories, continuously bringing happiness to ponies. After the Last Roundup, although Cheese Sandwich rarely hosted parties for ponies anymore, he continued to travel across different regions, secretly searching for ways to counter the Equestria Empire and retrieve the Elements of Harmony. At the same time, through Joyful Enterprises, he provided shelter and employment for thousands of persecuted ponies.
[zh.@DESC]
芝士三明治是欢乐集团的总裁和曾经小马利亚的头号派对专家，他一视同仁，致力于将笑容传递给这片大陆上的每一位善良的心灵。年幼的芝士三明治胆小而仿徨，与现在判若俩马，她背井离乡去寻找自己的使命，直到他在小马镇遇到了萍琪派和她举办的派对，芝士三明治从那一刻开始不但找到了自我，也与那个粉色的雌驹结下了不解之缘。
作为谐律们尤其是萍琪派的好友，芝士三明治为了更好的传播欢乐，他创立了欢乐集团和工厂，源源不断地为小马们带来快乐。终末之战后，芝士三明治虽然很少再为小马举办派对，但他仍然继续周游各个地区，暗中寻找可以对抗小马利亚帝国和找回谐律的办法。与此同时，他也通过欢乐集团为数以千计遭受迫害的小马提供庇护和工作。
