钻石浪涛公爵

[zh.@DESC]
钻石浪涛公爵有许多身份，她既是摩纳驹的最高统治者，也是摩纳驹魔法学院校长，更是塞拉斯蒂娅公主最可靠的盟友和伙伴之一。
她是一个严厉的教育家，因为她知道，危机永远不会事先告知自己的降临。她相信总有一天，她和她的朋友以及学生将面临着严峻的考验，因此她一直在为此做准备。
终末之战发生后，小马利亚全境陷入了和煦光流的掌控，而摩纳驹因为地理位置偏远成为了谐律的最后防线之一。
帝国与那些半人马们不可能永远忽视掉这里，为了这个美丽海滨地区的安危，也为了守护谐律并为她的挚友塞拉斯蒂娅公主复仇，现在她带领着所有杰出的学生一起再次为小马利亚而战。
[en.@DESC]
Duchess Diamond Waves is many things: the supreme ruler of Monacolt, the headmistress of Monacolt Magic Academy, and one of Princess Celestia’s most trusted allies and friends.
She is a strict educator, knowing that crises never announce their arrival. She believed that one day she, her friends, and her students will face a severe test, and she has been preparing for it the whole time.
After the Ultimate Battle, the whole of Equestria fell under the control of Cozy Glow, and Monacolt, due to its remote location, became one of the last lines of defense for Harmony.
The Empire and those centaurs will not be able to ignore this place forever. For the safety of this beautiful coastal area and to protect Harmony and avenge her friend Princess Celestia, she now leads all her outstanding students to fight for Equestria once again.
