[en.@DESC]
Banana Mash is the responsible horse of the Amusement Factory production line. She treats the products with great care, not only out of a serious and responsible attitude, but also out of gratitude for Cheese Sandwich. Before encountering Cheese Sandwich, the aftermath of the final battle destroyed her hometown, she lost her job and was forced to wander, living in Canterlot without a house to stay. Cheese Sandwich found her and brought her to the factory. With her outstanding management ability, she greatly improved the production efficiency and always remembered Cheese Sandwich’s kindness.
