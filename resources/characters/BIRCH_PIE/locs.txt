[en.@DESC]
Oak Pie and Acacia Pie are biological sisters but younger in age. Similarly, Oak Pie has more lineage from the Apple family. She possesses exceptional planting skills, especially when it comes to oak trees. Although she tends to have a somewhat irritable temperament, she becomes excited and practically immovable when she sees a party being organized.
