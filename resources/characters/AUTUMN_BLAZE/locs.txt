秋烨

[zh.@DESC]
秋烨从小就展现出非凡的语言天赋，著名作家、著名脱口秀演员、著名戏剧演员、导演、编剧、报纸编辑... 直到她，与其他麒麟一样，被雨晴禁止说话。
与其他麒麟接受“静默”的选择不同，她宁愿被驱逐出部族，也不愿放弃自己的声音；她宁愿数年生活在孤独之中自言自语，也不愿像其他麒麟一样安于现状。
秋烨的坚持最终得到了回报，苹果嘉儿与柔柔在友谊地图的指导下访问了麒麟社群，向麒麟们展示了友谊的力量，帮助秋烨解除了“静默”。也因此，秋烨成为了麒麟们心目中的准领袖，更重要的是，她重新成为了著名作家、著名脱口秀演员、著名戏剧演员、导演、编剧、报纸编辑...
[en.@DESC]
Autumn Blaze showed extraordinary language talent from an early age, a famous writer, a famous stand-up comedian, a famous actor, director, screenwriter, newspaper editor... Until she, like other Kirin, was forbidden to speak by Rain Shine.
Unlike other Kirins who chose to accept the “Silence”, she would rather be exiled from the tribe than give up her voice; she would rather live in solitude for years talking to herself, than be content with the status quo like other Kirins.
Autumn Blaze’s persistence eventually paid off. Applejack and Fluttershy visited the Kirin community under the guidance of the Friendship Map, showing the Kirins the power of friendship and helping Autumn Blaze lift the “Silence”. As a result, Autumn Blaze became the quasi-leader in the hearts of the Kirins, and more importantly, she became again a famous writer, a famous stand-up comedian, a famous actor, director, screenwriter, newspaper editor...
