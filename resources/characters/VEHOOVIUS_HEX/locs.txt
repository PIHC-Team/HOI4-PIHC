Vehoovius Hex
[zh.@NAME]
维蹄威秘法

[zh.@DESC]
许多落后的原始部落中的小马尚不理解魔法的运转原理，他们通过观察世界，用口口相传的方式将使用魔法的方法传承下去。其中可能会存在一些失真和神话，但其背后也隐式地蕴含着世界运转的原理。
比方说，马拉坡一带的居民们很早就发现了他们称之为“维蹄威神迹”的超自然现象：如果你选择在维蹄威火山口附近施展召唤术，召唤的方式是修建一座道场并在其中生活。保持耐心，然后你就会见证神迹：召唤术完成后十年内的某一天，你醒来时道场的地板会奇迹般地变成岩浆海。
[en.@DESC]
Many primitive tribes in the world do not understand the principles of magic, and they pass down the methods of using magic through their eyes and tribal traditions. There may be some distortions and myths, but they implicitly contain the principles of how the world works.
For example, the residents of the Marapore area have long discovered a supernatural phenomenon they call the “Vehoovius Hex”: if you choose to cast a summoning spell near the Vehoovius volcano, the way to summon is to build a dojo and live in it. Be patient, and you will witness the miracle: on a day within ten years after the summoning spell is completed, you will wake up to find the floor of the dojo miraculously transformed into a sea of lava.
