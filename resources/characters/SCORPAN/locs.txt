斯科潘

[zh.@DESC]
半人马国家的里的一只石像鬼，提雷克的弟弟，沃拉克国王与海顿王后的儿子。他有着一颗善良的心，但善良在提雷克那里并不算是受到欢迎的品质。斯科潘深爱着他的哥哥提雷克，但提雷克却厌恶斯科潘，厌恶他的懦弱、厌恶他的优柔寡断。
在得知提雷克入侵小马利亚的计划后，斯科潘再经过内心的反复纠结后，出于善良还是将这一消息偷偷告知了小马利亚的两位公主。最终，提雷克的邪恶计划失败，被关押在塔尔塔洛斯，而斯科潘则陷入纠结中 —— 他不知道自己究竟做得是对还是错。
后来，提雷克与和煦光流从监狱中逃出，再次对小马利亚发起了袭击。这一次，已经失去哥哥信任的斯科潘选择抛掷一枚硬币。最终，斯科潘选择了与硬币投掷相反的结果，因为他无法逃脱自己内心真正的选择 —— 对哥哥的爱。
他再也没说什么，他只是静静地看着提雷克凯旋归来，静静地看着父母被逼着退位，静静地看着那庞大的半人马哥哥座上皇位。
“祝贺你，哥哥。我为你感到开心。” 斯科潘在远处望着，小声说道。
[en.@DESC]
A gargoyle living in the country of centaurs, the younger brother of Tirek, and the son of King Vorak and Queen Haydon. He has a kind heart, but kindness is not a welcome quality in Tirek’s eyes. Scorpan loves his brother Tirek deeply, but Tirek despises Scorpan for his weakness and indecision.
After learning of Tirek’s plan to invade Equestria, Scorpan, after much inner turmoil, secretly informed the two princesses of Equestria of this news out of kindness. In the end, Tirek’s evil plan failed, and he was imprisoned in Tartarus, while Scorpan was left in a dilemma — he did not know whether he had done right or wrong.
Later, Tirek and Cozy Glow escaped from prison and launched another attack on Equestria. This time, Scorpan, who had lost his brother’s trust, chose to toss a coin. In the end, Scorpan chose the opposite result of the coin toss, because he could not escape the true feeling in his heart — his love for his brother.
He did not say anything more. He just quietly watched Tirek return in triumph, watched his parents forced to abdicate, and watched his huge centaur brother ascend the throne.
“Congratulations, brother. I am happy for you.” Scorpan whispered, from a distance.
