[en.@DESC]
Empress Cozy Glow had won everything she ever wanted through the war - power. But she seemed to dislike the perks of power - work.
Due to the suspicion and distrust of all other ponies, Empress Cozy Glow decided to centralize all the affairs of the Central Equestria into her own hooves, and she clearly underestimated the workload of the leader of Equestria. For a child who had not even finished her homework, the affairs of a nation were just too difficult.
She may know how to usurp a nation, but she doesn’t seem to know how to govern one.
