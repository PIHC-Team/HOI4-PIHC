前闪电天马学院学员，层云堡的骄傲之一。破空曾经以为自己是堪比云宝黛茜的飞行天才，直到... 他发现自己擅长飞行的原因是他的好友兼飞行搭档雾轨一直在帮助他稳定气流。幸运的是，破空真正的飞行技术也不算差，在闪电天马学院刻苦训练后，他的成绩也确实算是学员中最优秀的一批。好景不长，闪电天马学院的解散让破空与雾轨回到了老家，他们共同经营着一家小餐馆。破空这才发现了自己在商业经营中的天赋。
几年前，由于曾经在闪电天马学院的缘故，他们的餐馆愿意接纳“自由层云堡运动”成员，庇护他们躲避帝国的追捕。如今，“自由层云堡运动”取得阶段性胜利，他们的餐馆也称为层云堡居民的重要娱乐场所，生意兴隆。
[en.@DESC]
Former Wonderbolts Academy student, one of the prides of Stratusburg. Sky Stinger once thought he was a flying genius comparable to Rainbow Dash, until... he found out that the reason he was good at flying was that his friend / flight partner Vapor Trail had always helped him stabilize the airflow. Fortunately, his own flying skills are actually not so bad, and after hard training at the Wonderbolts Academy, his grades were indeed among the best students, without Vapor Trail’s help. Unfortunately, the dissolution of the Wonderbolts Academy brought Sky Stinger and Vapor Trail back to their hometown, where they run a small diner together. It was then that Sky Stinger discovered his talent for running a business.
A few years ago, due to their past at the Wonderbolts Academy, their diner was willing to accept members of the “Free Stratusburg Movement” and shelter them from the Empire’s pursuit. Now, the “Free Stratusburg Movement” has achieved a partial victory, and their diner has become an important entertainment venue for the residents of Stratusburg. Business is booming.
