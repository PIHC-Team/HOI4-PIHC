露娜公主

从千年的孤独中解脱，又花费了数年的时间与过去的自己和解，露娜公主的生活似乎刚刚就要走向正轨。她与姐姐修复了关系，她们即将享受和平的退休生活。但她们的退休未能获得太久的宁静，在终末之战后，小马国陷入动荡，两姐妹也锒铛入狱。
在塔尔塔洛斯监狱中，已经失去魔法的两姐妹只得依靠冥想来消磨时间，期盼着有一天能够重见天日。直到邪茧女王正在进攻塔尔塔洛斯，她们也趁着战火混乱逃出了监狱。奇怪的是，随着她们观察着外部的世界，她们似乎察觉到这已经不再是她们熟悉的世界。
也许是在监狱中的冥想期间，她们似乎离开了原本的世界，进入了另一个世界。塞拉斯蒂娅想起了曾经与麒麟领袖雨晴的对话，于是带着妹妹辗转来到了麒麟社群。
如今，随着千界之门的打通，她们终于回到了本世界线，她们已经做好准备纠正自己犯下的错误，让谐律重归小马国。
[en.@DESC]
Freed from a thousand years of loneliness and spending years reconciling with her past self, Princess Luna’s life seems to be on the right track. She and her sister have repaired their relationship, and they are about to enjoy a peaceful retirement. But their retirement did not last long. After the final battle, Equestria fell into chaos, and the two sisters were imprisoned.
In Tartarus, the two sisters, who had lost their magic, had to rely on meditation to pass the time, hoping to see the light of day again. Until Queen Chrysalis was attacking Tartarus, they also took advantage of the chaos of the war to escape from prison. Strangely, as they observed the outside world, they seemed to realize that this was no longer the world they were familiar with.
Perhaps during their meditation in prison, they seemed to have left their original world and entered another world. Celestia remembered a conversation she had with the Kirin leader Rainy, so she took her sister to the Kirin Commune.
Now, with the opening of the Culanika Lokadhātu, they have finally returned to this world. They are ready to correct their mistakes and restore harmony to Equestria.
