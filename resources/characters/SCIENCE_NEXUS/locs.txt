[en.@DESC]
An unexpected discovery in the exploration of the Crystal Cave - the Science Nexus, which contains a wealth of scientific and magical knowledge. The self-contained barrier can effectively protect the area.

Under the leadership of the Head of Research, Sunburst, the entire Research Department moved to the Science Nexus. The scientific research data inside and the environment where unicorns can use magic normally greatly helped the researchers to conduct research.
