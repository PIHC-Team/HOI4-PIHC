[zh.@DESC]
日光耀耀与星光熠熠在很小的时候便已结识并成为挚友。在发现自己的魔法天赋并离开星光到与星光重逢的很长一段时间中，他完成了一系列魔法课程，并前往重新出现的水晶帝国，成为了一名大魔法师。

终末之战后，日光耀耀逃亡至水晶帝国。直到三巨头开始征服各地，他意识到水晶帝国沦陷只是时间问题，自己会因为与暮光闪闪的关系而被清算。于是他离开了水晶帝国，在小马利亚尚未沦陷的地区辗转，最终到达了平等镇，去投靠他的挚友星光熠熠。现在他是星光熠熠身边幸存为数不多几个挚友之一，用自己的魔法知识贡献着一份前进的力量。
[en.@DESC]
Sunburst and Starlight have been friends since they were very young. After discovering his magical talent and leaving Starlight for a long time before reuniting with Starlight, he completed a series of magic courses and went to the re-emerged Crystal Empire to become a great sorcerer.
After the Ultimate Battle, Sunburst realized that the fall of the Crystal Empire was only a matter of time, and he would be liquidated because of his relationship with Twilight. So he left the Crystal Empire, traveled to the areas where Ponyville had not yet fallen, and finally arrived in Our Town to join his friend Starlight. Now he is one of the few surviving friends of Starlight, contributing to the power of progress with his magical knowledge.
