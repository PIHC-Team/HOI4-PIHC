乐天派

[zh.@DESC]
乐天派善于创造，他总有些新奇想法，能用气球吹出任何东西，时不时为镇上的小马们带来小惊喜。

凭借出其不意的创造力，乐天派被双钻举荐为陆军部长。他总能把点子变为战术，以出其不意的方式打击敌人：当敌人见到几枚气球向他们飞来时，他们就应该要跑了。
[en.@DESC]
Party Favor is a creative pony who always has a new idea up his sleeve. He can make anything out of balloons and loves to surprise the ponies of Ponyville with his creations.

With his unexpected creativity, Party Favor was recommended by Double Diamond to be the Head of Army. He can turn ideas into tactics and strike enemies in unexpected ways: when the enemy sees a few balloons flying towards them, they should run.
