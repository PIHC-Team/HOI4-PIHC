原油副产品
[en.@]
Crude Oil By-Products
[zh.@desc]
石油原油被称为液体黄金，因为它的价值不仅仅体现在燃料上，还可以用于生产各种化工产品和生活用品。
例如，塑料就是石油的副产品：我们可以用石油培养不挑食的精灵飞蝇 —— 他们什么都吃 —— 这些精灵飞蝇的分泌物可以用来制造塑料。
[en.@desc]
Crude oil is known as liquid gold because its value is not only reflected in fuel, but also in the production of various chemical products and daily necessities.
For example, plastic is a by-product of oil: we can use oil to cultivate the non-picky parasprites — they eat everything — and their secretions can be used to make plastic.
