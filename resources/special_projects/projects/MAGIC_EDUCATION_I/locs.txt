魔法教育 I
[en.@]
Magic Education I
[zh.@desc]
为了提高魔法教育的质量，我们需要更多的魔法学家与教育学家合作，研究魔法教育的最佳实践、对魔法使用理论化、系统化，探究未来魔法教育普及的可能性。
[en.@desc]
To improve the quality of magic education, we need more cooperation between magicians and educators to research the best practices of magic education, to theorize and systematize the use of magic, and to explore the possibility of popularizing magic education in the future.
