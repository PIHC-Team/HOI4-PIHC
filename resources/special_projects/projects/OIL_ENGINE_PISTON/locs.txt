高效活塞
[en.@]
Efficient Piston

[zh.@desc]
内燃机的活塞质量影响了发动机的性能，进而影响我们的坦克速度、使用寿命和燃油消耗。因此，我们提议研发更加润滑、耐磨且密封性好的活塞，以提高内燃机的效率。
我们预计高效活塞可以提高10%的坦克的最高速度，这意味着我们也许需要修改坦克的传动系统、以及我们城市马路交通规则中的最高速度限制。
[en.@desc]
The quality of the piston in the internal combustion engine affects the performance of the engine, which in turn affects the speed, service life, and fuel consumption of our tanks. Therefore, we propose to develop pistons that are more lubricated, wear-resistant, and have good sealing properties to improve the efficiency of the internal combustion engine.
We expect that the efficient piston can increase the maximum speed of the tank by 10%, which means that we may need to modify the transmission system of the tank and the maximum speed limit in our city road traffic rules.
