IC Engine Prototype
[zh.@desc]
蒸汽机作为小马工业的一个重大突破，超越了传统的魔法动力机器和劳动密集型的马力机械。然而，尽管它的贡献不可否认，蒸汽机也并非没有局限性。随着风之魔的威胁继续困扰着小马国，用于取暖煤炭的需求量也在不断增加，使得蒸汽机与炉火之间产生了竞争关系。
幸运的是，我们刚刚意识到一种长期以来被忽视的燃料：石油。虽然这些黑色液体并未被广泛用于生火，但它们已被证明是一种丰富的能源来源。通过利用石油的力量取代对煤炭的依赖，我们相信可以创造出一种能够再次彻底改变工业的新型发动机。至于为什么这个原型发动机被称为“内燃机”... 嗯... 这部分我们还在努力研究中。
[en.@desc]
The steam engine stands tall as a monumental breakthrough for Equestrian industries, surpassing traditional magic-powered machines and labor-intensive pony-driven mechanisms. However, despite its undeniable contributions, the steam engine is not without its limitations. As the Windigos of winter continue to threaten Equestria, coal is becoming increasingly demanded for heating, creating a competition between the steam engine and the hearth.
Luckily, we realized that another fuel source, oil, has been largely overlooked. While these black liquids have not been widely used for putting up fires, they have been proven to be a powerful source of energy. By harnessing the power of oil, a significant departure from the reliance on coal, we believe that we can create a new engine that can revolutionize the industry once again. As for why this prototype engine is called an “Internal Combustion Engine”... well... we are still working on that part.
