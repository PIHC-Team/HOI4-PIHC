蒸汽推进器
[en.@]
Steam Propeller
[zh.@desc]
自古以来，飞上天空就是小马们的梦想。据记载，曾经有一位木匠小马，将自己绑在椅子上，并在椅子上绑上了四门炮口冲下的礼花炮，试图靠此飞向天空。可惜的是，对地的礼花炮炸膛引发了巨大的爆炸，而不是飞上了天。爆炸后，围观的小马们都没有看到木匠小马的踪影，似乎已经在爆炸中消失了。这个故事告诉我们，飞上天空并不是一件容易的事情，需要科学的知识和技术的支持。如今，蒸汽推进器让小马们的梦想变得更加接近现实，可以让小马们更加安全地飞上天空。
哦，顺带一提，木匠小马的故事是真实的，他最后被一只路过的飞马救走了。
[en.@desc]
Since ancient times, soaring into the sky has been the dream of ponies. According to records, there was once a carpenter pony who tied himself to a chair with four confetti cannons tied to the chair, trying to fly into the sky. Unfortunately, the confetti cannons fired into the ground, causing a huge explosion instead of flying into the sky. After the explosion, the onlookers could not even see the carpenter pony anymore, as if he had disappeared in the explosion. This story tells us that flying into the sky is not an easy thing, it requires scientific knowledge and technological support. Today, the steam propeller makes the dream of ponies closer to reality, allowing ponies to fly into the sky more safely.
Oh, by the way, the story of the carpenter pony is true, and he actually was rescued by a passing pegasus.
