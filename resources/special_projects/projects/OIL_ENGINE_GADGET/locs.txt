内燃机零件改良
[en.@]
IC Engine Gadgets Improvement
[zh.@desc]
如今，内燃机已经成为了我们社会不可或缺的一部分，但它们的制作成本还是太高了，使用损耗也较大。我们需要研究如何改良内燃机的零件，使其更加耐用、更加节能。绝对不是偷工减料。
[en.@desc]
Nowadays, IC engines have become an indispensable part of our society, but their production costs are still too high, and their usage loss is also large. We need to research how to improve the parts of the IC engine to make it more durable and energy-saving. Definitely not cutting corners.
