风之魔正在让天气变得逐渐寒冷，我们需要用一种新的方式来保持温暖：友谊！
早在三族盟约时期，小马们的祖先就已经展示了他们是如何通过团结与友爱击退风之魔的，现在我们也需要这样做。
如何重拾这种友谊精神呢？我们首先需要一种新的城市建筑，它可以让小马们保持温暖 —— 因为只有在他们感到温暖时，才会更加友好，更容易结交新朋友。
[en.@desc]
The Windigo is making the weather colder and colder, we need a new way to keep warm: friendship!
Back in the days of the Three Tribes Alliance, the ancestors of the ponies showed how they could drive away the Windigo through unity and love, and now we need to do the same.
How can we rekindle this spirit of friendship? We need a new city building that can keep the ponies warm — because only when they feel warm will they be friendlier and more likely to make new friends.
