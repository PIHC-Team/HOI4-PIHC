[zh.@desc]
从油嘴滑舌开始，小马们就在思考如何用自动化设备替代马力进行生产 —— 当然，早期的半自动化设备也从未能脱离魔法的束缚。但是，随着科技的进步，小马们终于找到了一种能够完全替代马力的动力来源：蒸汽动力学。
使用魔法加热水，产生蒸汽，驱动机器，这是蒸汽机的基本原理。用蒸汽而不是魔法来驱动装备，我们就可以... 哦... 等等，不对，或许我们不该用魔法来加热水，而是用煤炭。
[en.@desc]
Ever since the invention of the oil nozzle, ponies have been thinking about how to replace horsepower with automated devices for production — though, early semi-automated devices have never been able to escape the shackles of magic. However, with the progress of technology, ponies have finally found a power source that can completely replace pony power: steam power.
The steam engine operates on the basic principle of using magic to heat water, generate steam, and drive machinery. By using steam instead of magic to power machinery, we can... oh... wait, no, that’s wrong. Maybe we shouldn’t use magic to heat water, but coal instead.
