厄灵召唤
[en.@]
Necromancy
[zh.@desc]
如果说魔法是一种艺术，那么死灵召唤就是其中最黑暗的一门。也许，所有已经离开这个世界的生物，都从未真正离开过我们。他们以另一种形式存在，继续在这个世界上游荡。
并且，无论他们生前的性格如何，这些通过复活术回来的厄灵都更擅长一件事：恶作剧。比如，拨动你的窗帘，敲翻你的马克杯，或者在学院认真学习两三年取得物理学学位、并考取专业电工证书以后到你的房间中把电灯电路改造成一闪一闪的样子。
[en.@desc]
If magic is an art, then necromancy is the darkest of them all. Perhaps, all the creatures that have left this world never truly leave us. They exist in another form, wandering in this world.
And, no matter what their personality was in life, these undead spirits are all good at one thing: mischief. Like, pulling your curtains, knocking over your mug, or after studying physics for two or three years in college to get a degree and a professional electrician certificate, they come to your room and change the lighting circuit to mess around with your light bulb powering circuit to make it flicker.
