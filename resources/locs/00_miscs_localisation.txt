[en.PIHC_WS_TAB_1_TXT]
§g“We once had magic, and friendship, but now, we don’t have either.”§!

What if Cozy Glow, Queen Chrysalis and Lord Tirek had won the final battle... What kind of world would it be?
In this alternate timeline scenario, the events diverged from MLP Season 9 Episode 24, where without help from ponies and other races from across Equestria, the M6 were defeated in the Ultimate Battle that determined the fate of Equestria. In the year 999, the “Big Three” villain alliance of Cozy Glow, Queen Chrysalis and Lord Tirek achieved victory in the final battle.
In the year 1001, the “Big Three” signed a treaty in Canterlot to divide Equestria:
Cozy Glow became the legitimate ruler of Equestria, self-proclaimed as the “Empress of Friendship”, using the absolute power of the alicorn and her seductive rhetoric to establish a loose feudal state on the ruins of Equestria.
After conquering the Changeling Empire by force, Queen Chrysalis cleared all opposition and restored her unquestionable, supreme leadership, turning the entire Changeling race back to their original unhatched state.
Lord Tirek was not obsessed with power, but more obsessed with strength - he used the Grogar’s Bell to take away half of Equestria’s magic, returned to his homeland of the centaurs, and enslaved all the creatures around his homeland.
The M6 and the two former Princesses were imprisoned in Tartarus - the same place that once held Tirek and Cozy. Different now, the M6 and princesses were separately imprisoned.
While ponies like Starlight Glimmer or Discord lost their magic, they escaped the chaos of the final battle and united disgruntled ponies across Equestria to organize resistance forces; however, most creatures had accepted their failure and their tragic fate...

[zh.PIHC_WS_TAB_3_HEADER]
高堡奇驹模组介绍
[en.PIHC_WS_TAB_3_HEADER]
The Pony In The High Castle Mod Introduction
[zh.PIHC_WS_TAB_3_TXT]
1. 高堡奇驹模组主要为剧情向模组，游戏中仅允许通过国策、事件和决议进行外交，不允许正当化战争目标和进行一些结盟行为。通过国策进行的剧情向外交是游戏的重要内容。不过，玩家可以选择通过使用控制台或战犯决议进行宣战。
2. 高堡奇驹模组对武器科技树进行了完全的重做，在模组中，科技的发展是非常迅速的（两三年的科技可以产生巨大的代差），同时也是缓慢的（所有国家的科学研究速度都被减缓了），这意味着通过事件、国策、决议等方式进行科研更加高效。
3. 模组主要关注于小马利亚中央大陆（小马利亚），其次为幻形灵大陆（幻形灵尼亚），对于狮鹫大陆（狮鹫尼加）和麒麟大陆（麒麟索尔）的关注较少。
4. 高堡奇驹模组中的建筑价格存在小幅调整；资源种类、分布和使用方式相对于钢4本体存在较大调整，玩家可以通过查看资源的说明来了解各类资源的使用方式。
5. 整合了地主模组：在地块视图右上角，可以展开菜单，支付政治点来取得一个地区的核心。制造宣称是不允许的。
6. 在屏幕右侧可以打开国家的物品栏，其中可能含有可以使用的物品、装备、剧情文本、关键道具等。
[en.PIHC_WS_TAB_3_TXT]
1. The PIHC mod is story-oriented, thus the game only allows diplomacy through national focus, events, and decisions, and does not allow war goals and some alliance behaviors. Story-oriented diplomacy through national focus is an important part of the game. However, players can choose to declare war through the use of the console or the war criminal decision.
2. The PIHC mod has completely redone the weapon technology tree. In the mod, the development of technology is very rapid (two or three years of technology can produce a huge difference), and it is also slow (the scientific research speed of all countries has been slowed down), which means that scientific research is more efficient through events, national focus, and decisions.
3. The mod mainly focuses on the central continent of Equestria (Equestria), followed by the continent of the Changelings (Changelinia), with less focus on the continent of the Griffons (Griffonica) and the continent of the Kirins (Kirinthal).
4. The PIHC mod has made minor adjustments to the price of buildings; the types, distribution, and usage of resources have been greatly adjusted compared to the base game of HoI4. Players can learn about the usage of various resources by viewing the description of the resources.
5. Integrated landlord mod: at the top right of the state view, you can expand the menu, pay political power to obtain a core of a state. It is not allowed to create claims.
6. On the right side of the screen, you can open the country’s inventory, which may contain items, equipment, story texts, key props, etc. that can be used.
