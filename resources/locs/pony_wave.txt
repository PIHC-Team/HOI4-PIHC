Pony Wave Offensive

[en.human_wave_offensive_desc]
A Pony Wave Offensive is an infantry tactic in which an attacker conducts a frontal assault with densely concentrated infantry formations against the enemy line, with the intent to overrun the defenders by engaging in melee combat.

[en.large_front_offensive_desc]
By conducting, or threatening, Pony Wave Offensives across multiple areas of the front we can prevent the enemy from concentrating his firepower against any single attack.
