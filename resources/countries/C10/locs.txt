Where does Chancellor Neighsay’s true loyalty lie? This is a question that no pony has ever asked, but Chancellor Neighsay often asks himself.
If Chancellor Neighsay is loyal to the princesses, then he would never choose to work with the Empire led by Cozy Glow, or the Royal Committee that she has already controlled; if Chancellor Neighsay is loyal to Harmonism, then he would never allow the Empire to violate and distort Harmonism definitions; but if Chancellor Neighsay is only loyal to his own interests, then he would certainly not maintain his tough stance in front of Cozy Glow.

Where does Chancellor Neighsay’s true loyalty lie? This is a question that no pony has ever asked, but Chancellor Neighsay often asks himself.
In the previous era, Chancellor Neighsay once firmly believed that friendship was a privilege exclusive to ponies. After the Friendship School of the M6, he began to recognize the cross-racial friendship thoughts of the M6. But soon, the failure of the Ultimate Battle made him begin to rethink and deny the M6.
During the Ultimate Battle, Chancellor Neighsay first identified the “Big Three” as a threat to Equestria. After the war, he blamed the failure on the disunity of the ponies. Finally, he believed that his failure to enforce EEA regulations was all his own fault.
When facing Cozy Glow, Chancellor Neighsay first insisted that the EEA regulations were sacred and could not be violated, even if it meant confronting Cozy Glow. He was forced to make minor reforms to the EEA, with strong objection of Cozy Glow and the neo-Harmonism. But he was then surprised to find that he had gradually begun to spontaneously defend Cozy Glow’s new regulations without coercion.

Where does Chancellor Neighsay’s true loyalty lie? This is a question that no pony has ever asked, but Chancellor Neighsay often asks himself.
Chancellor Neighsay is not a pony who only pursues power or interests, but his contradictory position has convinced him that he has nothing to do with “friendship” or “harmony”.
He is perhaps just an embodiment of the regulations, loyal only to the “rules” themselves, a fundamentalist defender of the “rules”, who does not care remotely about what these rules actually are...
