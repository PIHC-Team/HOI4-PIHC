[zh.C23_DESC]
风暴大王的铁骑折戟于坎特洛特之后，那个曾经令周边大小国家胆寒的帝国一夜之间崩塌。风暴岛，这片孤悬于南方大洋中的贫瘠之地在风暴大王死后就成为了危险的战场，军阀混战，匪患猖獗。来自各地各派的野心家与探险者们因各种目的云集于帝国的尸骸之上，混乱与战争是这一时期风暴岛的主题。终末之战后，大量小马利亚难民的到来继续冲击着岛上脆弱的稳定，使得当地爆发了空前的种族矛盾。在外界看来，这里是一片充斥着饥饿与战乱的世间炼狱。风暴岛上的各族似乎注定会在无休止的杀伐中走向消亡。

但无论在多么灰暗的日子里，都存在着一抹亮光。一匹琥珀色的独角兽在凄风苦雨中穿行，为困苦的民众带去安慰，她年轻的心中构思着一个庞大的理想。在深山中，前风暴大王副官纷争团结起一众势力，誓要为风暴岛带来一个全新的时代，即使那需要付出伤痛和鲜血。在传送门的另一侧，一个危险的阴谋也正在酝酿。

现在是1002年末，风暴岛依旧处于混乱。一边是支援抵抗，重现谐律的愿望。一边是渴望民族解放、和解与独立自由的理想。这片小小的苦寒之地无法承载两个同样沉重的梦想。一切将被托付于时间，而命运将告诉我们，这片苦寒之地和她受尽苦难的民众的命运将走向何方。
[en.C23_DESC]
After Storm King defeated in Canterlot, his Empire that once made the surrounding countries tremble in fear collapsed overnight with him. Storm Island, a barren land hanging in the southern ocean, became a dangerous battlefield after the death of the Storm King, with warlords fighting and bandits rampant. Ambitious ursurpers and adventurers from all over the world gathered on the ruins of the empire for one reason or another, choosing chaos and war as the theme of Storm Island during this period. After the Ultimate Battle, the arrival of a large number of refugees from Equestria continued to impact the fragile stability of the island, leading to unprecedented racial conflicts. In the eyes of the outside world, this is a purgatory filled with hunger and war, destined to perish in endless killing.

But no matter how dark the days are, there is a glimmer of light. An amber-colored unicorn walks through the bleak wind and rain, bringing comfort to the sufferings, and her young heart conceives a grand ideal. Meanwhile, in the mountains, Strife, the former second-in-command of the Storm King, rallied various factions, vowing to bring a new era to Storm Island, even if it required pain and bloodshed. On the other side of the portal, a dangerous conspiracy was also brewing.

By the end of 1002, Storm Island remained in chaos. On one side was the desire to support the resistance and restore harmony; on the other was the longing for racial liberation, reconciliation and independence. This small, frigid land could not bear the weight of two equally heavy dreams. Everything would be left to time, and fate would reveal the destiny of this harsh land and its long-suffering citizens.

After Storm King defeated in Canterlot, his Empire that once made the surrounding countries tremble in fear collapsed overnight with him. Storm Island, a barren land hanging in the southern ocean, became a dangerous battlefield after the death of the Storm King, with warlords fighting and bandits rampant. Ambitious ursurpers and adventurers from all over the world gathered on the ruins of the empire for one reason or another, choosing chaos and war as the theme of Storm Island during this period. After the Ultimate Battle, the arrival of a large number of refugees from Equestria continued to impact the fragile stability of the island, leading to unprecedented racial conflicts. In the eyes of the outside world, this is a purgatory filled with hunger and war, destined to perish in endless killing.

Be that as it may, a small island that has lost hope still attracts fresh blood to participate in this sacrifice, thanks to the huge profits that come with huge risks. Just shortly before the United Resistance’ first uprise, the remnants of the Royal Guard of Equestria, who had been exiled here, quickly unified most of the island using the military power left over from the Celestia era, forming the §g“Free Equestria”§!. They launched an overconfident expedition to claim Equastria back, but that grand attack ended in a disastrous defeat. The failure of the war deeply shocked every resident from ponies to storm creatures, and the government of Free Equestria began to split. A few months later, a conscription incident quickly escalated into a conflict that spread across the island, pushing this alien pony military regime to the brink of collapse.

But no matter how dark the days are, there is a glimmer of light. An amber-colored unicorn walks through the bleak wind and rain, bringing comfort to the sufferings, and her young heart conceives a grand ideal. Just when the new regime was on the verge of collapse, §O Sunset Shimmer§!, the former student of Princess Celestia who had been missing for years, suddenly appeared on the island. With her efforts and her unique deep insight into others’ hearts, the various forces on Storm Island briefly united. They eventually signed the §g“Declaration of New Ponyville”§!, officially forming a democratic government, turning a new page. After months of discussion among the various forces, Sunset Shimmer was elected as the §O interim Prime Minister §! of Free Equestria in a landslide. However, the new democratic government still found itself powerless in the face of the current situation. Free Equestria continued to sink deeper into the quagmire of division and racial conflict. Sunset Shimmer, exhausted both physically and mentally, faced endless internal government disputes and increasingly widening racial tensions. The citizens of Free Equestria gazed out at the cold tundra, clinging to the hope of a beautiful yet distant future. Meanwhile, in the mountains, Strife, the former second-in-command of the Storm King, rallied various factions, vowing to bring a new era to Storm Island, even if it required pain and bloodshed. On the other side of the portal, a dangerous conspiracy was also brewing.

By the end of 1002, Storm Island remained in chaos. On one side was the desire to support the resistance and restore harmony; on the other was the longing for racial liberation, reconciliation and independence. This small, frigid land could not bear the weight of two equally heavy dreams. Everything would be left to time, and fate would reveal the destiny of this harsh land and its long-suffering citizens.
