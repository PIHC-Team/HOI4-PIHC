[zh.C44_DESC]
在小马利亚的历史上，皇家卫队（E.U.P.）、闪电天马队与天角兽公主是主要的防卫力量，而从未有过成规模的海军。在偶链群岛的这批海军，其实严格意义上来说并非小马利亚海军部队，其前身为小马利亚皇家军事学院，由从石柱阵中苏醒的长老闪电马格努斯担任校长。那时，油嘴滑舌两兄弟的洛马基度假村生意刚刚小有起色，他们开始在西小马利亚倒卖物资，引起了塞拉斯蒂娅公主的注意。为监管洛马基的经营、治理列奥塔森林，随着公主一声令下，一批皇家卫队进驻瞭望塔。皇家军事学院也在那里建立了分部，并承担起皇家卫队的海运后勤工作。就这样，在南露娜洋的群岛上，少量皇家卫队老兵与教官，和许许多多可爱标记中都有着海军天赋的大量年轻学院，组成了这支名为“海军”的海运后勤部。

令马唏嘘的是，旧小马利亚政权倒台、瞭望塔的驻军解散时... 这批远驻偶链群岛的成员似乎被遗忘了。他们从未接到通知，只是随着工作一天比一天疑惑：为什么再也没有新的船只在码头停靠？为什么再也没有新的命令传达？偶链群岛资源贫瘠，失去了本土的物资供应后海军生活及其困难。他们甚至开始与海盗进行物资交换与合作... 直到洛马基与自由小马利亚的探险者们先后来到这片群岛，他们才终于意识到 —— 外界已经不再是过去他们所熟悉的那个小马利亚了。

[en.C44_DESC]
In the history of Equestria, the Royal Guard (E.U.P.), the Wonderbolts, and the Alicorn Princesses were the main defense forces, and there has never been an organized navy. This so-called navy on the Evenchain Isles was not strictly a navy department of Equestria but rather a branch of the former Royal Military Academy of Equestria, led by the Pillar Flash Magnus woke up from the Ponehenge. At the time, the Flim and Flam Brothers had just started their Las Pegasus resort business and began circulating goods around Western Equestria, attracting the attention of Princess Celestia. To supervise the operation of Las Pegasus and clean up the Leota Forest, with the Celestia’s order, a group of Royal Guards started to station at the Watch Tower. The Royal Military Academy also established a branch there and took over the maritime logistics of the Royal Guard. As a result, a small number of Royal Guard veterans and instructors, along with many young students with naval talents as their cutie marks, formed this maritime logistics department that is called “The Navy”.

What is a pity is that when the old Equestria regime fell and the garrison at the Watch Tower was disbanded... these members who were stationed on the Evenchain Isles seemed to be forgotten. They never received any notice, just growing more and more confused as the days went by: Why were there no new ships docking at the pier? Why were there no new orders being issued? The Evenchain Isles were resource-poor, and after losing the supply of goods from mainland, the navy life became difficult. They even started to exchange goods with pirates... This continued until Las Pegasus and Free Equestria explorers came to the isles one after another, and the sailors finally realized that the outside world was no longer the Equestria they were familiar with.
