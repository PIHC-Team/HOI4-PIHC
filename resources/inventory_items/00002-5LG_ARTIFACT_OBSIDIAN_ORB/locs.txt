Obsidian Orb

[zh.@DESC]
繁殖往往是生物所具有的独特才能，但幸运，又或者说不幸的是，经过风暴魔法的干预，这种能力也被赋予了黑曜石法球。被法球碎裂的碎片击中的生物，并不会受到伤害或是死亡，而是会被同化，成为黑曜石家族的一员。
所以，请注意小心保管，这种生命形式并不会对它的持有者开恩。它们的造物主当年的下场，就是最好的例证。
[en.@DESC]
Reproduction is often a unique ability possessed by creatures, but fortunately, or unfortunately, this ability has also been endowed with the Obsidian Orb through the intervention of storm magic. The creatures hit by the fragments shattered by the orb will not be harmed or killed, but will be assimilated and become a member of the obsidian family.
So please be careful to keep it safe, this kind of life form will not be merciful to its owner. The fate of their creator is the best example.
