[zh.@DESC]
一枚似乎是来自鞍拉伯的廉价纪念品。这枚胸针由一块良好的天然宝石的一半制成，原本是两枚吊坠中的一枚，损坏后被重新设计成胸针。
胸针表面刻有象征强大魔力的十字星，与星光的可爱标记十分相似。星光在几乎所有公共场合都会将这枚胸针佩戴在胸前，但却鲜有小马注意到。
[en.@DESC]
A seemingly cheap souvenir from Saddle Arabia, this brooch is half of a fine natural gemstone that was originally part of a pair of pendants. It was redesigned into a brooch after being damaged.
The brooch is engraved with a cross star symbolizing powerful magic, similar to the cute mark of Starlight. Starlight wears this brooch on her chest in almost all public occasions, but few ponies notice it.
