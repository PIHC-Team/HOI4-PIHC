关于坎特洛特高档香水公司:
1. 坎特洛特是一家帝国公司，小马谷是帝国的一部分。
2. 坎特洛特的雇员是小马谷居民，不是帝国员工。
3. 小马谷不存在帝国警察，小马谷警察不是帝国警察。遇到紧急情况请呼叫小马谷警察而不是帝国警察。
4. 坎特洛特员工的工作仅包括香水销售和仓储管理，不包括任何其他工作。请在小马谷警察询问时提供这一真实情况。

About Canterlot Luxury Perfume Company:
1. Canterlot is an imperial company, and Ponyville is part of the Empire.
2. Canterlot employees are residents of Ponyville, not Empire staff.
3. There is no imperial police in Ponyville, and Ponyville police are not imperial police. In case of emergency, please call Ponyville police instead of imperial police.
4. The work of Canterlot employees is limited to perfume sales and warehouse management, and does not include any other work. Please provide this information to the Ponyville police when asked.
