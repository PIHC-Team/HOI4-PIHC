Diamond of Lapis-Lux

[zh.@DESC]
我们尚不清楚它和一个普通的钻石有什么区别：勒克斯钻石的大小并不算大，形状并不特殊，唯独折射率有点异常的低... 所以搞不好可能是块玻璃。

[en.@DESC]
We are not sure what makes it different from a regular diamond: the Diamond of Lapis-Lux is not particularly large, nor is it of a special shape, but it does have an unusually low refractive index...
So it might just be a piece of glass.
But as the saying goes, the value of a diamond is simple to judge: the more it looks like glass, the more valuable it is, provided it is not glass.
