Somnambula Glowpaz

[zh.@DESC]
这种绿色鲜亮的宝石（别担心，没有辐射性，这又不是那种绿色发亮光就代表有辐射的子供向动画），早在长老时代沙南布拉就佩戴过用这种耀石制成的项链，如今也成为了同名村落沙南布拉村的特产。
卡巴雷隆博士曾经来到这个村子，尝试偷窃这些耀石。当那装满耀石的袋子在众目睽睽之下被打开的时候，卡巴雷隆博士知道他搞砸了，他犯下了一个严重的罪行 — 让村外的小马知道了这种“稀有”耀石原来是量产的。
[en.@DESC]
This bright green gemstone (don’t worry, it’s not radioactive, this isn’t that kind of cartoon for kids where green glowing things are always radioactive), was once worn as a necklace by Somnambula in the Pillars era, and has since become a specialty of the village of the same name, Somnambula.
Dr. Caballeron once came to the village and attempted to steal these glowpaz. When the bag full of glowpaz was opened in front of everypony, Dr. Caballeron knew he had messed up, committing a serious crime of letting the ponies outside the village know that these “rare” glowpaz were actually mass-produced.
