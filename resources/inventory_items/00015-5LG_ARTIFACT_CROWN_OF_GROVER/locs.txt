[en.@DESC]
One of the six ancient artifacts.
Collecting all six artifacts won’t let you shoot rainbow lasers like the Elements of Harmony, so it doesn’t sound like it’s worth it.
The only benefit of collecting all six artifacts is that you can use them to play a treasure hunt game, so don’t bother collecting them, because you absolutely can’t use them to absorb all the magic in the world.
