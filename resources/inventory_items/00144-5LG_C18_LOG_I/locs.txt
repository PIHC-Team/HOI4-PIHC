[zh.@DESC]
一本在泥土中发现的日志本，上面记录着一位矿工和一位科学家对于一面奇特的水晶墙的研究。
日志上有着残留的魔法气息：这意味着日志曾经被某种魔法力量加密过，但是现在魔法已经过期了，日志的内容变得可读。

[en.@DESC]
A logbook found in the dirt, recording a miner and a scientist’s research on a peculiar crystal wall.
The log has a residual magical aura: this means the log was once encrypted by some magical power, but now the magic has expired, and the content of the log has become readable.
