[zh.@DESC]
一枚似乎是来自鞍拉伯的廉价纪念品。这枚胸针由一块良好的天然宝石的一半制成，原本是两枚吊坠中的一枚，后来被重新设计成胸针。
胸针被改成月亮的形状，与特丽克西的可爱标志一样，估计也是魔术表演的象征。伟大又全能的特丽克西其实对饰品并不感兴趣，因为她本身就已经足够美貌了。不过特丽克西依然还是时不时会佩戴这个胸针，也许是为了炫耀自己曾经在鞍拉伯广受欢迎的表演。
[en.@DESC]
A seemingly cheap souvenir from Saddle Arabia, this brooch is half of a fine natural gemstone that was originally part of a pair of pendants before being redesigned into a brooch.
The brooch is shaped like a crescent moon, much like Trixie’s cutie mark, and is likely a symbol of her magic performances. The great and powerful Trixie isn’t really interested in accessories, as she’s already beautiful enough on her own. However, Trixie still occasionally wears this brooch, perhaps to brag about her once-popular performances in Saddle Arabia.
