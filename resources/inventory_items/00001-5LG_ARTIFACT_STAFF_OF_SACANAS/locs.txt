Some creature has always been unpredictable, plundering thousands of gold, building fleets that cover the sky, conquering one land after another, absorbing all the magic of the alicorns... When all the powerful magic in the world converges on this small staff, the supreme king chooses to use it to flirt with the sun and the moon.
The Staff of Sacanas hopes that its next owner can use its power more wisely, rather than treating all of this as a game like its predecessor. At least, be more mature.
