一本沾满泥土的魔术师笔记本，由安全部特工从马哈顿城外一片废旧矿区中找到。
前面半本笔记记载着许多魔术技巧的秘密，夹杂着平时的日记。
后面则没有多少魔术技巧了，只有一些充满污泥和油渍的日记。
[en.@DESC]
A muddy notebook belonging to a magician, found by a Security Department special agent in an abandoned mining area outside Manehattan.
The first half of the notebook contains many secrets of magic tricks, interspersed with daily diary entries.
The second half contains almost no magic tricks, only some diary entries full of mud and oil stains.
