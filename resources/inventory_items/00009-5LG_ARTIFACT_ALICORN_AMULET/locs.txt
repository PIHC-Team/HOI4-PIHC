It blesses the wearer with “untold powers”. However, the more the bearer uses the amulet, the more it corrupts them. The fact that it is still circulating in the world is a sufficient proof that no pony has ever truly mastered its power.
So, the choice is yours. Take it and become a contender to dominate Equestria, avoiding the fate of the past contenders; or leave it and be a normal but kind pony.
