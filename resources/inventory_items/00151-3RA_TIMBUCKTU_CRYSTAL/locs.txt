廷马克图水晶

[en.@]
Timbucktu Crystal

[zh.@DESC]
这种原产于廷马克图的水晶非常具有观赏价值，其内部似夜空的星辰一般闪烁着光芒。一方面，在层云堡本地的传说中，廷马克图水晶被称作“诅咒水晶”，会带来厄运。但另一方面，采矿业投机商们将原有的故事略加改动，称其为“许愿水晶”，并声称这种水晶可以满足配戴者的合理愿望。无论是哪种说法，仅仅是从其外观来看，制成水晶首饰都一定大有市场。可以买来送给朋友、情侣与家庭，当然也可以送给那些你只想表面讨好实则憎恨的小马，前提是如果你相信“诅咒水晶”的说法的话。
[en.@DESC]
This crystal, originating from Timbucktu, is very ornamental, with the inside shining like the stars in the night sky. On the one hoof, in the local legend of Cloudsdale, the Timbucktu crystal is called the “cursed crystal” and is said to bring bad luck. On the other hoof,speculative miners have slightly modified the original story, calling it the “wishing crystal” and claiming that this crystal can fulfill the reasonable wishes of the wearer. Regardless of which version is true, just from its appearance, crystal jewelry made from it is sure to be popular. You can buy it to give to friends, lovers, and family. You can also give it to those ponies you hat but need to pretend to like, that is, if you believe in the “cursed crystal” version of course.
