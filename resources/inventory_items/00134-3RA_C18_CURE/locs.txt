逆麟解药

[en.@]
Nirik Cure

[zh.@DESC]
即使是在日常生活中，麒麟也常常会因为愤怒转化为逆麟，这是正常的现象无需过度担忧。然而，有一些逆麟转化是永久的、不可逆的，这时就需要政府及时介入治疗，以避免怒火在麒麟中传染。
雨晴首先提出使用静默溪的溪水来治疗逆麟，然而这会导致后遗症——“静默”，恢复的麒麟将不再能发出声音。随后，秋烨发现花朵“稚马之息”可以治疗“静默”，因此，一桶静默溪水配合一枝稚马之息，就成为了逆麟的解药。

[en.@DESC]
Even in daily life, Kirin often turn into Nirik due to anger, which is a normal phenomenon and does not need to be overly concerned. However, some Nirik transformations are permanent and irreversible, and then the government needs to intervene in time to avoid the contagion of anger among the Kirins.
Rain Shine first proposed to use the water from the Stream of Silence to treat Nirik, but this will lead to sequelae — “Silence”, which means that the recovered Kirin will no longer be able to make a sound. Later, Autumn Blaze discovered that the flower “Foal’s Breath” can treat “Silence”, so a barrel of water from the Stream of Silence together with a foal’s breath became the cure for Nirik.
