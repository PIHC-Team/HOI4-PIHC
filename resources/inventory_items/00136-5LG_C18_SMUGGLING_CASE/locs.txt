[en.@DESC]
The national “de-Silencing” project, which was originally planned to be consume three years output of foal’s breath planting, actually consumed five years of output. During this period, the Eastern Region, which accounts for only 15% of the national Kirin population, consumed 40% of the foal’s breath of the entire country! Are there Kirins who are privately selling foal’s breath for profit during the “de-Silencing” project? We need an answer.
