发展债券

[en.@]
Development Bond

[zh.@DESC]
最早由名流谷在战后重建期间提出的理念。受到洛马基等多个周边地区的支持，并得到了皇家委员会和后来的帝国财政部默许。
由于名流谷、洛马基与帝国的巨大金融影响力，这种债券有着极高的保值性，因此也常常被当作交易货币；如今，发展债券已经成为了西小马国的主要交换物，由名流谷及拥有其授权的地区管控发行量与回收量。
于是，我们生活在这样一个新奇的时代，一个名不见经传的边缘小村庄内的一个负责债券事务的小办公室书呆子职员，可以戴着眼镜拿着写字板，决定整个西小马国的经济走向。这不是很神奇吗？
[en.@DESC]
A concept first proposed by Sire’s Hollow during the post-war reconstruction period, supported by multiple surrounding regions such as Las Pegasus, and tacitly approved by the Royal Commission and later the Equestria Empire Ministry of Finance.
Due to the enormous financial influence of Sire’s Hollow, Las Pegasus, and the Empire, this bond has extremely high value retention, and is often used as a trading currency; today, Development Bonds have become the main exchange medium of western Equestria, with the issuance and redemption controlled by Sire’s Hollow and regions authorized by it.
Thus, we live in such a novel era, where a nerdy office clerk in a small office in an obscure village on the edge of the world, wearing glasses, holding a clipboard, can decide the economic direction of the entire western Equestria. Isn’t that magical?
