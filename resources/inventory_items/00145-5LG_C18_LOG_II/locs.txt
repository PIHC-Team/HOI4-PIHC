[zh.@DESC]
在麒麟之森的青草住处找到了一本日志本，日志被青草使用他自己的魔法加密，我们只有通过解密才能阅读日志的内容。
奇怪的是，这本日志本的风格完全不像是青草的平日爱好，他似乎不会使用这样的日志本。这种日志本肯定属于另一只麒麟。
仔细分析，我们意识到这本日志的魔法封印与繁花的日志本完全不同，这其中似乎混杂着三只麒麟的魔法密码。

[en.@DESC]
A logbook found at the residence of Grass-Green in the Kirin Forest, the log was encrypted by Grass-Green using his own magic, and we can only read the content of the log through decryption.
Strangely, the style of this logbook is completely unlike Grass-Green’s usual taste, he seems unlikely to use such a logbook. This logbook must belong to another Kirin.
Upon careful analysis, we realized that the magic passwords of this logbook is completely different from Blossom Burst’s logbook, and it seems to be mixed with the magic passwords of three Kirins.
