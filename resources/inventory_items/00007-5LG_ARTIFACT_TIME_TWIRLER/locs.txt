溯时护符

[en.@]
Time Twirler

[zh.@DESC]
这种神奇的护符可以将一整个城市大的区域封锁在一天的循环之内，让时间在这个区域内不停倒流到前一天。这个护符的使用者则可以在这个区域内自由穿梭，不会受到时间倒流的影响。
不过值得注意的是，由于溯时护符之前被踩碎了一次，所以可能没有原来那么好用了。
无论如何，一位曾经受到这种护符的控制，但有幸逃脱了影响的逃生者特别指出：“请记住，如果你和你的朋友被困在了一天的时间循环内，一定要注意让你和你的朋友远离酥脆肉桂汁吉事果。”

[en.@DESC]
This magical amulet can trap an entire city-sized area in a day-long loop, causing time to flow backwards to the previous day within that area. The user of this amulet can move freely within this area without being affected by the time reversal.
However, it is worth noting that since the Time Twirler was previously crushed once, it may not be as useful as it used to be.
Anyway, one survivor who was once controlled by this amulet but managed to escape its influence has a special warning: “Remember, if you and your friends are trapped in a day-long time loop, be sure to keep you and your friends away from cinnamon-dipped churros.”
