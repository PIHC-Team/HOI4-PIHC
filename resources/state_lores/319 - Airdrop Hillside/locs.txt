空降坡过高的海拔让这里的积雪如糖块屋的蛋糕奶油一般厚，因此空降可能是唯一可以抵达这里的选项，这处山坡因此玩笑般地得名。只不过，鲜有小马知道，空降坡中有一条秘密通道连向早已废弃的小马-狮鹫火车线，曾经是连接小马利亚大陆与狮鹫尼加大陆的唯一陆上交通线。
据不可靠的史料记载，这条山中的隧道在天马帝国时期就已经建成，随着风之魔入侵，天马帝国西迁，这铁路线就停止了维护。在小马利亚王国的最后几年里，为改善与狮鹫王国的关系，这里的铁轨曾经进行了短暂的重修和运营，但随后不久风之魔就又一次摧毁了这里。风雪很快吞没了整个山谷，如果曾经有小马在此居住，如今也绝对失去了所有痕迹。
真正将这荒无人烟的雪山坡变得小有名气的是比较离谱的两段阴谋论故事。一段声称当星光熠熠还是一只以铁蹄管理着小村庄时，就早已计划好了一张遍布小马利亚东北部的地下网络用于逃脱，空降坡则是其中关键的一环，给予了她退向狮鹫大陆的通道；另一段则更加不着边际，声称天马帝国当年并未举国西迁，有一半的天马留在了这里与寒冷对抗，还有少数天马血统不太纯洁的“贱民”与独角兽们向东逃往了狮鹫尼加大陆；更加离谱的是，有的小马认为这里的隧道另一头并非火车线，而是连接着天空中的星座，那些留守的天马们最终飞升到了星空之中。

The high altitude of Airdrop Hillside gives it a thick layer of snow like the frosting on cakes from sugar cube corner, making airdrop the only viable option to reach here. The hillside is thus jokingly named. However, few ponies know that there is a secret passage in Airdrop Hillside leading to the long-abandoned Pony-Griffon railway line, which was once the only land route connecting the Equestria continent and the Griffonica continent.
According to unreliable historical records, this mountain tunnel was built during the Pegasi Empire period. With the invasion of the Windigos and the westward migration of the Pegasi Empire, the railway line ceased to be maintained. In the last years of the Equestria Kingdom, to improve relations with the Griffon Kingdom, the railway tracks here were briefly repaired and operated, but soon after, the Windigos destroyed it once again. The snow quickly engulfed the entire valley, and if any ponies had lived here before, all traces would have been lost by now.
What really made this deserted snowy hillside somewhat famous were two rather far-fetched conspiracy theories. One claimed that when Starlight Glimmer was still a mare managing a small village with an iron hoof, she had already planned an underground network throughout northeastern Equestria for escape, with Airdrop Hillside being a key part, providing a route to retreat to Griffonica; the other was even more outlandish, claiming that the Pegasi Empire did not migrate westward as a whole, with half of the Pegasi remaining here to fight against the cold, and some “lowborn” pegasi with impure bloodlines and unicorns fleeing eastward to Griffonica; even more absurdly, some ponies believed that the other end of the tunnel was not the railway line, but connected to the constellations in the sky, and that the remaining pegasi had ultimately ascended to the stars.
