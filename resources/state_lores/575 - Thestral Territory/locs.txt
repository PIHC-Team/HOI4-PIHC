夜骐领地，位于塔尔塔洛斯西侧的一篇夜骐族聚居区。这里的夜骐们生活在永恒的夜晚中，那不是因为缺乏阳光或是睡懒觉不想起床，而是因为他们的忠诚如同夜幕般深沉。夜骐们自愿选择了这种生活，只为能更贴近他们梦魇一般的伟大的统治者，即使那领袖最憎恨的便是梦魇化的自己。当然，除了这个核心聚居地，你也能在那些最不明亮的地方找到他们的远亲，比如驼特西瓦尼亚或是阴影之井。那些夜骐仍然偏爱夜晚，但已经或多或少放弃了让世界堕入永夜的原教旨目标。
只不过，即使这里的夜骐们仍然严格遵循着先祖的一切传统，许多年轻的夜骐也并不希望世界真的陷入永夜 — 如果没有日光的照耀，树上怎么能长出肥美多汁的芒果呢？如果是善良的露娜公主，也许会为夜骐族专门划出一小片有光之地种植芒果，但梦魇之月显然不会。那时，他们将会忠于谁，或者说忠于哪棵树，夜骐们小小的大脑里装不下这么复杂的问题。

The Thestral Territory, located west of Tartarus, is a settlement area for the Thestral tribe. Here, Thestrals live in eternal night, not due to a lack of sunlight or a desire to sleep in, but because their loyalty runs as deep as the night sky itself. Thestrals willingly chose this life, solely to be closer to their nightmare-like, great ruler, even though that leader despises her own nightmarish past. Of course, beyond this core settlement, you can also find their distant relatives in the least bright places, such as Trotsylvania or the Well of Shade. These Thestrals still prefer the night but have more or less abandoned their fundamentalist goal of plunging the world into perpetual darkness.
Well, though the Thestrals here still strictly adhere to all their ancestors’ traditions, many younger Thestrals don’t actually wish for the world to fall into eternal night — after all, how could plump, juicy mangoes grow on trees without the sun’s illumination? A benevolent Princess Luna might have even designated a small sunlit plot for the Thestral tribe to grow mangoes, but Nightmare Moon clearly wouldn’t. At that point, who, or rather which tree, they would be loyal to, is a question too complex for the Thestrals’ tiny brains.
