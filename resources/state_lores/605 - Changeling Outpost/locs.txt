在幻形灵族的古老历史中，曾有一种体型硕大的生物，名为恐爪鼹熊。它们是幻形灵最大的天敌之一——并非因为它们的獠牙或利爪，而是因为它们那无止境的食欲。
如果它们是嗜血的掠食者，幻形灵还能集结虫群，以强大的防御与尚武精神来抵御入侵。然而，这些以素食为生的庞然大物对幻形灵本身毫无兴趣，它们唯一的目的就是将虫巢外的植被啃食殆尽。
幻形灵尼亚大陆本就贫瘠，坚硬的岩石裸露在外，土壤稀少，植被本寥寥无几。恐爪鼹熊的肆虐，更是将这片大地变成了寸草不生的石头荒漠。没有植被，就没有小型食草动物，更没有它们的捕食者。就连聪慧的小马，也几乎不曾跨越未知西部，仅有少数族群聚集在橡果平原与东海岸的森林中。
缺少了生物，幻形灵们又无法捕捉到那些终日生活在地洞中的恐爪鼹熊，这片荒凉的西部平原便无法为幻形灵提供足够的“爱意”。因此，幻形灵族形成了自古以来的东进传统，不断向东方的繁盛之地迁徙（或者说入侵），这一生存策略也彻底塑造了他们的文化与历史。

直到邪茧女王崛起，幻形灵族才开始向小马利亚学习，建立起了现代化的王国，恐爪鼹熊的威胁也随之成为了过去。幻形灵巡逻队主动出击，将这些巨兽驱逐出幻形灵尼亚的西部与中央大陆，最终划定了幻形灵如今的疆界。
时至今日，在漫长的边境线上，尤其是在南部，依旧遍布着密集的幻形灵哨塔。然而，鲜有幻形灵记得，这些高耸的塔楼最初的使命并非是为了防范小马或监管卡萨布朗科，而是为了严防那个曾让整个种族陷入饥荒与绝望的古老威胁——恐爪鼹熊的再次回归。

In the long history of Changelings, there was once a massive creature known as the Maulwurf. They were one of the Changelings’ greatest natural enemies — not because of their fangs or claws, but because of their insatiable appetite.
If only they were bloodthirsty predators, the Changelings could have rallied their swarms, defending themselves with their strong martial spirit. However, these colossal herbivores had no interest in the Changelings themselves. Their sole purpose was to completely devour all the vegetation outside their hive.
The Changelinia continent was already barren, with hard, exposed rock, scarce soil, and very little plant life to begin with. The Maulwurf’s rampage turned this land into a lifeless, rocky desert. With no vegetation, there were no small herbivores, and thus no predators for them. Even the clever ponies rarely ventured across the Undiscovered West; only a few herds gathered in the forests of the Acorn Plains and the Eastern coast.
With life scarce and the Changelings unable to capture the Maulwurf, which lived in burrows all day, the desolate western plains could not provide the Changelings with enough “love”. As a result, the Changeling race developed a long-standing tradition of moving eastward, constantly migrating (or rather, invading) toward the fertile lands of the East. This survival strategy completely shaped their culture and history.

It wasn’t until the rise of Queen Chrysalis that the Changelings began to learn from the ponies and established a modern kingdom. The threat of the Maulwurf became a thing of the past. Changeling patrols proactively drove these giants out of the western and central continent of Changelinia, ultimately establishing the Changelings’ current borders.
To this day, along the long border, especially in the south, there are still many Changeling outposts. However, few Changelings remember that the original purpose of these towering structures was not to guard against ponies or monitor the Casabronco, but to vigilantly prevent the return of that ancient threat — the Maulwurf — that once plunged their entire race into famine and despair.
