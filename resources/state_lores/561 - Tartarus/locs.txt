塔尔塔洛斯是小马利亚管理最严密的监狱，至少以前是。
这里关押着小马利亚最危险的罪犯，或者曾经关押着。
有些罪犯成为了国家的领袖，而有些国家领袖则成为了罪犯。更多危险的生物则继续在那里打发着他们的无聊时光，在三头犬的看守下度过他们的日子。混乱中，他们中的有些得以逃脱，如今这里对于外界来说更像是一个自然保护区，或者开放式动物园。夜骐、蜂熊、鸡蛇兽... 不同的生物管理着不同的领地。文明曾经就不曾在这里有过繁荣，如今更是已经完全消失，只有那座监狱还矗立在那里。

Tartarus is the most heavily guarded prison in Equestria, or was.
It holds the most dangerous criminals in Equestria, or held.
Some criminals became leaders of nations, and some leaders of nations became criminals. More dangerous creatures continue to pass their time there, spending their days under the watch of Cerberus. In the chaos, some of them managed to escape, and now it is more like a nature reserve or an open zoo to the outside world. Thestrals, bugbears, cockatrices... different creatures manage different territories. Civilization has never thrived here, and now it has completely disappeared, only the prison still stands there as it was.
