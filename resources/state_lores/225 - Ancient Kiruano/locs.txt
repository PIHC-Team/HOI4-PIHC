麟兰古城，麒麟族最北方的文明，建立的时间也相当久远难以考证。北迁中的麒麟们发现，从阴山顶取来的积雪与山泉水，可以有效地压制怒火，避免逆麟之乱，于是便在阴山脚下建立了村庄和城镇。
随着城镇的发展，逆麟的诅咒再次降临，越来越多的麒麟不得不放下农耕或是织布的工作，前去阴山顶取雪。这种情况持续了很长时间，直到他们决定抛弃山脚下的城市，举族搬迁至阴山顶。
最终，他们成为了麒麟历史上为数不多避免逆麟化的文明之一，可惜的是 —— 后来一场肆虐的风之魔灾害将他们的文明永远冰冻在了雪山之巅。

Ancient Kiruano, the northernmost civilization of the Kirin, was established a long time ago and is difficult to verify the exact time. The Kirins who migrated northward found that the snow and mountain spring water taken from the Yin Mountain could effectively suppress the anger, avoid the Nirik Rebellion, and thus built villages and towns at the hoof of the Yin Mountain.
As the towns developed, the curse of the Nirik struck again, and more and more Kirins had to give up farming or weaving work and go to the top of the Yin Mountain to fetch snow. This situation lasted for a long time until they decided to abandon the city at the hoof of the mountain. Every Kirin moved to the top of the Yin Mountain.
In the end, they became one of the few civilizations in Kirin history to avoid Nirik, but unfortunately — a devastating Windigo disaster later froze their civilization forever on the top of the snow-capped mountain.
