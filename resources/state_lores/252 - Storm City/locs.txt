风暴城曾经是风暴帝国的首都，也是过去风暴岛最大的城市。而现在，这里只剩下断壁残垣诉说着那个旧日帝国的辉煌，那些巍峨的雕像被扯倒在地，庞大的宫殿被洗劫一空，走在路上只见一片萧条。

风暴帝国的崩溃对风暴城的影响是灾难性的，在之后的数年里，这里始终是混乱漩涡的中心。即使是自由小马利亚政府成立后，风暴城也处于贫困状态。这是因为当初风暴大王将此作为首都时，从未考虑过发展军工之外的可能。帝国消亡后，为生产军火而建立的工业很快废弃殆尽，而其他产业的发展也举步维艰。

如今的风暴城依然算是一座大城市，但似乎依然被困在帝国崩溃的那段时光里。新政府在尝试修缮这里的基础设施，重建或者改造那些工厂。或许风暴城会有再次崛起的那一天。

Storm City was once the capital of the Storm Empire and the largest city on Storm Island. Now, only the ruins remain to tell the glory of the old empire. The towering statues are torn down, the huge palaces are looted, and the streets are desolate.

The collapse of the Storm Empire had a disastrous impact on the city, which remained the center of chaos for several years. Even after the establishment of the Free Equestria government, Storm City remained in poverty. This is because when the Storm King made it the capital, he never considered the possibility of developing anything other than its military industry. After the empire fell, the industry built to produce weapons quickly fell into disuse, and the development of other industries was also difficult.

Today’s Storm City is still considered a large city, but it seems to be forever trapped at the moment of the empire’s collapse. The new government is trying to repair the city’s infrastructure, rebuild or renovate those factories. Perhaps Storm City will have the day to rise again.
