虽然是从流水线中生产出来的，但官方检测报告显示彩虹与云朵一样都符合“100%水蒸气与阳光”的行业标准，那怎么可能不是天然的呢？只不过，与此同时，工马们总说这里的彩虹特别“有劲” —— 毕竟经过几十道提纯工序的彩虹，怎么能和天上那些寡淡的天然产物相提并论呢？
那些装在精致玻璃管中的彩虹浓缩液，标签上总印着“医用级情绪调节剂”的字样，管理层更爱用“云朵精华”这个雅称，工马们私下却喜欢叫它“天马快乐水”。偶尔会有客户和员工出现彩虹成瘾症状：过度兴奋、瞳孔放大、对普通彩虹产生耐受性... 但管理层坚称这只是个别体质问题。流水线末端的包装车间整日里弥漫着甜腻而又辛辣的彩虹蒸汽，新来的工马第一天就会学会用蹄子扇开这些“无害的副产品”，而懂行的老员工们更愿意在午休时多吸两口，毕竟这能让他们忘记自己已经三个月没看见真正的天空了。唯一的坏处是，彩虹吸多之后他们时常感到饥饿，然后他们就回去找工厂里随处可见的彩虹饼干，吃完之后感到更加饥饿。

Although they are produced from the assembly line, official inspection reports show that rainbows, like clouds, meet the industry standard of “100% water vapor and sunlight”. How could they not be natural? Though admittedly, as the worker ponies often remark, these rainbows are particularly “full-bodied” — after all, how could rainbows refined through dozens of purification processes compare to those insipid natural variants in the sky?
The rainbow concentrates, elegantly packaged in glass vials, bear labels proclaiming them “medical-grade mood regulators”. Management prefers the refined term “Cloud Essence”, while the workers affectionately dub it “Pegasus Joy Juice”. Occasionally, clients and employees exhibit rainbow addiction symptoms: hyperactivity, dilated pupils, developing tolerance to ordinary rainbows... but management insists these are merely isolated physiological reactions. The packaging area perpetually swirls with cloyingly sweet yet pungent rainbow vapors. New hires learn on day one to fan away these “harmless byproducts” with their hooves, while seasoned workers deliberately inhale deeper during breaks — it helps them forget they haven’t seen actual sunlight in three months. The only downside? Post-rainbow munchies inevitably drive them to the factory’s omnipresent rainbow cookies, which only leave them hungry even more.
