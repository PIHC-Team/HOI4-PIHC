樱桃丘牧场，小马谷南部一片充斥着野心与甜味的土地。过去，这里因盛产鲜甜的樱桃而闻名，如今，这片土地的甜意则更多地来自樱桃朱比莉那日渐膨胀的野心。当特丽克西忙于在坎特洛特的贵族面前巧言令色换取小马谷的岁月静好，长久以来“霸占”小马谷地区的苹果家族和与他们联姻的梨家族终于出局的时候，樱桃丘牧场趁着权力的真空，将她们的粉色版图扩张到了极限。
然而，这片小马利亚的“文明世界”最南部的土地也并非完全与世隔绝，牧场的独立并非没有代价。当与特丽克西不同的另一股势力在小马谷大区崛起时，精明的花花短裤很快发现了制衡的机遇。在坎特洛特的干预下，樱桃朱比莉还是被迫向特丽克西领主宣誓效忠，但这却换取了樱桃家族对于道奇路口的掌控权。如今，她们接受着来自坎特洛特的密信，成为了花花短裤的眼线，监视着小马利亚最大铁路交汇处的每一列车厢。毕竟，在铁蹄的统治下，每一颗不安分的心都可能乘坐下一班火车，而掌握了交通命脉，便掌握了那些心怀异见的“乘客”的命运。当然，这些“服务”的代价不菲，帝国秩序部每月都要因此进口一批又一批的新鲜樱桃。
对于朱比莉夫人来说，这不仅仅是金钱的交易，更是权力的游戏——她深知，在帝国的棋盘上，即便是最微不足道的棋子，只要摆放得当，也能撬动整个局势。权力正如地产，位置是重中之重。

Cherry Hill Ranch, a land brimming with ambition and sweetness in southern Ponyville. In the past, it was renowned for its luscious cherries; now, the “sweetness” of this land stems more from Cherry Jubilee’s escalating ambitions. As Trixie busied herself charming Canterlot’s nobility to secure Ponyville’s tranquility, and the Apple family, long "occupying" the Ponyville region, along with their in-laws, the Pear family, were finally out of the picture, Cherry Hill Ranch seized the power vacuum, expanding their pink domain to its utmost limits.
However, this southernmost land of Equestria’s “civilized world” is not entirely isolated, and the ranch’s independence did not come without a price. When a force distinct from Trixie began to rise in the greater Ponyville area, the astute Fancy Pants quickly spotted an opportunity for counterbalance. Under Canterlot’s intervention, Cherry Jubilee was compelled to swear fealty to Duchess Trixie, in return for Cherry family’s control over Dodge Junction. Now, they receive clandestine messages from Canterlot, acting as Fancy Pants’ eyes and ears, monitoring every railcar at Equestria’s largest railway junction. After all, under the iron hooves of the regime, every restless heart might board the next train, and by controlling the transportation lifeline, one controls the fate of those dissenting “passengers”. Of course, these “services” come at a hefty price, with the Empire’ Ministry of Order importing boxes after boxes of freshly picked cherries each month.
For Madam Jubilee, this is more than just a monetary transaction; it’s a game of power. She knows well that on the Empire’s chessboard, even the most insignificant piece, if positioned correctly, can sway the entire game. Power is a lot like real estate. It's all about location, location, location.
