故都，麒麟语中“曾经的首都”之意。早在“长河”修建以前，翠绿洲就已经有麒麟居住。因担心轮回的烈火毁灭着来之不易的水美丰足之地，麒麟们将都城建设在这绿洲的西侧，而非绿洲的中央。
事实证明，麒麟们的克制得到了回报——千百年来，即使故都已经陷落又重建无数回，翠绿洲的绿意依然如故。最终，这保留下来的绿洲成为了“长河”水源，祝福着整个麒麟社群。

Gu Du, meaning “used to be the capital” in Kirinese. Long before the construction of the “Great Moat”, Kirins had already lived in the Emerald Oasis. Fearing that the flames of the cycles would destroy this precious and rich land, the Kirins built the capital on the west side of the oasis, rather than in the center of the oasis.
As it turned out, the Kirins’ restraint paid off — for thousands of years, even though Gu Du had fallen and been rebuilt countless times, the greenery of the Emerald Oasis remained the same. Eventually, this preserved oasis became the source of the “Great Moat”, blessing the entire Kirin commune.
