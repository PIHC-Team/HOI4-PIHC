廷马克图水晶首饰有着一个传说：廷马克图是千年前的一个天马城邦，那里富裕而繁荣、盛产水晶。直到，虫茧女王率领幻形灵东征廷马克图，老国王率军与幻形灵决一死战。
在决战前，老国王向水晶许愿自己能够“将永远守护廷马克图的天空”，这个愿望随后被水晶认可，于是 —— 廷马克图最终还是被摧毁，老国王被虫茧女王杀死，他的灵魂升上天空成为了如今的猎户星座。老国王的愿望确实以扭曲的方式实现了：他永远守护了廷马克图的天空，却忘记了守护廷马克图的城邦。这也是为什么自那以后，云中城、层云堡等新一代城邦都不在地面和低层云朵上建城，而是在更高的云层上建城，这样便能享受到猎户星座的庇护。

There is a legend about Timbucktu crystal jewelry: Timbucktu was a pegasus city-state a thousand years ago, which was wealthy and prosperous from crystal production. However, the changeling Queen Chrysalis led her changeling army to conquer Timbucktu, and the old king had to stand against her in a final battle.
Before the battle, the old king made a wish to the crystal that he could “forever guard the sky of Timbucktu”. This wish was later recognized by the crystal, so — Timbucktu was eventually destroyed, the old king was killed by Queen Chrysalis, and his soul ascended to the night sky to become the constellation Orion. The old king’s wish was indeed fulfilled in a twisted way: he forever guarded the sky of Timbucktu, but forgot to guard the city of Timbucktu. This is why since then, new city-states like Cloudsdale or Stratusburg are not built on the ground or lower-altitude clouds, but on the higher-altitude clouds, so they can enjoy the protection of the Orion constellation.

§b“Well, some busybody jewelers did, and they became the Gemini constellation.”§!
