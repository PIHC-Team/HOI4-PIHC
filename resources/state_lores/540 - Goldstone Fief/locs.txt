格斯塔尔是一位优秀的狮鹫领主，他拥有沉着冷静的头脑，深不见底的城府，杀伐果断的手段，以及向往谐律、和平与善良的心灵。但如果要说谁能扶大厦于将倾，挽狂澜于既倒，那格斯塔尔则并不具备这种能力。至少，戈德斯通如此认为。
戈德斯通承认，格斯塔尔是和平时期一位难得的领袖。但如今的狮鹫王国正面临内忧外患，在他看来，唯有那种敢于在风暴来临前的海面上低空捕鱼的狮鹫，才能拯救这个国家。阴谋与诡计仿佛流淌在戈德斯通家族的血液中，策划一场环环相扣的政变，或是设计一份阴险的合同，于他而言就如张开翅膀一般自然。凭借一些不那么光明的手段，他从一个没落贵族一跃成为狮鹫王国少数掌握实权的领主之一。但长桌末席远远无法满足他的野心。通过更多算计与笼络，他逐步争取到其他几位狮鹫领主的支持，形成了领主议会中仅次于格斯塔尔的第二大派系。
当戈德斯通意外从古老文献中发现一个秘密时，他意识到，自己等待已久的机会终于到来。数千年前，风之魔刚刚消散，小马们才刚开始东迁归乡，狮鹫一族却早已抓住时机，跨过冰封的海洋，接管了小马遗弃的土地。当那些风雪中的废弃村落逐渐变成狮鹫居住的城市，他们自然不愿将建设成果拱手归还自西方陆续归来的小马们，于是用军队筑起了一道坚固的屏障。当时的小马领袖在牦牛的担保下做出艰难决定：以极其高昂的价格向狮鹫赎回土地，并承诺以未来百年的收成兑换狮鹫金币，分期偿还债务。出于未知原因，这笔债务最终并未完全清偿——而债权人，正是戈德斯通家族数百代前的直系祖先。
这意味着，只要戈德斯通要求小马利亚立即偿还这笔逾期债务及其利息，对方很可能无力支付，进而被迫出让包括坎特洛特在内的大片领土。这些土地将成为他的封地，使他统治一个比狮鹫王国更强大的国家。他选择隐藏这份条约，花费数年时间从小马利亚购入黄金，同时煽动狮鹫与小马之间的种族对立舆论，并以公益之名修建了一座用于保存狮鹫古籍的图书馆。终于，等到众生大会召开——世界各地种族领袖齐聚之时——他诱导小马们“自行发现”了那份条约，再以无辜受害者的姿态现身会场，要求偿还债务。只可惜，某些不该出现的博学小马，最终竟找到了古老债务已被结清的证据。戈德斯通不仅血本无归，还因销毁证据、煽动种族冲突而银铛入狱。
越是复杂的计划，越容易因细微偏差而全盘溃败。戈德斯通数年谋划，终究功亏一篑...吗？终末之战降临，身陷囹圄的戈德斯通反而躲过了半人马对狮鹫领主的清算。当格斯塔尔派系因与小马过从甚密而遭受重创时，戈德斯通与其追随者却完好无损。借助古斯塔夫上台引发的狮鹫领主议会分裂，刚刚出狱、没有政治包袱的戈德斯通迅速崛起，在半人马与狮鹫的矛盾中左右逢源，最终建立起自己的独立领地与武装力量：表面看来，他是抵抗半人马的狮鹫英雄，指责格斯塔尔软弱无能；暗地里，他却计划逐步整合原狮鹫帝国的领土，建立戈德斯通帝国，与半人马瓜分大陆。
或许，这也仍只是他宏图的一部分？

Gestal is an excellent Griffon Lord, with a calm and collected mind, unreadable thoughts, decisive means of action, and a heart that yearns for harmony, peace, and goodness. However, when it comes to a Griffon who can stop the fall of an Empire or face a storm head-on, Gestal is not the one. At least, that’s what Goldstone argues.
Goldstone acknowledges that Gestal is a treasure among Griffon leaders in times of peace, but with the Griffon Kingdom facing both internal and external threats, only a Griffon who dares to fly low over stormy seas to fish can save the nation. That’s Goldstone. Conspiracies and schemes run in his bloodline, and plotting a series of interconnected coups or drafting insidious contract clauses is as easy to him as spreading his wings. Through some less-than-honorable means, Goldstone rose from a fallen noble to become one of the few Lords who control the fate of the Griffon Kingdom. Yet a position at the edge of the high table was not enough to satisfy his ambitions. With more schemes and intrigues, he gradually rallied other Griffon Lords and became the second-largest faction in the Lords’ Council, just behind Gestal’s faction.
When Goldstone stumbled upon a secret in an ancient document, he realized his opportunity had arrived — thousands of years ago, when the Windigos had just dissipated and the ponies were beginning their eastward migration back towards their home, the griffons seized the chance to cross the frozen ocean and take over lands abandoned by the ponies. As those abandoned villages in the snow gradually transformed into Griffon cities, they were naturally reluctant to return the lands they had built to the ponies returning from the west. Griffons erected a strong military barrier, forcing the leaders of ponies to make a difficult decision. With the guarantee of the Yaks, they agreed to buy back the land from the griffons at an extremely high price, promising to repay the debt with a portion of their future harvests over the next hundred years, paying in installments with Griffon Gold Bits. For unknown reasons, this debt was never fully repaid — and the creditor was none other than Goldstone’s direct ancestor from hundreds of generations ago.
This meant that if Goldstone demanded immediate repayment of the overdue debt with interest, the ponies would likely be unable to pay, forcing them to cede large tracts of land, including Canterlot. These lands would become his fiefdom, allowing him to rule a nation more powerful than the Griffon Kingdom. He chose to hide this treaty, spending years secretly purchasing gold from the ponies, while stirring up racial tensions between griffons and ponies, and building a library to house ancient Griffon texts in the name of public welfare. Finally, at the Convocation of the Creatures — where leaders of countries and creatures all around the world gathered — he induced the ponies to “discover” the treaty themselves, then appeared at the conference as an innocent victim, demanding repayment of the debt. To his dismay, some scholarly ponies who shouldn’t have been there found evidence that the ancient debt had already been settled. Not only did Goldstone lose everything, but he was also imprisoned for destroying evidence and inciting racial conflict.
The more complex the plan, the easier it is to fail due to minor deviations. After years of plotting, Goldstone’s grand scheme ultimately failed... or did it? When the Ultimate Battle came, Goldstone, due to his imprisonment, avoided the purge on Griffon Lords held by the centaurs. While Gestal’s faction suffered heavy losses due to their close ties with the ponies, Goldstone and his followers remained intact. Taking advantage of the division in the Griffon Lords’ Council caused by Gustave’s rise to power, Goldstone, newly released from prison and without political baggage, quickly rose to prominence. He skillfully switched sides back and forth between the centaurs and griffons, establishing his own independent territory and armed forces. On the surface, he appeared as a griffon hero resisting the centaurs, criticizing Gestal for his weakness; but behind the scenes, he planned to gradually consolidate the territories of the former Griffon Empire, establish the Goldstone Empire to share the continent with the centaurs.
Or, perhaps, this is still just a part of his grand plan?
