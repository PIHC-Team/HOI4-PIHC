柔柔港，麒麟社群第一批对外开放的港口之一，以柔柔的名字命名，以纪念苹果嘉儿与柔柔帮助解除麒麟“静默”。
自港口开放以来，这里一直是外界了解麒麟社群的重要窗口。但可能也是由于港口的名字，来自小马利亚帝国的商马很少在这里交易，商船主要来自名流谷、洛马基、风暴岛、马哈顿等地区。

[en.@]
Fluttershy Harbour, one of the first ports opened to the outside world by the Kirin Commune, is named after Fluttershy to commemorate Applejack and Fluttershy’s help in breaking the Kirin’s “Silence”.
Since the opening of the port, it has been an important window for the outside world to understand the Kirin Commune. But perhaps due to the name of the port, there are few trade ships from the Equestria Empire. Most of the merchant ships come from Sire’s Hollow, Las Pegasus, Storm Island, Manehattan and other regions.
