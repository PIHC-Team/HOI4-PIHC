从坎特洛特那些宏伟的地图上看，未知西部似乎是世界尽头。古老的开拓者传说将这里描绘成小马利亚的最后一片土地，再往西一步，便是深渊。他们相信，如果胆敢越过那片传说中的“落日之地”，就会从世界的边缘坠落，直抵永恒的虚无。当然，现在我们都知道，那些古老的地图和所谓的“地理学家”不过是些未经考证的空想家，他们的“科学理论”和“地理边界”不过是用来限制那些不安分的蹄子。
然而，尽管坎特洛特的学者们早在很久以前就“澄清”了这些“无稽之谈”，并且帝国也早已与西部的幻形灵帝国建立了“友好”的商贸和监视关系，未知西部这个名字却奇怪地流传了下来。也许，这片土地真正“未知”的，并非其地理边界，而是那些被强行“遗忘”的历史，那些未能被帝国地图所收录的真实。每当西风从幻形灵帝国方向吹来，总会带来一种异样的爱意的甜味，有时还会夹杂着一些不知名的嘶吼和微弱的求救声。这些声音，在坎特洛特的官方报道中，总是被归结为“风蚀”或“野兽的正常活动”。但生活在这里的小马们却心照不宣——他们知道，真正的未知，往往隐藏在最显而易见的谎言之下。

On the grand maps of Canterlot, the Undiscovered West appears to be the end of the world. Ancient pioneer legends depicted it as Equestria’s final frontier; one more step west, they claimed, and you’d plummet into the abyss. They believed that daring to cross the fabled “Land of the Setting Sun” would send you tumbling off the edge of the world, straight into eternal oblivion. Of course, by now, we all know those ancient maps and so-called “geographers” were nothing but unverified fantasists, their “scientific theories” and “geographical boundaries” merely tools to restrain restless hooves.
Yet, even though Canterlot scholars “clarified” these “absurdities” long ago, and the Empire has long established “friendly” trade and surveillance relations with the Changeling Empire on the west, the name “Undiscovered West” has strangely persisted. Perhaps what’s truly “undiscovered” about this land isn’t its geographical limits, but the history forcibly “forgotten,” the truths that failed to be cataloged on the Empire’s maps. Whenever the west wind blows from the direction of the Changeling Empire, it always carries an unfamiliar sweetness of love, sometimes mixed with unknown screeches and faint cries for help. These sounds, in Canterlot’s official reports, are always attributed to “wind erosion” or “normal animal activity.” But the ponies living here share an unspoken understanding – they know that the true unknown often hides beneath the most obvious lies.
