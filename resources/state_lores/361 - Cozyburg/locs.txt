和煦堡的居民们或许不记得（或者不敢记得），这里曾经是抵抗军最活跃的据点之一。如今，那些被拆除的“非法”友谊雕像的残骸，都被碾碎混入了铺设和煦堡街道的沥青里。走在上面，你或许会感受到脚下偶尔传来的轻微震动——那不是地壳运动，而是昔日反抗的余音，被永久地淹没在“和谐”之下。
如今的和煦堡建筑涂着令人安心的柔和色彩，街道上时刻播放着精心挑选的友谊赞歌，甚至连空气都带着一股甜腻的草莓蛋糕味。和煦堡军区的居民是整个帝国幸福感最高的公民 —— 他们每日准时起床，用最饱满的热情投入到帝国的建设中，脸上总是洋溢着真诚的笑容。那些安装在路灯杆、房屋外墙乃至是你午餐面包里的微型摄像头忠实地记录着每个公民的“幸福指数”。当你将一切献给军队、国家与女皇，你将得到的就是无忧无虑的幸福生活，这便是帝国教授给你的友谊课程。当然，如果说完全无忧无虑倒也不太准确，毕竟这里是军区，这里的居民还是偶尔要担心一些微不足道的爆炸。

The residents of Cozyburg might not remember (or perhaps dare not remember) that this was once one of the most active strongholds of the resistance. Now, the remnants of those demolished “illegal” friendship statues have been crushed and mixed into the asphalt paving Cozyburg’s streets. Walking upon them, you might occasionally feel a faint tremor beneath your hooves — that’s not tectonic activity, but the lingering echoes of past rebellion, permanently drowned beneath “harmony”.
Today, Cozyburg’s buildings are painted in reassuringly soft colors, its streets constantly hum with carefully curated anthems of friendship, and even the air carries a cloyingly sweet scent of strawberry cake. The residents of the Cozyburg Military District are the happiest citizens in the entire Empire—they rise promptly each day, dedicate themselves with the fullest enthusiasm to the Empire’s construction, and their faces are always beaming with genuine smiles. The miniature cameras installed on lampposts, building facades, and even in your lunch bread faithfully record each citizen’s “happiness index”. When you dedicate everything to the army, the nation, and the Empress, you receive a life of carefree bliss; this is the friendship lesson the Empire teaches you. Of course, calling it entirely carefree might not be perfectly accurate, as this is, after all, a military district, and its residents still occasionally have to worry about the occasional insignificant explosion.
