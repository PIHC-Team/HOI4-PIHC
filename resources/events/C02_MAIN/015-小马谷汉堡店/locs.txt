[zh.@desc]
今日，马哈顿新开业的小马谷汉堡店吸引了整个马哈顿名流的目光。虽说干草汉堡算不上什么高贵的食物，但小马谷汉堡店绝对不同。
小马谷汉堡店在小马谷有着数十年的经营历史，几乎每一只小马谷的居民都在这里享用过干草汉堡，长久以来都是如此。
直到几个月前，小马谷政府提出发展小马谷地方品牌的口号，在据说是公爵自掏腰包的资助和政府政策支持下，小马谷汉堡店在道奇路口开设了第一家分店。
随后，小马谷汉堡店大获成功，连锁店也像得了可爱标志瘤的小马身上的橙色斑点一样在东小马利亚迅速蔓延，从南边的马蹄湾到小马德菲亚，再到北边的马哈顿，都有了小马谷汉堡店的身影。也许很快，小马谷汉堡店也将进驻坎特洛特，甚至更狂野一点，苹果鲁萨、驼丁汉、名流谷和水晶广场！小马谷汉堡店不仅获得了巨大的利润，也带动了小马谷经济的发展，游客们也纷纷慕名而来。
如今，特丽克西每个月都会来到小马谷总店，当着所有媒体的面，穿着正式的政府工作服，梳起头发戴着胸针，卷好餐巾，优雅地品尝一次干草汉堡，一边向群众微笑示意，以表示对小马谷汉堡店的支持。
真是一个奇迹的故事！

Today, the newly opened Ponyville Hay Burger Store in Manehattan has attracted the attention of the entire Manehattan celebrities. Although hay burgers are not considered noble food, the Ponyville Hay Burger Store is definitely different.
The Ponyville Hay Burger Store has been in business in Ponyville for decades, and almost every resident of Ponyville has enjoyed hay burgers here, and has been so for a long time.
Until a few months ago, the Ponyville government proposed the slogan of developing local brands in Ponyville. With the support of the Duchess’s own money and government policy support, the Ponyville Hay Burger Store opened its first branch at the Dodge Junction.
Subsequently, the Ponyville Hay Burger Store was a great success, and the chain stores spread rapidly in Eastern Equestria like the orange spots on the ponies with cutie pox. From the south of Horseshoe Bay to Fillydelphia, and then to the north of Manehattan, there is the always a Ponyville Hay Burger Store. Soon, the Ponyville Hay Burger Store will also be stationed in Canterlot, and even more wild, Appleloosa, Trottingham, Sire’s Hollow, and Crystal Square!
Now, Trixie comes to the Ponyville headquarters every month, in front of all the media, wearing formal government work clothes, combing her hair and wearing a brooch, rolling up the napkin, elegantly tasting a hay burger, smiling to the crowd, to show support for the Ponyville Hay Burger Store.
What a miraculous story!
