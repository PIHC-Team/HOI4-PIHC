[zh.@desc]
马哈顿战争结束后，帝国对星光抵抗军进行了多次谴责和多轮制裁。但很显然，这些并不能阻止抵抗军的扩张与对东小马利亚的整合。
小马利亚帝国日报记者在小马德菲亚、马哈顿等多个地区拍摄到抵抗军的军工厂在全速运转，士兵们在训练场上进行着操练。面对记者的镜头，这些小马们甚至毫不掩饰，反而直接了当地对记者表示，务必将新闻报道给整个小马利亚。“我们要告诉帝国，我们已经准备好夺回属于我们的一切了！为了自由，为了友谊！” 一名接受采访的军工厂工马如是说。

报道一经发出，和煦光流女皇立刻下令，调集帝国军队在边境集结准备防守。然而，不同于外界的预期，尽管和煦光流女皇频繁密集讲话要剿灭星光叛军，但却迟迟没有下达宣战令。直到今日，女皇与特丽克西进行了一次电话会谈，将特丽克西封为“小马利亚帝国元帅”，并下达了一份特别的光荣命令：小马谷公爵领将代表帝国出兵，剿灭叛军。

这一天，终于还是到来了。
[en.@desc]
After the Manehattan War, the Empire has repeatedly condemned and imposed multiple sanctions on the Starlight Resistance. But obviously, these cannot stop the expansion and integration of Eastern Equestria.
Reporters from the Equestria Daily of the Empire have photographed the military factories of the Resistance running at full speed. In many regions such as Ponyville and Baltimare, soldiers are training on the training ground. Facing the camera of the reporter, these ponies do not even conceal it, but directly tell the reporter that they must report the news to the entire Equestria. “We want to tell the Empire that we are ready to take back everything that belonged to us! For freedom, for friendship!” A interviewed factory worker pony said.

Once the report was released, Empress Cozy Glow immediately ordered the Empire’s army to gather at the border to prepare for defense. However, contrary to expectations, although Empress Cozy Glow frequently and intensively spoke to eradicate the Starlight rebels, she has not yet issued a declaration of war, until today. The Empress had a phone call with Trixie, appointing Trixie as the “Marshal of the Equestria Empire” and issued a special order of honor: Duke of Ponyville will lead the Empire to send troops to eradicate the rebels.

This day, has finally come.
