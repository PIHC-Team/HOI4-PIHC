苹果鲁萨抗议小马谷二次农改
[en.@]
Appleloosa Protest Against the Second Ag-reform
[zh.@desc]
在小马谷宣布计划进行第二次农业改革的次日，苹果鲁萨方面就发出了强烈抗议。苹果家族表示，第一次农业改革就是一场灾难——批量砍伐苹果树改种其他树种是对苹果家族的一次公然迫害，最终也造成了悲剧的结果。
苹果鲁萨区长布雷本强硬表态，不能接受小马谷对领地内的苹果家族进行“第二次迫害”，并威胁称，若小马谷不撤回这一计划，苹果鲁萨将采取一切必要措施维护自己的利益，包括对终止苹果鲁萨-小马谷贸易协定等经济制裁。
[en.@desc]
On the day after the announcement of the second agriculture reform plan in Ponyville, Appleloosa has issued a strong protest. The Apple family said that the first ag-reform was a disaster - the mass felling of apple trees and the planting of other tree species was nothing but a blatant persecution of the Apple family, and ultimately led to tragic results.
Braeburn, district head of Appleloosa has made a strong statement, saying that he cannot accept “a second persecution” of the Apple family in Ponyville’s territory, and threatened that if Ponyville does not withdraw this plan, Appleloosa will take all necessary measures to protect its interests, including economic sanctions such as terminating the Appleloosa-Ponyville trade agreement.
