余晖烁烁的回归
[en.@]
Return of Sunset
[zh.@desc]
在“新元年”过后，余晖烁烁便失踪在大家的视线里。这段时间里自由小马利亚因此起彼伏的抗议已经一片混乱，临时总理的消失让这一情况雪上加霜。许多猜测都认为：余晖烁烁无法接受M6在“新元年”后集体失踪，更无法承受她们可能已经遇害的猜想，精神已经崩溃。

但谁也没想到的是，就在元帅闪卫广播全国进入紧急状态时，余晖烁烁突然出现。并向全国发表了一场激动心灵的演讲：她向自己过去的失败道歉，并鼓励自由马国居民重拾信心，并在演讲的最后宣布了一项庞大的计划：「虹爆」计划。这一计划宣布实行大规模改革以实现种族平等，保障就业，振兴经济，缩小贫富差距等。

演讲感染力空前，被怀疑并非原创。但无论如何，这场演讲拉高了余晖烁烁的声望，自由马国各大城市的街头巷尾，支持总理的群体盖过了已经持续一个月的抗议队伍。但是很明显，一场演讲还远没到能解决风暴岛已经在泥潭中挣扎多年的经济的程度。「虹爆」计划能否成功实行仍然是个未知数。
[en.@desc]
After “Jahr Null”, Sunset Shimmer disappeared from the public eye. Free Equestria had already been thrown into chaos by continuous protests, and the disappearance of its interim prime minister only made matters worse. Many speculated that Sunset, unable to accept the M6’s collective disappearance—or endure the suspicion that they might already have been killed—had suffered a mental breakdown.

However, no one expected that just as Marshal Flash Sentry declared a nationwide state of emergency, Sunset Shimmer suddenly reappeared. She delivered a stirring speech to the nation: she apologized for her past failures, encouraged the citizens of Free Equestria to regain their confidence, and at the end of her speech, announced an ambitious plan: Project <=Rainboom=>. This plan proposed large-scale reforms to achieve racial equality, ensure employment, revitalize the economy, and reduce the wealth gap.

The speech was unprecedentedly moving, leading some to suspect it was not original. Regardless, the speech significantly boosted Sunset Shimmer’s popularity. In the streets and alleys of Free Equestria’s major cities, groups supporting the Prime Minister overshadowed the protests that had persisted for a month. However, it was clear that a single speech was far from enough to solve the long-struggling economy of Storm Island. Whether the “Rainboom” plan can be successfully implemented remains a question mark.
