余晖烁烁合上书本，但那些泛黄纸张上的字迹却依旧浮现在自己眼前。余晖闭上眼睛，但思绪却又回到了几分钟前，自己翻开书本时的场景。伴着淡紫色的的荧光，一行行清晰的字迹浮现出来，它们摇晃，扭曲，慢慢缩小成一个极小的黑点。周围的声音慢慢消失了，只剩下嗡嗡作响的耳鸣。

一股强烈的悲伤涌上余晖烁烁的心头，扯动着她的锁骨，令她呼吸紊乱。那些美好的回忆不合时宜地出现：秋季舞会，击败海妖的歌曲，城市街头的午后漫步... 这些画面如同一幕幕诡异的幻觉。余晖烁烁试图调整气息，但这股情绪却开始慢慢凝固起来，仿佛成了一块压迫力极强的实体，余晖大口呼吸，但却感到周围的空气不断收缩，不断减少... 她跌倒在地，桌上的东西被一并带下，纷纷砸在余晖烁烁身边的地板和自己的脑袋上。周围变得好冷，这个暖炉夜比往年冷多了……不！没有什么暖炉夜了，只剩下那个该死的“新元年”。在未来的许多日子里，自己都将被这个词折磨到精神衰弱，它的背后是无数的鲜血与痛苦，它象征着一段友谊以最残忍的方式被击碎，一个时代就此结束了。

余晖尝试着爬向门口，但后蹄似乎有千斤重。她只能无助地躺在房间的中央，新元年第一天的最后一缕阳光静静地洒在她的身上，如同淋上一层鲜红的血浆。当这束光芒消失在余晖的视线中时，她痛苦地闭上了眼睛。

五分钟后，昏迷不醒的余晖烁烁被发现。她被立刻送往自由港最好的医院并被专门安排了一间病房。四个小时后，护士进入房间时，发出了尖叫。

Sunset Shimmer closed the book, but the words on the yellowed pages still lingered before her eyes. She shut her eyes, yet her thoughts drifted back to the moment just minutes ago when she had opened the book. Accompanied by a faint purple glow, lines of clear text emerged, swaying, twisting, and gradually shrinking into a tiny black dot. The surrounding sounds faded away, leaving only a ringing in her ears.

A wave of intense sorrow washed over Sunset Shimmer, tugging at her collarbone and disrupting her breathing. Memories of happier times surfaced unbidden: the Fall Formal, the song that defeated the Sirens, afternoon strolls through the city streets... images flashed like eerie hallucinations. Sunset tried to steady her breath, but the emotion began to solidify, as if turning into a heavy, oppressive weight. She gasped for air, yet it felt as though the atmosphere around her was shrinking, thinning... She collapsed to the floor, dragging down the items on the table with her, which clattered onto the ground and onto her head. The room grew cold — this Hearth’s Warming Eve is far colder than any before... Oh right, there is no Hearth’s Warming Eve anymore, only that damned “Jahr Null”. In the days to come, she would be tormented by this term to the brink of mental exhaustion. Behind it lay countless blood and pain, symbolizing a friendship shattered in the cruelest way possible, an era coming to an end.

Sunset tried to crawl toward the door, but her hind legs felt as heavy as lead. She lay helplessly in the center of the room as the last rays of sunlight on the first day of the Jahr Null gently bathed her in a crimson hue. As the light vanished from Sunset’s sight, she closed her eyes in agony.

Five minutes later, Sunset Shimmer was found unconscious. She was immediately rushed to the best hospital in Filport and given a private room. Four hours later, a nurse entered the room, and let out a scream.
