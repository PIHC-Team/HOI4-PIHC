Storm Creatures on Strike
[zh.@desc]
近日，自由港内一名风暴兽因盗窃被捕。而这名风暴兽是参加过第一次起义的老兵，因此该事件被风暴兽视为对战争老兵，乃至整个风暴兽群体的歧视。十几名风暴兽聚集起来围堵了警察局试图逼迫当局释放老兵，而在双方对峙时产生了肢体冲突，进而导致了事态升级。

截至目前，罢工和抗议的规模仍在迅速扩大，各地风暴兽商贩，学生，工兽甚至部分前军警单位成员都参与了此次罢工。政府内部也有群体借此事件来向我们施压。
[en.@desc]
Recently, a storm creature was arrested for theft in Freeport. The storm creature was a veteran who had participated in the first uprising, so the incident was seen by the storm creatures as discrimination against war veterans, and even the entire storm creature community. A dozen storm creatures gathered to surround the police station and tried to force the authorities to release the veteran. A physical conflict occurred between the two sides during the confrontation, which escalated the situation.

As of now, the scale of the strike and protest is still rapidly expanding. Storm creature merchants, students, working creatures, and even some former military police unit members have participated in the strike. There are also groups within the government that are using this incident to pressure us.
