April 25th
[zh.@desc]
列车正在做最后的货物转载，满贯也已经偷渡上车了，我让他装扮成货物搬运工，没有小马怀疑。
我一直和满贯这么说，是油嘴滑舌那两个家伙欺骗了我们，乐善先生被他们陷害出局，洛马基才会变成今天这个样子。我看的出来满贯在这点上不相信我，但很快，我就可以让他亲眼看到马哈顿的繁华了。
听说乐善先生不仅在马哈顿生意兴隆，还当上了市长。等我们到了马哈顿，我们一起去去拜访他，他说不定能以马哈顿市长的身份推广我们的魔术表演呢！想象一下，东小马利亚城市里那群最奢华的上流小马们，他们将会我们的表演而疯狂！不过满贯似乎对这些不太上心，他只关心能不能到小马谷见到他的女儿。如果他决定退出魔术表演，我也不会怪他，毕竟我之前成为火车司机的时候，他也没有说什么。
发车铃声已经响了，就这样吧，再见了，洛马基！你好，马哈顿！
[en.@desc]
The train is doing the final cargo transfer, and Jackpot has also sneaked onto the train. I had him disguise himself as a cargo handler, and no pony suspected anything.
I have been telling Jackpot all along that it was the Flim and Flam brothers who deceived us, that Mr. Gladmane was framed by them and forced out of Las Pegasus, which is why Las Pegasus has become what it is today. I can tell that Jackpot doesn’t believe me on this matter, but soon, I will be able to show him the splendor of Manehattan with his own eyes.
We heard that Mr. Gladmane not only has a thriving business in Manehattan, but has also become the mayor. When we arrive in Manehattan, we will visit him together. He might be able to help promote our magic show with his status as the mayor of Manehattan! Imagine the most luxurious upper-class ponies in the city of Manehattan go crazy for our show! But Jackpot seems to be indifferent to these things, he only cares about whether he can see his daughter in Ponyville. If he decides to quit the magic show business, I won’t blame him. After all, he didn’t say anything when I became a train driver.
The departure bell has already rung, that’s it, goodbye Las Pegasus! Hello, Manehattan!
