Father’s Letter
[zh.@desc]
致我亲爱的女儿：

自那次大战后，我们已经三年没有联系过了。近来收到暮光公主的噩耗，爸爸才敢鼓起勇气，偷偷写一封信给你，十分对不起。

我相信你现在的心情一定很难过，爸爸很了解你的心情。暮光公主被打败后，所有小马都说你为了保护暮光公主牺牲了。那时爸爸的生活真的很灰暗，每天都在努力回忆与你的点点滴滴。直到后来你起义的消息上了新闻头条，那真的是爸爸这辈子最开心的时刻，即使你成了通缉犯，即使爸爸被迫站在了你的对立面，可你还活着，这点就足够了。

爸爸写信给你，希望你可以走出阴影，鼓起勇气。小马利亚的小马们都希望你带领他们赶走坎特洛特城堡里那只邪恶的小马，他们需要你的帮助。向前走吧！暮光公主一定会保佑你的，愿你能一帆风顺，爸爸在名流谷等着你的凯旋！

对了，代我问候一下日光耀耀，他的妈妈很想他，可外面到处都是眼线，第二封信送出去几乎不可能，只能托我代她问好了。
[en.@desc]
Dear my beloved daughter,

It has been three years since we last contacted after the war. It was not until recently that I received the horrible news of Princess Twilight’s death that I dared to gather the courage to secretly write a letter to you, I am very sorry for everything.

I believe you must be very sad now, dad understands your feelings very well. After Princess Twilight was defeated, I heard said that you sacrificed yourself to protect Princess Twilight. It was really dark times for me. I was drowning in memories of you every day. Until the news of your uprising made headlines, that was the happiest moment of my life: Even though you became a wanted criminal, even though I was forced to stand on the opposite side of you, all that mattered was that you were still alive.

The reason dad wrote this letter to you is to hope that you can step out of the shadow and gather the courage. The ponies of Equestria must all be hoping that you can lead them to drive away the evil pony in Canterlot Castle. They need your help. Move forward! Princess Twilight will definitely bless you. May you have a bright journey ahead, and your dad in Sire’s Hollow will be waiting for your triumphant return.

By the way, please send my regards to Sunburst. His mother misses him very much, but there are spies everywhere, and it is almost impossible to send out a second letter. I can only ask you to pass on her greetings.
