自从收到那则毁灭性的消息起，星光熠熠就每天无精打采，时常因想起从前的回忆而流泪不止，眼中的血丝在黑眼圈的衬托下格外明显。双钻和糖蓓儿多次以阵线发展和小马们需要她的领导为由劝星光振作起来，结果是星光每天处理完必要的日常事务后，便恢复无精打采的状态。

为此，日光耀耀决心要将他的这位老友拉出泥潭。在一天晚上星光准备踏入家门时，日光叫住了她，如同当年暮光闪闪叫住星光熠熠那样。

“星光熠熠！那个，”日光调整了下情绪，“关于暮光她们的死，我也很难过，但...”

“但她们已经死了！”星光打断了日光“这么多年来，除了你，我好不容易交到了几个朋友，甚至还当上了友谊学校校长，可当初教会我友谊的小马，她已经...已经...!”星光强忍住眼泪，几乎哭了出来。

日光叹了口气。“暮光闪闪死了，小马们会永远缅怀她的。而她之所以这么努力地教导你，是因为她希望你能够独当一面，带领小马们找到友谊的真谛。我想，如果你能走出阴影，再次带领小马们为小马利亚带来谐律的话，暮光闪闪的在天之灵也会为此感到欣慰吧。”

“星光啊...”日光耀耀伸出蹄子，帮星光熠熠擦了擦眼泪。

“留在后面的是回忆...”

Ever since receiving that devastating news, Starlight Glimmer has been listless every day, often crying uncontrollably when she recalls the memories of the past. Her bloodshot eyes became particularly prominent against the backdrop of her dark circles. Sunburst and Sugar Belle have repeatedly tried to persuade Starlight to cheer up, citing the development of the front and the need for her leadership, but Starlight returned to her listless state after completing the necessary daily tasks.

So, Sunburst decided to pull his old friend out of the mire. One night, as Starlight was about to step into her house, Sunburst stopped her, just as Twilight Sparkle had stopped Starlight Glimmer years ago.

“Starlight Glimmer!” Sunburst adjusted his emotions, “I am also sad about the death of Twilight and the others, but...”

“But they are already dead!” Starlight interrupted Sunburst, “For so many years, except for you, I have made a few friends with great difficulty, and even became the principal of the School of Friendship. But the pony who taught me friendship in the first place, she has already... already...!” Starlight held back her tears and almost cried.

Sunburst sighed. “Twilight Sparkle is dead, but the ponies will always remember her. The reason she worked so hard to teach you was because she hoped you could stand on your own and lead the ponies to find the true essence of friendship. I think, if you can step out of the shadow and lead the ponies to bring harmony to Equestria again, Twilight Sparkle’s spirit in heaven will also be pleased.”

“Starlight...” Sunburst reached out his hoof and wiped Starlight’s tears.
