[zh.@desc]
每个月的中旬，星光熠熠、无序和余晖烁烁都会进行联络，以交换各自的情报。因为魔法日记本的存在，星光与余晖的联络十分方便。星光熠熠拿起笔记本，在纸上写下联络内容：

“我们目前缺少物资，每天都有难民来到平等镇...”笔记本上的魔法痕迹，确保余晖已经收到了联络内容。

放下笔记本，星光熠熠抬头望了一眼时钟，日光耀耀推开门，走了进来。

“日光，无序还没有回信吗？”

“没有，额...我觉得信使似乎已经在路上被伪帝国抓住了？毕竟无序那的动物们送信可从来不会迟到...”

星光熠熠早就预料到了这个情况，自从断开陆上联系开始，星光和无序就只能通过信使交流。信使不但要躲开伪帝国的搜查，有时还得翻山越岭，导致双方情报交流线路极其不畅。她叹了口气：

“每次送信都可能让我们失去一位小马，我们简直就是让小马去送死。日光，下个联络日让信使最后一次送信，给余晖烁烁和无序各一次。”

“做什么？”
[en.@desc]
Every mid-month, Starlight, Discord, and Sunset will contact each other to exchange their information.
Thanks to the existence of the magical diary, it is very convenient for Starlight and Sunset to contact each other. Starlight picked up the notebook and wrote down the contact content on the paper:
“We are currently short of supplies, and refugees come to Our Town every day...”
The magical traces on the notebook ensure that Sunset has received the contact content.

Putting down the notebook, Starlight looked at the clock. Sunburst pushed the door open and walked in.
“Sunburst, hasn’t Discord replied yet?”
“No, um... I think the messenger seems to have been caught by the illegal empire on the way? After all, Discord’s animals never deliver messages late...”
Starlight had long anticipated this situation. Since the cut-off of land contact, Starlight and Discord could only communicate through messengers. The messengers not only had to evade the search of the illegal empire, but sometimes they also had to cross mountains and ridges, resulting in extremely unsmooth information exchange routes between the two sides.
Starlight sighed:
“Every time a message is sent, we may lose a pony. We are simply sending ponies to their deaths. Sunburst, let the messenger send the message for the last time on the next Day of Contact, one to Sunset and one to Discord.”
