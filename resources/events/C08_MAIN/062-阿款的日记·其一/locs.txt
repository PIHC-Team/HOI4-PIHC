April 22nd
[zh.@desc]
自从成为火车司机以后，我已经有好久没有写日记了，但是今天我觉得有必要记录一下。
这应该会是我在洛马基的最后一个星期了。我和满贯一起，与之前魔术表演的助理和同行们都悄悄聚了餐，道了别。我们已经准备好了，明天我们就要离开这里，去马哈顿。
我请了几天假，我要好好珍惜这最后一个周，重新再欣赏一下这个我们曾经热爱的地方，我们经营了十数年魔术表演的地方。
[en.@desc]
It’s been a long time since I became a train driver and wrote a diary, but today I feel it’s necessary to record something.	
This should be my last week in Las Pegasus. Together with Jackpot, we had a secret dinner with the assistants and other magicians from our magic show, and said goodbye. We are ready, and tomorrow we will leave here and go to Manehattan.
I took a few days off work, because I want to cherish this last week, to appreciate this place we once loved, the place where we ran our magic show for over a decade.
