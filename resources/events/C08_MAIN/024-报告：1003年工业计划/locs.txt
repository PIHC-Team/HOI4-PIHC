主题：1003年工业计划

目前，抵抗军境内民用工业尚能维持运转。自伪帝国流亡的难民填补了劳动力缺口。民用工业相关工马已初步完成防袭扰训练，伪帝国袭扰造成民用工业破坏性已大大减弱，计划维持民用工业现状，将重心放在军事工业上。

军事工业难以供应军队需要。原有的军事工业薄弱，在伪帝国袭扰下难以快速扩张军事工业。根据国家安全部报告（编号11-A.2a），两种针对防区的军事工业扩张方案可行：
1.突袭女皇山军火库：预计能即刻补充军备缺口
2.潜入圣光流镇军工厂：预计能获取当地生产机床

除此之外，资源方面，水晶山脉勘探队报告称在水晶山脉发现大量水晶矿藏，并有疑似小马造建筑物，计划向水晶山脉派遣一支大型勘探队执行开发计划，预计耗时一个月。

署名：经济部长 糖蓓儿

Subject: Industrial Plan 1003

Currently, the civil industry within the territory of the Resistance can still operate. The labor shortage has been filled by refugees from the illegal empire. The civil industry-related workponies have all completed basic anti-raid training. The destructive power to the civil industry caused by the raids of the illegal empire has been greatly reduced. The plan is to maintain the current state of the civil industry and focus on the military industry.

The military industry is currently unable to meet the needs of the army. The ones that we originally had were weak, and under the raids of the illegal empire, it is difficult to expand our military industry soon. According to the report from the National Security Department (No. 11-A.2a), two feasible plans for the expansion of the military industry by exploiting the EEMD Jurisdiction are as follows:
1. Raid the Empress Cozy Glow’s arsenal: It is expected to immediately replenish the military equipment gap.
2. Infiltrate the military factory in Saint Glow Town: It is expected to obtain their lathes and machines.

Apart from this, in terms of resources, the Crystal Mountain Exploration Team reported that a large amount of crystal ore has been discovered in the Crystal Mountains, and there seems to be buildings built by ponies. A large exploration team is planned to be sent to the Crystal Mountains to execute the development plan, which is expected to take one month.

Signature: Head of Economy, Sugar Belle
