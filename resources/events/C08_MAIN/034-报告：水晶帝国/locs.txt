主题：水晶帝国

《坎特洛特协定》签订以后，水晶帝国被迫遵从了伪小马利亚帝国和幻形灵帝国的诸多无理要求：《协定》规定水晶帝国去军事化，臣服于伪帝国，其政府由伪帝国参与管理。幻形灵宣称保障水晶帝国独立，同时要求水晶帝国提供爱意贡赋。实质上水晶帝国已成为伪帝国与幻形灵的殖民地。

水晶帝国拥有一个理事会，其内主要力量构成如下：
• 小马利亚团结委员会：由伪帝国派遣代表构成，代表坎特洛特的利益，操纵理事会选举
• 幻形灵顾问团：由幻形灵帝国派遣代表构成，代表幻形灵（邪茧）的利益
• 水晶帝国皇室内阁：由水晶帝国皇室代表构成，代表皇室的利益
次要力量构成如下：
• 水晶帝国和谐党：由谐律主义代表构成
• 水晶帝国自由党：由民主主义代表构成
• 红色水晶：由平等主义代表构成
其中，皇室内阁与和谐党、自由党及红色水晶有一定的反伪帝国及反幻形灵倾向，可能发展为一股反对势力。


《坎特洛特协定》判决音韵公主犯有“战争罪”，要求音韵公主退位，并将音韵公主及闪耀盔甲软禁在水晶庄园。风雪之心被推举为水晶帝国君主，而原本的皇室只支持者也被多数保留。他们的政治势力正在逐渐恢复，但仍未能与伪帝国及幻形灵帝国抗衡。
如未来水晶帝国与伪帝国发生正面冲突或发送变故时，音韵公主将是一股重要的谐律力量，应重点关注并保护。

Subject: The Crystal Empire

After the signing of the Canterlot Pact, the Crystal Empire was forced to comply with many unreasonable demands from the illegal Equestria Empire and the Changeling Empire: the PACT stipulates that the Crystal Empire must be demilitarized, subject to the illegal empire, and its government is managed by the illegal empire. The Changeling Empire claims to guarantee the independence of the Crystal Empire, while demanding that the Crystal Empire provide love tribute. The Crystal Empire has become a de facto colony of the illegal empire and the Changeling Empire.

The Crystal Empire has a council, the main forces of which are as follows:
- Equestria Unity Commission: composed of representatives sent by the illegal empire, representing the interests of Canterlot, and manipulating council elections.
- Changeling Consultants Delegate: composed of representatives sent by the Changeling Empire, representing the interests of the Changelings (Chrysalis).
- Crystal Empire Council: composed of representatives of the Crystal Empire royal family, representing the interests of the royal family.
The secondary forces are as follows:
- Crystal Empire Harmonicist Party: composed of representatives of harmonism.
- Crystal Empire Liberation Party: composed of representatives of democracy.
- Red Crystal: composed of representatives of equatism.
Among them, the Royal Cabinet and the Harmonicist Party, the Liberation Party, and the Red Crystal have a certain anti- illegal empire and anti- Changeling Empire tendency. So they may develop into an opposition force.

The Canterlot Pact ruled that Princess Cadance was guilty of “war crimes”, punished her to abdicate, and confined her and Shining Armor in the Crystal Manor. Flurry Heart was proposed as the monarch of the Crystal Empire. The original royal supporters were mostly retained. Their political power is gradually recovering, but they have not yet been able to compete with the illegal empire and the Changeling Empire.
If the Crystal Empire has a direct conflict with the illegal empire or an accident occurs in the future, Princess Cadance will be an important force of harmonism and should be focused on and protected.
