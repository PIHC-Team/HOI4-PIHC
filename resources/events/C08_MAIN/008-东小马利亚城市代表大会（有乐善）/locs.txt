东小马利亚城市代表大会
[en.@]
Eastern Equestria Cities’ Summit
[zh.@desc]
马哈顿奴隶贸易问题解决后，星光熠熠邀请了东小马利亚各个城市的利益团体代表前往马哈顿共同参加一次峰会，商讨东小马利亚未来发展的问题。
会议出席的城市代表总计9名：解放阵线2名、蹄铁丘1名、蹄林城1名、马哈顿市2名、小马德菲亚市1名、巴尔的马市1名、马蹄湾1名。
虽然解放阵线在会议中只有两名代表，但由于蹄铁丘和蹄林城也在解放阵线的管理之下，巴尔的马市又轻微亲近解放阵线，因此解放阵线在会议中已经占据了半数的优势。
然而，小马德菲亚、马哈顿、马蹄湾，作为东小马利亚能够与马哈顿平起平坐的重要港口城市和商业城市，他们的代表在会议中表现出了强烈的不满情绪，他们认为解放阵线的平等主义政策对他们的利益造成了严重的损害。他们也并不希望与小马利亚帝国产生争执。与此同时，巴尔的马则是在会议中表现出了中立的态度。
最终，解放阵线提出的议案以4票赞成、1票弃权、4票反对的结果陷入僵持。小马德菲亚与马蹄湾的代表并未敢于公开在会议上反对星光熠熠，但他们的态度已经表明了他们的立场。如果得不到他们的支持，议案也仅仅是一纸无法通过的空文。
[en.@desc]
After the resolution of the slave trade problem in Manehattan, Starlight Glimmer invited representatives of various interest groups from cities in Eastern Equestria to attend a summit in Manehattan to discuss the future development of Eastern Equestria.
A total of 9 city representatives attended the meeting: 2 from the Liberation Front, 1 from Hooflyn, 1 from Farrier Hill, 2 from Manehattan, 1 from Fillydelphia, 1 from Baltimare, and 1 from Horseshoe Bay.
Although the Liberation Front had only two representatives at the meeting, since Hooflyn and Farrier Hill were also under the control of the Liberation Front, and Baltimare was slightly pro-Liberation Front, the Liberation Front already had a majority at the meeting.
However, the representatives of Fillydelphia, Manehattan, and Horseshoe Bay, as important portal and commercial cities in Eastern Equestria that could sit on an equal footing with Manehattan, showed strong dissatisfaction at the meeting. They believed that the Liberation Front’s equatism policy had seriously damaged their interests. They did not want to have disputes with the Equestria Empire. Meanwhile, Baltimare showed a neutral attitude at the meeting.
In the end, the proposal put forward by the Liberation Front was deadlocked with 4 votes in favor, 1 abstention, and 4 votes against. The representatives of the three cities did not dare to openly oppose Starlight Glimmer at the meeting, but their attitude had already indicated their position. If we did not get their support, the proposal would be nothing more than a piece of paper that could never be passed.
