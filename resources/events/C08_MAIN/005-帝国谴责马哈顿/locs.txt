帝国谴责马哈顿奴隶贸易
[en.@]
Empire condemns Manehattan for Slave Trade
[zh.@desc]
随着近日马哈顿奴隶贸易曝光后舆论愈演愈烈，要求帝国中央对此做出回应的呼声也日益高涨。今日上午，和煦光流女皇发布了公告：
“小马利亚帝国旨在团结所有小马，努力建立一个小马们生活幸福安稳的国度，奴隶贸易是帝国所不能接受的行为...”
“...因此，帝国中央对相关信息高度关切，要求马哈顿及其周边地区即刻进行整改。在整改完成前，取消乐善集团的特许经营权限，乐善的市长职务暂停...”
“...但是，历史问题错综复杂，马哈顿及其周边地区长期以来享有着高度自治的地位，本着团结友好的精神，帝国将不会破坏这一自治传统，也不会对马哈顿及其周边地区的内部，不涉及奴隶贸易的事务妄加干涉，更不会在马哈顿派驻驻军...”
针对这一表态，抵抗军领袖星光熠熠认为这是一种很暧昧的免责声明，而小马利亚帝国新闻则称这体现了帝国的责任感，并强调要注意防止部分势力借题发挥抹黑帝国。
[en.@desc]
As the Manehattan slave trade was exposed in recent days, the voices calling for the central government of the Empire to respond to this issue have also increased. This morning, Empress Cozy Glow issued a notice:
“The Equestria Empire is committed to uniting all ponies and building a country where ponies can live a happy and stable life. The slave trade is an unacceptable behavior of the Empire...”
“Therefore, the central government of the Empire is highly concerned about the relevant information and requires Manehattan and its surrounding areas to rectify immediately. Before the rectification is completed, the special license of Gladmane Group will be cancelled, and the mayor of Gladmane will be suspended...”
“However, the historical issues are complicated. Manehattan and its surrounding areas have long enjoyed a high degree of autonomy. In the spirit of unity and friendship, the Empire will not destroy this autonomous tradition, nor will it interfere with the internal affairs of Manehattan and its surrounding areas that do not involve slave trade, and will not station troops in Manehattan...”
In response to this statement, the leader of the Resistance, Starlight Glimmer, believes that this is a very ambiguous disclaimer, while the Equestria Empire News believes that it reflects the Empire’s sense of responsibility and emphasizes the need to prevent some particular forces from taking advantage of the situation to discredit the Empire.
