马哈顿解放后，星光熠熠邀请了东小马利亚各个大区的代表前往平等镇参加会议，以让东小马利亚各城市更加团结一致。

据报道，帝国已经向其东部增派更多兵力，称“小马谷的行为是保护帝国的公民”，督促小马谷做好准备，对抗解放阵线。对此，小马谷尚未发表正式回应。
[en.@desc]
After the liberation of Manehattan, Starlight Glimmer invited representatives from various districts of Eastern Equestria to attend a meeting in Equality Town to make the cities of Eastern Equestria more united.

Representatives from Trottingham, Hooflyn, Manehattan, and Fillydelphia attended the meeting, but Baltimare was absent because it was not under the control of the Liberation Front. At the meeting, Starlight Glimmer strongly condemned the “criminals harbored by Ponyville” and said that Ponyville would “pay the price”. The representatives of the cities present expressed their support.

It is reported that the Empire has sent more troops to its eastern border, and stated that the Ponyville should be prepared to fight against the Liberation Front, claiming that “Ponyville’s actions are protecting the citizens of the Empire”. Ponyville has not yet made a formal response.
