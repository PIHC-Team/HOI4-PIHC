《逃离马哈顿》出版

Escape From Manehattan
[zh.@desc]
近日，马哈顿著名作家马基塔出版了系列小说《逃离马哈顿》的测试版序章，向读者们描绘了一个马哈顿陷入混乱的世界线。

故事背景发生在乐善集团崩溃后的马哈顿，帝国对马哈顿的封锁导致马哈顿陷入混乱，小马们被困在马哈顿这座孤岛中。主角马基塔是帝国的一名士兵，他的母亲因为奴隶贸易被卖往了马哈顿，他因此报名加入了帝国驻马哈顿的维和部队，却被帝国抛弃，困在了马哈顿。

为了逃离马哈顿，马基塔开始在四方势力中周旋。作者刻画了一个个生动的角色，如生意至上的前保安部队军官、利益熏心的维和部队后勤官、遍布马哈顿的黑帮网络、占领商场和化工厂的小马两兄弟Killa和Tagilla... 作品一经发表，便受全世界读者们的热烈欢迎，截止目前，销量已经突破了数万，为表感谢，马基塔称将在不久的将来出版《逃离马哈顿》的正式版，并亲切地称读者们为“真正的信徒”。
[en.@desc]
Recently, the famous Manehattan writer Makita Hayanov published the prologue of the test version of the novel series “Escape From Manehattan”, depicting a world line where Manehattan is in chaos to the readers.

The story takes place in Manehattan after the collapse of the Gladmane Group. The Empire’s blockade of Manehattan has led to chaos in Manehattan, and the ponies are trapped on this island. The protagonist Makita is a soldier of the Empire, whose mother was sold to Manehattan because of the slave trade. He therefore signed up to join the Empire’s peacekeeping force stationed in Manehattan. However, he was abandoned by the Empire and trapped in Manehattan.

To escape from Manehattan, Makita was forced to fight with and against four different forces. The author portrayed vivid characters, such as the former security force officer who puts business above all else, the logistics officer of the peacekeeping force who is driven by interests, the gang network that spreads throughout Manehattan, and the two brothers Killa and Tagilla who occupy the mall and chemical factory... The work was warmly welcomed by readers around the world once it was published. As of now, the sales have exceeded tens of thousands. To express his gratitude, Makita announced that he will publish the official version of “Escape From Manehattan” in the near future, and affectionately referred to the readers as “true believers”.
