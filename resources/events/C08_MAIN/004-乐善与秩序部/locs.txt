主题：马哈顿与伪帝国

早在和煦加冕仪式前后，乐善就已经开始深度参与小马利亚帝国中央的政治决策。

传言称乐善当时在坎特洛特参与了一次秘密会议，而会议的主题就是关于成立小马利亚帝国秩序部的决议。或者毫不避讳地说，乐善就是秩序部的实质创始者，也只有他才能提出对所有小马实行监控和审查等一系列恐怖措施的想法。

证据表明，乐善虽然明面上与小马利亚帝国政府和秩序部没有什么联系，但仍是秩序部背后最大的合作伙伴。事实上，马哈顿在进行的奴隶贸易中，相当一部分奴隶，甚至乐善自己手下的一些得力干将，都被派往坎特洛特，成为秩序部的员工。也正是因为秩序部对劳动力的大量需求，才得以让坎特洛特对马哈顿的事情视而不见。

Subject: Manehattan and the illegal empire

As early as the coronation ceremony of Cozy Glow, Gladmane had already begun to deeply participate in the political decision-making of the central government of the Equestria Empire.

It is rumored that Gladmane participated in a secret meeting in Canterlot around the time of the coronation, and the main topic of the meeting was the resolution to establish the Equestria Empire Ministry of Order. Or, to put it bluntly, Gladmane is the actual founder of the Ministry of Order, and only he can propose the idea of implementing a series of terrifying measures such as monitoring and reviewing all ponies.

Evidence shows that although Gladmane has no direct connection with the Equestria Empire government and the Ministry of Order on the surface, he is still the biggest partner behind the Ministry of Order. In fact, in the slave trade conducted by Manehattan, a considerable number of slaves, and even some of Gladmane’s trusted subordinates, have been sent to Canterlot to become employees of the Ministry of Order. It is precisely because of the Ministry of Order’s huge demand for labor that Canterlot can turn a blind eye to what is happening in Manehattan.
