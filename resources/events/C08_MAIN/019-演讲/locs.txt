[zh.@desc]
小马们聚集在了广场中央，自“新元年”的爆炸性新闻以来，抵抗军的小马们一直情绪高涨，恨不得马上冲到坎特洛特活捉和煦光流，把她处刑成千上万遍。今天他们纷纷聚到广场，希望星光熠熠能做点什么，以报处决之仇。

尽管星光熠熠仍为导师的死而悲痛不止，她也明白暮光的死已成事实，现在她能做的就是团结抵抗军的小马，为以后的解放事业做准备。

“各位小马们！”星光喊了一句，台下所有小马瞬间停止了交谈，一齐向台上看去。“大家都听说了，暮光闪闪，我们的导师，友谊公主，被邪恶的和煦光流害死了，他们怎敢？！”

“他们怎敢？！”小马们异口同声，群情激愤。

“暮光闪闪已经离我们而去了，可她的死并非没有意义！所有的小马都见到了所谓‘小马利亚帝国’伪政权的邪恶，我们必须团结起来，为暮光闪闪公主报仇！”

星光熠熠深吸了一口气，调整了一下情绪：

“不是我们必须这样做，而是我们选择这样做！”
[en.@desc]
Ponies are gathered in the center of the square. Since the devastating news of “Jahr Null”, ponies in the resistance have been in high spirits, eager to rush to Canterlot to capture Empress Cozy Glow and execute her thousands of times. Today they gathered in the square, hoping that Starlight Glimmer could do something to avenge the execution of Twilight.

Although Starlight Glimmer is still grieving for the death of her mentor, she also understands that Twilight’s is already an unchangeable history. Now what she can do is to unite the ponies of the resistance and prepare for the future liberation cause.

“Everypony!” Starlight shouted, and all the ponies below stopped talking instantly, looking up at the stage. “You all heard about it, Twilight Sparkle, our mentor, the Princess of Friendship, was killed by the evil Cozy Glow. How dare they?!”

“How dare they?!” The ponies echoed in unison, their emotions running high.

“Twilight Sparkle has left us, but her death is not meaningless! All ponies have seen the evil of the so-called ‘Equestria Empire’. We must unite and avenge Princess Twilight Sparkle!”

Starlight Glimmer took a deep breath and adjusted her emotions:

“It’s not that we have to do this, it’s that we choose to do this!”
