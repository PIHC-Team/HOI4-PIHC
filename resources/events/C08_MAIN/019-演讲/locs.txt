The Speech
[zh.@desc]
小马们聚集在了广场中央，自“新元年”的爆炸性新闻以来，抵抗军的小马们一直情绪高涨，恨不得马上冲到坎特洛特活捉和煦光流，把她处刑成千上万遍。今天他们纷纷聚到广场，希望星光熠熠能做点什么，为新元年后失踪的朋友们讨回公道。

尽管星光熠熠仍因导师杳无音讯而悲痛不止，她也明白，自己不能继续等待帝国主动交出真相。现在她能做的就是团结抵抗军的小马，为以后的解放事业做准备。

“各位小马们！”星光喊了一句，台下所有小马瞬间停止了交谈，一齐向台上看去。“大家都听说了，暮光闪闪，我们的导师、友谊公主，被和煦光流从这个世界上抹去了。帝国不肯告诉我们她在哪里，甚至不肯证明她是否还活着。他们怎敢？！”

“他们怎敢？！”小马们异口同声，群情激愤。

“我们不知道暮光闪闪是否还能听见我们的声音，但她的失踪不能被帝国的沉默掩埋！所有的小马都见到了所谓‘小马利亚帝国’伪政权的邪恶。我们必须团结起来，找到暮光闪闪，也要让所有制造这一切的小马付出代价！”

星光熠熠深吸了一口气，调整了一下情绪：

“不是我们必须这样做，而是我们选择这样做！”
[en.@desc]
Ponies gathered in the center of the square. Since the devastating news of “Jahr Null”, the resistance had been in an uproar, eager to rush to Canterlot, capture Empress Cozy Glow, and execute her a thousand times over. Today they gathered in the square, hoping Starlight Glimmer would do something to win justice for their missing friends.

Although Starlight Glimmer still grieved over the silence surrounding her mentor, she understood that she could no longer wait for the Empire to surrender the truth. What she could do now was unite the resistance and prepare for the liberation to come.

“Everypony!” Starlight shouted, and every pony below fell silent. “You have all heard. Twilight Sparkle—our mentor, the Princess of Friendship—has been erased from the world by Cozy Glow. The Empire will not tell us where she is. It will not even prove that she is alive. How dare they?!”

“How dare they?!” The ponies echoed in unison, their emotions running high.

“We do not know whether Twilight Sparkle can still hear us, but her disappearance will not be buried beneath the Empire’s silence! Every pony has seen the evil of the so-called ‘Equestria Empire’. We must unite, find Twilight Sparkle, and make every architect of this crime pay!”

Starlight Glimmer took a deep breath and adjusted her emotions:

“It’s not that we have to do this, it’s that we choose to do this!”
