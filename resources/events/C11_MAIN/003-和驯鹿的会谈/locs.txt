与驯鹿的外交会谈
[en.@]
Diplomatic Talks with Reindeers
[zh.@desc]
驯鹿在会谈上丝毫没有回答我们的使臣所提出的任何要求，没有接受也没有拒绝，无论我们如何威胁，她们只是在自顾自地讲着莫名其妙的预言和深奥的道理。
其中一位爱丽丝自称可以预知未来，她警告我们的使臣，说：“我们的命运记载于星辰之上。”
随后爱丽丝解释道，如果选择武力，那么幻形灵对驯鹿的征服将会是成功的，但后果，将不会是幻形灵想要的。
[en.@desc]
Reindeers did not answer any of the requests made by our envoys at the talks, neither accepting nor rejecting. No matter how we threatened, they just kept talking about inexplicable prophecies and profound truths.
One of them, Alice, claimed to be able to predict the future. She warned our ambassadors, saying, “Our destinies are written in the stars.”
Then Alice explained that if we chose to use force, the conquest of the reindeers by the changelings would be successful, but the consequences would not be what the changelings wanted.
