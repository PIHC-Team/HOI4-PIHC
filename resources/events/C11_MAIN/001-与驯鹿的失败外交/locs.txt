与驯鹿的失败外交
[en.@]
A Failed Diplomacy with Reindeers
[zh.@desc]
幻形灵帝国试图向驯鹿们传达邪茧女王的旨意：驯鹿们必须要臣服于幻形灵帝国，并且定期向幻形灵贡奉爱意，否则幻形灵军队将踏平海尔伯格！
然而，这一次外交恐吓却遭遇了空前的失败：并不是驯鹿们敢于违抗幻形灵帝国的命令，而是幻形灵帝国的命令根本就没有传达到驯鹿们的耳朵里。
我们的使臣从未成功登上雪山山腰，屡次被暴风雪驱赶，不得不返回营地。那些生活在雪上山腰和山顶的家伙们究竟是怎么做到的呢？真是费解。
无论如何，我们必须找到一种方法，让他们臣服于我们，或者至少听到我们的声音。
[en.@desc]
Changeling Empire tried to convey the will of Queen Chrysalis to the reindeers: the reindeers must submit to the Changeling Empire and regularly pay love as tribute to the Changelings, otherwise the Changeling army will destroy Hailberg!
However, this diplomatic threat encountered an unprecedented failure: it was not that the reindeers dared to disobey the orders of the Changeling Empire, but that the orders of the Changeling Empire simply never reached the ears of the reindeers.
Our ambassador never succeeded in climbing the snow mountain, and was repeatedly driven back by blizzards, having to return to the camp. How did those things living on the snow mountain and the mountaintop manage to do it? It’s really puzzling.
Nevertheless, we have to find a way to make them submit to us, or at least hear us.
