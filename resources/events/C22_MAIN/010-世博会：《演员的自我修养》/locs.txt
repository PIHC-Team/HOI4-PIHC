《闪电天马的自我修养》

A Wonderbolt Prepares
[zh.@desc]
乘风不仅是一名闪电天马队前成员，依然存活的长距离飞行最快纪录保持者，更是一名出色的演员。乘风并在许多悬疑犯罪影视剧中饰演了令观众印象深刻的反派形象，甚至还曾经与海顿女王有过对手戏。本书是乘风虚构的一本日记，讲述一名闪电天马队老队员被用假纸条诬陷后坚持立场而被踢出闪电天马队的故事。通过这样一个虚构但改编自真实事件的故事，乘风在书中介绍了许多表演方法与想象力技巧。尽管背景是闪电天马队，但这本书如今已经成为了许多表演学校的教材。

§b... 没有过慢的表演，只有过慢的闪电天马。闪电天马必须使用想象力来回答所有问题（何时、何地、为何、如何），让虚构的存在更加具体化。§!

[en.@desc]
Wind Rider is not only a former Wonderbolt and the current record holder for the fastest long-distance flyer who is still alive, but also a talented actor. He has played memorable villains in many mystery and crime dramas, and even shared the screen with Queen Haydon. This book is a fictional diary by Wind Rider, telling the story of a former Wonderbolt who was framed with a fake note and kicked out of the Wonderbolts for standing his ground. Through this fictional story (based on a real event), Wind Rider introduces many acting techniques and imagination skills. Although set in the Wonderbolts, this book has become a textbook in many acting schools.

§b... There are no slow parts, only slow wonderbolts. The wonderbolt must use his imagination to be able to answer all questions (when, where, why, how). Make the make-believer existence more definite.”§!
