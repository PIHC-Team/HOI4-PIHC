麒麟之森
[en.@]
Kirin Forest
[zh.@desc]
在主会场的一角，摆放着一些似乎是来自丛林的植物，和丛林中的小树屋模型。据说，这是一支半人马贵族的丛林探险队从南方的麒麟之森带回来的样品，记录了生活在原始丛林中的麒麟族的生活方式和文化。
这些原始的生物大多单独居住，偶尔会聚集在一起，形成一个有着初具规模的农业的小型部落。他们的生活方式和文化与提雷克领地这样的文明王国完全不同：这不仅展现了半人马王国对于多元文化的包容核对与自然野生生物的尊重，也展现了半人马贵族们并非传言那样的高高在上蔑视一切，他们有着好奇心与探索精神，并在提雷克的领导下探索着半人马王国的未知边界。

[en.@desc]
In a corner of the main venue, there are some plants that seem to come from the jungle, and models of tree houses in the jungle. It is said that this is a sample brought back by a centaur noble jungle exploration team from the Kirin Forest in the south, recording the lifestyle and culture of the Kirin tribe living in the primitive jungle.
These primitive creatures mostly live alone, occasionally gathering together to form a small tribe with a rudimentary agriculture. Their lifestyle and culture are completely different from civilized kingdoms like Tirek’s realm: this not only shows the centaur kingdom’s tolerance and respect for multiculturalism and natural wildlife, but also shows that the centaur nobles are not as snobbish as the rumors say, they have curiosity and spirit of exploration, and are exploring the unknown boundaries of the centaur kingdom under Tirek’s leadership.
