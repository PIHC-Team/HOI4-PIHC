还没走进蒸汽机会场，游客们就能够听到轰隆作响的无数黄铜机械，接着是蒸汽嘶嘶的声音和鸣笛声。为了吸引各国的军官与采购商，蒸汽机会场第一间就是洛马基军工集团的展馆。
展馆中央是一台巨大的蒸汽机，由镶嵌着无数齿轮的黄铜外壳和被煤灰覆盖的玻璃窗组成。蒸汽机旁的介绍，讲述了两兄弟从超级快速苹果榨汁机6000开始，一步一步改良机械动力，最终发现蒸汽能源的故事。
在蒸汽机的旁边，还有一个黄铜做的盒子 —— “坦克”，这是洛马基军工集团最新的产品。与木制或铁制的战车不同，坦克不仅用黄铜包裹了整个车身，还有一台小型蒸汽机在其内部为其提供前进的动力。对于无法理解这一概念的小马，一个通俗易懂的解释是：坦克就是一个可以移动的堡垒。
围绕展馆一圈的，则是各式各样的火器，其中最新式的燧发蹄铳最受欢迎。在燧发蹄铳旁围着来自各个地区的军官，牦牛、幻形灵、夜骐、鹦鹉... 他们激烈地讨论着，而在一旁，独角兽们则在与一位洛马基的顾问交谈如何用过魔法的方式提高燧发蹄铳的射速与精度。

Not even entering the steam engine exhibition, visitors can already hear the thunderous sound of countless brass machinery, followed by the hissing of steam and the sound of whistles. To attract military officers and buyers from various countries, the LPMIG’s pavilion is the first one in the steam engine exhibition.
In the center of the pavilion is a huge steam engine, composed of a brass shell inlaid with countless gears decorated on it. It also has glass windows covered with coal ash. The introduction next to the steam engine tells the story of two brothers who started with the Super Speedy Cider Squeezy 6000, improved mechanical power step by step, and finally discovered steam energy.
Next to the steam engine, there is another brass box — the “tank”, which is the latest product of the LPMIG. Unlike wooden or iron chariots, the tank is fully wrapped in brass and has a small steam engine inside to provide power for it to move forward. For ponies who cannot understand this concept, a simple explanation is: a tank is a mobile fortress.
Surrounding the pavilion is a variety of firearms, with the latest hoofgun being the most popular. Military officers from various regions are gathered around the hoofgun, including yaks, changelings, thestrals, and parrots... They are discussing fiercely, while unicorns are talking to an LPMIG consultant on how to improve the firing speed and accuracy of the hoofgun using magic.
