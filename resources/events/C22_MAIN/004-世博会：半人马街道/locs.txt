半人马街景
[en.@]
Centaur Streets View
[zh.@desc]
主会场中的照片展示了半人马的街道景象：贵族半人马坐在珠光宝气的轿车里吃着杯糕，其身影隐约从帘子后面透出，引来街上的目光：衣衫褴褛的龙族在道路旁低头致意，而石像鬼女士们则从沿街的房子里探出头来，向轿车微笑着打招呼。
在轿车的前方，是一位身着西装、器宇轩昂的狮鹫，手中拿着属于其后座雇主的金色的权杖，为轿车开路，指挥着拉动轿车的... 似乎是几只奴隶陆马，不过照片似乎被模糊处理了：此次世界博览会有许多来自小马利亚的商马与官员，显然主办方并不希望引起争议。

[en.@desc]
Photos in the main venue show the street scenes of the centaurs: noble centaurs sit in a luxurious palanquin eating cup cakes, their figures faintly visible from behind the curtains, attracting the attention of the street. Ragged dragons bow along the road, while gargoyle ladies poke their heads out of the houses along the street, smiling and waving to the palanquin.
In front of the palanquin is a griffon in a suit, holding a golden scepter belonging to his employer in the back seat, leading the way for the palanquin and directing the... seemingly several slave earth ponies. However, the photo seems to have been blurred: there are many business ponies and officials from Equestria at this Greate Exhibition, and the organizers apparently do not want to cause controversy.
