Baltimare Warfare
[zh.@desc]
在星光抵抗军南下之前，乐善在南方的重要地区都实行了“堡垒政策”，乐善宣称在层层堡垒和街垒的保护下，星光抵抗军的军队将会承受重大伤亡并无法发起进攻。
但出乎守军意料的是，最坚固的堡垒，往往从内部被攻破——在红色电台的宣传中，巴尔的马的守军不战而溃，星光抵抗军的军队与起义军在短短几天内就攻占了巴尔的马。
[en.@desc]
Before the southward aggression of the Starlight Resistance, Gladmane implemented the “fortress policy” in all the important areas in the south of eastern Equestria. Gladmane claimed that under the protection of layers of fortresses and barricades, the army of the Starlight Resistance would suffer heavy casualties and be unable to launch an attack.
However, to the surprise of the defenders, the most solid fortresses were often broken from the inside. In the propaganda of the Red Radio, the defenders of Baltimare collapsed without a fight, and the army of the Starlight Resistance and the rebels captured Baltimare in just a few days.
