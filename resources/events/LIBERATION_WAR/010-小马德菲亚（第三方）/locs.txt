Fillydelphia Warfare
[zh.@desc]
在万籁俱寂的夜晚，小马德菲亚的居民们早已入睡，此时的整座城市已经毫无烟火气息。突然，一阵炮声打破了这个城市宁静的夜晚，毫无疑问，星光抵抗军向该市发起了总攻。尽管炮声震耳，但市民们表现得并不慌张，对他们而言，这是星光抵抗军发起的第n次进攻，每次进攻都使星光抵抗军的军队付出重大伤亡，但收效甚微。这既使城内的乐善集团军洋洋得意，又使小马们逐步适应了这场围城之战。但第二天清晨，小马们发现城市街道上的乐善旗帜早已被换成了星光抵抗军的旗帜。至此，乐善集团在南方战区的屏障就这样被突破了。
[en.@desc]
At a peaceful and quiet night, the residents of Fillydelphia had already fallen asleep, and the whole city was completely in its dream. Suddenly, a burst of gunfire broke the tranquility of the city, and there was no doubt that the Starlight Resistance launched a general attack on the city. Although the gunfire was deafening, the citizens did not panic. For them, this was the who-knows-how-many-th time that the Starlight Resistance had attacked the city. Every attack made the army of the Starlight Resistance suffer heavy casualties, but to no avail. This made the Gladmane Group in the city proud, and the ponies gradually adapted to the siege. But the next morning, the ponies woke up only to find that the flags of the Gladmane Group on the streets had been replaced by the flags of the Starlight Resistance. Thus, the barrier of the Gladmane Group in the southern war zone was broken.
