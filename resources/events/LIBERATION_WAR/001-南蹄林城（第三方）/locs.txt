Meanwhile, Gladmane also issued a statement that the security forces of Manehattan have been assembled and can “reoccupy the city” at any time. However, there are also ponies within the security forces who have expressed that the loss of the Southern Hooflyn City has disrupted the entire deployment of the security forces, and the so-called reinforcements are just self-deceiving lies. As after the loss of the area, Gladmane has already withdrawn the remaining forces in the area to the southern area or the Manehattan capital.
