列车劫案
[en.@]
Train Heist
[zh.@desc]
帝国东北部边界，一辆列车刚刚出发准备开向坎特洛特，突然一位蒙面小马闯进了列车驾驶室，用一把铳枪抵住了列车长的头。
列车长虽然吓得脸色苍白，但还是很快意识到发生了什么：“你是抵抗军吧？要劫持列车，开到平等镇去？你说什么我都配合，只要你别开枪就行。”
蒙面小马说：“不，往坎特洛特开。”
列车长有些疑惑：“可是这列车本来就是要开往坎特洛特的啊？”
蒙面小马听完非常生气：“混蛋，我就是想去坎特洛特办点事，买了三次火车票都被劫车到了平等镇！这次我要抢在他们前面劫车！”
[en.@desc]
At the northeastern border of the Empire, a train has just departed and is about to head to Canterlot, when a masked pony suddenly broke into the driver’s cab, pointing a hoofgun at the train conductor.
Although the conductor was scared pale, he quickly realized what was happening: “You are a rebel, right? You want to hijack the train and head to Our Town? I will cooperate with you no matter what you say, as long as you don’t shoot.”
The masked pony said: “No, head to Canterlot.”
The conductor was puzzled: “But this train is already heading to Canterlot, what do you want?”
The masked pony was very angry after hearing this: “Damn it, I just want to go to Canterlot. I’ve bought three tickets and everytime my train was hijacked to Our Town! This time I will act before the rebels do!”
