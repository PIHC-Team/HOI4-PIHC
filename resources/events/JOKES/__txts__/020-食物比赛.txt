食物比赛
[en.@]
Food Competition

[zh.@desc]
一只化茧幻形灵，一只未化茧幻形灵，和一只小马在一起讨论自己的食物。
小马很骄傲的说：“我来自小马谷，在小马谷，我们有着世界上最好的干草汉堡！吃一个，你就会感到非常幸福！”
未化茧的幻形灵说：“哼！不论你们小马有什么，我们只需要抢过来吃就行了，这才是更幸福的！”
化茧幻形灵说：“我们分享爱意，甚至不需要进食就是幸福的。”
这时，一只路过的小马突然加入了对话：
“我来自小马利亚帝国，在和煦女皇的统治下，我们也不需要进食，而且我们也必须很幸福。”

[en.@desc]
A metamorphosed Changeling, an unmetamorphosed Changeling, and a pony were discussing their food.
The pony said proudly, “I come from Ponyville. In Ponyville, we have the best hayburgers in the world! Eat one, and you will feel very happy!”
The unmetamorphosed Changeling said, “Puff! No matter what you ponies have, we just need to rob it and eat it. That is the real happiness!”
The metamorphosed Changeling said, “We share love. We don’t need to eat to be happy.”
At this time, a passing pony suddenly joined the conversation:
“I come from the Equestria Empire. Under the rule of Empress Cozy Glow, we also don’t need to eat, and we must be very happy too.”
