复古运动
[en.@]
Retro Movement

[zh.@desc]
火光在名流谷开展复古运动，保护名流谷的历史文化。复古运动进行了，一段时间后，火光开设了一个新的历史展馆，里面是各种历史时期的物件。
首先，游客们看到一个硬到不能吃的面包，导游介绍到：“这是1000年前我们的祖先吃的面包。”
随后，游客们看到一堆积满灰尘的古书，导游介绍到：“这是300年前名流谷作家的著作。”
在展览的最后，游客们惊讶地看到一个展区里充满了科幻科技：智能手机、悬浮汽车、魔法机器...各种难以想象的先进技术。
一名游客问：“是不是放错展区了？我们是不是走到恒星耀斑的未来展区了？”
“不是的，” 导游解释道 “这是名流谷还没有开展复古运动时的科技。”

[en.@desc]
Firelight launched a retro movement in the Celebrity Valley to protect the historical culture of Sire’s Hollow. After the retro movement was carried out for a period of time, Firelight opened a new historical exhibition hall, which contains objects from various historical periods.
First, the tourists saw a bread that was so hard that it couldn’t be eaten, and the guide introduced: “This is the bread that our ancestors ate 1000 years ago.”
Then, the tourists saw a pile of dusty ancient books, and the guide introduced: “These are the works of the writers of Sire’s Hollow 300 years ago.”
At the end of the exhibition, the tourists were surprised to see an exhibition area full of science fiction technology: smartphones, flying cars, magic machines... all kinds of unimaginable advanced technology.
A tourist asked: “Is it in the wrong exhibition area? Did we walk into the future exhibition by Stellar Flare?”
“No,” the guide explained, “this is the technology of Sire’s Hollow before the retro movement was launched.”
