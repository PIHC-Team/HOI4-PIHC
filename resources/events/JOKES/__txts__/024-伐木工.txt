伐木工
[en.@]
Lumberjack
[zh.@desc]
四处流浪的石蹄找到了小马利亚边缘的一家林业公司，准备用假名字申请一份工作安定下来。老板安排他做一名伐木工，给了他一把电锯。
第一天，他只砍下了六棵树。老板非常生气，“我们这里最弱不禁风、最偷懒的伐木工都能砍下十棵树，你这么强壮却只砍下六棵树，怎么回事？”
第二天，石蹄十分努力，但还是只砍下了七棵树，老板更加生气了。
第三天，他由于过度劳累，只砍下了三棵树，老板终于忍不住了：“我年轻的时候一天能砍十八棵树！明天让我来演示一下！”
第四天，老板亲自上阵，当着石蹄的面用电锯砍下了一棵树。石蹄疑惑地问：“电锯发出的那个嗡嗡的噪音是什么？”
[en.@desc]
Wanderingg Rockhoof found a forestry company on the edge of Equestria and prepared to apply for a job with a fake name to settle down. The boss arranged for him to be a lumberjack and gave him a chainsaw.
On the first day, he only cut down six trees. The boss was very angry. “Even the weakest and laziest lumberjack here can cut down ten trees. You are so strong but only cut down six trees. What’s going on?”
On the second day, Rockhoof worked very hard, but still only cut down seven trees, making the boss even angrier.
On the third day, due to excessive fatigue, he only cut down three trees, and the boss finally couldn’t help it: “When I was young, I could cut down eighteen trees in a day! Let me demonstrate tomorrow!”
On the fourth day, the boss personally took the field, and in front of Rockhoof, he cut down a tree with the chainsaw. Rockhoof asked in confusion, “What is that buzzing noise from the chainsaw?”
