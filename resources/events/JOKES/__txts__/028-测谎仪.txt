测谎仪
[en.@]
Lie Detector
[zh.@desc]
帝国听说小马谷的蹄博士刚刚发明了一个测谎仪，于是派委员前去视察。
蹄博士首先为委员演示了测谎仪，说了一些明显的真话和谎话，测谎仪都准确识别：“哔！”说明是真话，“嘟！”说明是假话。
委员非常高兴，问旁边随行的小马：“你对和煦女皇和小马利亚帝国怎么看？” 旁边的小马支支吾吾：“我...我热爱小马利亚帝国...我非常尊敬和煦女皇！”
“嘟！”

随行的小马立刻被警卫押走。委员有点不悦，又连续问了三四个小马，结果都是假话。蹄博士看到委员的表情明显阴沉了下来，也十分紧张。
就在这时，委员问旁边的摄影师——一位绿色的、可爱标志是一只瓢虫的小马：“你呢？你对你的国家是什么想法？”
看到之前有许多小马被押走，她紧张地说：“我...我非常热爱我的祖国！我相信世界上所有的小马都应该自觉把自己的爱，无私奉献给我们的祖国！”
“哔！”

[en.@desc]
The Empire heard that Dr. Hooves in Ponyville had just invented a lie detector, so they sent a commissioner to inspect it.
Dr. Hooves first demonstrated the lie detector to the commissioner, saying some obvious truths and lies, and the lie detector accurately identified them: “Beep!” means it’s true, “Boop!” means it’s false.
The commissioner was very happy and asked the pony who was traveling with him: “What do you think of Empress Cozy Glow and the Equestria Empire?” The pony next to him stammered: “I... I love the Equestria Empire... I respect Empress Cozy Glow very much!”
“Boop!”

The pony was immediately taken away by the guards. The commissioner was a little unhappy, and asked three or four more ponies in a row, and the results were all lies. Dr. Hooves saw the commissioner’s expression clearly darkened and became also very nervous.
Just then, the commissioner asked the photographer next to him —— a green, cute pony with a ladybug as her mark: “What about you? What do you think of your country?”
Seeing that many ponies were taken away previously, she nervously replied: “I... I love my country very much! I believe that all the ponies in the world should consciously give their love selflessly to our country!”
“Beep!”
