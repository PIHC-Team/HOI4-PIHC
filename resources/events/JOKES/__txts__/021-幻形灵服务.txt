Changeling Service

[zh.@desc]
随着小马谷的贸易开放，幻形灵的数量在小马谷越来越多，也因此发展出了一项新的服务业——幻形灵服务。
一天，富商钱多多看到了一家幻形灵服务店，店里的幻形灵可以变成任何小马，甚至是知名小马。
钱多多有点好奇，于是走进店里，店员问他需要什么服务。他有些犹豫，但是还是试了试，要求店员变成了歌星科罗拉。钱多多度过了愉快的一晚，满意的离开了。
几天后，钱多多没有忍住又来到了幻形灵服务店，这次他要求店员变成了特丽克西公爵。这次的体验也让他非常满意。
再过几天，钱多多又来到了幻形灵服务店，让店员变成了自己的妻子权多多，但是要店员温柔一点。钱多多又度过了愉快的一晚。
第四次，钱多多再次来到了幻形灵服务店，这次他想要追求一点刺激，要求和蜂熊一起度过一个晚上。
又过了一个周，第五次来到幻形灵服务店，闲聊时他夸赞起店员的服务，可店员非常诧异：
“上个周由于这个街区闹了蜂熊灾，我们关门歇业了呀？”

[en.@desc]
As the trade in Ponyville opened up, the number of Changelings in Ponyville increased, and a new service industry was developed — Changeling Service.
One day, the rich merchant Filty Rich saw a Changeling Service store, where the Changelings in the store could transform into any pony, even a well-known pony.
Filty Rich was a little curious, so he walked into the store, and the clerk asked him what service he needed. He hesitated a bit, but still tried it out, asking the clerk to transform into the singer Coloratura. Filty Rich had a pleasant evening and left satisfied.
A few days later, Filty Rich couldn’t help but come to the Changeling Service store again. This time he asked the clerk to transform into Duchess Trixie. This experience also made him very satisfied.
A few days later, Filty Rich came to the Changeling Service store again, asking the clerk to transform into his wife, Spoiled Rich, but asked the clerk to be gentler. Filty Rich had another pleasant evening.
The fourth time, Filty Rich came to the Changeling Service store again, this time he wanted a little excitement, asking to spend the night with a bugbear.
A week later, Filty Rich came to the Changeling Service store for the fifth time. During the chat, he praised the clerk’s service, but the clerk was very surprised:
"Last week, due to a bugbear disaster in this neighborhood, we closed the store temporarily!"
