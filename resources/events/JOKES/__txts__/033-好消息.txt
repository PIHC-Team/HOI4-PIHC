一名幻形灵帝国士兵拦住一名小马外交官。告知外交官，有一个好消息和一个坏消息。
外交官：“坏消息是什么？”
士兵：“邪茧女王帝国昨天有令，你国外交官必须在24小时内离境，否则将被抓去吸取爱意充国库。”
外交官大惊失色：“那好消息呢？”
士兵：“我们找了您一整天，可算把这个消息传达给您了！”
[en.@desc]
A Changeling Empire soldier stopped a pony diplomat. He told the diplomat that there is a good news and a bad news.
Diplomat: “What is the bad news?”
Soldier: “Queen Chrysalis issued an order yesterday that diplomats from your country must leave within 24 hours, otherwise they will be captured and their love will be drained to fill the national depository.”
The diplomat was shocked: “Then what is the good news?”
Soldier: “We have been looking for you all day, and finally we have delivered this message to you!”
