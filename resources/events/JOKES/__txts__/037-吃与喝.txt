吃与喝
[en.@]
Eating and Drinking

[zh.@desc]
有一天，帝国最高委员长的妻子鸢尾花，与帝国秩序部秘密警察领袖提亚拉一起走在街上。鸢尾花穿着名贵的衣服、画着浓妆，花枝招展，而提亚拉却穿着皮衣带着帽子、墨镜和口罩。
看到这一幕，小马们在巷子里议论纷纷，一只小马说：“我敢打赌，鸢尾花更喜欢吃，而提亚拉更喜欢喝。”
“你又不知道她们的饮食习惯，怎么，难道从穿着上可以看出来吗？”
“当然，因为吃的时候会满嘴流油大家都能看得出来，喝汤的时候端起盘子才会遮住脸。”
[en.@desc]
One day, the wife of the Supreme Chairpony of the Empire, Fleur de Lis, and head of the EEMO Secret Police, Diamond Tiara, walked down the street together. Fleur de Lis was dressed in expensive clothes, wearing heavy make-ups, while Tiara was wearing a leather jacket, a hat, sunglasses, and a mask.
Seeing this scene, the ponies in the alley were talking about it. One pony said, “I bet Fleur de Lis likes to eat more, while Tiara likes to drink more.”
“How can you tell their eating habits? Can you tell from their clothes?”
“Of course, because when eating, everyone can see oil all over her face, while only when drinking soup will somepony cover her face with a plate.”
