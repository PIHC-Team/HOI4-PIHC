小马利亚帝国统计局局长看着新出炉的年度统计报告：“嗯让我看看...就业率...不错...幸福指数...马均面包数...哇哦！死亡率降到了2‰！千分之二！今年的数据太棒了！和煦光流女皇看到这个报告一定会很开心的！”
不过为了保险起见，他还是叫来了一位调查员：“你们确定这些报告数据是真实的吗？死亡率降到了千分之二？这太了不起了！”
调查员回答：“当然是真实的，我们是通过抽样调查得出的。我们与秩序部展开联合调查，他们在街头随机采访了一千只小马，其中只有两只在采访过程中被枪毙。”

Equestria Empire Bureau of Statistics Director looked at the newly released annual statistical report: “Let me see... employment rate... good... happiness index... per capita bread number... Wow! The death rate has dropped to 2‰! Two per thousand! This year’s data is great! Empress Cozy Glow will be very happy to see this report!”
However, just to be safe, he still called in an investigator: “Are you sure these report data are real? The death rate has dropped to two per thousand? This is amazing!”
The investigator replied: “Of course it is real, we obtained it through random sampling. We conducted a joint investigation with the Ministry of Order, they randomly interviewed a thousand ponies on the street, and only two of them were shot during the interview.”
