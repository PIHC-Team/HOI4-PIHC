“Why are you monitoring us? We are all loyal ministers of Her Majesty!”
“Sorry... two ministers, but this is the policy of the Ministry of Order, not our decision. Monitoring is a powerful tool for the Empire to combat organized crime.”
“This is outrageous! Do you think that Her Majesty appointed us to be in charge of the Ministry of Defence of the Equestria Empire is ‘organized crime’?”
“Oh, of course not, but we also need to combat ‘disorganized crime’.”
