高档西装
[en.@]
High-end Suit

[zh.@desc]
一日，乘风穿着高档西装去参加宴会。宴会中，一只脏兮兮的小马突然闯入了会场抗议：“只有你们这些百万富翁一个个西装革履，你们看得到街上的其他小马的死活吗？”
听闻此话，乘风大惊失色，立刻脱掉了西装换上了朴素的皮夹克。
后来，乘风的朋友问起，他解释道：“我也是听到那只小马的话，才意识到，只有那些百万富翁才一个个西装革履。我可不想别的小马把我误认成那些穷鬼。”
[en.@desc]
One day, Wind Rider wore a high-end suit to attend a banquet. During the banquet, a dirty pony suddenly broke into the venue and protested: “Only you millionaires are dressed in suits and leather shoes. Can you even see the hardships of those poor homeless ponies on the street?”
Hearing this, Wind Rider was shocked and immediately took off his suit and put on a simple leather jacket.
Later, when Wind Rider’s friends asked him, he explained: “Only after hearing that pony’s words did I realize that only those millionaires are dressed in suits and leather shoes. I don’t want other ponies to mistake me for those poor millionaires.”
