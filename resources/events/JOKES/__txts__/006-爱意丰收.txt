爱意丰收
[en.@]
Love Harvest

[zh.@desc]
邪茧女王视察幻形灵领地，了解食物供给情况。一名幻形灵向女王报告：“今年我们收获的爱意已经可以堆到塞拉斯蒂娅的角那么高了！”
女王听完眉头一皱，有点不悦：“塞拉斯蒂娅？不要再提起这个名字！现在这个家伙已经从世界上消失了！”
幻形灵：“是的，现在我们的爱意供给也消失了。”

[en.@desc]
Queen Chrysalis inspected the Changeling territory and asked about the food supply. A Changeling reported to the Queen, “This year, the love we harvested can be piled as high as the horn tip of Celestia!”
The Queen frowned a little after hearing this, “Celestia? Don’t mention that name again! That b**** has disappeared from this world!”
Changeling: “Yes, and our love supply has also disappeared.”
