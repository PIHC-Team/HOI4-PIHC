最大的威胁
[en.@]
The Greatest Threat

[zh.@desc]
和煦光流召集了所有的部长和公爵，讨论小马利亚帝国的现状和潜在的危机。皇家委员会最高委员长花花短裤帮助主持了这次会议，和煦光流女皇也叫来了她着重提拔的提亚拉旁听会议。
会议的最后，和煦光流单独问提亚拉：“你认为小马利亚帝国最大的威胁是什么？”
提亚拉思考了一会儿，回答：“是东部的星光熠熠，对吗？” 和煦光流扭了扭头。
提亚拉又说：“难道是南部的无序吗？虽然他们实力不强，但是丛林游击战确实会是个麻烦。” 和煦光流又扭了扭头。
提亚拉疑惑：“北部的水晶帝国一直在争取独立，他们确实是个威胁。” 和煦光流又扭了扭头。
提亚拉彻底懵了：“那...那是什么？”
和煦光流说：“我只是扭头示意你瞧瞧坐在那边的一桌子小马。”

[en.@desc]
Cozy Glow convened all the ministers and dukes to discuss the current situation and potential crises of the Equestria Empire. The Supreme Chairpony of the Empire’s Royal Committee, Smarty Pants, helped to preside over the meeting, and Cozy Glow also called in Tiara, whom she had promoted.
At the end of the meeting, Cozy Glow asked Tiara alone, “What do you think is the greatest threat to the Equestria Empire?”
Tiara thought for a moment and replied, “Is it the Starlight Glimmer in the east?” Cozy Glow turned her head.
Tiara continued, “Is it the Discord in the south? Although they are not strong, jungle guerrilla warfare could indeed be troublesome.” Cozy Glow turned her head again.
Tiara asked in confusion, “Is it the Crystal Empire in the north that has been striving for independence? They are indeed a threat.” Cozy Glow turned her head again.
Tiara was completely baffled, “Then... what is it?”
Cozy Glow said, “I just turned my head to indicate that you should look at the table and all ponies sitting over there.”
