帝国的边防
[en.@]
The Empire’s Border Defense

[zh.@desc]
和煦光流女皇正在视察小马利亚帝国与幻形灵帝国的边界，惊讶地发现边界上密密麻麻驻扎着小马国军队。军营一直延伸到距离边界两三公里远的地方，还有许多小马士兵在临时搭建的广场上操练。
回到坎特洛特后，和煦光流疑惑地叫来了帝国国防部长。
“幻形灵不是我们的盟友吗？在西部边界上象征性的部署一点军队，意思一下就可以了。部署这么多军队不是浪费吗？光是军饷就要花多少金币？”
“报告女皇陛下，一分钱都不用花，因为那些不是我们的军队，是幻形灵的。”

[en.@desc]
Empress Cozy Glow was inspecting the border between the Equestria Empire and the Changeling Empire. She was surprised to find that the border was densely packed with Equestrian troops. The military camp extended to three kilometers away from the border, and many Equestrian soldiers were practicing on the temporary square.
After returning to Canterlot, with confusion, Empress Cozy Glow called the Minister of Defence.
“Aren’t the Changelings our allies? We should just symbolically deploy a few troops on the western border. Isn’t it a waste to deploy so many troops? How much gold will be spent on military pay?”
“Report to Your Majesty, not a single coin will be spent, because those are not our troops, they are the Changelings.”
