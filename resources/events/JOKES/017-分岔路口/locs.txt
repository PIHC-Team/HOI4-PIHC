分岔路口
[en.@]
Fork in the Road

[zh.@desc]
邪茧、暮光闪闪与和煦光流一起走到一个分岔路口。路口有东西两个方向，向东的路牌上写着“通向谐律”，向西的路牌上写着“通向专制”。
暮光闪闪没有丝毫犹豫就走向了东边的路；邪茧看了看东边的路牌，发出一声轻蔑的嘲笑，然后向西边走去。
和煦光流思考了一会儿，将两个路牌交换之后，头也不回地朝着“通向谐律”走去。

[en.@desc]
Chrysalis, Twilight Sparkle, and Cozy Glow walked to a fork in the road together. There were two directions at the fork, with a signpost to the east reading “To Harmony” and a signpost to the west reading “To Tyranny”.
Twilight Sparkle walked to the east without any hesitation; Chrysalis looked at the signpost to the east, sneered, and then walked to the west.
Cozy Glow thought for a moment, exchanged the two signposts, and then walked towards “To Harmony” without looking back.
