马哈顿游记
[en.@]
Manehattan Travelogue

[zh.@desc]
两只富商小马听说马哈顿灯红酒绿非常繁荣，准备去看一看。但是他们不太放心，于是让其中一只富商小马先去看看，然后送信回来报告情况。
他们约定了一个暗号，如果随信附上了约定好的稀有贵重首饰，那么说明被马哈顿控制了，信中的内容都是假话，这些贵重首饰应该被妥善保存起来；否则如果什么都没附，则说的都是真话。
几个月后，回信来了，里面什么也没附，并且热情洋溢盛赞了马哈顿的繁荣。信的最后，富商小马写道：
“马哈顿这里什么都好，就是我的开销有点大，已经把贵重首饰卖了还债去了。债还没有还清，我在马哈顿再多呆一段时间。”

[en.@desc]
Two wealthy ponies heard that Manehattan was very prosperous and decided to go and see it. However, they were not very reassured, so they let one of the wealthy ponies go first to take a look, and then send a letter back to report the situation.
They agreed on a secret code. If the agreed rare and precious jewelry was attached to the letter, it meant that Manehattan was under control, the contents of the letter were all lies, and these precious jewelry should be properly preserved; otherwise, if nothing was attached, everything said was true.
A few months later, the reply came, with nothing attached, and it was full of praise for the prosperity of Manehattan. At the end of the letter, the wealthy pony wrote:
"Everything is good in Manehattan, except that my expenses are a bit high. I have sold the precious jewelry to pay off the debt. The debt has not been paid off yet, so I will stay in Manehattan for a while longer."
