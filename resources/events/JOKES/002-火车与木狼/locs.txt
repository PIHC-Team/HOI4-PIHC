火车与木狼
[en.@]
Train and timber wolf

[zh.@desc]
一只幻形灵，一只狮鹫，一只小马在火车上。忽然他们发现有只木狼在跟着他们。
幻形灵将自己变形成性感木狼的照片全部扔了出去后说：“也许这可以让木狼知足。”
一个狮鹫将无数尸体扔了出去说：“也许他们只是饿了。”
木狼仍然穷追不舍。
于是小马将自己的头伸出窗外说了一句话，木狼瞬间就逃走了。
当同伴问他说的是什么，这个小马说：“我告诉木狼，这个火车马上就要进入小马利亚帝国了。”

[en.@desc]
A changeling, a griffon, and a pony are on a train. Suddenly they notice a timber wolf following them.
The changeling throws out all the pictures of himself transformed into a sexy timber wolf and says, “Maybe this will satisfy him.”
The griffon throws out countless corpses and says, “Maybe they’re just hungry.”
The timber wolf still follows them.
So the pony sticks his head out the window and says something, and the timber wolf instantly runs away.
When his companions ask what he said, the pony says, “I just told the timber wolf that this train is about to enter the Equestria Empire.”
