小马谷水疗馆
[en.@]
Ponyville Day Spa

[zh.@desc]
一天，和煦光流女皇正在宫殿门口对着广场上的小马讲话，突然有一个从远处传来的喇叭声音打断了她的讲话：
“小马谷水疗馆，放松您的身心，让您的灵魂得到净化！”
应该是广告声音被传到了喇叭上，女皇皱了皱眉头，不过很快又露出了微笑，对着广场上的小马们挥了挥蹄子，示意他们继续听她的讲话。可是没讲两句，和煦又被打断：
“小马谷水疗馆，放松您的身心，让您的灵魂得到净化！”
女皇有点不高兴，但还是等待了一会儿，等广告声音停下来，继续讲话。可是没讲几句，广场上又传来了广告声音：
“小马谷水疗馆，放松您的身心，让您的灵魂得到净化！”
女皇非常生气：
“我不知道是谁在我讲话的时候播放广告！谁再播广告，被我抓到，就把你踹到小马谷去！”
突然，台下所有的小马都开始大喊：
“小马谷水疗馆，放松您的身心，让您的灵魂得到净化！”
[en.@desc]
One day, Empress Cozy Glow was speaking to the ponies in the square in front of the palace, when suddenly a speaker sound from a distance interrupted her speech:
“Ponyville Day Spa, relax your body and mind, purify your soul!”
It seemed that the advertisement sound was transmitted to the speaker. The Empress frowned, but soon smiled again, waved her hoof at the ponies in the square, and signaled them to continue listening to her speech. But before she could say a few words, Cozy was interrupted again:
“Ponyville Day Spa, relax your body and mind, purify your soul!”
The Empress was a little upset, but she waited for a while until the advertisement sound stopped, and then continued her speech. But before she could say a few words, the advertisement sound came from the square again:
“Ponyville Day Spa, relax your body and mind, purify your soul!”
The Empress was very angry:
“I don’t know who is playing that advertisement while I’m speaking! If I catch anypony playing that advertisement again, I will kick you to Ponyville!”
Suddenly, all the ponies below began to shout:
“Ponyville Day Spa, relax your body and mind, purify your soul!”
