落水自救法
[en.@]
Self-Rescue when Drowning

[zh.@desc]
一匹小马在桥上闲逛，不小心掉到旁边河中，他立马高呼救命！
旁边路过的两名小马利亚帝国警察听到，假装没有看见，仍然边走边聊天。
小马急中生智，随即高呼：“暮光闪闪万岁！”两警察听到了大惊失色，马上跳入河中，将小马拖上岸来铐问。

[en.@desc]
A pony was strolling on the bridge, accidentally fell into the river beside. He immediately shouted for help.
Two police ponies of the Equestria Empire passing by heard it, pretended not to see, and continued chatting.
The pony, in desperation, immediately shouted, “We love Twilight Sparkle!” The two police ponies were shocked to hear it, immediately jumped into the river, dragged the pony ashore, and interrogated him.
