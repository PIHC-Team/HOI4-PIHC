拿把刀
[en.@]
Take a Knife

[zh.@desc]
一日，乐善陪同和煦光流去苹果鲁萨视察，听说苹果鲁萨的特产苹果派非常知名，就在晚宴上点了一只。
和煦女皇坐在乐善对面，看着乐善用笨拙地将整个头埋在苹果派里啃，有点看不下去了，提醒乐善：“乐善先生，你要不要拿把刀？”
乐善听闻，立即拿了一把刀，然后站了起来四处张望：“女皇您要杀谁？”
[en.@desc]
One day, Gladmane accompanied Empress Cozy Glow to visit Appleloosa. He heard that the special apple pie of Appleloosa was very famous, so he ordered one at the banquet.
Empress Cozy Glow sat opposite Gladmane, watching him awkwardly bury his entire head in the apple pie and gnaw at it. She couldn’t bear to watch, and reminded Gladmane, "Mr. Gladmane, do you want to take a knife?"
Upon hearing this, Gladmane immediately took a knife and then stood up and looked around, "Empress, who do you want me to kill?"
