街头采访
[en.@]
Vox Pop

[zh.@desc]
风暴岛上混居着小马、狮鹫、风暴守卫等多个种族，种族之间似乎存在着比较严重的矛盾与刻板印象。
于是，自由小马利亚政府组织了一群风暴守卫记者，在街头随机采访小马与狮鹫如何看待种族歧视问题。
结果发现，风暴岛不仅不存在种族歧视问题，所有小马和狮鹫都非常友好，看到采访记者就主动让路，离着老远就举双蹄双爪向记者打招呼，还有小马将钱包慷慨赠送给记者们后就匆匆离去。

[en.@desc]
On Storm Island lives a mix of species like ponies, griffons, and storm guards. There seems to be serious conflicts and stereotypes.
Therefore, the Equestria government organized a group of storm guard journalists to randomly interview ponies and griffons on the street about how they view racial discrimination.
The result was that there was no racial discrimination on Storm Island. Not only that, but all ponies and griffons were very friendly. They would actively make way for the journalists, wave to them from afar with both their hooves or claws, and some ponies even generously gave their wallets to the journalists before leaving in a hurry.
