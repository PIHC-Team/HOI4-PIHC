[zh.@desc]
一场技术与魔法的革命正在席卷马哈顿！
此前，需要100只奴隶小马工作一整天，才能生产出一门加农炮。效率十分低下。
如今，高效创造魔法被发明 —— 拥有魔法的独角兽，可以以极低的魔法消耗，凭空制造任意精密的物品。
使用创造魔法，一位经验丰富的独角兽，在学习物品创造魔法后，只需要不到两个小时 ——
就可以创造出100只奴隶小马。
[en.@desc]
A revolution of technology and magic is sweeping through Manehattan!
Previously, it took 100 slave ponies a whole day to produce a cannon. It was very inefficient.
Now, efficient creation magic has been invented —— unicorns with magic can create any sophisticated item with extremely low magic consumption.
Using creation magic, an experienced unicorn, after learning the item creation magic, only needs less than two hours ——
to create 100 slave ponies.
