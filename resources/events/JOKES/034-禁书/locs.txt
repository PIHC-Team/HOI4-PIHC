禁书
[en.@]
The Banned Book

[zh.@desc]
一只小马利亚帝国的小马因为在家中的阁楼里面搜出了禁书——暮光闪闪的《友谊日记》而被捕，在法庭上，他直截了当地承认了自己的罪行，但他向法官哀求道：
“求求你了！让我留下这本书吧！这本书是我已故的妻子买的，这是她的遗物。我保证绝对不看这本书，我甚至都不知道书中的内容是什么！”
听到这个解释，法官也心软了。法官再三确认这只小马确实没有阅读过《友谊日记》，也不知道其内容，于是宣布了免罪的判决。
判决结束后，小马心怀感激，与法官聊起天来。法官偷偷地说：“以后你不要把禁书放在阁楼，而是藏在地板下面。我知道那些警察一般不会搜索地板。”
小马摇摇头：“谢谢您的建议，法官，但是我妻子的书都是放在阁楼的。地板下面放我自己的书就已经放不下了，比如《沉重的狮鹫》、《和煦光流写真集》什么的。”
[en.@desc]
A pony from the Equestria Empire, was arrested because he found a banned book, the Twilight Sparkle’s “Friendship Diary”, in the attic of his home. In court, he admitted his guilt directly, but he begged the judge for mercy:
“Please! Let me keep this book! This book was bought by my deceased wife, it is her legacy. I promise not to read this book, I don’t even know what the content of the book is!”
Hearing this explanation, the judge also softened. The judge repeatedly confirmed that the pony had indeed not read the “Friendship Diary” and did not know its content, so he announced the verdict of acquittal.
After the verdict, the pony was grateful and chatted with the judge. The judge secretly said: “Next time, don’t put banned books in the attic, but hide them under the floor. I know that the police generally do not search the floor.”
The pony shook his head: “Thank you for your advice, judge, but my wife’s books are all in the attic. I can barely fit my own books under the floor, such as ‘The Griffon Lies Heavy’ and ‘Cozy Glow Pornography’.”
