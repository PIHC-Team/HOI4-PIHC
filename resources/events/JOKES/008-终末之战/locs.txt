终末之战
[en.@]
Ending of the End

[zh.@desc]
和煦光流女皇听说终末之战已经被马哈顿的戏剧作家改编为舞台剧，正在热映。和煦光流非常欣慰，于是她也想去体验一下，买了一张普通票。
现场，当舞台上的和煦光流角色登场时，全场爆发了热烈的鼓蹄声与欢呼声。和煦光流女皇非常满意。
这时，旁边的小马赶快拍了一下她，紧张地说：“小朋友你不要命了？怎么不欢呼？”

[en.@desc]
Empress Cozy Glow heard that the Ending of the End has been adapted into a stage play by the playwrights of Manehattan and is now showing. Empress Cozy Glow was very pleased, so she also wanted to experience it and bought a regular ticket.
At the scene, when the character Cozy Glow appeared on stage, the audience burst into enthusiastic applause and cheers. Empress Cozy Glow was very satisfied.
At this time, the pony next to her quickly pat her and nervously ask, “Kid, why aren’t you cheering? Do you want to die?”
