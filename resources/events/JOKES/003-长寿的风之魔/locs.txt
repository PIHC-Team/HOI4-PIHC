长寿的风之魔
[en.@]
Long Live the Windigos

[zh.@desc]
风之魔是一种古老的生物，他们存在的时间可以追溯到很久很久以前，甚至比提雷克大王出生还要早得多。
问及他们可以活这么长寿的原因，风之魔说：我们出门的时候一般不带铃铛。

[en.@desc]
Windigos are ancient creatures, their existence can be traced back to a long, long time ago, even much earlier than the birth of Lord Tirek.
When asked about the reason why they can live so long, Windigos said: We usually don’t bring bells when we go out.
