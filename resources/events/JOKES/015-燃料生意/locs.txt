Fuel Business

[zh.@desc]
小马谷的冬天非常寒冷，于是特丽克西公爵联系油嘴滑舌的洛马基供暖有限公司购买煤炭做为小马谷取暖的燃料。
特丽克西走进洛洛马基供暖有限公司，拍下一张面值100发展债券，说要买煤炭。
油嘴滑舌解释道：“不好意思特丽克西公爵，为支援南露娜洋发展公司的建设，现在所有的交易都要支付50%的‘捐款’。”
特丽克西很生气：“这不是变相涨价吗？这涨价也太多了吧！” 思来想去，特丽克西没有别的选择，点点头。
油嘴滑舌收下面值100的发展债券，然后很快给特丽克西还了一张面值50的发展债券，说：“不好意思，煤炭已经卖完了。”

[en.@desc]
The winter in Ponyville is very cold, so Duchess Trixie contacted the Las Pegasus Heating Co., Ltd. by Flim and Flam to purchase coal as heating fuel in Ponyville.
Trixie walked into Las Pegasus Heating Co., Ltd., took out a Development Bond that worth 100, and said she wanted to buy coal.
Flim and Flam explained: “Sorry, Duchess Trixie, to support the construction of the South Luna Ocean Development Company, all transactions now require a ‘donation’ of 50%.”
Trixie was very angry: “Isn’t this just a price rise in disguise? This price rise is too much!” But after thinking about it, Trixie had no other choice but nodded.
Flim and Flam took the Development Bond worth 100, and then quickly gave Trixie a Development Bond worth 50, saying: “Sorry, the coal has been sold out.”
