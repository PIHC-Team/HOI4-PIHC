[en.@]
The Crown
[zh.@desc]
和煦光流发现自己的皇冠不见了，便叫秘密警察帮忙找找。
但过了一会，和煦找到了皇冠。便把秘密警察叫来说不用找了。秘密警察听完说：可是，我们刚找了十匹小马，他们都说是自己干的。
[en.@desc]
Cozy Glow found that her crown was missing, so she asked the secret police to help find it.
But after a while, Cozy found the crown. She called the secret police and told them not to look for it. After listening, the secret police said: But we just found ten ponies, and they all said they did it themselves.
