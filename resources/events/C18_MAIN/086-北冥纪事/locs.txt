[zh.@desc]
书中描述，在麒麟社群的北方，有一座名为“烁”的山，其实是一头名为“炬”的巨大的龙，这只龙有几千里长，飞行时遮天蔽日。
根据我们如今对北方龙族的认知，当时在北部边境建立文明的麒麟很可能是将多位不同的龙族首领合并成了同一个神话生物。不过无论如何，这些麒麟的结局更加吸引我们的注意：与会喷火的龙长期共处，让他们以为自己是龙族的一部分，于是不再抵触逆麟，转而以逆麟之火为信仰。持有这样信仰的部族在逆麟化后向北方迁徙，最终消失在了龙族领地中，据说其中一只得名为“规”的逆麟还一度抢夺了血石权杖成为了龙王。

§b“北冥有山，其名为烁。烁之大，不知其几千里也；化而为龙，其名为炬。炬之背，不知其几千里也；怒而飞，其翼若垂天之云。” ——《北冥纪事》原文§!

[en.@desc]
In the books’ narrative, in the north of the Kirin commune was a mountain called “Scintilla”, which was actually a huge dragon named “Torch”. This dragon was thousands of miles long and can cover the entire sky when flying.
According to our current knowledge of the northern dragon clan, the Kirins who established civilization on the northern border at that time probably merged several different dragon clan leaders into the same mythical creature. However, the fate of these Kirins is more intriguing: living with the fire-breathing dragons for a long time, they thought they were part of the dragon clan, so they no longer resisted the Niriks, but instead worshipped the fire of the Niriks. The tribes that held such beliefs migrated northward after becoming Niriks, eventually disappearing in the territory of the dragon clan. It is said that one of the Niriks, named “Scales”, even once seized the Bloodstone Scepter and became the Dragon Lord.

§b“’i the Northern Nether doth rest a mountain of legend, hight Scintilla. The vastness of Scintilla is shrouded ’i mystery, spanning perchance thousands of leagues; it doth metamorphose into a dragon of dread, known as Torch. The expanse of Torch’s back is veiled ’i uncertainty, stretching mayhap thousands of leagues; when provoked, its wings resemble unto clouds adorning the firmament.” — Original text of the “Northern Nether Chronicle”§!
