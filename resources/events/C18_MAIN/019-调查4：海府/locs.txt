海府调查报告
[en.@]
Hai Fu Investigation Report
[zh.@desc]
虽然东部地区只有柔柔港一个港口，但是海府南侧也有渔民聚集的村庄，有可能是走私团伙与当地渔民合作将稚马之息不经过港口而经过渔船直接分销出海 —— 如果情况真的是这样，那么如何处置海府的渔民将会成为一个困难的选择。
幸运的是，目前我们暂时还没有发现这一猜想的证据：海府的渔民收入状况正常，没有明显的暴富迹象。面对专案组的调查，当地淳朴的村民也有问必答，回答真诚自然且没有矛盾。除了当初解除“静默”时之外，海府的渔民从未见过成批量的稚马之息。
因为渔民们都属于渔民协会，关系紧密，汇总来自渔民与协会的调查结果，我们获得了一个重要的信息 —— §2整个东部地区的南部海岸，不存在渔船走私行为§!。也就是说，走私专案那批失踪的稚马之息，不可能是不经港口从海路走私出去的。
[en.@desc]
Though the Eastern Region only has one port, the Port of Softness, there are also fishing villages on the southern side of Hai Fu where fisherponies gather. It is possible that smuggling groups are cooperating with local fisherponies to distribute foal’s breath directly through fishing boats without going through the port — if this is the case, how to deal with the fisherponies of Hai Fu will become a difficult choice.
Fortunately, we have not yet found evidence to support this speculation: the income of Hai Fu fisherponies is normal, and there are no obvious signs of sudden wealth. Faced with the investigation of the task force, the naive local villagers answered questions sincerely and naturally without any contradictions. Except for the time when the “Silence” was lifted, the fisherponies of Hai Fu have never seen batches of foal’s breath.
Because the fisherponies all belong to the Fisherponies’ Association, their relationships are close. By summarizing the investigation results from the fisherponies and the association, we obtained an important piece of information — §2there is no smuggling of fishing boats along the southern coast of the entire Eastern Region§!. In other words, the missing foal’s breath in the smuggling case could not have been smuggled out to sea without going through the port.
