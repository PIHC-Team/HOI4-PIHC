调查奥尼西亚

Investigate Ornithia
[zh.@desc]
南方的奥尼西亚王国是塞拉伊诺船长的家乡，而塞拉伊诺船长则是现在横行三大洋的各海盗组织公认的大姐头。他们对海上航行与空中飞行的每一艘船了如指掌，从哪里到哪里，载了什么货物，他们都或多或少能得到些风声。因此，向他们打听一下，或许能够找到稚马之息最终的去处。
遗憾的是，虽然是我们南方的邻国，但隔海相望，我们与他们的交流并不多。向一个陌生的国家有所请求，看来是需要不少的耐心。不过最终，他们还是大方地为我们提供了信息：他们可以确认，没有任何大规模的稚马之息在他们的眼皮下流通过。这也意味着，稚马之息从未通过空运或海运离开麒麟社群。

[en.@desc]
The Ornithia Kingdom in the South is the hometown of Captain Celaeno, who is now the recognized leader of the various pirate organizations that dominate the three oceans. They know every ship that sails the seas and flies the skies, and they can get some information about any ship, where they are going and what they are carrying. Therefore, asking them may help us find the final destination of the foal’s breath.
Unfortunately, although they are our southern neighbors, we do not have much communication with them across the sea. It seems that it takes a lot of patience to make a request to a foreign country. However, in the end, they generously provided us with information: they can confirm that no large-scale foal’s breath has passed under their noses. This also means that the foal’s breath has never left the Kirin community by air or sea.
