Investigate Unicornia
[zh.@desc]
遵循线索来到边境，一切线索似乎都指向了我们古老的邻国 —— 独角兽仙境。
在过去的几年中，独角兽仙境一直在国际市场上大规模地购入各式各样的花卉。即使他们是世界上最大的花卉种植国，但他们不仅不出口花卉，还要大量进口。他们对花卉的消耗就像是一头愤怒而贪婪的逆麟对可燃物的需求一样。虽然我们并不清楚他们收购的原因，但为什么我们要过问呢？独角兽仙境是麒麟社群经济作物的最大贸易伙伴，而他们小小的国家里似乎有着无穷无尽的财富足以支撑他们持续的贸易逆差。
这一次，涉及到走私案，我们也是十分勉强地选择介入，向独角兽仙境咨询了市场情况。他们的回复非常利落：他们的目的是购买尽可能多的花卉，品种有影响但不大，所以即使是走私与黑市，他们也没有理由购买昂贵的稚马之息。所以此次走私案，与他们必定无关。

[en.@desc]
Following the clues to the border, all the clues seem to point to our ancient neighbor — Unicornia.
In the past few years, Unicornia has been buying a large number of flowers of various kinds on the international market. Even though they are the world’s largest flower-growing country, they not only do not export flowers, but also import a large number of them. Their consumption of flowers is like an angry and greedy Nirik’s demand for combustibles. Although we are not sure why they are buying flowers, why should we care? Unicornia is the largest trading partner of the Kirin community’s economic crops, and their small country seems to have endless wealth to support their continued trade deficit.
This time, involving a smuggling case, we reluctantly chose to intervene and consulted Unicornia on the market situation. Their reply was very straightforward: their goal was to buy as many flowers as possible, and the variety had little impact, so even if it was smuggling and the black market, they had no reason to buy the expensive foal’s breath. So this smuggling case is definitely not related to them.
