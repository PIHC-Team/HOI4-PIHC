Talk Record: Ochre
[zh.@desc]
黄褐，§2麒麟社群货运局局长§!，生于险崇峰，现于§2柔柔港§!组织货运。

黄褐是§2雨晴的狂热追随者§!，即使是“静默”已经解除，她也并不喜欢说话，甚至反感其他麒麟对她说话。当然，她对秋烨或者现在的政策并没有意见，只是她本身就§2不喜欢说话§!，与政治无关。
所以，她也没有回答调查组的任何问题，调查组只能通过询问是或否的方式来获取信息。

“你认识§2繁花§!吗？” 点头。
“你与繁花有过交流吗？” 摇头。调查组并不意外。
“繁花曾任货运局长时，她作为你的上级，有指示过你做一些不寻常的工作吗？” 摇头。
“那从你的观察来讲，她有什么不正常的表现，或者可疑的伙伴吗？” 摇头。

“运输向狭关的稚马之息是由你或者说§2货运局§!负责吗？” 摇头。
“那... 是§2东部地区政府或者南都§!负责吗？” 点头。
“你有曾经注意到东部地区的稚马之息供应量不正常吗？” 摇头。
“除此之外，你有什么与案件有关的信息认为有必要与调查组分享吗？” 摇头。

[en.@desc]
Ochre, §2Director of Kirin Commune Freight Bureau§!, born in the Peaks of Peril, now organizing freight in §2Fluttershy Harbour§!.

Ochre is a §2fervent follower of Rain Shine§!, and she does not like to talk, even after the “Silence” has been lifted, and even dislikes other Kirins talking to her. Of course, she has no objection to Autumn Blaze or the current policy, she just §2doesn’t like to talk§!, it has nothing to do with politics.
Therefore, she did not answer any questions from the investigation team, and the investigation team could only obtain information by asking yes or no questions.

“Do you know §2Blossom Burst§!?” Nod.
“Have you ever communicated with Blossom Burst?” Shake head. The investigation team was not surprised.
“When Blossom Burst was the director of the Freight Bureau, did she instruct you to do any unusual work as your superior?” Shake head.
“From your observation, does she have any abnormal behavior, or suspicious partners?” Shake head.

“Is the transportation of foal’s breath to Narrow Gate part of your, or say, the §2Freight Bureau§!’s responsibility?” Shake head.
“Then... is it the responsibility of the §2Eastern Region government or Nan Du§!?” Nod.
“Have you ever noticed any abnormal supply of foal’s breath to the Eastern Region?” Shake head.
“Besides, do you have any information related to the case that you think is necessary to share with the investigation team?” Shake head.
