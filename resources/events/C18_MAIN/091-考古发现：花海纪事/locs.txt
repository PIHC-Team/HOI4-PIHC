Archeological Discovery: “Flower Sea Chronicle”

[zh.@desc]
我们在[FROM.FROM.GetName]的考古过程中发现了一本古书，收录了关于一种神奇的神话生物的见闻。
书中描述，在麒麟之森居住的麒麟们曾见到一片青山之中的花海，其中居住着一只妖怪，浑身棕黄色像一只狐狸。她有无数只尾巴，贪婪地吸取花香，被吸取的花朵都会枯萎。
但是我们发现了《花海纪事》的多个不同的版本，版本之间描述互有不同，又有许多相似之处：有的版本里说，妖怪不是通体棕黄色，而是青色与粉色的混合体，并且她不是在吸食花海而是在培育种植花海。有的版本里则认为妖怪确实是棕黄色，但是吸取花海的目的不是为了自己吸食，而是为了将花香传播给青山中居住的独角兽。有的版本将妖怪描述为树精而不是狐妖，其“九尾”其实是藤蔓。还有的版本认为青山中的妖怪形似独角兽，与麒麟一样具有两种形态，在心情好的时候名为“独角兽”，呈现青色与粉色，喷吐花香；心情不好时则变为棕黄色，名为“逆角兽”，吸食花香。

[en.@desc]
We found an ancient book during the archeological process in [FROM.FROM.GetName], compiling a series of sightings of a mythical creature.
In the books’ narrative, the Kirins living in the Kirin Forest once saw a flower sea in green mountains, where a monster resided. The monster was brownish-yellow all over like a fox. She had nine tails and greedily absorbed the fragrance of the flowers, causing the flowers she absorbed to wither.
However, we have found multiple different versions of the “Flower Sea Chronicle”, each with different descriptions and many similarities. Some versions say that the monster is not entirely brownish-yellow, but a mixture of cyan and pink, and she is not absorbing the flower sea but cultivating it. Some versions believe that the monster is indeed brownish-yellow, but the purpose of absorbing the flower sea is not for her own consumption, but to spread the fragrance of the flowers to the unicorns living in the green mountains. Some versions describe the monster as a tree spirit rather than a fox monster, and its “nine tails” are actually vines. Some versions believe that the monster in the green mountains resembles a unicorn, having two forms like the Kirins, named “Unicorn” when in a good mood, appearing cyan and pink, and emitting the fragrance of flowers; when in a bad mood, it turns brownish-yellow and is named “Nrocinu”, absorbing the fragrance of flowers.
