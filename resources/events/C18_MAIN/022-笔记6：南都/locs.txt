Nan Du Investigation Report
[zh.@desc]
南都，做为东部地区的行政中心，管理着所有的物资调动。查阅这里的调动记录，我们发现了异常：虽然不存在大批量的稚马之息调动，但是向§2东方§!调配的稚马之息数量远多于正常值，向南方沿海调配的稚马之息数量则相对正常。
根据行政中心一些官员的口供，在他们印象中，确实在东部地区§2经常爆发小规模的逆麟事件§!，因此常常需要因灾补给稚马之息。所以，走私专案的结论应该是：其实没有走私，只是东部地区的逆麟事件频繁，导致了稚马之息的消耗增多？
又或者 —— 稚马之息沿铁路向东调度的过程中被偷梁换柱？我们需要查询东部地区的收货记录。
[en.@desc]
Nan Du, as the administrative center of the Eastern Region, manages all material movements. By checking the movement records here, we found an anomaly: although there is no large-scale movement of foal’s breath, the amount of foal’s breath dispatched to the §2east§! is far greater than the normal value, while the amount of foal’s breath dispatched to the southern coastal areas is relatively normal.
According to the confessions of some officials in the administrative center, they do remember that §2small-scale Nirik incidents often occur§! in the Eastern Region, so they often need to supply foal’s breath due to disasters. Therefore, the conclusion of the smuggling case should be: there is actually no smuggling, but the frequent Nirik incidents in the Eastern Region have led to an increase in the consumption of foal’s breath, right?
Or — was the foal’s breath stolen during the dispatch process along the railway to the east? We need to check the receiving records in the Eastern Region.
