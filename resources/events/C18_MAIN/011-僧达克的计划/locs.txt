Sendak’s Plan
[zh.@desc]
在找到麒麟之前，僧达克和他的教团已经秘密联系了小马利亚各个地方的势力：提雷克的弟弟斯科潘、狮鹫、洛马基的科学家、甚至是无序与泽科拉。不过得到的结果却是非常失望的，如今如山一般的巨型提雷克，几乎是无敌的，不存在弱点。
如此一来，解决提雷克的方法只剩下了一个：蛮力。汇聚足够大量级的魔力，在一瞬间强行击穿提雷克的魔法护盾，将其击倒。僧达克的教团进行了严谨的计算，发现这一过程所需要的最小魔力量级，即使是一个天文数字，似乎并非不可能。
这大概需要汇聚一个国家量级的魔法，在小马利亚或者幻形灵帝国收集这样巨大量级的魔法，必然会引起当局的注意和处置，提雷克也会因此察觉。而唯一一个蕴含着大量魔力，却不被外界所知的地方，就是麒麟社群。

当然，要榨干麒麟全国的力量为一个成功性未知、回报也未知的疯狂计划服务，麒麟们自然不会同意这种离谱的合作。于是，僧达克提议通过三到五年的时间逐渐收集魔力，做为交换，他们将向我们传授黑魔法来弥补我们流失的魔力。而麒麟的贸易方面，他们也会补足全部的损失——只是，不要问钱从哪里来。

[en.@desc]
Before finding the unicorn, Sendak and his church had secretly contacted forces in various places in Equestria: Tirek’s brother Scorpan, griffons, scientists in Las Pegasus, even Discord and Zecora. However, the results were very disappointing. The current giant Tirek, like a mountain, is almost invincible, with no weaknesses.
As a result, there is only one way to deal with Tirek: brute force. Gather an enormous amount of magic, forcibly break through Tirek’s magic shield in an instant to knock him down. Sendak’s church has made careful calculations and found that the minimum magic level required for this process, even though it is an astronomical number, seems not impossible.
This probably requires gathering a country-level amount of magic. Collecting such a huge amount of magic in Equestria or the Changeling Empire will inevitably attract the attention and action of the authorities, and Tirek will notice it. The only place that contains a large amount of magic but is not known to the outside world is the Kirin commune.

Of course, the Kirins will not agree to this absurd cooperation to drain the power of the entire country for a crazy plan with unknown success rate and unknown rewards. Sendak instead proposed to gradually collect magic over three to five years. In exchange, they will teach us dark magic to make up for our lost magic. As for the Kirins’ trade, they will also make up for all the losses — just don’t ask where the money comes from.
