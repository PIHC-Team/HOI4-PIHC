[zh.@desc]
任何一个头脑清醒的麒麟此刻都该明白，这一轮文明已经再次走向了她生命的最后时刻。逆麟肆虐，原有的社会组织已经彻底失效，整个社群正在缓慢地崩溃、解体。
我想，也许我们还有机会留下些什么：在整个麒麟的历史上，这样的轮回并非罕见之事，但这次是第一次，一个全国范围内组织持续了数十年之久。我们与小马利亚建立了联系，我们与龙族和仙境建立了联系。他们将见证我们的末日，也将见证我们的重生。
那么再一次起笔吧，用火焰熔化磐石，在其上一笔一划地刻写下我们的故事：... 然后，从星星上落下了天火。天火所到之处，险崇峰的城市以及麒麟们创造的一切也焚烧殆尽。

[en.@desc]
Any sane Kirin should understand by now that this civilization has once again reached the final moment of her life. The Niriks are rampant, the original social organization has completely failed, and the entire community is slowly collapsing and disintegrating.
Maybe, we still have a chance to leave something behind: in the entire history of Kirins, such a cycle is not uncommon, but this is the first time that an organization has lasted for decades nationwide. We established contacts with Equestria, we established contacts with the Dragons and Unicornia. They will witness our doomsday, and they will witness our rebirth.
So let’s start writing again, using flames to melt the rocks, and writing our story on them stroke by stroke: ... from the stars there then reached down the heavenly flame, and where it touched, the citis of Peaks of Peirl flamed to utter destruction, so that of Kirins and of the works of Kirins nought remained.
