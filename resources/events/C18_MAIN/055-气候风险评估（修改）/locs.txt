气候风险评估
[en.@]
Climate Risk Assessment

[zh.@desc]
一些从小马利亚留学回来的气象观测员，与我们从小马谷请来的专业气象学家共同分析了险崇峰南麓与麒麟群岛的若干观测站数据，我们得出结论：
在过去的一年中，风之魔的活动在全世界范围内有所提升。交叉对比靠近南部风之魔领地的麒麟社群地区数据与世界范围数据，我们认为，麒麟族将在未来的一两年内受到风之魔的严重威胁。
数据表明，相较于小马利亚中央大陆，麒麟索尔大陆受到的影响更为显著，我们有必要保持警惕，启动紧急的御寒科研工作。
综上所述，报告认为麒麟将很快受到风之魔的严重威胁，我们需要立即采取行动。
[en.@desc]
Some meteorological observers who returned from studying abroad in Equestria, together with professional meteorologists we invited from Ponyville, have analyzed the data from several observation stations near the Southern Peaks of Peril and Kirin Isles with us. We have come to the following conclusion:
In the past year, the activity of the Windigos has indeed increased worldwide. When cross-referencing the data from the Kirin commune local areas near the southern Windigo territory with the global data, we believe that the Kirin tribe will be severely threatened by the Windigos in the next one or two years.
The data shows that the impact on the Kirinthal continent is more significant than that on the central Equestria continent. We need to be vigilant and start emergency research on cold protection.
In conclusion, the report believes that the Kirin will soon be severely threatened by the Windigos, and we need to take immediate action.
