Foal Mania
[zh.@desc]
在初步的对外开放中，我们发现所有出口的商品中，我们的特产花朵“稚马之息”在国际市场上最受欢迎。这种散发着淡淡清香的花朵，还有提神醒脑的功效，可以让使用者思考迅速，语言流畅。
不同于外界所认为的高档装饰花卉，“稚马之息”对于麒麟们来说是重要的战略物资 —— 解除“静默”的唯一解药。长久以来，因为解除“静默”所需要的“稚马之息”数量巨大，政府严格限制了其出口，以优先保证国内的供应，这进一步推高了“稚马之息”的价格。
如今，在全国范围内的“静默”已经被成功解除，“稚马之息”不再是我们的必需品，而全国还有无数的种植园正在种植这种花朵。也许是时候放开这一限制，让更多种植“稚马之息”的农民改善生活了。
不过，专家提醒，全面放开“稚马之息”的出口反而会导致其价格下跌，种植利润降低。我们可能需要更加谨慎地考虑这一点。
除经济之外，一个更重要的担忧是：半人马此前对我们兴趣不大，在他们的刻板印象中，麒麟社群不过是丛林中的未开化部落罢了。这也是为什么终末之战后我们有幸逃过了半人马的绝大部分压榨。但当“稚马之息”在半人马上层贵族中兴起热潮时，我们是否会招来提雷克的注意呢？
[en.@desc]
In the initial opening to the outside world, we found that our specialty flower “Foal’s Breath” is the most popular in the international market among all exported goods. This flower, which emits a faint fragrance, also has the effect of refreshing the mind, allowing the user to think quickly and speak fluently.
Different from the high-end decorative flowers recognized by the outside world, “Foal’s Breath” is an important strategic material for the Kirin —— the only antidote to the “Silence”. For a long time, due to the large quantity of “Foal’s Breath” is needed to lift the “Silence”, the government strictly limited its export to prioritize domestic supply, which further raised the price of “Foal’s Breath”.
Now that the “Silence” has been successfully lifted nationwide, “Foal’s Breath” is no longer our necessity, and there are countless plantations across the country growing this flower. Perhaps it is time to lift this restriction and allow more farmers who grow “Foal’s Breath” to improve their lives.
However, experts warn that fully opening the export of “Foal’s Breath” may lead to a drop in its price and a decrease in planting profits. We may need to consider this more carefully.
Apart from the economy, a more important concern is: the centaurs have not been very interested in us before. In their stereotyped impression, the Kirin commune is nothing more than an uncivilized tribe in the jungle. That’s why we were lucky to escape most of the centaur’s exploitation after the Ultimate Battle. But when “Foal’s Breath” becomes a hot trend among the upper-class of the centaurs, will we attract the attention of Tirek?
