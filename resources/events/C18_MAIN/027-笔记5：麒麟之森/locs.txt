麒麟之森调查报告
[en.@]
Kirin Forest Investigation Report
[zh.@desc]
麒麟之森是相对封闭的农业地区，居民以小村庄甚至个体为单位从事农耕和采集打猎活动，即使是地区政府有时也很难组织起居民。所以最初，专案组认为麒麟之森不太可能与走私有关。
但在调查的过程中，我们发现了一些不太寻常的信息：邻国§2独角兽仙境§!在过去的几年间一直在大量从市场上§2收购各类花卉§!，数量到达了一种不正常的程度。麒麟之森中的§2万花源§!也因此收到了许多额外的订单，改善了当地居民的生活水平。
虽然我们尚不清楚独角兽仙境收购花卉的动机，但这足以将此次的稚马之息走私专案与麒麟之森联系起来。

[en.@desc]
Kirin Forest is a relatively closed agricultural area. Residents engage in farming, hunting, and gathering activities on a village or even individual basis. Even the local government sometimes finds it difficult to organize the residents. Therefore, initially, the task force believed that Kirin Forest was unlikely to be related to smuggling.
However, during the investigation, we discovered some unusual information: the neighboring country, §2Unicornia§!, has been §2purchasing various flowers in large quantities§! from the market in the past few years, reaching an abnormal level. As a result, the §2Home of Flowers§! in Kirin Forest has received many additional orders, improving the living standards of local residents.
Although we are not yet clear about the motivation of Unicornia to purchase flowers, this is enough to link the foal’s breath smuggling case with Kirin Forest.
