与龙族的友谊
[en.@]
Friendship with Dragons
[zh.@desc]
不同于我们麒麟，龙族从不擅长生产，他们的生活物资大多来源于捡拾、掠夺和交易。他们拥有大量的宝石，但却缺乏食物和衣物。
他们希望我们能够将我们的农作物、纺织品和工艺品交换给他们，以换取他们的宝石和其他珍贵物品。
另外，值得一提的是，龙族非常擅长训练战士。
[en.@desc]
Unlike us Kirins, the Dragons are not good at producing things. They mostly rely on scavenging, plundering and trading for their daily necessities. They have a lot of gems but lack food and clothing.
They hope that we can exchange our crops, textiles and handicrafts for their gems and other precious items.
In addition, by the way, the Dragons are very good at training warriors.
