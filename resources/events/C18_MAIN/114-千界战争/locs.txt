“如果这是不可避免的，我会让麒麟们拿起武器。麒麟族将要生存下去，即使这意味着我们的一部分，甚至我自己变成逆麟或者在战场上牺牲，即使这意味着与昔日的同胞和挚友兵戎相见，我也会选择这样做。”

另一个世界的秋烨’最终还是诉诸了武力。在生存面前，秋烨*相信自己也会做出相同的选择。战争究竟是谁的错呢？这件事已经不再重要。
无论谁是胜者，都将承载着麒麟数千年的历史与轮回，都将在这片土地上延续下去。
[en.@desc]
“If it is inevitable, I will let the Kirins take up arms. The Kirins will survive, even if it means that some of us, or even I myself, become Niriks or die on the battlefield, even if it means facing my former fellows and dear friends with arms, I will choose to do so.”

In the end, Autumn Blaze’ from the other world resorted to force. In the face of survival, Autumn Blaze* believes that she would make the same choice. Whose fault is the war? This is no longer important.
Regardless of who is the winner, they will carry the history and reincarnation of the Kirins for thousands of years, and will continue to survive on this land.
