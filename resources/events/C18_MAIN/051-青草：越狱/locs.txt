He left no records of leaving the country, especially since the prison where he was held was in the capital, Peaks of Peril. The government immediately blocked this news, and currently, the outside world does not know that Grass-Green has escaped.
We quickly arrested the prison guards who assisted in the escape, and even the warden... The evidence we have now, points to the worst assumption — Grass-Green is still not the biggest boss behind the “Blossom Case”, there are higher-level forces involved...
