焦赭的审判
[en.@]
Burnt Umber’s Trial
[zh.@desc]
最终，“繁花案”的幕后主使 —— 焦赭，被送上了法庭。与调查组会谈是他的紧张态度让他成为了嫌疑最大的麒麟之一。这次，焦赭在法庭上、公众面前的表现也并不好，他的黑历史被他自己说出了不少，他与繁花曾是邻居的关系更加让他脱不开干系。更重要的是，“繁花”失踪后，警方搜查了整个麒麟之森，也在全国张贴了通缉令，却并未发现繁花。焦赭对繁花可能仍在国内的描述像是画蛇添足，加重了他的疑点，也提醒了法官他对身为军队领袖对边检部队的控制权。
尽管焦赭继续否认自己是“繁花案”的幕后主使，法庭依然判决焦赭有罪，处以终身监禁，关押于险崇峰监狱。舆论对于这次的结案意见分歧很大，有不少民众认为焦赭有些太愚钝了，不像是可以指挥走私数年不被发现的那种麒麟；也有很多民众认为焦赭确实是做贼心虚。之后，抗议与支持审判结果的声音在全国各地响起，焦赭的案件也成为了全国热议的话题。

[en.@desc]
At the very end, the mastermind behind the “Blossom Case” — Burnt Umber, was brought to court. His nervous attitude during the investigation team’s meeting made him one of the most suspected Kirins. This time, Burnt Umber’s performance in court and in public was not good either, he revealed a lot of his dark history himself, and his relationship with Blossom Burst, who used to be his neighbor, made him even more suspicious. More importantly, after Blossom Burst disappeared, the police searched the entire Kirin Forest and posted wanted posters nationwide, but did not find Blossom Burst. Burnt Umber’s description of Blossom Burst possibly still being in the country seemed like a red herring, deepening his suspicions and reminding the judge of his control over the border inspection forces as a military leader.
Though Burnt Umber continued to deny that he was the mastermind behind the “Blossom Case”, the court still ruled Burnt Umber guilty and sentenced him to life imprisonment, to be held in the Peaks of Peril Prison. There was a lot of disagreement in public opinion on this case, with many Kirins thinking that Burnt Umber was too dull to be the kind of Kirin who could command smuggling for years without being discovered; many others believed that Burnt Umber was indeed guilty. After that, protests and support for the verdict were heard across the country, and Burnt Umber’s case became a hot topic of discussion nationwide.
