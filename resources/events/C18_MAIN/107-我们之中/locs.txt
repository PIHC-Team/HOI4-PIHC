最初，这只是一次统计数据上的小失误：有些地区上报的逆麟率与麒麟率加和居然超过了100%。但当一次不经意的调查开始时，麒麟们却突然发现一切再也无法回到过去。
首先，在统计局，东部地区、麒麟之森等地的原始数据都被检查为真，这意味着在普查阶段就已经出现了问题。
接下来，一些恐怖的记录引起了麒麟们的注意：所有被重复统计的麒麟，早就已经去世，有的去世有一年多，有的则有四五年。
接着，调查组询问了一些基层普查员，他们以自己的名誉和生命起誓，从未重复统计过任何麒麟，更不可能统计已经去世的麒麟...
然后，警察逮捕了几位已经在数年前被登记为“死亡”的麒麟，他们的身份证明、照片等信息都被证明是真实的，魔法也能正常使用。只有档案馆里面的档案却显示他们已经去世多年。
最终，在审问下，一个反复出现的奇怪名字引起了调查组的注意：“千界俱乐部”，这些被误认为死亡的麒麟似乎都是这个俱乐部的成员，据他们所说只是一个戒酒互助团体...
[en.@desc]
Originally, this was just a small mistake in the statistical data: the rate of Kirins and Niriks summed up to more than 100% in some regions. But when an inadvertent investigation began, the Kirins suddenly found that everything started to lose control.
First, at the Bureau of Statistics, the original data of the Eastern Region and Kirin Forest were checked to be true, which meant that there was a problem in the poll stage.
Next, some terrifying records caught the attention of the Kirins: all the Kirins that were double-counted had long been dead, some for more than a year, and some for four or five years.
Then, the investigation team asked some grassroots pollsters, who swore by their reputation and life that they had never double-counted, let alone counted Kirins who had already died...
Then, the police arrested several Kirins who had been registered as “dead” several years ago. Their identity documents, photos, and other information were all proven to be true, and they can also use magic normally. Only the archives showed that they had died many years ago.
Finally, under interrogation, a strange name that repeatedly appeared caught the attention of the investigation team: “The CLUB”. It seems that all the Kirins who were mistakenly considered dead were members of “The CLUB”, and they claimed that the CLUB is just a sobriety mutual aid group...
