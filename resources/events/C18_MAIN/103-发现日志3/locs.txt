[zh.@desc]
在繁花和她的同伙相继消失后，调查组失去了方向。不得已，调查组回到了狭关，繁花的老家，希望能够找到一些线索。
雨晴曾经告诉我们，繁花辞职回到狭关是因为有一个妹妹，旁遮普照片也似乎证明了这一点，然而 —— 当调查组查询了当地的居民后，却发现繁花从来没有在狭关有过妹妹。
正当调查组陷入困境之时，在繁花的家中，发现了一本日志本。日志本被繁花亲自使用过，且使用繁花自己的魔法加密过，虽然里面可能含有重要的线索，但是我们必须要繁花的魔法才能解密。
这本日志，也许就包含着解开一切困扰着调查组的谜题的关键。只可惜，我们现在还无法打开。
[en.@desc]
After Blossom and her accomplices disappeared one after another, the investigation team lost direction. Helplessly, the investigation team returned to the Narrow Gate, Blossom’s hometown, hoping to find some clues.
Rain Shine once told us that Blossom resigned and returned to the Narrow Gate because she had a sister, and the photos of Punjab seemed to prove this. However —— when the investigation team inquired with the local residents, they found that Blossom had never had a sister in the Narrow Gate.
Just as the investigation team was in trouble, a logbook was found in Blossom’s home. The logbook was personally used by Blossom and encrypted with Blossom’s own magic. Although it may contain important clues, we need Blossom’s magic to decrypt it.
This logbook may contain the key to solving all the puzzles that trouble the investigation team. Too bad we don’t have the means to open it yet.
