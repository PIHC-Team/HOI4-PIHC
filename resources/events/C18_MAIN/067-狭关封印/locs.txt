小小的阻碍

Small Obstacle

[zh.@desc]
随着挖掘工作的深入，越来越多的古老水晶被发现，它们在市场上的估价也越来越高。成批的水晶被运向柔柔港，期待在国际市场上获得更高的价格。无数商单已经签署，甚至超出我们的开采速度。投资、新的采矿作业设备、和麒麟，每天都在涌入。
然而，继续向山洞深处开掘的工作不得不停止 —— 我们抵达了当年矿难事故的地点，这里矗立着的是一堵魔法形成的厚厚的墙。墙边还有一些头盔、矿镐，和一块小小的遇难者纪念石碑。
透过半透明的魔法墙，我们依稀可以看得见矿洞的深处：那是一处巨大宽阔的地下空间，依稀还能看得见里面的一座座小楼、铁轨与矿车，那是遗留下来的矿洞设施。甚至还有一些不知道如何在这不见天日的环境下生存下来的树木和灌木丛，规则地分布在建筑的周围。
在当年的矿难事故后，雨晴在这里用魔法封印了矿洞，禁止了一切采矿业务。现在我们只能让雨晴回来，解开这道封印。
[en.@desc]
As the excavation work goes deeper, more and more ancient crystals are discovered, and their value on the market is getting higher and higher. Batches of crystals are being shipped to Fluttershy Harbour, expecting to get higher prices on the international market. Countless contracts have been signed, even beyond our mining speed. Investments, new mining equipment, and Kirins are pouring in every day.
However, the work of continuing to dig into the depths of the cave has to stop — we have reached the site of the mine disaster that year, where a thick wall formed by magic stands. There are some helmets, pickaxes, and a small memorial stone for the victims at the wall.
Through the translucent magic wall, we can vaguely see the depths of the mine: it is a huge and wide underground space, and you can still see the small buildings, iron rails, and mine carts inside, which are the remaining mine facilities. There are even some trees and bushes that don’t know how to survive in this environment without sunlight, distributed regularly around the buildings.
After the mine disaster that year, Rain Shine sealed the mine with magic here, prohibiting all mining operations. Now we can only let Rain Shine come back and unlock this seal.
