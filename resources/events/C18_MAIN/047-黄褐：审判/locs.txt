黄褐的审判
[en.@]
Ochre’s Trial
[zh.@desc]
最终，“繁花案”的幕后主使 —— 黄褐，被送上了法庭。旁遮普照片中货物上的货运总局标志，以及焦赭无意间的证词“几年前，他还在货运总局的仓库偷了几株香草，带回家自己种”，都从侧面证明了那时货运总局在负责稚马之息的调度，符合海蓝的证词。
法庭上，黄褐依然沉默不语，甚至对于喧闹的陪审团展现出了憎恶与不耐烦的态度。但面对法庭的讯问，黄褐竟然没有丝毫的隐瞒：
“所以，你在背后指导繁花，策划了持续数年的稚马之息走私么？” 点头。
“那么之前，你对调查组的回答都是谎言？” 点头。
“你还有什么想说的么？是否遭到胁迫或有其他原因？” 摇头。
干净利落地，法庭判决安详有罪，处以终身监禁，关押于险崇峰监狱。

[en.@desc]
At the very end, the mastermind behind the “Blossom Case” — Ochre, was brought to court. The logo of the Freight Bureau on the goods in the Punjab photo, and the inadvertent testimony of Burnt Umber, that “A few years ago, he stole a few fragrant herbs from the Freight Bureau’s warehouse and brought them home to grow.”, both proved that the Freight Bureau was in charge of the transfer of the foal’s breath at that time, which matched Aquamarine’s testimony.
In court, Ochre remained silent, and even showed a disdainful and impatient attitude towards the noisy jury. But when questioned by the court, Ochre did not hide anything:
“So, you were behind the scenes guiding Blossom Burst, planning the smuggling of foal’s breath for several years?” Nod.
“So, were your answers to the investigation team all lies?” Nod.
“Do you have anything else to say? Were you coerced or for other reasons?” Shake head.
Cleanly, the court ruled Ochre guilty and sentenced her to life imprisonment, to be held in the Peaks of Peril Prison.
