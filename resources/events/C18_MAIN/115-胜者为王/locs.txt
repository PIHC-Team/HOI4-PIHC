胜者为王
[en.@]
Winner Takes All
[zh.@desc]
两个世界的战争结束了，胜者将获得一切，而败者将失去一切... 吗？此时，秋烨*与秋烨’互相注视着对方，她们都知道，战争还剩下最后一步：胜者将决定败者的命运。
塞拉斯蒂娅曾警告过我们：来自不同世界的相同个体，大规模出现在同一个世界，那整个世界都会在不稳定中崩溃。狭关历史上的数次地震已经证明了这一点，而这也恰恰是我们走向与自我的战争的起点。
所以，胜者将如何选择？将败者驱逐到那个世界，让他们在风之魔呼啸下的冰天雪地中自生自灭？还是将他们收留在这个世界，与我们一同，面对未知的不稳定和世界的崩溃？
[en.@desc]
The war between two worlds has ended, the winner will take all, and the loser will lose everything... right? At this moment, Autumn Blaze* and Autumn Blaze’ are staring at each other, they both know that there is one last stroke of the war: the winner will decide the fate of the loser.
Celestia once warned us: when the same individual from different worlds appears on the same world on a large scale, the entire world will collapse in instability. The numerous earthquakes in the history of Narrow Gate have proven this point, and this is also the starting point for us to go to war with ourselves.
So, how will the winner choose? Will the loser be driven to that world, to survive and die in the ice and snow under the howling Windigos? Or will they be sheltered in this world, facing the unknown instability and the collapse of the world with us?
