[zh.@desc]
• 6月9日
§R「绝密」§!
通道开启已经一年零四个月了，秘密小组现在也已经总计招募了三十二名员工，或者我应该说六十四名员工才对？还要外加一只松鼠，我们的通道首席科学家养的那只。昨天，雨晴*亲自来到了狭关，与雨晴’进行了一次友好的会谈。除了确定接下来的工作需求外，她们强调通道的存在一定要严格保密，不能让外界得知。问题在于，即使我们把这整片矿区封锁起来，这么多员工、实验设备的物资需求，真的很难不引起注意。但无论工作多么艰巨，作为麒麟社群货运局长，和通道项目的临时主管，我一定会完成雨晴*交代的任务。
• 7月9日
§R「绝密」§!
这个月是通道项目组正式建立后双方第一次交换研究成果：上个月，青草*在研究稚马之息与野草的杂交，而青草’则在研究土壤改良。如今我们同时拥有了更好的杂交种与改良的土壤。另外，雨晴*和我进行了一次秘密谈话：我将暂时辞去货运局长的职位，担任狭关货运局长，从而更顺理成章地常驻在狭关，指导这个秘密的通道项目。雨晴*与我的继任者黄褐*将帮助协调物资调配，而我将负责掩盖物资在狭关的去向。雨晴*也不清楚这项任务将要持续多久，但她承诺，等通道真相大白的那一天，麒麟们一定会感激我的付出。至于现在，如果有谁问起辞职的原因，我就随便胡扯，比如自己在狭关其实有个妹妹需要照顾之类的理由就行。

[en.@desc]
- June 9
§R<=TOP SECRET=>§!
The tunnel has been open for a year and four months, and the secret group has now recruited a total of thirty-two employees, or should I say sixty-four employees? Plus a squirrel, the one that our tunnel’s chief scientist raised. Yesterday, Rain Shine* came to the Narrow Gate by herself and had a friendly talk with Rain Shine’. In addition to determining the next work requirements, they emphasized that the existence of the tunnel must be kept strictly confidential and cannot be known to the outside world. The problem is, even if we lockdown this entire mining area, it’s really hard not to attract public attention with so many employees and material needs for experimental equipment. But no matter how difficult the work is, as the director of the Kirin Commune Freight Bureau and the temporary supervisor of the tunnel project, I will definitely get my duty done as Rain Shine* instructed.
- July 9
§R<=TOP SECRET=>§!
This is the month for our first exchange of research results between the two sides after the establishment of the tunnel project: last month, Grass-Green* was researching the hybridization of the breath of foals and wild grass, while Grass-Green’ was researching soil improvement. Now we have better hybrid seeds and improved soil at the same time. In addition, Rain Shine* and I had a secret conversation: I will temporarily resign from the position of the director of the Commune Freight Bureau and take up the position of the director of the Narrow Gate Freight Bureau, so as to stay in the Narrow Gate more naturally and guide this secret tunnel project. Rain Shine* and my successor, Ochre*, will help coordinate the allocation of materials, and I will be responsible for covering up the whereabouts of the materials in the Narrow Gate. Rain Shine* is also unsure how long this task will last, but she promised that the day when the truth of the tunnel is revealed, the Kirins will surely appreciate my efforts. As for now, if anyone asks about the reason for my resignation, I’ll just make up something, like I actually have a sister in the Narrow Gate who needs to be taken care of.
