考古发现：《黑沙纪事》
[en.@]
Archeological Discovery: “Black Sand Chronicle”

[zh.@desc]
我们在[FROM.FROM.GetName]的考古过程中发现了一本古书，时间似乎来自于历史上险崇峰麒麟与南都逆麟的战争结束不久后，其中记录了在战场上幸存的一群麒麟们的生活。虽然说是《纪事》，但书中似乎存在不少神话情节。
书中描述，大火在战争结束后依然持续燃烧了一个月，土地都全部烧成黑色... 东方是被逆麟燃烧得更彻底的南都，南方的天气正在变冷，黑色的沙尘暴阻拦了他们向西向北的退路。在最后的食物耗尽后，他们只能被困在这里等待他们的末日。
而就在他们将要放弃希望的时候，一只神秘的独角兽出现在他们面前：她并没有驻足拯救这群麒麟，而是从东侧的火焰中走出，径直走向了西侧的沙尘暴，消失其中。但她路过之处，无数花朵在烧焦的土地上生出，这些花朵成为了麒麟们的食物，让他们得以继续生存。
战争结束后，定居在此的这麒麟建立了如今的黑沙府，黑色的沙漠已经变成了一片绿洲。这大概就是《黑沙纪事》的主要内容，虽然其中的情节可能有真有假，也许是他们在艰苦环境中的幻觉。但这本书解释了现代黑沙府的起源，弥补了考古学家们对于这段历史时期的空白。
更有趣的是，其中详细记录了战争后第一代在此生活的麒麟的农耕生活，包含了不少农业技术，如何在彻底烧焦的土地上种植作物，以及如何利用黑沙的独特性质进行农业生产。

[en.@desc]
We found an ancient book during the archeological process in [FROM.FROM.GetName], which seems to come from the era shortly after the war between the Peaks of Peril Kirins and Nan Du Niriks, recording the lives of a group of Kirins who survived on the battlefield. Although it is called the “Chronicle”, the book seems to contain many mythical elements.
In the books’ narrative, the fire continued to burn for a month after the war ended, turning the entire land black... To the east was the more thoroughly burned Nan Du, to the south the weather was turning cold, and black sandstorms blocked their retreat to the west and north. After their food ran out, they were trapped here, waiting for their end.
Just as they were about to give up hope, a mysterious unicorn appeared before them: she did not stop to save these Kirins, but walked out of the flames to the east and straight into the sandstorm to the west, disappearing within. But wherever she passed, countless flowers sprouted from the scorched earth, becoming the Kirins’ food and allowing them to survive.
After the war, the Kirins who settled here established the current Hei Sha Fu, the city on the black sand desert that has now become an oasis. This is probably the main content of the “Black Sand Chronicle”, although the events in it may be only half-true, perhaps the hallucinations of these Kirins in their harsh environment. But this book explains the origin of modern Hei Sha Fu, filling in the gaps in the history of this period for archaeologists.
What is more interesting is that it details the agricultural life of the first generation of Kirins who lived here after the war, including many agricultural techniques, such as how to grow crops on completely scorched land and how to use the unique properties of black sand for agricultural production.
