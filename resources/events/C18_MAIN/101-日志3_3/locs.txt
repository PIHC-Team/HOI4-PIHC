• 10月9日
§R「绝密」§!
不幸的事还是发生了，在又一次地震中，矿洞被掩埋，不少麒麟遇难。我们也是花了数天的挖掘才重新恢复了与对面的连接。这次，不少员工的意见有些大，局势似乎正在恶化。好在雨晴*’赶到这里讨论了三四次之后，最终双方达成了一致。科技研究方面互相共享。我们将向他们提供稚马之息的援助，而他们将给我们提供矿石、花卉、金币等做为交换。矿难虽然是悲惨的，但也带来了一个机会：雨晴*有了正当的理由宣布封锁狭关，我们的通道项目组终于可以在完全的秘密中运作，不用担心被发现了。接下来，雨晴*打算出国一趟，去访问塞拉斯蒂娅*：塞拉斯蒂娅*是生活在西方大陆的一只天角兽。就像雨晴*领导我们一样，塞拉斯蒂娅*是那群小马的领袖。不得不承认，小马们，尤其是独角兽们，对于魔法的理解和研究比我们多得多，也许塞拉斯蒂娅*对于这种通道能有更多的了解。
• 11月9日
§R「绝密」§!
雨晴*从小马利亚回来了，似乎没有得到什么有用的信息，塞拉斯蒂娅*对于这种通道也没什么了解。不过，塞拉斯蒂娅*在了解到我们的矿难事故后说，她也曾遇到相同的事情：她的学生，一只名为暮光闪闪*的公主曾经前往过另一个世界，遇到了物种为“人类”的另一个自己。据说，如果很多不同世界的自己同时存在于同一个世界，就容易造成空间不稳定。所以矿难事故可能确实是来自通道那边的矿工导致的，只不是不是有意的，而是——双方的同时存在。
雨晴*也带回了一些关于小马利亚的消息 —— 那边的情况似乎不容乐观。塞拉斯蒂娅*不久之后就要退位，暮光闪闪*将要继位。小马利亚似乎正处在某种内战的边缘：独角兽、天马与陆马各自抱团互相敌对，政令不出坎特洛特。小马利亚可是麒麟族开放的启蒙者和友谊的导师，如今他们所面临的局面确实值得担忧。雨晴*表示，麒麟要避免被卷入其中，我们将保持观望。
通道方面，两个世界的研究合作进展迅速，预计只要三到五年，我们就可以在两个世界都完成“去静默化”工作。我们的小组正式更名为“千界之门保密局”，又称“千界俱乐部”，直接接受雨晴的秘密领导。在矿难的废墟中，新的地下大楼——“千界办公室”被建设起来，雨晴在险崇峰、南都、麒麟之森等多个地方也组建了当地的“千界俱乐部”秘密分支，主要渗透在货运局和警察局中，确保我们这里的物资供应和保密事宜。

- October 9
§R<=TOP SECRET=>§!
Unfortunately, our worst concern has come true. In another earthquake, the mine was buried, and many Kirins died. It took us several days to restore the connection with the other side. This time, many employees had strong opinions, and the situation seemed to be deteriorating. Fortunately, after Rain Shine*’ arrived here and held about four negotiations, an agreement was finally reached. Both sides will share the research progress. We will be aiding them with foal’s breath while they will provide us with ores, flowers, and gold coins, etc., as an exchange. Although the mine disaster was tragic, it also brought an opportunity: Rain Shine* had a legitimate reason to announce the blockade of the Narrow Gate. Our tunnel project team can finally operate in complete secrecy without worrying about being discovered. Next, Rain Shine* plans to go abroad to visit Celestia*: Celestia* is a unicorn living on the continent on our west. Just like Rain Shine* leads us, Celestia* is the leader of those ponies. I have to admit that ponies, especially unicorns, have a much better understanding and research of magic than us. Maybe Celestia* will have a better understanding of this tunnel.
- November 9
§R<=TOP SECRET=>§!
Rain Shine* has returned from Equestria, and it seems that she didn’t get any useful information. Celestia* also doesn’t know much about this tunnel. However, after Celestia* learned about our mine disaster, she said that she had encountered the same thing: her student, a princess named Twilight Sparkle*, once went to another world and met another self of the species called “human”. It is said that spatial instability can be caused if many selves from different worlds exist in the same world at the same time. So the mine disaster may indeed have been caused by the miners from the other side, but not intentionally. It was because of the simultaneous existence of both sides.
Rain Shine* also brought back some news about Equestria — the situation over there doesn’t seem optimistic. Celestia* is about to abdicate soon, and Twilight Sparkle* is about to succeed. Equestria seems to be on the brink of some kind of civil war: unicorns, pegasi, and earth ponies are all hostile to each other, and Canterlot is in chaos. Equestria is the enlightener and mentor of friendship of the Kirins, and the current situation they are facing is indeed worrying. Rain Shine* said that the Kirins should avoid being involved, and we will remain vigilant.
Regarding the tunnel, the research cooperation between the two worlds is progressing rapidly. It is expected that we will be able to complete the “de-Silencing” work in both worlds in just three to five years. Our group has officially been renamed as the “Culanika Lokadhātu Undercover Bureau”, or “The CLUB”, directly under Rain Shine’s secret leadership. In the ruins of the mine disaster, a new underground building — the “Lokadhātu Office” — has been constructed. Rain Shine has also established local CLUB secret branches in Peaks of Peril, Nan Du, and the Kirin Forest, mainly infiltrating the Bureau of Freight and the Police Department, ensuring our material supply and confidentiality matters here.
