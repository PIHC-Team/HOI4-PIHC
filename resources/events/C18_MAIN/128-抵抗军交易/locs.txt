[en.@desc]
Dealing with the Resistance should be done with caution, as we do not want to offend the upholders of order: the Empire, the Shapeshifters and the Centaurs.
Therefore, we will not provide any military support to the Resistance, but they are willing to train our troops in the use of modern firearms for free.
They only ask that we secretly disclose some information to them when trading with those Imperial officials.
