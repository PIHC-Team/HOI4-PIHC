时间已经过去了一个月，可以说，失踪的矿工已经大概率全部遇难了，除非——
地震发生点近邻我们当时发现的魔法封印，当我们挖开塌方的隧道后，魔法封印和其中的建筑依旧完好无损。这意味着，如果他们以某种方式进入了封印，那他们可能还活着。
也许，防止地震破坏地下矿洞内的建筑本身就是雨晴设立这个封印的目的之一？那样我们或许不该找雨晴去解除这个封印 —— 如果我们早些时候解开了这个封印，那可能整个地区都会被地震摧毁。
就在犹豫之中，秋烨接到报告，在魔法封印附近，搜救队挖出了一部分破旧的日志。看上去是一位矿工留下的，似乎还有一位科学家，但是时间却不太对...
[en.@desc]
It has been a month, and it can be said that the missing miners have probably all died, unless —
The epicenter of the earthquake is close to the magic seal we discovered at the time. After we dug out the collapsed tunnel, the magic seal and the building inside were still intact. This means that if they somehow entered the seal, they might still be alive.
Perhaps, preventing the earthquake from destroying the buildings in the underground mine itself was one of Rain Shine’s purposes for setting up this seal? Then maybe we shouldn’t ask Rain Shine to lift this seal — if we had lifted this seal earlier, the entire area might have been destroyed by the earthquake.
While hesitating, Autumn Blaze received a report that near the magic seal, the rescue team dug out a part of a dilapidated log. It looks like it was left by a miner, and there seems to be a scientist there, but the time of the log doesn’t seem to match...
