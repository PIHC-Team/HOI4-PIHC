[zh.@desc]
书中描述，为了应对灾难，麒麟们勇猛地化作逆麟，而这些逆麟却成为了文明的终结者。我们并不清楚他们是否赢得了战争，但这已经不再重要。
第一次，一个全国范围内组织持续了数十年之久。他们与小马利亚建立了联系，他们与龙族和仙境建立了联系。外族见证了他们的末日，也见证了我们的新生。
或许，开放本身就是一个错误？又或许，在“静默”中他们也无法避免相同的结局？

§b“险山四顾，楼宇一炬，当时奢侈今何处？只见草萧疏，水萦纡。” ——《焚天纪事》原文§!

[en.@desc]
In the books’ narrative, to face the disaster, the Kirins bravely transformed into Niriks, but these Niriks became the enders of civilization. We are not sure if they won the war, but that does not matter anymore.
For the first time, an organization lasted for decades nationwide. They established contacts with Equestria, they established contacts with the Dragons and Unicornia. The outsiders witnessed their doomsday, and also witnessed our birth.
Perhaps, openness itself was a mistake? Or perhaps, even in the “Silence” they could not avoid the same fate?
