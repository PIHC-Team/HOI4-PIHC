海蓝，§2东部地区区长§!，生于险崇峰，现居住于§2南都§!。

海蓝是§2繁花曾经的挚友§!，§2长相也与繁花十分相似§!。谈话中，即使已知繁花涉嫌走私已经被通缉，海蓝毫不避讳自己与繁花的关系。但她表示，自从繁花到偏远的狭关地区后，§2双方就没有见过面§!。
关于§2柔柔港§!，她保证自己管理下，南部港口不存在任何走私行为。这也与调查组此前的调查结果相符。
关于§2南都的稚马之息转运记录§!，海蓝承认狭关曾经多次申请因灾补充物资，但自己并未产生过怀疑。而且，海蓝声称当时是特殊时期，稚马之息是国家重要战略物资，即使是在东部地区的调动，也不归区长管辖。那个时候的§2所有花卉都受货运总局直接指挥§!。
我们还想进一步确认关于“特殊时期”的具体政策和时间，但谈话的时间已过，§2海蓝以还有事情要忙的理由，离开了谈话§!。

Aquamarine, §2District Governor of the Eastern Region§!, borned in Peaks of Peril, currently residing in §2Nan Du§!.

Aquamarine is a §2dear friend of Blossom Burst§!, she also §2bears a striking resemblance to Blossom Burst in appearance§!. During the conversation, even though it is known that Blossom Burst is suspected of smuggling and has been wanted, Aquamarine does not shy away from her relationship with Blossom Burst. However, she stated that since Blossom Burst went to the remote Narrow Gate area, §2the two have not met§!.
Regarding §2Fluttershy Harbour§!, she assured that there was no smuggling under her management in the southern ports. This is also consistent with the previous investigation results of the investigation team.
Regarding the §2transfer records of the foal’s breath in Nan Du§!, Aquamarine admitted that Narrow Gate had applied for disaster relief supplies several times, but she did not have any doubts. Furthermore, Aquamarine claimed that it was a special period, and the foal’s breath was an important national strategic material. Even if it was transferred to the Eastern Region, it was not within the jurisdiction of the District Governor. §2All flowers at that time were under the direct command of the Freight Bureau§!.
We also wanted to further confirm the specific policies and time of the “special period”, but the time was up, and §2Aquamarine left the conversation on the grounds that she had other things to attend to§!.
