很快，斯科潘带着两只石像鬼侍卫，来到了秋烨的面前。正如僧达克一样，半人马王国的居民总是喜欢直接的对话。
“你好，麒麟。” 斯科潘面无表情的说道。“我知道你们一直在和僧达克接触。我不知道你们是否有实际合作，合作程度有多深，总之你们现在还有退出的机会。”
“虽然我也并不怎么认同哥哥在做的那些事，但他毕竟是我的哥哥，僧达克那个家伙居然会愚蠢到认为我会合作，这也太可笑了。”

还没等秋烨开口，斯科潘示意打断了秋烨。“不要对我抱有期待，我不如传言的那般友好，尤其是涉及我哥哥的事情。”
说完，斯科潘的语气突然缓和了下来：“你们与僧达克无关，我不想看到你们受到牵连。你们也清楚我哥哥的脾气：他现在还不知道，但一旦我把这件事告诉他，那就没有什么能救得了你们了。”
“所以，你们只有两个选择，要么你们立即处理掉僧达克，我们可以当做什么都没发生。要么，我将一切告诉我哥哥，你们会和僧达克一起被处理掉。”

斯科潘转身离开了树屋办公室，只留下了什么都没说的秋烨。
[en.@desc]
Soon, Scorpan arrived in front of Autumn Blaze with two gargoyle guards. Just like Sendak, the residents of the Centaur Kingdom always prefer direct dialogue.
“Hello, little Kirin.” Scorpan said expressionlessly. “I know you have been contacting Sendak. I don’t know if you have actually cooperated, or how deep the cooperation is, but you still have a chance to withdraw now.”
“Although I don’t really agree with what my elder brother is doing, he is my brother after all. That Sendak dare to think that I would cooperate with him, that’s just ridiculous.”

Before Autumn Blaze could speak, Scorpan gestured to interrupt her. “Don’t expect anything from me, I am less kind than they say, especially when it comes to my brother.”
After that, Scorpan’s tone suddenly softened: “You have nothing to do with Sendak, I don’t want to see you implicated. You also know my brother’s temper: he doesn’t know this yet, but once I tell him about this, nothing can save you.”
“So, you only have two choices, either you make Sendak disappear immediately, and we can pretend that nothing happened. Or, I will tell my brother everything, and you will be dealt with together with Sendak.”

Scorpan turned and left the treehouse office, leaving Autumn Blaze who said nothing.
