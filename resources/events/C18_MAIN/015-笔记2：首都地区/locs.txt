首都以及南部地区调查报告
[en.@]
Capital and Southern Regions Investigation Report
[zh.@desc]
经过调查，我们在§2首都以及南部地区§!发现了部分走私稚马之息的地方官员，已经严肃处理。然而，这些官员大多数都是独自行动、临时起意，而非有组织的走私团伙，多数也与东部地区的重大走私专案没有关联。
虽然这些独立案件与走私专案无关，但这些官员的走私行为都利用了一个相同的漏洞 —— 地方稚马之息因灾补缺申请表的审批流程过于宽松。这意味着，即使首都地区没有直接牵涉进走私专案，中央政府所制定的过于宽松的审批流程难辞其咎。
如今，这仅仅被解释为“流程设计上的失误”，但调查发现，在民众多次举报后，中央政府仅仅是要求地区政府严肃处理具体案件的官员，而并未对审批流程进行任何调整。§2中央政府内可能存在走私专案的包庇者§!，有意或者无意。
[en.@desc]
After the investigation, we found some local officials who smuggled foal’s breath in the §2capital and Southern Regions§!, and they have been dealt with seriously. However, most of these officials acted alone, on a temporary whim, rather than as part of an organized smuggling ring, and most of them are not related to the key smuggling case in the Eastern Region.
Although these independent cases are not related to the smuggling case, the smuggling activities of these officials all exploited the same loophole — the approval process of foal’s breath disaster relief application forms in local areas is too loose. This means that even if the capital region is not directly involved in the smuggling case, the overly loose approval process formulated by the central government is to blame.
Today, this is only explained as a “design flaw in the process”, but the investigation found that after multiple reports from the public, the central government only required the local government to seriously deal with the officials involved in specific cases, and did not make any adjustments to the approval process. §2There may be protectors of the smuggling case within the central government§!, intentionally or unintentionally.
