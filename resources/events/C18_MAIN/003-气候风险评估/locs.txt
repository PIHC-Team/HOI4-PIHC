气候风险评估
[en.@]
Climate Risk Assessment
[zh.@desc]
一些从小马利亚留学回来的气象观测员，与我们从小马谷请来的专业气象学家共同分析了险崇峰南麓与麒麟群岛的若干观测站数据，我们得出结论：
在过去的一年中，风之魔的活动在全世界范围内确实有所提升。然而交叉对比靠近南部风之魔领地的麒麟社群地区数据与世界范围数据，我们并未发现显著区别。
这意味着，风之魔的活动增加并未给麒麟族带来额外的降温降雪或者任何气候风险。相比之下，数据表明海马利亚与克鲁格镇等地区具有更加严重的降温趋势，相关方更加有必要保持警惕。
综上所述，报告认为麒麟目前并未受到气候风险的威胁，但是面对一个全球性的气候变化，我们与其他国家仍然需要一同保持努力。
[en.@desc]
Some meteorological observers who returned from studying abroad in Equestria, together with professional meteorologists we invited from Ponyville, have analyzed the data from several observation stations near the Southern Peaks of Peril and Kirin Isles with us. We have come to the following conclusion:
In the past year, the activity of the Windigos has indeed increased worldwide. However, when cross-referencing the data from the Kirin commune local areas near the southern Windigo territory with the global data, we did not observe any significant differences.
This means that the increased activity of the Windigos has not brought any additional cooling, snowfall, or any climate risks to the Kirin tribes. In contrast, the data shows that areas such as Seaquestria and Kluge Town have more severe cooling trends, and the relevant parties need to be more vigilant.
In conclusion, the report believes that the Kirin are currently not threatened by climate risks, but in the face of a global climate change, we still need to work together with other countries to maintain our efforts.
