The muffin mania started by Derpy Hooves is now a global phenomenon. More and more ponies are worshiping muffins and of course, Saint Derpy Hooves.
Just now, citizens of Stratusburg have jointly built a statue of Derpy Hooves in the city center. And they are planning to merge with the Cloudsdale to form a brand new type of regime. There will no longer be difference in cities, countries, or even species. Everypony will be united under the name of muffins.
It is believed that Stratusburg has been receiving muffins from Cloudsdale for a long time, thanks to the direct intervention of Saint Derpy Hooves. The citizens of both cities are now living in peace and prosperity, and they are willing to share their happiness with the rest of the world.
Meteorologist has also observed that the climate in the two cities has been improving significantly. Spring and Summer is returning to the land, and the sky is getting clearer. So perhaps in the long last, the Windigos problem unable to be solved by all previous rulers for thousands of years, will finally be resolved by the power of muffins.
The world is changing, and it’s changing fast. Are you ready for the new era of muffins?
