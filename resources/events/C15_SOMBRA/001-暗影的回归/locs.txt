暗影的回归
[en.@]
Return of The Shadow
[zh.@desc]
黑晶王是如何回归的，他自己或许也记不清楚了。
他只记得自己毁掉了谐律之树，攻入坎特洛特，在皇宫中击败了无序，然后就莫名其妙地被M6的友谊魔法炸成了碎片。
在他意识不清的时间段内他似乎看到了许多，但又什么都没有看清：他似乎模糊看到了曾经接纳他的影之血水晶，他似乎模糊看到了一位熟悉的老者，他似乎看到了邪茧、提雷克和那只不知道名字的粉色幼驹，他似乎看到了M6，他似乎看到了水晶帝国——在不知道是谁的一群小马管理下。
然后他便醒来，发现自己在一群荒原影魔面前，还有许多奴隶小马部队，整齐划一的部队已经在他面前等待听取他的命令。
稀里糊涂地，他下达了进攻的命令，那些部队便遵循命令向着水晶帝国南下进攻。
稀里糊涂地，水晶帝国的军队不堪一击，他不费吹灰之力重新坐上了水晶帝国的王位。
这让他终于清醒了过来：发生了什么或许没那么重要，他只知道他开始变得再次熟悉，就像那些美好的旧日子。
水晶小马们，你们的皇帝回来了！
[en.@desc]
How did Sombra return? He doesn’t seem to remember it himself.
He only remembers that he destroyed the Tree of Harmony, invaded Canterlot, defeated Discord in the palace, and then was inexplicably blown to pieces by the friendship magic of the M6.
During the time when he was unconscious, he seemed to have seen a lot, but he didn’t see anything clearly: he seemed to have seen the blood crystal that once accepted him, he seemed to have seen a familiar elder, he seemed to have seen Chrysalis, Tirek, and that pink foal whose name he didn’t know, he seemed to have seen the M6, he seemed to have seen the Crystal Empire — under the management of some ponies he didn’t know.
Then he woke up and found himself in front of the Umbrums, with many enslaved pony troops, all of which were well-organized and waiting for his orders.
Confused, he gave the order to attack, and those troops followed the order to march southward towards the Crystal Empire.
Confused, the Crystal Empire’s army was no match for his forces, and he easily regained the throne of the Crystal Empire.
This finally woke him up: what just happened might not be that important, he just knew that things are becoming familiar again, just like those good old days.
Crystal ponies, your emperor has returned!
