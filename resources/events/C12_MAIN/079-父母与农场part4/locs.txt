Limestone Pie and Maud Pie exchanged glances, both stunned by their father’s unexpected words. Marble Pie lifted her tear-filled eyes, gazing at her father in shock, forgetting to wipe the snot and tears from her face.

"You... shouldn’t have handed the farm’s future to the Empire for my sake," Igneous Rock Pie said slowly, his earlier realization dawning on him.

"Limestone ... Maud... Marble... and my little Pinkamina..." he continued, "Having you as my daughters in this life... is my fortune. But..." He shook his head with effort. "You shouldn’t have given up your lives for me, and you certainly shouldn’t have colluded with those murderers who harmed Pinkamina..." His voice was weak but filled with conviction. "Look ahead, my children."

"Father!" Marble Pie cried out again, her sobs intensifying. Maud Pie lowered her head in silence, and Limestone Pie’s face turned crimson. She hated herself, hated her current helplessness.
