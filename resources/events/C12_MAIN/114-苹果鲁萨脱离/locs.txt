[zh.@desc]
小萍花带着数十名苹果家族的成员大步横冲直撞进了苹果鲁萨的市政厅，执勤的卫兵们看到这一幕，既不敢阻拦也不敢多作询问，他们唯一能做的只是让行，闪到一边。很快，当小萍花使用苹果鲁萨的广播塔向整个小马利亚公布这个瞠目结舌的消息之前，留在市政厅工作的苹果家族成员就同样得知了布雷本遇害并非帝国作祟，而是石青派一蹄策划的事故。虽然一部分苹果家族成员仍然对此犹豫不决并害怕于石青派的威严。但大部分苹果家族成员仍然发动起来，迅速接管了苹果鲁萨周边的部队，并且宣布从大洛克维尔独立，这些部队的苹果家族指挥官早在今天之前就私下得到莫丝清香等小马的通知和信号。大洛克维尔的旗帜被降下，苹果家族的绿星苹果旗再次被升起时，整个苹果鲁萨的小马都知道这意味着什么。


[en.@desc]
Apple Bloom stormed into Appleloosa’s town hall with dozens of members of the Apple Family at her side. The guards on duty, seeing this sudden and forceful intrusion, didn’t dare to stop them or even ask questions. All they could do was step aside and make way.

Soon, before Apple Bloom used Appleloosa’s broadcast tower to announce this shocking news to all of Equestria, the Apple Family members still working inside the town hall had already learned the truth—that Braeburn’s death had not been the work of the Empire, but rather an accident secretly orchestrated by Periwinkle’s faction.

Although some members of the Apple Family still hesitated and feared the power of Limestone’s group, most of them quickly rose up. They seized control of the nearby Appleloosa garrisons and declared independence from Greater Rockville. The Apple Family commanders of these units had, even before today, already received secret warnings and signals from ponies such as Mossy Scent.

When the flag of Greater Rockville was lowered and the Apple Family’s green star-and-apple banner was raised again, everypony in Appleloosa knew exactly what that meant.
