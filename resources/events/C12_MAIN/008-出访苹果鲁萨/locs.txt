[zh.@desc]
石灰派、泥枝、玉琪派，以及其他一些采石农场的新晋官员坐上了这趟列车，向留在站台的石青派点了点头，需要有小马负责在代表团出访的这几天内管理好自治领。列车从鸣雷矿脉出发，开往苹果鲁萨的西郊火车站，路途中，火车经过了俩姐妹城堡，这座城堡目前没有发生太多的变化，和多年前一样，茂密的树林不受任何小马管控地生长着，破败的碎石砖块和杂草随处可见，但是城堡最高的塔楼仍然耸立着，逐渐出现在石灰派遥远的视野里。要是终末之战没有发生，现在又会是什么样子，采石农场和苹果鲁萨会发生什么，萍琪会回来吗，许多的问题涌入石灰派的脑海里，让她有些心烦意乱。
“我不太想说破，但我还是想指明这一点，你看起来有些心事，从我们列车目前所处的位置来判断，你大概是想起了以前的时光，我对此也表示十分惋惜”，泥枝发话了，用上了他最关切的词句，然而石灰派只是看着他的未婚夫，平淡的说道“我很好”，随后转身继续看着窗外出神
[en.@desc]
Maud Pie, Mudbriar, Marble Pie, and some newly appointed officials from the rock farm boarded the train, nodding to Limestone Pie who remained on the platform. Someone had to manage the autonomy in the days the delegation was away on their visit. The train departed from the Big Thunder Mine, heading towards the western outskirts of Appleloosa at the train station. Along the way, the train passed by the two Sisters’ Castle. Not much had changed about the castle: the dense forest grew unchecked, with broken stones and weeds scattered about as they had been for years. However, the tallest tower of the castle still stood, gradually appearing in Maud Pie’s distant view. What would things be like now if the Ultimate Battle hadn’t happened? What would happen to the rock farm and Appleloosa? Would Pinkie Pie come back? Many questions flooded Maud Pie’s mind, leaving her feeling a bit restless.
“I don’t mean to pry, but it seems like you have something on your mind. Judging by where our train is currently, you’re probably reminiscing about the past. I’m deeply sorry for that”, Mudbriar spoke, choosing his words carefully, showing genuine concern. However, Maud Pie just looked at her fiancé, calmly stating “I’m fine” before turning back to gaze out the window, lost in thought.
