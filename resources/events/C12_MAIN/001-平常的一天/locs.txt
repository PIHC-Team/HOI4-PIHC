近日传来的噩耗让整个石头家族笼罩在乌云之中——虽然此前也差不多是这样。这勾起了石灰派的回忆，那是去年平常的一天...

§b又是采石农场繁忙的一天，石灰丽娜·黛西·派，或者说，石灰派，走出了刚刚完工不久的会议厅，她的姐姐石青派仍留在那里和几位来自庞德罗莎、洛克维尔的管理小马为采石农场这周的供应量而喋喋不休。玉琪派已经在岩石农场的医院里照料她们的父母——火岩派和云母，他们卧病在床，需要时时有马驹看护。石灰派慢慢走到了派家族的荣耀，那块巨大的奠基顽石跟前进行仔细的擦拭，忙碌的小马从她身边经过，向她问好。
不久以前，这里还只是一大片的荒地，了无生息，一栋双层小屋，一座风车以及一栋石仓是这个农场的全部，而现在成千上万的小马在这块土地上定居，随处可见临时搭建的营帐和低矮的建筑，好在这块土地蕴含着的矿物和石料足够通过铁路与洛马基和苹果鲁萨交换到足够的日用品，能在这块土地上生活的小马还算是幸福的，但是再远一些的其他地区就没那么好运了，冬天来临了，刺骨的寒风凌冽着，温饱还是一件奢侈的事情。
石灰派目光扫到一栋破旧的房屋，那是她们之前共同居住的地方，但是现在由于繁忙的事务她们已经很久没有睡在一起了，在擦拭完奠基顽石后，石灰派面无表情的走向了那里，蹄底下的木板被踩得嘎吱作响，这个房屋这几天就得彻底收拾干净，以便作为临时医院容纳下更多的灾民和可能的产妇，坚硬的加固木墙足以抵抗任何寒风。虽然派家族的成员们更倾向于让其保持空置的状态来留念往日的时光，但是至少又多了许多生命可以在艰难的冬日得到保障。石灰派并不介意其他马驹住在她们的老房子里，但她挂念着一个房间，那个房间属于她的妹妹，那个总是欢笑的天使，每天总是第一个醒来并且为她准备惊喜。
她走进了那个房间，只是静静的坐着，远离外界嘈杂的杂音，感受这里的每一秒，冬日清晨的阳光照在石灰派灰暗的身体上，仿佛萍琪派的轻抚，石灰派仍然保持着冷漠的神情，但是她的嘴角控制不住的微微向下，任何熟悉她的小马都知道，那是她最难过的时刻。§!

但无论当时有多么绝望，生活还充满着希望，不像如今。
也正是在那一天，她遇见了那个改变派家族的小木盒——妹妹萍琪派失踪前留下的礼物……

The recent news had cast a shadow over the entire Pie family, though it had been that way for a while. This brought back memories for Maud Pie, of a typical day last year...

§bIt was another busy day at the Rock Farm. Maudalina Daisy Pie, or Maud Pie as she was known, stepped out of the recently finished meeting hall. Her sister, Limestone Pie, remained inside with several managerial ponies from Pondrosa and Rockville, discussing the Rock Farm’s weekly supply quotas.Marble Pie was already tending to their parents — Igneous Rock and Cloudy Quartz, at the farm’s hospital. Both were bedridden and required constant care. Maud Pie made her way slowly to the pride of the Pie family, Holder’s Boulder, carefully wiping it clean. Busy ponies passed by, greeting her as they went.
Not long ago, this had been barren land with nothing but a small two-story house, a windmill, and a stone silo. Now, thousands of ponies called this place home, with tents and low buildings scattered across the landscape.Luckily, the minerals and stones in the land could be exchanged via rail with cities like Manehattan and Appleloosa for the necessities of life. Despite the harsh winter winds, life was relatively comfortable here compared to the more distant regions, where survival itself was a luxury.
Maud’s gaze fell upon a rundown building, their former shared residence. It had been a while since they’d slept there together, given their busy schedules. After finishing with Holder’s Boulder, Maud walked towards it, the wooden boards creaking under her hooves. The house needed to be cleaned thoroughly to serve as a temporary hospital for more refugees and possible mothers-to-be. Its sturdy reinforced walls could withstand the biting cold. Though the Pie family preferred to leave it as a memorial to bygone days, having more lives sheltered during harsh winters was a consolation. Maud didn’t mind other ponies staying in their old house, but she missed one room in particular—the room belonging to her sister, the always cheerful Pinkie Pie, who would wake up early every day to prepare surprises for her.
Entering that room, Maud sat quietly, away from the noise of the outside world, savoring every moment. The winter sun cast its light upon Maud’s dull gray coat, almost like Pinkie Pie’s gentle touch. Despite maintaining her usual impassive demeanor, a slight downturn at the corner of her mouth betrayed her true feelings. Any pony familiar with her would know that was her saddest moment.§!

But no matter how hopeless things seemed at the time, life was still full of hope, unlike now.
It was on that day that she found the small wooden box that would change the Pie family forever—a gift Pinkie Pie had left behind before she disappeared...
