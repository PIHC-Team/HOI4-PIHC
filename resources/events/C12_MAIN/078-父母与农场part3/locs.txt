[en.@desc]
Igneous Rock Pie lay in his hospital bed, awakened by the irregular beeping of nearby machines. He breathed heavily, each inhale filling his lungs with scalding steam, causing him to groan in discomfort. Summoning all his strength, he coughed twice, which provided a slight relief. However, when he struggled to open his eyes, he saw the Empire’s insignia. Attempting to sit up, a nurse quickly stopped him, warning that it was too dangerous. He looked to the other side; his wife, who had shared his life, remained unconscious.
"Call the children," he rasped.

Soon, the Pie sisters gathered at his bedside. Marble Pie, overjoyed, exclaimed, "Father!" She leaped to his side, crying uncontrollably. Limestone Pie and Maud Pie watched the scene with pained expressions.

"How did I end up with such unfilial fillies?" Igneous Rock Pie suddenly shouted at them.
