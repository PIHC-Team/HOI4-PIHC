萍琪派的掩藏技术十分出色，她之前从没见过这个东西，小心地擦拭完上面的灰尘，箱子上的一行字令石灰派露出难得的微笑：“给最好的姐姐石灰派”。
她打开小小的箱子，一团团彩带从触发的机关里喷了出来，伴随着欢乐的响声洒落在石灰派身上，里面竟装着远超出其看上去的容纳能力的各类物品：烟花、贺卡、一些意义不明的草稿、甚至还有气球——充满气的。

§bMaud Pie began sorting through the clutter in the room, preparing to move it to the nearby stone silo. However, one box hidden behind the bed board caught her attention.
Pinkie Pie’s hiding skills were impressive, as she had never seen it before. Carefully wiping off the dust, a line of text on the box made Maud  break into a rare smile: “For the best sister, Maud Pie”
She opened the small box, and a cascade of ribbons burst out from the triggered mechanism, accompanied by joyful sounds, sprinkling over Maud Pie. Inside the box, it contained a variety of items that far exceeded the box’s capacity: fireworks, greeting cards, some drafts of unknown significance, and even balloons — inflated.
Maud Pie raised an eyebrow slightly, her expression did not change much, as always, without saying a word. She stayed alone in the room for a long time, thinking of a friend that Pinkie Pie had mentioned many times, a pony who was as crazy and whimsical as her — Cheese Sandwich.§!
