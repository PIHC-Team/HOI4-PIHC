There was no prior warning, and no sign of what was about to happen. Yet when Apple Bloom arrived, breathless, at a funeral for members of the Apple family, everypony present froze in surprise.

Apple Bloom quickly scanned the crowd. Mossy Scent and a few other ponies she had already reached out to beforehand were there. They exchanged a brief, knowing glance.

“I have something to tell everyone,” Apple Bloom called out loudly, running to the priest’s side.

Two hours later, she led dozens of Apple family members, charging boldly into the military base Marble Pie had mentioned. The guards on duty, witnessing the scene, neither dared stop them nor question their actions. All they could do was step aside and let them pass.

Soon, even the Apple family members still working at Appleloosa’s town hall learned the truth: Braeburn was alive, and the Apple Pie family was now reclaiming its legacy.

Although some members of the Apple family hesitated, intimidated by Limestone Pie’s authority, the majority mobilized swiftly, taking control of the surrounding Appleloosa forces.

By evening, when the flag of Great Rockville was lowered and the Apple family’s green-starred apple banner was raised once again, everypony in Appleloosa knew what it meant. The old borders between Appleloosa and the quarry farms had erupted into unprecedented tension. Armies that had fought side by side yesterday were suddenly adversaries.

Now, both forces waited for orders from their superiors. The future of the Apple Pie family hung in the balance once more, torn apart because of Braeburn.
