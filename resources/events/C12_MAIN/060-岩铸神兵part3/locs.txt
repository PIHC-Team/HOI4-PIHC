When the carefully selected Earth pony soldier appeared before Limestone Pie, her eyes lit up, and a long-lost smile graced her face. At that moment, platinum cure stood nearby, explaining the significant achievements he had made in potionology over the past few months and the number of fearless Earth ponies who had sacrificed their lives. However, Limestone Pie paid no attention to these details, for this soldier embodied much of the hope to defeat the Empire and any formidable enemy. Before platinum cure could finish his speech, Limestone Pie promptly interrupted him, saying, "We can discuss these matters later. Now, let the experiment begin."

platinum cure wisely fell silent. As the command was given, the Earth pony stood motionless at the center of the experimental platform, just as he had months ago. The research ponies equipped him with weapons and armor. Over the past few months, there had been breakthroughs in weapon integration, successfully reducing weight without compromising firepower. Once the final installation was complete, the soldier remained composed.

"Take a few steps forward to test it," came the command, just as before. The soldier first took a few steps forward, then broke into a swift run, circling the area. Next, he aimed at the designated target and fired, demonstrating astonishing combat prowess. The experimental team present finally broke into smiles—the experiment was a success.

Limestone Pie then instructed the team to standardize the process as soon as possible to establish an armed force and, at any cost, recruit qualified participants throughout the entire army.

That night, Limestone Pie tossed and turned, unable to sleep. A legion dedicated solely to her would soon appear on any battlefield, delivering the most devastating blows to the enemy. However, a deeper desire began to emerge in her mind.
