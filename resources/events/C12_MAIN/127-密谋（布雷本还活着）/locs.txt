然而，就在今天，当几名士官带着邀请函出现在苹果丽丽面前时，她先是吓了一跳，不过得知是什么事情后又陷入了慌张。原来，石青派希望她能代表苹果家族的重要成员与其一同参加多种族的联谊宴会，除了苹果派家族，还包括鹦鹉、野牛和一些来自友好国家的小马。石青派在邀请函中说她十分想念苹果丽丽，她想念她与苹果丽丽、苹果嘉儿一同在采石农场的小屋中共度暖炉夜的温馨和快乐，自战胜帝国后还没有好好与这位她最关心的妹妹的聊过。苹果丽丽没有准备好与石青派再次碰面，但是为了不让石青派起疑心，她还是去了。


[en.@desc]
After Mossy Scent temporarily returned, Apple Bloom compiled a list of the Apple Pie family members who were still present and those who were missing. To ensure the success of any potential plan, she could only approach a few trusted family members working in the government under the pretense of a visit, attempting to gain their support.

Although she still didn’t know the exact locations where Braeburn and the other ponies were being held, perhaps she could pick up some clues.

Today, however, when several sergeants arrived in front of Apple Bloom with an invitation, she was startled at first. But once she understood the situation, her panic deepened.

It turned out that Limestone Pie wanted her to represent the Apple family as an important member at a multi-species social banquet. In addition to the Apple Pie family, there would be parrots, buffalo, and ponies from some friendly nations.

In the invitation, Limestone Pie expressed how much she missed Apple Bloom. She longed for the warmth and joy of the nights by the stove in the quarry farm cabin with Apple Bloom and Applejack, and since the Empire was defeated, she hadn’t properly spoken with her most cherished sister.

Apple Bloom wasn’t ready to meet Limestone Pie again. But to avoid arousing suspicion, she decided to go anyway.
