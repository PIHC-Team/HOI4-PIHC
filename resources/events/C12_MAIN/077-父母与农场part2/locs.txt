The three Pie sisters found a secluded room and sat on one side of a table, silent and still. The air was thick with tension.


Two officials from the Empire, accompanied by other officers from the quarry farm, began their further inspection. Of course, these two officials weren’t genuinely interested in inspecting the “backward” farm; they were merely using this opportunity to wait for their response.Marble Pie began to miss the little things of the Pie family, unable to hold back her sobs.Limestone Pie rolled her eyes. Marble Pie’s crying only made her more agitated. She was about to tell her to stop, but the words wouldn’t come out.

“No matter what identities we’ve been given, we’ll always be family,” Maud Pie broke the silence first. Marble Pie and Limestone Pie looked at her in surprise. However, Maud Pie didn’t elaborate further; she just left it at that.Limestone Pie was stunned, but she quickly snapped out of it. Understanding, she stood up. “Then I’ll go tell those two damn bastards that we agree,” she said.

Maud Pie gave a slight nod. It was an excruciating decision for the Pie family.Just as Limestone Pie braced herself to open the door and find the two officials, she collided with the attending doctor.“Your parents have regained some consciousness,” he said, breathing heavily.
