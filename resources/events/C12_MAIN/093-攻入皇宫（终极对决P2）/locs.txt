Cozy Glow stared in shock at Limestone Pie. Blood streamed down Limestone’s cheeks and features, her armor battered beyond recognition, with shards embedded in her body. Yet, she remained standing, catching her breath and regaining composure.Limestone raised her head, refusing to give Cozy a second chance. She lunged forward, delivering a decisive blow. Cozy, caught off guard, felt an unprecedented dizziness as blood spurted from her mouth. Her body, shattered like stone, crashed heavily against a broken pillar.

Limestone approached step by step. Cozy scrambled backward, attempting to fly, but her wings collapsed limply against her back. She closed her eyes, muttering a teleportation spell, but a sudden surge of pain overwhelmed her, breaking her concentration. Limestone was methodically tearing off her wings.

"Please, don’t kill me," Cozy Glow sobbed. Gone was the lofty Empress of Equestria; she was now just a frightened filly. Limestone lifted her like a rabbit.

"What hell are you?" Cozy cried louder, "I was totally wrong! I'm sorry for all the harm I've caused your family. Let me face justice." Her pleas intensified as she saw a spring-loaded bayonet extend from Limestone’s hoof armor, aiming for her horn.

Cozy’s screams and the surge of magic echoed throughout Canterlot. On the battlefield, soldiers collectively lowered their weapons, stunned. Realizing what had transpired, imperial troops emerged from trenches, surrendering. When the main forces reached Cozy’s palace, they found only blood and remnants.
