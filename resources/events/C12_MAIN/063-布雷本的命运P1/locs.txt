Mossy Scent picked up the two packets of powder, her heart pounding. She hadn’t caught a single word from Octavio Pie, the Air Marshal of the rock farm, who stood before her. Tomorrow, she would return to Appleloosa, and as Braeburn’s secretary, she was to introduce this special powder into his teacup. Mossy Scent had always admired her sister, Limestone Pie. In her younger years, she had never heard of the Pie family or the rock farm. It wasn’t until a few years ago, with the help of Applejack and Pinkie Pie, that the two families reconnected, and she naturally came to know Limestone. To her, although Limestone often appeared stern, she always had everyone’s best interests at heart.

"Did you understand?" Octavio gave Mossy Scent a slight shake, startling her.

"I understand what needs to be done," Mossy Scent nodded, then pulled up her cloak and quickly left the place, ensuring no other pony noticed her.
