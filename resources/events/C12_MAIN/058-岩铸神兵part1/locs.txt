A few months ago, during a routine high-level meeting at the rock farm, Limestone Pie presented a set of blueprints left behind by Pinkie Pie. Pinkie had envisioned a suit of earth pony armor—or rather, a miniature weapons platform—that was not only robust enough to withstand most light weaponry but also equipped with the firepower of an entire squad, all mounted on a single platform. This design promised both mobility and formidable strength.

However, while the concept was promising, its implementation posed significant challenges. Simply mounting weapons and armor onto an earth pony without modifications would be overwhelming, potentially crushing the wearer under the weight.

In the ensuing months, the research team assembled the best experts available at the rock farm to initiate the project and commence rigorous testing. Today marked the first official trial. Limestone Pie, Banana Mash, and Platinum Cure were among those present to observe.

Upon arriving at the testing grounds, a remarkably sturdy earth pony saluted Limestone Pie. Banana Mash explained, "This pony is an elite selected from our active forces, possessing one of the most robust physiques in the army. Now, please give the order."
