Catastrophe of the Golden Stable Airship
[zh.@desc]
帝国引以为傲的金厩号新型飞艇今天迎来了令马意料的结局，它在尝试降落坎特洛特时发生了爆炸事故。
这款飞艇拥有极其豪华的内部和外部装饰，并且加装了帝国研发的蒸汽引擎和各种现代化改装以提高时速。此前一直被帝国官员用于出访帝国各地。
此次事故造成了数十名小马的伤亡，同时帝国正在尝试封锁任何跟这次事故有关的消息。有报道称，这是因为这款飞艇搭在了一些神秘的货物，但是如今已经全部焚毁。
[en.@desc]
The Empire’s pride, the newly commissioned Golden Stable airship, met an unexpected and tragic end today when it exploded while attempting to land in Canterlot.

This airship boasted luxurious interiors and exteriors, equipped with steam engines and various modern modifications developed by the Empire to enhance its speed. Previously, it had been used by imperial officials for visits across the Empire.

The accident resulted in dozens of pony casualties. The Empire is currently attempting to suppress any information related to the incident. Reports suggest that the airship was carrying some mysterious cargo, but all of it has now been completely incinerated.
