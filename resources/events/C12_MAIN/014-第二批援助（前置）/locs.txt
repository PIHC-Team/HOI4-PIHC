如果我们现在转而于苹果鲁萨合作，或许再也没法获取如此多丰厚的资助，但不必再去猜测洛马基的真实企图。当然，先接受这笔物资对采石农场很明显也是更有利的，我们可以之后再去打探洛马基的意图，并且贸然结束合作也肯定会遭到两兄弟的报复。

A variety of equipment manufactured in Las Pegasus is being loaded onto trains, awaiting transport to our farm. While one might occasionally interpret this as charity from Las Pegasus, their commercial nature proves they undoubtedly have ulterior motives. Appleloosa also telegrammed us upon learning of this. The rulers of Las Pegasus, the Flim and Flam brothers, have repeatedly attempted to deceive important members of the Apple family and plotted against the prized apple orchards.

If we were to switch to cooperation with Appleloosa now, perhaps we wouldn’t receive such generous support anymore, but we wouldn’t have to speculate about Las Pegasus’ true intentions. Of course, accepting this aid is clearly more advantageous for the quarry farm. We can investigate Las Pegasus’ intentions later, and abruptly ending the cooperation would undoubtedly incur retaliation from the Flim Flam brothers.
