小萍花敲开了约翰布尔的门，当天莫丝清香暂时返回后，她整理了一份苹果派家族的名单，为了确保任何潜在计划的成功，先只能透露真相给少部分可信的家族成员并尝试得到他们的支持。一切都很好，哥哥姐姐们都对此义愤填膺，不过当小萍花将真相转述给这位亲戚时，她却没有听到她想要的回答：

“去街上走走吧妹妹，很快苹果鲁萨会拥有跟马哈顿一样多的摩天大楼，只需要几年我们就能漫步在跟苹果树林一样的大城市里，我们家族在这里生根发芽，而石青派却真正让这里得以生长，小马们会因我们是苹果派家族的一份子而尊敬我们，我们的生活甚至远比战前还要富足............虽然或许就是她失蹄害死了布雷本，但我相信石青派是为了我们家族着想。”“你只是放不下这虚假的荣华富贵，这一切都是..都是...建立在阴谋和鲜血上的”小萍花急切的反驳道。“不，小萍花妹妹，目光再长远些，如果我们推翻了石青派，当小马们意识到他们一直在被石青派欺骗，他们可能会对我们整个家族反目成仇，我们会失去我们的一切，我们可能会再次让这片大地陷入战乱和分歧，而这一次的导火索则是家族内更加惨烈的蹄足相残，所以，这是你想要的结果吗？”

小萍花沉默不语，他终于意识到这件事并不会如她想象般的那么顺利，无论石青派是否只是在以各种方式满足自己扭曲的欲望，她也确实为俩个家族带来了更美好的生活和普通小马们的追捧，推翻石青派可能会轻而易举的毁掉俩个家族的一切。就算得知了真相，也至少不是所有的家族成员都愿意接受这一点。
 小萍花再次推开了那扇门，走了出去，她叹了口气，要考虑的问题太多了，也许现在还不是时候。石青派已经开始在边境挑兵遣将，所有小马都知道她的企图，当石青派的野心展露无疑时，即便她再怎么宣称要给苹果派家族带来无上荣光，小萍花相信俩个家族都会站在她这一边。

Apple Bloom knocked on John Bull’s door. Earlier that day, after Mossy Scent had temporarily returned, she had compiled a list of members of the Apple Pie family. To ensure the success of any potential plan, the truth could only be revealed to a few trusted relatives at first — to win their understanding and support.

Everything had gone well so far. Her brothers and sisters were furious when they heard the truth. But when Apple Bloom told this particular relative, she didn’t hear the answer she wanted.

“Take a walk outside, sister,” John Bull said calmly. “Soon, Appleloosa will have just as many skyscrapers as Manehattan. In just a few years, we’ll be strolling through a great city rising from the apple fields.

Our family has taken root here — and it’s Limestone Pie who made it grow. Ponies respect us because we’re part of the Apple Pie lineage. Our lives are richer now than they were before the war...

And yes, perhaps it was her misstep that led to Braeburn’s death — but I truly believe Limestone acted for the good of our family.”

Apple Bloom’s voice trembled as she cut him off.

“You’re clinging to a false prosperity! All of this — everything — is built on conspiracy and blood!”

John Bull sighed and shook his head.

“No, Apple Bloom. You must look further ahead.

If we overthrow Limestone Pie, when the ponies realize they’ve been deceived by her all this time, they might turn against our entire family. We could lose everything — and worse, plunge this land back into chaos and division.

And if that happens, the spark of the next war would come not from strangers… but from our own bloodline tearing itself apart.

Is that truly the outcome you want?”

Apple Bloom fell silent.
She finally realized this wouldn’t go as she had imagined.

Whether Limestone Pie was only satisfying her own twisted desires or not, the fact remained — she had brought prosperity to both families and admiration from ordinary ponies. To topple her could easily destroy everything the two families had built.

Even with the truth now known, not every family member would be willing to accept it.

Apple Bloom opened the door again and stepped out. She sighed.
There were too many questions to consider — and perhaps, this simply wasn’t the right time.

Limestone Pie had already begun mustering troops along the border. Everypony knew what she was planning. And when her ambition finally became undeniable, no matter how loudly she claimed to fight for the glory of the Apple family, Apple Bloom believed both families would, at last, stand against her.
