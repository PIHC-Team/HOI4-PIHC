“云母？”他呼唤到，然而，他微弱的嗓音和蒸汽引擎声相比显得微不足道。他把眼睛睁的再大一些，努力朝其他地方看去，当看到那个红色的标志时，他心一寒，验证了他刚才就已有的猜想：他的女儿们不仅没有听从他的指示，反而答应了帝国将他送上了飞机，他不想知道女儿们与帝国究竟商量着的是什么样的协议，但他知道，如果连如此憎恶帝国的石青派都答应了，那么想必也跟他和云母有关。此刻，她和云母都在一个狭小的舱室里，没有看见别的医疗小马。

When Igneous Rock Pie awoke once more, he guessed it was another loud noise that had disturbed him. However, this time, the noise wasn’t the strange beeping of medical equipment but the deafening roar of steam engines. His body felt the same as before; the air in his lungs burned like fire, scorching his entire being. He slowly opened his eyes and looked around. A window above his head reflected the drifting clouds outside onto his wife, Cloudy Quartz, who lay quietly beside him with a gentle smile. Some tubes extended from her body under the blanket into equipment similar to his own.

"Cloudy Quartz?" he called weakly, but his faint voice was drowned out by the sound of the steam engines. He opened his eyes wider and tried to look around. When he saw the red insignia, a chill ran down his spine, confirming his earlier suspicion: his daughters hadn’t followed his instructions. Instead, they had agreed to the Empire’s plan to put him on the plane. He didn’t want to know what kind of agreement his daughters had made with the Empire, but he knew that if even Limestone Pie, who despised the Empire, had agreed, it must have something to do with him and Cloudy Quartz. At this moment, they were both in a small cabin, with no other medical ponies in sight.
