“I… I’m sorry,Sister Limestone … this matter… it’s too serious… I need some time to think about it,” Apple Bloom panted as she spoke.

“It’s alright. You can take your time to think about your future,” Limestone Pie placed a hoof on her shoulder. “There’s no need to rush your answer. For today, just enjoy the banquet. You must know—I’ll always love you…”

Apple Bloom nodded lightly. Limestone Pie kept talking, but her words only drifted faintly past Apple Bloom’s ears.

Maybe… maybe Limestone Pie truly cared for the family?
Maybe Braeburn’s death wasn’t entirely her fault?

Apple Bloom quickly shook her head, forcing those thoughts away. No—those were just Limestone Pie’s smokescreens. Even if she did care deeply for the Apple family and her, and even if she was a good sister in some ways, that could never erase the sins she had committed—the atrocities she inflicted on so many, and the ruthless heart that would stop at nothing to reach her goals.
