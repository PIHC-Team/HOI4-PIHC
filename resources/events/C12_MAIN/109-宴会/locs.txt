When several officers showed up in Apple Bloom’s presence carrying an invitation, she was startled at first. But after learning what it was about, panic began to set in. Limestone Pie had invited her to represent the Apple family at a multi-species friendship banquet. Besides the Apple family, guests would include parrots, buffalo, and ponies from several allied nations.

In the invitation, Limestone Pie wrote that she deeply missed Apple Bloom — she missed the warmth and joy of spending Hearth’s Warming Eve together with her and Applejack in the little house on the rock farm. Ever since their victory over the Empire, she hadn’t had a proper talk with the sister she cared about most.

Apple Bloom wasn’t ready to see Limestone Pie again, but she went anyway — she didn’t want Limestone to suspect anything.
