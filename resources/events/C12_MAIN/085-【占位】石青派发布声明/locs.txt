[en.@desc]
The power struggle at the rock farm had come to an end. The eldest of the Pie sisters, Limestone Pie, replaced her sister Maud Pie, declaring herself the new leader who would guide not only the Pie family but also the Apple family forward. However, due to her extremely volatile and unstable personality, as well as her less-than-stellar track record, outsiders remained concerned about her capabilities and foreign policies, speculating that she might initiate a new round of conflict between the rock farm and the Empire at any moment.

Recently, Limestone Pie issued a public statement, openly declaring that the previous rumors about her parents’ deaths and Maud Pie’s disappearance had no connection with the Empire. This move aimed to demonstrate her loyalty and willingness to cooperate with the Empire, breaking the external stereotype of her.
