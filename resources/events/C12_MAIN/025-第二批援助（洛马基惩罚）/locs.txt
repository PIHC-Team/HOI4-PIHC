“机械学不存在了。”

会面后，洛马基终止了对采石农场的援助协议，撤回了驻扎在采石农场的所有机械维护师。起先，所有小马都以为这只是一次普通的合作谈判失败，直到这变得和每一台采石农场的机器息息相关。

[en.@desc]
“Machinery doesn’t exist.”

After the meeting, Las Pegasus suspended the aid agreement with the rock farm, and withdrew all the mechanical maintenance ponies stationed at the rock farm. At first, all ponies thought this was just a normal failed negotiation, until it became closely related to every machine on the rock farm.

If a mineral refiner, with five rough crystal ores put in, spits out five crystals with higher purity extracted from the ores, that is a normal operation of the machine. But imagine another result: the first time, the machine spits out coal after the crystal ores are put in; the second time, the machine spits out nothing; the third time, the ores fly to the ceiling; the fourth time, the machine spits out a muffin that flies around the room and finally dives into your pocket; the fifth time, the ores fly out at nearly the speed of light, smashing a hole in the machine, piercing the wall, and then flying out of the room. What would you think then?
