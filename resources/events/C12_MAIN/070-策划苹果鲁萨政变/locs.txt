进军苹果鲁萨
[en.@]
Advancing into Appleloosa
[zh.@desc]
当采石农场的边防军得知上级要求他们进驻苹果鲁萨时，没有小马不感到疑惑，然而，他们只需要服从命令就好了。苹果农场的小马便眼睁睁的看着挥舞石青派和采石农场旗帜的士兵队伍浩浩荡荡从他们身边走过。一些巡逻的苹果鲁萨部队很快便与采石农场的军队发生了交火，不过还有更多的苹果鲁萨部队选择保持了沉默和无动于衷，他们没有收到任何上级的反应整个过程没有遇到任何抵抗。
几个小时前，在得到了石青派的指示后，苹果家族内部的主战派迅速行动，她们拿着武器劫持了布雷本和他们的其他一众亲戚，整个过程没有爆发任何流血事件，内部消息人士称，布雷本原本有机会下令反击逮捕肇事者，又或者暂时逃走，但是他不忍与自己的亲戚作对，选择了放弃抵抗。整场事件中，苹果鲁萨的军队没有丝毫介入这场政变，因为许多军队的高层正是这次政变的主事者。
在得知政变成功的消息后，石青派和她的部队以胜利者的姿态进入苹果鲁萨，并对外宣布苹果派俩家族再次重归于好，并将着力于“血债血偿”。
[en.@desc]
When the border guards of the rock farm received orders from their superiors to enter Appleloosa, none of the ponies could hide their confusion. However, their duty was to obey. The Appleloosa ponies watched in astonishment as columns of soldiers, bearing the banners of Limestone Pie and the rock farm, marched past them.

Some patrols from Appleloosa’s own forces soon engaged in skirmishes with the rock farm troops. Yet, many more chose silence and inaction, having received no directives from their higher-ups. Throughout the operation, no significant resistance was encountered.

Hours earlier, following Limestone Pie’s instructions, the pro-war faction within the Apple family acted swiftly. Armed, they detained Braeburn and several other relatives. The operation proceeded without bloodshed. Insider reports suggest that Braeburn had opportunities to order a counterattack or escape but refrained, unwilling to oppose his kin.

Notably, Appleloosa’s military remained uninvolved in the coup, as many of its senior officers were orchestrators of the takeover.

Upon confirmation of the successful coup, Limestone Pie and her forces entered Appleloosa triumphantly, proclaiming the reunion of the Pie and Apple families and committing to a campaign of "blood for blood."
