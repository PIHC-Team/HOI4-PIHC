[en.@desc]
In the beginning, there were sporadic reports from soldiers on the battlefield with the rock farm, claiming to have seen a swift-moving shadow destroying our tanks and camps during the night. However, these reports increased over the course of nearly a month and were no longer confined to the night or the battlefield. Soldiers described it in various ways, and it severely impacted our morale. After systematic analysis by the intelligence department, we can generally conclude that the rock farm has developed a high-performance combat weapon, possessing the size of an ordinary pony and extremely high firepower.
