Floating Among the Clouds
[zh.@desc]
但是当火岩派看到窗外美丽的景色时，一切疼痛忽然如被飘荡的风吹散了。他身处的飞艇正漂浮在云端，太阳温柔的光线以如此接近的距离照射在他的脸庞上，这是他第一次，也估计是最后一次身处在如此之高的地方，在他年轻的时候，他也短暂的羡慕过，希望自己成为一匹天马，从其他小马的口中幻想着云中城的模样。然而，数十年如一日的农场生活早已磨平了他的棱角，他也乐于接受这一点。他苦笑着摇了摇头，心想着，要是云母也能看见这一幕该多好。
很快，他如同下定了某这决心一般，他从床上挣扎着滚下，插进他身体的管子被活生生扯断，让他的身体再次感到一阵剧痛，鲜血从身上流了下来，让他几乎再次昏厥过去，他剧烈咳嗽了起来，同时朝着云母的床爬过去，在地下铺着一层木头的的地毯上爬出了一道道血迹。“云母？”

[en.@desc]
However, when Igneous Rock Pie gazed out at the beautiful scenery beyond the window, all his pain seemed to dissipate, as if carried away by the drifting wind. The airship he was aboard floated amidst the clouds, and the sun’s gentle rays bathed his face from such a close distance. This was his first time—and likely his last—being so high up. In his youth, he had briefly envied the idea of becoming a pegasus, imagining the city in the clouds as described by other ponies. Yet, decades of farm life had softened his edges, and he had come to accept that. He chuckled softly, shaking his head, thinking, "If only Cloudy Quartz could see this."

Soon, as if making a firm decision, he struggled to roll off the bed. The tubes inserted into his body were forcefully ripped out, sending a sharp pain through him. Blood began to flow from his wounds, and he nearly lost consciousness again. He coughed violently while crawling toward Cloudy Quartz’s bed, leaving a trail of blood on the wooden carpet beneath him.

"Cloudy Quartz?"
