Maud pie’s speech
[zh.@desc]
在一个明朗的上午，石灰派走上了临时搭建的演讲台，下面的座位上坐满了来自全自治领的小马代表们，当石灰派清了清嗓子后，全场逐渐安静了下来，石灰派说道：“自治领的小马们，我是石灰派。数年之前的农场仿佛蛇纹石，但现在的我们，已经成为了长石杂砂岩。这正如我和我的顽石相遇，我以前说过这个故事，那是一个风雨交加的黑夜。当时我丝毫不知，生活会就此改变。我们此前不认为自己足够带领你们，我们试图离开，但我们留了下来。石头，是坚固的，这块土地上的小马们，也会是坚固的。一些新的举措将被实施，帮助大家变得更加坚硬。为此，我写了一首诗献给大家。”随后，她开始了以下的诗：

“石头，我亲爱的石头，
沉积岩，我为你沉淀。
有深，有浅，如心，相连，
深邃，沉眠，融合，改变。

石头，我亲爱的石头
石英岩，我们永远团结。”

演讲稿内用复制魔法分发到整个自治领，来照顾那些没有收音机或者缺乏一点必要耐心的小马。其中的一份也被放在了小马利亚帝国秩序部的办公桌上，来分析石灰派的发言里是否隐藏着什么深意。

[en.@desc]
On a bright morning, Maud Pie ascended the makeshift podium. The seats below were filled with representatives from across the autonomous territory. As Maud Pie cleared her throat, the audience gradually quieted down. She began, “Ponies of the autonomous territory, I am Maud Pie. Just a few years ago, our farm was like limestone, but now we have become conglomerate. Just as I met my boulder, I have told this story before, on a stormy night. At that time, I had no idea that life would change forever. We didn’t believe we were capable of leading you; we tried to leave, but we stayed. Just as rocks are strong, the ponies on this land will be strong. Some new initiatives will be implemented to help everyone become even stronger. To this end, I have written a poem for everyone”. She then started the following poem:

“Rocks, these are my rocks.
Sediments make me sedimental.
depth, shallowness, like hearts, intertwined,
Deep, asleep, merging, changing.

Rocks, these are my rocks.
Quartzite, we’re forever united.”

Copies of the speech were distributed throughout the autonomous territory using duplication magic, to cater to those without radios or lacking a bit of patience. One copy was also placed on the desk of the Ministry of Order of the Equestria Empire to analyze whether Maud Pie’s words held any hidden meaning.
