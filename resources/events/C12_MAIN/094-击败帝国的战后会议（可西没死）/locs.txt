“恭喜，帝国的残余势力正在四散而逃，现在我们可以共同重建小马利亚的未来”星光熠熠朝大家挤出一丝微笑着宣布，的确，这确实是值得所有小马庆祝的一件事，不过庆祝狂欢早在半个月前就已经举办了，今天所有领导者汇聚一堂并不是为此。
“但是这一切的罪魁祸首并没有被抓到”石青派冷冷的说着，声音里带着一丝怨恨。星光熠熠无奈的点了点头，“抵抗军的部队正在对整个坎特洛特和周边地区进行地毯式的搜查，但凭借她自身的魔力，我们不得不接受她可能会出现在世界任何一个地方的事实，如果她已经决定将自己藏匿起来，永远隐姓埋名下去，那么将她绳之以法可能会花费很长很长时间”
“我们将让她再也没有复辟的可能性”石青派说道，星光熠熠内心松了口气，看来石青派心中还是有些许“团队感”，她刚才的措辞中至少用的是“我们”，不过很快石青派的话语让星光熠熠后悔这么想，石青派突然加大音量：“我们要彻底粉碎帝国，抹除帝国的一切痕迹，任何曾与帝国勾连过的小马都要接受最严厉的审判，把所有曾经在帝国服役过的家伙都送入牢笼，让他们赎罪到死...”石青派突然被飞火严肃的声音打断：“这么做未免有些过于极端了吧...派家族和苹果家族不也接受了帝国的统治，成为自治领了吗，那么难道你不也是所谓“与帝国勾结的小马””石青派怒目而视着飞火，飞火带着一贯的闪电天马的太阳墨镜，墨镜下是同样犀利的目光，会场瞬间混乱起来，大家互相争吵着
“好了够了，都停下！”  星光熠熠释放出静音魔法，淡蓝色的魔法光晕回荡在整个房间，让这里终于回到了石青派来到之前的状态。她真希望以前用来“对付”崔克西的怒气收集瓶没被她摔碎。静音魔法解除了，石青派刚要开口，她看到余晖烁烁用恳求的眼神朝她轻微摇了摇头，不过她还是说道：“别用你的魔法招呼我，独角兽，你或许以为你很了解我的妹妹们，但我告诉你，我跟我的妹妹们不一样”石青派咬牙切齿的说道,“对帝国的战争已经结束了，抵抗组织同盟已经没有必要存在了，我会按照律法扫除任何帝国的痕迹并且抓住那个可恶的女皇，无论你们愿不愿意奉陪我都不在乎，但是我要警告你们，千万不要把自己挡在那些家伙前面”随后站起身来，径直离开了会场，这再次带来了混乱。
星光熠熠感到有些心酸，石青派只是一个缩影，一个象征，她放不下对帝国的愤怒，有些小马可能永远无法从自终末之战开始的伤痛中走出来。

The starry night sky gently parted a corner of the curtain. Starlight Glimmer anxiously peered outside, her gaze fixed on the chaotic square below. Ponies shouted and waved banners, clashing with soldiers attempting to maintain order. Their anger wasn’t directed at her; all eyes were on the gray figure now entering the building.

"Has she arrived?" Starlight turned to glance at Sunset Shimmer, who nodded solemnly. Starlight sighed and pulled the curtain back into place.

The heavy doors to the hall creaked open, and Starlight held her breath. The doors slammed shut with a resounding thud, and the room fell into an eerie silence. Starlight could hear her own heartbeat.

Limestone Pie sat quietly beside her, observing the gathering with a cold, calculating gaze. Thankfully, Unordered hadn’t arrived yet. Starlight had deliberately sent him away earlier, giving him an excuse to "host his Imperial Destruction Heavy Metal Party" elsewhere. Dealing with Limestone was already enough of a headache.

"Congratulations," Starlight began, forcing a smile. "The remnants of the Empire are scattering. Now we can rebuild Equestria’s future together."

Limestone’s lips curled into a sneer. "But the mastermind is still at large," she retorted coldly, her voice laced with bitterness.

Starlight nodded, her expression weary. "The Resistance is conducting a thorough search of Canterlot and the surrounding areas. But with her magical prowess, she could be anywhere. If she’s chosen to disappear, it could take a long time to find her."

"We will ensure she can never rise again," Limestone declared, her tone unwavering. Starlight felt a brief sense of relief; perhaps Limestone still valued their shared cause. But her next words shattered that hope.

"We will obliterate the Empire, erase every trace of it. Anypony who collaborated with the Empire will face the harshest judgment. Those who served in the Empire’s military will be imprisoned and made to atone until death."

Before Starlight could respond, Fireflash interjected, his voice stern. "That’s extreme. The Pie and Apple families accepted the Empire’s rule and became autonomous regions. Does that make them 'collaborators’ in your eyes?"

Limestone glared at Fireflash, her piercing gaze intensified by the reflection in her sunglasses. The room erupted into heated arguments.

"Enough!" Starlight cast a silencing spell, the soft blue glow calming the room. She wished she hadn’t broken the anger containment jar she once used on Trixie.

As the spell lifted, Limestone opened her mouth to speak. But Sunset Shimmer subtly shook her head, silently urging restraint.

"Don’t use your magic on me, unicorn," Limestone sneered. "You may think you understand my sisters, but I assure you, I'm nothing like them."

"The war against the Empire is over," Limestone continued, her voice cold. "The Resistance Alliance is no longer necessary. I will erase every trace of the Empire and capture that vile queen. Whether you join me or not is irrelevant. But heed this warning: do not stand in my way."

With that, she stood and exited the hall, leaving a trail of tension in her wake.

Starlight sighed, her heart heavy. Limestone was but a reflection of the pain many ponies felt. Some might never recover from the war.
