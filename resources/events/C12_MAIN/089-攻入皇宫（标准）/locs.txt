[zh.@desc]
石青派指挥着士兵对坎特洛特发起新一轮的冲锋，剩余的帝国士兵正在拼死反抗，石青派来不及等待重型武器完全进入战场，她带着一批士兵，身先士卒冲在最前面。正当她身边的一个冲锋队比她先赶到下一个街道口时，身体上却瞬间迸发出一滩血雾，倒在地上抽搐不止，那有个隐蔽的火力输出点。没死透的士兵在地上发出阵阵哀嚎，惊恐的看着石青派。石青派面无表情，朝着身边的另一个连队做了个蹄势，让他们立刻填补战场上的空缺。她不在乎自己的生命，也不在乎其他士兵的。
当天色已晚，坎特洛特只剩皇宫没被占领，各个部队将这里团团围住，，反魔法屏障被打开，虽然这对天角兽意义不是很明显，但至少能给士兵带来些心理安慰。石灰派抑制不住自己冲动而急迫的情绪，她此刻就想要亲自把那个该死的天角兽剁成肉泥，但是她明白自己不是她的对手
[en.@desc]
Limestone Pie led the charge against Canterlot, commanding her troops in a renewed assault. The remaining Imperial soldiers fought desperately, but Limestone couldn’t afford to wait for the heavy artillery to arrive. She led a contingent of soldiers, charging at the forefront.

As one of her squads reached the next intersection ahead of her, a sudden burst of blood erupted from a hidden sniper’s shot, and the soldier collapsed, convulsing on the ground. The concealed firing point had been well-placed. The wounded soldier’s anguished cries filled the air as he looked at Limestone with terror. Unfazed, Limestone signaled to a nearby unit with a hoof gesture, ordering them to fill the gap left on the battlefield. She cared little for her own life or that of her soldiers.

By nightfall, only the royal palace in Canterlot remained unconquered. Various units surrounded it completely. The anti-magic barrier was activated; while it had little effect on alicorns, it provided some psychological comfort to the troops. Maud Pie struggled to suppress her impulsive and urgent emotions. She wanted nothing more than to personally reduce that cursed alicorn to a pulp, but she knew she was no match for her.
