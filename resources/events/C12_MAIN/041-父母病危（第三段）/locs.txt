Marble Pie stumbled out of the hospital room, her steps unsteady. A wave of dizziness washed over her, and her body still trembled slightly from the sobs that had wracked her moments ago. She brought her forehooves together in a silent plea, yet she didn’t know to whom she was praying. Tears welled up once more, slipping down her cheeks.

Suddenly, she recalled the argument between Mudbriar and the doctor. If what the doctor had said was true, then sacrificing the interests of the rock farm to restore Igneous Rock and Cloudy Quartz to health would be worth it. Moreover, it might provide leverage in negotiations with the Empire. With this thought, Marble Pie nodded shakily. She took a deep breath and sprinted towards the rock farm’s town hall. Her speed and determined expression startled the ponies she passed, who quickly stepped aside.

Upon reaching the telegraph office, she dismissed the other ponies present. Then, she quietly sent a telegram to both the Empire and Las Pegasus.
