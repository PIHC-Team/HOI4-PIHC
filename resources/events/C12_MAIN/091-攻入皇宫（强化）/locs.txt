采石农场的军队对坎特洛特发起新一轮的冲锋，大部分士兵已经投降了，剩余的帝国士兵正在拼死反抗，重型武器还没完全进入战场，石青派身穿铸岩者的重型装甲，带着一批士兵，身先士卒冲在最前面。正当她身边的一个冲锋队比她先赶到下一个街道口时，身体上却瞬间迸发出一滩血雾，倒在地上抽搐不止，那有个隐蔽的火力输出点。没死透的士兵在地上发出阵阵哀嚎，惊恐的看着石青派。石青派面无表情看着这些士兵，朝着身边的另一个连队做了个蹄势，让他们立刻填补战场上的空缺。看来，围攻还要持续很久，她无法接受普通部队的推进速度，每多一分钟被浪费，可西逃走的可能性就越大。
石青派再也抑制不住自己冲动而急迫的情绪，她此刻就想要亲自把那个该死的天角兽剁成肉泥。已经来不及等待大部队了，她立刻召集一队精英的铸岩者紧跟在她的身后，然后他们冒着枪林弹雨，在各个战场上穿梭，不由分说的直奔皇宫而去。
[en.@desc]
The quarry farm’s army launched a new assault on Canterlot. Most of the soldiers had already surrendered, but the remaining Imperial troops were fiercely resisting. Heavy weaponry had yet to fully enter the battlefield. Limestone Pie, clad in Rockbreaker heavy armor, led a squad of soldiers at the forefront.

As one of her vanguard units reached the next intersection ahead of her, a sudden burst of blood erupted from the soldier’s body, causing them to collapse and convulse on the ground. A concealed firing point had been lying in wait. The wounded soldier emitted agonized wails, eyes filled with terror as they looked toward Limestone Pie. She remained expressionless, signaling to another platoon nearby to immediately fill the gap on the battlefield.

It was evident that the siege would persist for some time. Limestone Pie couldn’t tolerate the sluggish pace of the regular troops; every wasted minute increased the likelihood of Cozy Glow’s escape.

Unable to suppress her impulsive and urgent emotions any longer, she yearned to personally reduce that accursed alicorn to minced meat. There was no time to wait for the main force. She promptly assembled a team of elite Rockbreakers to follow her lead. Braving a hail of bullets, they weaved through various battlefields, heading straight for the royal palace without hesitation.
