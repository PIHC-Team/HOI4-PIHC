木质化石展览
[en.@]
Wooden Fossil Exhibition
[zh.@desc]
这个阳光明媚的早上，全自治领的地区代表和前来凑热闹的小马们都聚集在了采石农场的展会广场，为了搭建展览设施和运送珍贵的化石，石灰派和泥枝已经为此准备了一个多月。泥枝站在一处高台上，开始一板一眼的挨个介绍今天所要展出的物品。与此同时，害羞的玉琪派躲藏了起来，而石青派谨慎的站在小马中央，将警惕的目光投向任何行为可疑的小马。在长达一个小时的漫长介绍后，泥枝终于宣布展览会开始，昏昏欲睡的小马们突然抖擞起精神，开始热烈的鼓掌，陆续进场参观这些他们看不懂的化石。在展览结束后，还有一个小马民乐团进行演奏石灰派新写的“石头之歌”。
不管是不是真心的，在场的大多数小马仍然给了这场展览不错的评价，当然也许更多的是展会上无限量供应的苹果酒的功劳。
[en.@desc]
On this sunny morning, representatives from across the autonomy and curious onlookers gathered at the exhibition square of the farm. Maud Pie and Mudbriar had spent over a month preparing for this, setting up exhibition facilities and transporting precious fossils. Mudbriar stood on a platform, meticulously introducing each item to be displayed. Meanwhile, the shy Marble Pie had hidden herself away, while the cautious Limestone Pie stood amidst the crowd, casting vigilant glances at any pony exhibiting suspicious behavior. After a long hour of introductions, Mudbriar finally declared the exhibition open. The sleepy ponies suddenly perked up, bursting into enthusiastic applause, and began to enter the exhibition hall to view these fossils they couldn’t understand.
Following the conclusion of the exhibition, a small pony folk band performed the new song “Rock Anthem” written by Maud Pie. Whether genuine or not, most ponies present still gave the exhibition favorable reviews, though perhaps more credit was due to the unlimited supply of apple cider available at the event.
