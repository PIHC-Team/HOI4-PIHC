今天是采石农场沉重的一天，在毫无预兆下，我们的洛克维尔地区发生了6.8级地震，并在周边地区引发了山洪，许多老旧的房屋倒塌，而一些矿工现在还被掩埋在矿洞深处等待着救援，当地政府正在制定救援计划，一些居民正在自发的互相帮助，苹果鲁萨等周边地区也向我们派来了救援队协助我们。洛马基的一些缺德的评论员嘲笑我们这是我们挖掘矿石应得的报应，但我们暂时顾不上回骂他们，小马利亚帝国暂时并未对此事做出评价
[en.@desc]
Today has been a somber day at the rock farm. Without warning, our Rockville region experienced a 6.8 magnitude earthquake, triggering flash floods in the surrounding areas. Many old houses collapsed, and some miners are still trapped deep in the mines awaiting rescue. The local government is formulating rescue plans, while residents are spontaneously helping each other. Nearby areas like Appleloosa have also sent rescue teams to assist us. Some unscrupulous commentators in Las Pegasus mock us, saying that our mining activities are just desserts for us. However, we currently cannot afford to retort to them.As of now, the Equestria Empire has not issued any statement regarding the incident.
