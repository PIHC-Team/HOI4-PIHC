Limestone Pie received the latest word from the front: the Buffalo Tribe had refused to submit and was prepared to fight to the death. She sat back in her chair, closed her eyes, and rubbed her face with her hoof to ease her anxiety. She couldn’t understand why the Buffalo, knowing they were outmatched, refused to yield—especially when all she’d wanted was to resolve the conflict on her own terms and prove her convictions. Did she really have to go to war against them, even if it meant countless casualties and leaving scores of buffalo displaced?

“Sister Limestone , this isn’t the real you. If you go through with this, how are you any different from that fellow in Canterlot?” the Appleloosa envoy whispered.

“Shut up! You have no say in this!” Limestone Pie could not contain her anger and snapped at the poor filly. Almost immediately, regret welled up—she shouldn’t have been so harsh. But there was no turning back now: her army awaiting her final orders at the front could not be held in check. If she did not subdue the Buffalo Tribe swiftly, she would lose any claim on the local minerals and forego the chance to launch an unexpected strike on the Empire from Cherry Hills.

After a moment’s thought, Limestone Pie softened. She offered the filly a gentle apology and then spoke with firm resolve:
“To defeat the Empire, the Buffalo left me no choice—it’s their own fault.”
