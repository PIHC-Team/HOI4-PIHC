当帝国的巨型飞艇出现在采石农场的上空，红色的帝国女王旗飘扬在每一匹小马的头顶并投下阴影，任何小马哪怕不需要抬头都会震撼于帝国的威严并回想起采石农场仍然只是一个自治领而已。可西光辉会亲自到访吗，为什么是偏偏这个时候？石青派有些心烦意乱，但时间已经不允许她再进一步思考，飞艇已经在奠基顽石所在的广场缓缓下降。
一条红毯铺了下来，当清晨桂冠和双重美味从上面缓缓走下时，俩侧的采石农场仪仗队向他们致敬。石灰派脸上看不见任何表情，那些敬礼的士兵同样也是如此，大家都知道是在走流程而已，彼此心照不宣，好在，至少那些仪仗队端着的枪里此刻装着的不是真枪实弹。

“我原本以为这是一次秘密访问”双重美味对着清晨桂冠小声说道，声音中透露着一丝难堪。“只有这种形式才能让这块土地上的小马明白谁才是他们真正的主宰者”清晨桂冠提醒道，然后继续补充“不过，我们的任务仍然没变。”双重美味点了点头，走上前，开始跟石灰派说着客套话，随着欢迎仪式的结束，一行马步入了采石农场的私密会客厅。

Maud Pie felt a flutter of apprehension as the Empire’s colossal airship appeared over the rock farm, its shadow sweeping across every pony below. The red banner of the Imperial Empress fluttered atop the vessel, and even those who dared not look up were struck by the Empire’s majesty—and reminded that the rock farm remained merely an autonomous territory. Would Cozy Glow herself pay a personal visit? Why now of all times? Limestone Pie’s thoughts were in turmoil, but there was no time to ponder further: the airship was already descending slowly above the plaza where Holder’s Boulder stood.

A crimson carpet unrolled from the ship’s hatch, and when Dawn Crown and Double Delight swept down its length, the rock farm honor guard on either side presented arms in salute. Maud Pie’s face remained impassive, and the saluting soldiers mirrored her composure—everyone knew it was mere formality, an unspoken understanding. Thankfully, at least the rifles in their hands were loaded only with blanks.

“I thought this was supposed to be a secret visit,” Double Delight murmured to Dawn Crown, a note of awkwardness in her voice.
“Only a show like this will remind these ponies who truly rules them,” Dawn Crown replied, then added softly, “But our mission remains unchanged.”

Double Delight nodded and stepped forward to offer Maud Pie the expected pleasantries. As the welcoming ceremony concluded, the imperial party and their entourage made their way into the rock farm’s private reception hall.
