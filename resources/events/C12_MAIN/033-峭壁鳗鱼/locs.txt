Quarray eel
[zh.@desc]
随着地质学家和科学家夜以继日的研究，事实的真相另所有小马都感到十分新奇。原来在麦金托什山的深处生活着大量峭壁鳗鱼，这种神秘的肉食性鳗鱼因其在天堑峡谷一带的分布而被地理学者们所了解和命名。他们在地下和岩层中伺机而动来捕猎倒霉的鸟类或者动物。但是这批麦金托什山的鳗鱼比他们的其他同类要更大。和生活在海中的鳗鱼不同，通常峭壁鳗鱼可以生活于陆地和地底且可以直接呼吸空气，而麦金托什山的峭壁鳗鱼甚至可以在岩石中穿行。
虽然一些科学家仍然对此感到好奇，但很明显峭壁鳗鱼的感官很容易受到雷达波的影响，当洛克维尔附近的雷达站建成后，大量的峭壁鳗鱼对此感到躁动不安，并集体迁徙，破坏了洛克维尔下面沉重的地基岩石并最终引发了地下崩塌和地震。军方对此表示出了强烈的兴趣，如果我们可以使用恰当的电波，我们就有机会驯服这些凶猛的掠食者，并让它们为我们的军队而战，从敌军意想不到的地方发动猛攻。
[en.@desc]
As geologists and scientists worked tirelessly day and night, the truth of the matter left all ponies greatly intrigued. It turned out that deep within the Macintosh Mountains lived a vast population of quarray eels. This mysterious carnivorous eel, known and named by geographers for its distribution in the Ghastly Gorge. They lurked in the underground and rock layers, waiting for the opportunity to hunt down unfortunate birds or animals. However, the Macintosh Mountain quarray eels were larger than their other kin. Unlike eels living in the sea, quarray eels usually live on land and underground and can breathe air directly. The Macintosh Mountain quarray eels could even move through rocks.
While some scientists remained curious about this phenomenon, it became apparent that the sensory organs of quarray eels were easily affected by radar waves. When the radar station near Rockville was completed, a large number of quarray eels became restless and collectively migrated, damaging the heavy bedrock beneath Rockville and eventually causing underground collapses and earthquakes. The military expressed strong interest in this, as if we could use the appropriate radio waves, we would have the opportunity to tame these fierce predators and make them fight for our army, launching a fierce attack from unexpected places on the enemy.
