布雷本的葬礼
[en.@]
Braeburn’s Funeral
[zh.@desc]
当布雷本静静的躺在灵柩里时，周围的苹果家族小马无不陷入了深深的绝望，苹果嘉儿、麦托什和糖蓓儿甚至还有小萍花，现在又轮到布雷本，苹果家族在这些年遭受了太多不该承受的意外和苦难。石青派和派家族的小马也被邀请参加了葬礼，然而，出乎所有小马意料的是，当所有小马都身穿深黑色衣服，石青派却选择身穿一身军装，不过，她这一举动并没有得到大多数小马的反对。在布雷本最亲的几个兄弟姐妹泪流满面的哭诉着对布雷本的追忆，在得到几名苹果家族小马的示意后，她快步走向前台发表演说:
"苹果派家族的小马都知道，在过去，布雷本与我一直有着深刻的分歧，他指责我，不该一心想着为家族寻仇，说我被仇恨驱使着向前而看不清方向，自我们俩家族的会议以来，他便一直挑动着俩个家族的对立。然而，我想说的是，我一点也不怪他，他说的不乏有一番道理，他希望，苹果家族能在这个混乱的时代能像暴雨中的苹果树一样，继续开花结果。但我也知道，在他的内心深处，他也支持着我之前的所作所为..."
[en.@desc]
As Braeburn lay quietly in his casket, the surrounding Apple family ponies were engulfed in profound despair. Applejack, Big McIntosh, Sugar Belle, and even Apple Bloom—now it was Braeburn’s turn. Over the years, the Apple family had endured far too many unexpected tragedies and hardships. Limestone Pie and other members of the Pie family were also invited to the funeral. However, to everyone’s surprise, while all the ponies wore deep black attire, Limestone Pie chose to don a military uniform. Yet, this action did not meet with much opposition from most ponies.

As Braeburn’s closest siblings tearfully recounted their memories of him, and after receiving a signal from a few Apple family members, Limestone Pie stepped forward to deliver a speech:

"All the ponies of the Apple and Pie families know that in the past, Braeburn and I had deep disagreements. He accused me of being consumed by a desire for vengeance for our family, saying that I was driven by hatred and couldn’t see the right path forward. Since our families’ meetings, he had been stirring up opposition between the two clans. However, I want to say that I don’t blame him at all. There was some truth in his words. He hoped that the Apple family could continue to blossom and bear fruit in this chaotic era, like an apple tree in a storm. But I also know that deep down, he supported my previous actions..."
