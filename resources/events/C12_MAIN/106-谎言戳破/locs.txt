Apple Bloom held her head in pain. Outside the window, the flag of Greater Rockville fluttered silently in the wind.

Mossy Scent wiped away her unstoppable tears, her mouth slightly open, wanting to say something comforting — but no words would come. In truth, she was the one who needed comfort the most.

Moments earlier, she had confessed everything — how she met Limestone Pie, and how Limestone had asked her to assassinate Braeburn. She had thought she would keep that secret buried forever. Or perhaps that revealing it might make her feel lighter. But neither happened.

Now, everything had gone far beyond what she could have imagined. As the one who set all of this in motion, she knew she had to bear the responsibility.

Limestone Pie, in the name of both the Apple family and the Pie family, had turned her wrath toward one nation after another. Peace, like an apple hanging from a tree, was always just within reach — yet forever unreachable.

She had changed. And so had Appleloosa, and the ponies working the stony soil of the quarry farm.

At Braeburn’s public funeral not long ago, Limestone Pie had stood before the crowd, falsely recounting how her deep and unwavering bond with him was destroyed by the Empire. Below the stage, countless ponies shouted her name again and again. The scene made Mossy Scent sick to her stomach — and terrified.

She was grateful, at least, that she was still alive. That she hadn’t been thrown into some lightless cell by Rockville’s Bureau of Public Affairs under a fabricated charge. Perhaps it meant Limestone Pie still trusted her — that she believed Mossy Scent would never betray her. Or maybe… Limestone truly saw her as family.

“She betrayed two families,” Apple Bloom whispered hoarsely. “She doesn’t deserve to be my sister anymore.”

She looked at Mossy Scent with those sorrowful, wide eyes.
“This isn’t your fault. At least… not yours alone.”

“No,” Mossy Scent said, trembling all over. “I know what I did. And it was that one thought — that single choice — that brought all of this to life.”
