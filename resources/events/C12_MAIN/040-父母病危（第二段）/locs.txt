[en.@desc]
When Marble Pie arrived, she found Igneous Rock and Cloudy Quartz lying unconscious on separate hospital beds. The room was sparsely populated, with her sister Maud Pie and Mudbriar engaged in a tense conversation with a pony dressed as a doctor.

"If you're truly the best doctor the rock farm has," Mudbriar argued, "then you should be able to find a solution." He added curtly, "We can’t let this information leak. The Empire and Las Pegasus would seize any opportunity to exploit this situation."

The doctor responded with concern, "This appears to be a rare, acute illness. Given the current medical facilities on the farm, I'm not optimistic..."

Mudbriar shook his head, interrupting, "I don’t see that as an option. If Igneous Rock and Cloudy Quartz were conscious, they wouldn’t agree to that either."

Maud Pie remained silent, leaning against the bedside. Marble Pie had never seen her sister so sorrowful.

Marble slowly approached and leaned beside Maud. Though she had told herself to stay strong since the Last Roundup, tears streamed down her face uncontrollably. Maud embraced Marble, closed her eyes, and tears fell onto Cloudy Quartz’s hoof.

Cloudy Quartz opened her eyes weakly and said to Marble, "Maudileena... Marble... my sweethearts, I will always love you. It’s time... for your father and me to depart."
