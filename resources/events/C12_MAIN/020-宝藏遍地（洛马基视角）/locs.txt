Rock Farm Seeks Cooperation
[zh.@desc]
我们的邻国采石农场一直被认为是一块贫瘠的土地，许多小马还在温饱线上挣扎。但是前不久，他们的领导马石灰派组织了大型的针对整个自治领的地质勘探活动。许多珍贵的矿产被他们开发出来，但他们的能力不足以很好的利用它们。为了能够发展经济，采石农场居然向我们发出了优先合作的邀请，邀请我们来到他们的土地上。如果我们可以想到合适的办法，我们完全可以借此机会把他们的资产和财富据为己有，划入我们的势力范围。

嘿！我们其实一直在构思着一种新的发明... 你说，如果我们在我们的机器上安装特制的关键零件，它们会在到达一定期限后自动报废，此时如果再想使用那些机器，就只能依靠我们进行更换。那时，价格可就由不得他们说话了...
我们可以据此制定一个绝妙的方案！当我们的机器遍布采石农场大地时，派家族将无法拒绝我们的任何要求，除非他们想要采石农场的经济彻底崩溃。
[en.@desc]
Our neighboring rock farm has long been considered barren land, with many ponies struggling to make ends meet. However, not long ago, their leader, Maud Pie, organized a large-scale geological exploration activity for the entire autonomous territory. Many valuable minerals were discovered, but their capacity to utilize them effectively is lacking. In order to develop their economy, the rock farm has surprisingly extended a priority cooperation invitation to us, inviting us to their land. If we can come up with the right approach, we could seize this opportunity to claim their assets and wealth as our own, incorporating them into our sphere of influence.

Hey there! We’ve actually been brainstorming a new invention... What if you say, we install special key components in our machines that will automatically expire after a certain period of time? If they want to continue using those machines, they’ll have to rely on us for replacements. At that point, the price will be entirely up to us...
We can use this to devise a brilliant plan! When our machines are spread across the rock farm’s land, the Pie family won’t be able to refuse any of our requests unless they want the rock farm’s economy to collapse completely.
