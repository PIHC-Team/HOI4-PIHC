[en.@desc]
Overnight, many of our factories’ machines ceased operation. Our industrial sector immediately organized engineers to investigate. Unexpectedly, Amusement Factory and some facilities constructed entirely by ourselves remained operational. All the malfunctioning machines had originated from Las Pegasus or had excessively utilized blueprints and parts from Las Pegasus. An urgent telegram was dispatched to Las Pegasus demanding an explanation, but all we received in return was mockery from the Flim and Flam brothers and a request for a face-to-face meeting. It was clear that they had orchestrated this major incident. Despite warnings from Appleloosa about the twins’ profit-driven nature, our rapid economic growth had caused us to relax our vigilance. Now, we were forced to confront difficult choices.
