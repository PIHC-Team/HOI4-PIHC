首先是我们的亲戚，苹果家族控制的地区苹果鲁萨，在过去的岁月里，我们与他们建立了深厚的情谊并一同度过了许多暖炉夜。但是沉重的战争撕碎了整个世界，也撕碎了苹果家族：大麦、苹果嘉儿等重要的家族成员下落不明，苹果鲁萨处于衰退当中尚未完全恢复。但即便如此，如果我们需要，相信它们一定会提供力所能及的帮助。
另一个选择是油嘴滑舌两兄弟控制的新兴地区洛马基，在这俩位毁誉参半的发明家和资本家的推动后，洛马基成为了整个大陆的科技中心和经济中心。只有名流谷等少数地区能像洛马基一样富丽堂皇，但这背后是两兄弟贪得无厌的利益索取。如果我们向他们寻求合作，他们肯定会同意，并给予我们丰厚得多的条件，但他俩一定也会对我们另有所求。
[en.@desc]
The rich mineral deposits recently discovered at the rock farm have attracted attention from various factions. However, due to our lack of facilities and funds, we may not be able to utilize them effectively for a long time, unless we choose to cooperate with other countries. Currently, we have two main options before us:
First is the region of Appleloosa, controlled by our relatives, the Apple family. Over the years, we have built a deep friendship with them and spent many Hearth’s Warming Eves together. However, the heavy wars have torn apart the entire world and the Apple family as well: important family members such as Big Mac and Applejack have gone missing, leaving Appleloosa in a state of decline that has yet to fully recover. Nevertheless, if we need help, we believe they will provide enthusiastic assistance.
The other option is the emerging region of Las Pegasus, controlled by the Flim and Flam brothers. With the push of these two inventors and capitalists, who have quite a mixed reputation, Las Pegasus has become the technological and economic center of the entire continent. Only a few regions like Sire’s Hollow can rival Las Pegasus in grandeur, but behind this is the brothers’ insatiable greed. If we seek cooperation with them, they will certainly agree and offer us much more favorable conditions, but they will also have other demands of us.
