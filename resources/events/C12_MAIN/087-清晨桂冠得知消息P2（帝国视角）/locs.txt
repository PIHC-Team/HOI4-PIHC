“不好了，陛下，飞艇失火了！”，可西听到这话一惊，赶紧从位子上飞下来“什么飞艇？”她用稚嫩的声音焦急的问道，实际上比任何小马都清楚，只是抱着一丝希望而已。“您派往采石农场的飞艇”那个灰头土脸的独角兽回复道，可西光辉随机念动咒语，一阵强烈的魔法辉光闪过，她瞬间来到了事故现场。场面一片荒凉，飞艇几分钟前已经摔在了地面上，周围散落着各种碎块，不时能听到飞艇内部传来了爆炸声。一些小马互相搀扶着从里面尖叫着爬出来。
“清晨桂冠她们呢”可西对着逃出来的小马赶紧问道，他们惊恐的摇摇头，表示没看到她们从里面逃出来。可西光辉也不再等了，她闭上眼睛，耀眼的光芒从她的角上迸发出来，头发开始在空气中漂浮，仿佛失去了引力限制，魔法的光晕瞬间包裹住整个接近几百米长的飞艇，在场的小马无不感到震撼，他们中的许多立刻跪倒在地，大气也不敢喘。光晕内部，火焰肉眼可见的小了起来，不绝于耳的爆炸声也停息了。很快，可西缓缓睁开眼睛，与赶到的坎特洛特救火队一起冲进事故现场寻找幸存者。她找到了清晨桂冠，此刻正躺在一个房间内，浑身都是烟尘，可西将她救了出来。
很快，事故调查只能给出推测是其降落系统或加装引擎故障导致的，因为一切发生的太快了。类似的故障这在这款新型号的飞艇上并不少见，但是从未发生如此严重的事故过。最令可西紧张不安的是，火岩派和云母都已经确认死于了这起事故中，这远远超出她的预料之外。

[en.@desc]
"Your Majesty, the airship is on fire!" Upon hearing this alarming news, Cozy Glow was startled and quickly flew down from her seat. "Which airship?" she asked anxiously in her youthful voice, though she already knew the answer all too well, clinging to a sliver of hope.

"The one you dispatched to the rock farm," replied the soot-covered unicorn.

Without hesitation, Cozy Glow cast a teleportation spell. A burst of intense magical light enveloped her, and in an instant, she arrived at the scene of the accident.The area was desolate. The airship had crashed minutes earlier, debris scattered everywhere. Occasional explosions erupted from within the wreckage. Some ponies, supporting each other, screamed as they crawled out from the wreckage.

"Where are Dawn Crown and the others?" Cozy Glow urgently inquired of the escaping ponies. They shook their heads in terror, indicating they hadn’t seen them escape.

Without delay, Cozy Glow closed her eyes. A dazzling light burst from her horn, causing her mane to float as if defying gravity. A magical aura rapidly enveloped the entire, nearly hundred-meter-long airship. Everypony present was awestruck; many immediately knelt, barely daring to breathe.Within the aura, the flames visibly diminished, and the relentless explosions ceased. Soon, Cozy Glow slowly opened her eyes and, alongside the arriving Canterlot fire brigade, rushed into the wreckage to search for survivors.She found Dawn Crown lying in a room, covered in soot. Cozy Glow rescued her.The subsequent investigation could only speculate that the crash resulted from a malfunction in the landing system or the newly installed engine, as everything happened too quickly. Similar malfunctions weren’t uncommon in this new airship model, but such a severe accident had never occurred before.

What unsettled Cozy Glow the most was the confirmation that Igneous Rock Pie and Cloudy Quartz had perished in the accident—an outcome far beyond her expectations.
