[en.@]
Limestone Pie’s Claim on the Buffalo Tribe
[zh.@desc]
石青派自从上次家族会议后便开始疯狂的扩军备战，为了在战场上正面击败帝国，她愿意付出任何代价，哪怕是我们。近日，我们驻采石农场的苹果家族成员代表通过电报告诉了我们一条可怕的消息，石青派为了检验她的武装力量，同时打通与帝国的第二战线，决定把矛头指向与我们素来有纠纷的野牛家族，逼迫野牛家族彻底臣服于她。虽然我们与野牛家族经常爆发一些冲突，但我们从没有像石青派那样丧失了理智。同时，所有小马都知道，如果这次石青派获得了她想要的结果，那么她非常有可能继续这么做下去，直到有一天类似的事情发生在我们身上，如果我们被她认为挡住了她的路的话。
[en.@desc]
Since the last family council, Limestone Pie has been feverishly expanding her forces and preparing for war. Determined to defeat the Empire outright on the battlefield, she is willing to pay any price—even if it means sacrificing us.

Recently, our Apple family envoy at the rock farm sent us a terrifying dispatch: in order to test her armed strength and to open a second front against the Empire, Limestone Pie has decided to turn her attention to the Buffalo Tribe—long-standing rivals of ours—and force them into total subjugation. Although we and the Buffalo Tribe have frequently clashed, we have never lost our reason as Limestone Pie has.

Everypony knows that if Limestone Pie succeeds this time in imposing her will, she is highly likely to repeat the same tactic again—until one day it may be used against us, should we stand in her way.
