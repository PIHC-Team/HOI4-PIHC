云中城协议
[en.@]
Cloudsdale Agreement
[zh.@desc]
一支来自采石农场的代表神色疲倦的出现在了呆蹄面前。
为了能登上高空中的云中城，这名一辈子没怎么仰望过天空的陆马特使差点把自己的命都吓掉了。
呆蹄并不知道采石农场在哪里，但她从这名陆马的嘴中知道只要在这张纸上签上自己的名字后，就能吃到来自采石农场的特制石头马芬。同时协议也已经经过了下级官员的审批，不会有什么让云中城吃亏的问题。
然而，呆蹄一反常态的表示了对石头马芬的不感兴趣，虽然下级官员们都想促成这份协议的达成，但如果没有呆蹄批准，这件事看来要继续搁置下去了。
本来特使和官员们还想劝说，但是午餐时间到了。所有小马都知道，没有什么比呆蹄公爵的午餐时间更重要的事情了。
[en.@desc]
A representative from the rock farm appeared in front of Derpy Hooves.
To be able to ascend to Cloudsdale in the sky, this earth pony envoy, who had never looked up at the sky in his life, almost scared himself to death.
Derpy didn’t know where the rock farm was, but she learned from the envoy that as long as she signed her name on this paper, she could eat the special stone muffins from the rock farm. At the same time, the agreement has been approved by the lower officials, and there will be no loss to Cloudsdale.
However, Derpy Hooves, in an unusual turn of events, expressed no interest in the stone muffins. Although the lower officials wanted to facilitate the conclusion of this agreement, if Derpy did not approve it, it seemed that the matter would have to be shelved.
Originally, the envoy and the officials wanted to persuade her, but it was lunchtime. Everypony knows that there is nothing more important than Duchess Derpy’s lunchtime.
