[en.@desc]
According to the secret instructions from the Empress Cozy Glow, the loyalty of Duchess Trixie Lulamoon is questionable. Therefore, the Ministry of Order has never given up searching for potential evidence that Trixie is disloyal to the Empire. Evidences indicate that Trixie may still be in contact with the Starlight Rebels, but the means of contact is unknown.
After her appointment as Duchess, Trixie rarely wears her magic hat anymore, but her magic hat is still preserved at home. Although this can be regarded as a symbol that she cannot completely cut off from the past, it is not enough to be substantial evidence. We will continue to investigate.
