Meeting of the Families
[zh.@desc]
终于，雷蹄酋长代表野牛家族前来与苹果鲁萨进行会谈。雷蹄酋长首先祝贺了苹果鲁萨家族聚会的胜利召开，并代表野牛部落赠送了纪念品，然而，他在笑脸相迎后不就便话锋一转。
雷蹄酋长向“表哥”布雷本表示，野牛部落已经不再满足于原先商定的苹果派供给数量了，并且暗示小马谷和其他家族已经给野牛部落开出了更好的条件：更多的苹果派加工厂为野牛部落免费提供苹果派。
因此，野牛与苹果家族之间的合作关系，就全部依仗于苹果家族可以拿出多少“诚意”了。
[en.@desc]
Finally, Chief Thunderhooves, representing the Buffalo Family, has come to negotiate with Appleloosa. He first congratulated the Appleloosa family on the successful reunion and presented a gift on behalf of the Buffalo Tribe. However, he quickly changed the subject after the warm welcome.
Chief Thunderhooves told “cousin” Braeburn that the Buffalo Tribe is no longer satisfied with the agreed supply of apple pies and hinted that the Buffalo Tribe has been offered better terms by other families in Appleloosa: more apple pie factories providing free pies for the Buffalo Tribe.
Therefore, the cooperation between the Buffalo and Apple families depends entirely on how much “sincerity” the Apple family can show.
