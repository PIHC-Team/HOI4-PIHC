[zh.@desc]
昨日，小马利亚帝国与苹果鲁萨自治区的陨铁冲突，以帝国的胜利而告终。帝国方面与自治区领导者“表哥”布雷本签订了一份协议，帝国将获得苹果鲁萨的驻军权力。
这场陨铁冲突起源于一段时间前，小马利亚帝国国家安全部称，一颗陨铁坠落在苹果鲁萨和采石农场交界处，切断了小马国中部的铁路运输大动脉。
苹果鲁萨方面怀疑是这一场蓄意破坏，计划前往目标地点进行调查，但是帝国国家安全部立刻向该地区派遣了特别行动小队，女皇以“保护具有魔法研究价值陨铁”为理由命令苹果鲁萨的调查团不得靠近。对于苹果鲁萨质疑“帝国科学与魔法部天文台为何没有陨铁观测记录”的问题，帝国科学与魔法部尚未对此做出回应，并以“科学研究安全”为由拒绝公开观测记录。
随后，苹果鲁萨爆发了大规模抗议，苹果鲁萨也强行派军队进入目标地点，随即与帝国的特别行动小队产生交火。
如今冲突已经结束，但是我们依然未能得知事件的真相：是否真的存在陨铁？
[en.@desc]
Yesterday, the Equestria Empire and the Appleloosa Autonomous Region ended the meteorite conflict with the victory of the Empire. The Empire signed an agreement with the leader of the autonomous region, “Cousin” Braeburn, and the Empire will gain the right to station troops in Appleloosa.
This Meteorite Conflict started when Equestria Empire Ministry of National Security claimed that a meteorite fell at the border of Appleloosa and Rock Farm, cutting off the central railway transportation artery of Equestria.
Appleloosa suspected that this was a deliberate sabotage and planned to go to the target location for investigation, but the Ministry of National Security immediately dispatched a special action team to the area. The Empress ordered the investigation team of Appleloosa not to approach the area on the grounds of “protecting meteorites with magical research value”. The Ministry of Science and Magic has not responded to Appleloosa’s question of “why the Imperial Observatory of the Ministry of Science and Magic did not have meteorite observation records”, and refused to disclose the observation records on the grounds of “scientific research safety”.
Subsequently, Appleloosa erupted in large-scale protests, and Appleloosa forcibly sent troops to the target location, immediately engaging in a firefight with the Empire’s special action team.
Now that the conflict has ended, we still do not know the truth of the event: does the meteorite really exist?
