突发新闻！女皇和煦光流在视察苹果鲁萨时，突然遭到一伙不明小马的袭击。在女皇的车队拐入一个小巷时，一伙蒙面小马突然从四面八方涌出，他们向女皇丢掷了大量苹果派，随后便很快离开了。
女皇没有受伤，因为她的保镖挡住了绝大部分的派——虽然最终整个车队被埋在了成堆的派下面。女皇的车队被迫提前结束了视察。帝国发言者表示，女皇对这次袭击感到非常愤怒，认为此次袭击是苹果鲁萨的恐吓：由于陨铁事件，苹果鲁萨民众近期正在抗议帝国的干预，袭击很可能为抗议者所为，而苹果鲁萨官方默许了这种行为。
对此，“表哥”布雷本否认了袭击者与抗议者或者苹果鲁萨有关，并承诺会展开调查，在调查结果出来之前，他呼吁为女皇安全考虑，不要再前往苹果鲁萨。

Breaking News! The Empress and her entourage were attacked by a group of unknown ponies while inspecting Appleloosa. As the Empress’s convoy turned into an alley, a group of masked ponies suddenly emerged from all sides and threw a large number of apple pies at the Empress, then quickly left.
The Empress was not injured as her bodyguards blocked most of the pies — though, her entire convoy was eventually buried under piles of pies. The Empress’s convoy was forced to end the inspection early. The Imperial Spokespony stated that the Empress was very angry about the attack and believed it was a threat from Appleloosa: due to the Meteorite Incident, the Appleloosa ponies have been protesting the Empire’s intervention recently, and the attack was likely carried out by the protesters, with the tacit approval of the Appleloosa authorities.
In response, “Cousin” Braeburn denied that the attackers were related to the protesters or Appleloosa, and promised to launch an investigation. Until the investigation results are out, he called for the Empress’s safety to be considered and for her never to visit Appleloosa again.
