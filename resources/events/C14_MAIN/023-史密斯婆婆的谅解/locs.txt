[en.@]
Granny Smith’s Forgiveness
[zh.@desc]
在一个晴朗的下午，天空中的乌云难得散去，寒冷褪去世界变得温暖。在苹果鲁萨边陲的小木屋外，史密斯婆婆正在躺椅上闭着眼睛晒着太阳。在金色的光晕中，她梦到了小马谷的大片苹果树，梦到了苹果嘉儿与大麦在院子里嬉戏，梦到了小萍花第一次参与丰收...
而巨大的嘈杂声打断了她的美梦。她费力睁开眼睛，看到了面前乌泱泱的一群小马——是特丽克西和其他来自小马谷的外交使团成员。她不耐烦地坐起身来，向特丽克西的身后望了望，也看到了低着头的梅耶。
“史密斯婆婆，”特丽克西说，“我们来向您道歉。”
“对不起，史密斯——” 梅耶被史密斯婆婆挥挥蹄打断了。史密斯婆婆只想继续睡觉。
之后的事情，史密斯婆婆就记得不那么清楚了，只记得特丽克西和梅耶一直围绕着她滔滔不绝。她感到烦躁了，就让他们去帮着把整个院子打扫了一遍，把屋外的几棵苹果树都浇了水。然后他们全程微笑着把家中的杂务也都做了。
他们大概是带来了很多礼物，史密斯婆婆就让他们都丢到后院仓库里去。之后他们又聊了好久好久，拉着婆婆一起吃饭和唱歌——具体的内容她记不清了，因为相机的闪光灯总是在她的眼前闪烁，“咔嚓”的声音也让她的耳朵嗡嗡作响。
又过了不知道几个小时，史密斯婆婆实在厌烦了，便点了点头，在“史密斯婆婆代表苹果家族原谅小马谷农业改革”的文件上签了个名，才终于与特丽克西、梅耶和那一屋子拥挤的外交使团告了别。
史密斯婆婆回到了躺椅上，但温暖的金色阳光已经变成了冰冷的月光。这并不影响她重新闭上了眼睛，但是她却没能再继续做上次的梦。
“他奶奶的。” 她骂道。
[en.@desc]
On a sunny afternoon, the dark clouds in the sky were rare to disperse, and the cold faded away, making the world warm. Outside the small wooden house on the border of Appleloosa, Granny Smith was sunbathing in a lounge chair with her eyes closed. In the golden halo, she dreamed of the large apple trees in Ponyville, of Applejack and Big Mac playing in the yard, and of Apple Bloom participating in the harvest for the first time...
But the loud noise interrupted her beautiful dream. She opened her eyes with effort and saw a large group of ponies in front of her — It was Trixie and other members of the diplomatic mission from Ponyville. She sat up impatiently and looked behind Trixie, and saw the ashamed former mayor Mare.
“Granny Smith,” Trixie said, “we come to apologize to you.”
“Sorry, Granny——” Mare was interrupted by Granny Smith’s hoof wave. Granny Smith just wanted to go back to sleep.
After that, Granny Smith didn’t remember things very clearly, only that Trixie and Mare had been talking to her non-stop. She felt annoyed, so she let them help clean the entire yard and water the few apple trees outside. Then they smiled all the way and did all the chores in the house.
They probably brought a lot of gifts, and Granny Smith let them throw them all into the backyard warehouse. Then they talked for a long, long time, and pulled Granny Smith to eat and sing together — she couldn’t remember the specific content, because the camera’s flash was always flashing in front of her eyes, and the “click” sound made her ears buzz.
After a few hours, Granny Smith was really fed up, so she nodded, signed the document “Granny Smith Represents the Apple Family to Forgive the Agricultural Reform of Ponyville”, and finally said goodbye to Trixie, Mare, and the crowded diplomatic mission.
Granny Smith returned to her lounge chair, but the warm golden sunlight had turned into cold moonlight. This did not prevent her from closing her eyes again, but she could not continue the dream she had last time.
“Aw, darn it.” She cursed.
