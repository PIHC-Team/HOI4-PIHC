A Broadcaster’s Encounter
[zh.@desc]
“今天下午，伟大的友谊女皇和熙光流率领军队，剿灭了一群企图推翻伟大友谊政权的反动分子，叛军...”
一只广播小马正播报着今天的新闻。其实大战前他不过是个排字工，但随着身边的同事一天天的变少，他惊奇地发现自己竟然变成了广播站主持，纵使他一点也不想读这些明显带有矫揉造作味道的新闻稿，但...谁会和那些金灿灿的金币过不去呢？一个“自由”的养不活自己的无产者和一个不自由的但有地位的中产者之间，他更乐意成为后者。
“住嘴！”一个声音打断了他的广播，或者说是录音。他身后的门开了，走进来的是一个穿着制服的军官。
“格林姆莱长官，今天您...”他站起身，卑躬地笑着。
“今天根本没有什么叛乱，您被捕了，我希望您在牢里可以学会如何讲真话”。那个军官挥了一下蹄子，那个可怜的广播员就被拖了出去，他哭求的声音听不见了。
“这里还有没有广播员了？唔，没有了，那就不要广播了。女皇不喜欢这些”军官这样吩咐他的部下。
[en.@desc]
“This afternoon, the mighty Friendship Empress Cozy Glow squashed yet another group of rebels who tried to overthrow the great Friendship regime. The rebels...”
A radio pony was broadcasting the news. Before the war, he was just a typesetter, but as his colleagues disappeared one by one, he was surprised to find himself becoming the host of the radio station. Even though he didn’t want to read these obviously artificial news, who would refuse those shiny gold coins? Between a “free” proletarian who could not support himself and a unfree middle class who had status, he would rather be the latter.
“Shut up!” A voice interrupted his broadcast, or recording. The door behind him opened, and a uniformed officer walked in.
“Commissar Grimmler, you look...” He stood up and bowed with a smile.
“There was no rebellion today. You are under arrest. I hope you can learn how to tell the truth in prison.” The officer waved his hoof, and the poor broadcaster was dragged out, his pleading voice disappeared soon.
“Is there any broadcaster left? Hmm, I guess no, then stop broadcasting. The Empress doesn’t like these.” The officer ordered his subordinates as such.
