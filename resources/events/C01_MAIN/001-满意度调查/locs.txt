[en.@]
Satisfaction Survey
[zh.@desc]
好消息！最新一期对小马利亚帝国居民的随机抽样问卷调查统计数据显示，94.6%的小马利亚帝国居民认为和熙光流女皇的统治“非常满意”；3.2%的小马利亚帝国居民认为和熙光流女皇的统治“比较满意”，但在后续跟进调查中表示实际满意度高于选项所描述；剩余2.2%的调查对象由于受伤或无法前往投票站等不可抗因素而未能交还问卷。
针对调查中的极少数不满意意见，帝国秩序部已经派出特别工作组，逐个听取改进意见，帮助其改善居住环境，安排就业等。针对地方机关反映强烈的监狱、医院容量不足的若干民生问题，接下来几个月内，帝国秩序部的工作重点将侧重于监狱扩容、增加劳动改造项目等，帝国医学部也计划设立新的精神类疾病集体治疗中心。
[en.@desc]
Good news! According to the latest random sampling survey statistics of the residents of the Empire of Equestria, 94.6% of the residents are “very satisfied” with the rule of Empress Cozy Glow; 3.2% of the residents are “comparatively satisfied” with the rule of Empress Cozy Glow, but in the follow-up survey, they expressed that the actual satisfaction is higher than the option. The remaining 2.2% of the respondents were unable to return the questionnaire due to uncontrollable factors such as injury or inability to go to the polling station.
To address the very few dissatisfied opinions in the survey, the Ministry of Order has sent a special working group to listen to the residents one by one for improvement opinions. The working group will help them improve their living environment and arrange employment. In response to several livelihood issues strongly reflected by local agencies, such as the shortage of prison and hospital capacity, the Ministry of Order will focus on prison expansion and increasing labor reform projects in the next few months. The Ministry of Order is also planning to establish a new collective treatment center for mental illnesses.
