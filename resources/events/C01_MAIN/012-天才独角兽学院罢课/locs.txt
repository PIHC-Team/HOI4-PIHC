天才独角兽学院罢课
[en.@]
Students of the School for Gifted Unicorns are on Strike
[zh.@desc]
从上周起，天才独角兽学院的学生们开始了罢课。他们要求帝国改善学校环境和研究环境，并要求帝国政府允许学术上的自由言论。学生们表示，他们将继续罢课，直到他们的要求得到满足。
帝国秩序部第一时间出动了大量警察和军事部队包围了学校，但是由于重新开放天才独角兽学校的命令来自于女皇，所以秩序部并没有对罢课学生们采取进一步行动。
天才独角兽学院是战前塞拉斯蒂娅所开办的学校，学校以培养顶尖独角兽学生的魔法天赋而闻名。学校的学生和老师们在学术上有着很高的声誉。受到战争影响，天才独角兽学院已关闭多年，直到最近才重新开放。
帝国秩序部新闻发言者称，这次学生罢课是某平等主义境外势力的煽动，帝国政府将会严厉打击这些势力。
第二天，帝国秩序部新的新闻发言者，强调星光叛军不是境外势力，而是帝国的一部分，帝国终将粉碎一切反对帝国的叛乱势力并维护帝国的统一。
[en.@desc]
Since last week, students of the School for Gifted Unicorns have been on strike. They demand the Empire to improve the school environment and research environment, and demand the Empire to allow academic freedom of speech. The students said they will continue the strike until their demands are met.
The Ministry of Order dispatched a large number of police and military forces to surround the school, but since the order to reopen the School for Gifted Unicorns came from the Empress, the Ministry of Order did not take further action against the striking students.
The School for Gifted Unicorns was founded by Celestia before the war, and the school is known for educating the magical talents of top-class unicorn students. The students and teachers of the school have a high reputation in academics. Affected by the war, the School for Gifted Unicorns has been closed for many years, and has only recently reopened.
The spokespony of the Ministry of Order said that the student strike was incited by some equatist foreign forces, and the Empire will severely crack down on these forces.
The next day, the new spokespony of the Ministry of Order emphasized that the Starlight Rebels is not a foreign force, but a part of the Empire, and the Empire will eventually crush all the rebellious forces against the Empire and maintain the unity of the Empire.
