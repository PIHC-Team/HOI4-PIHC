Escape from the Cage
[zh.@desc]
当军队进入坎特洛特的皇宫时，和煦光流早已不在宫殿内，她没有带走卫队，也没有带走除了她的皇冠之外的任何物品。尽管下令搜寻，但她却消失得无影无踪。

据说和煦光流逃出了坎特洛特城，最后一次被目击者发现是在无尽之森旁边的一个小村庄。昔日的女皇如今邋里邋遢衣衫不整，但即使是在灌木丛中睡觉时她也会死死地抱着自己的皇冠，也正是那个皇冠才会让目击者没有把她当成一直普通的流浪小马。
当时，村庄还没有接到全国通缉和煦的消息，和煦来村庄里面问了路，想要沿着商队开辟出来的道路逃入无尽之森。在森林的入口处，她被一条藤蔓绊倒，皇冠也掉落在地上，滚进了旁边的泥潭。
和煦光流趴在那里，没有选择去捡回她的皇冠，而只是平静地凝视着泥潭中的皇冠一点一点沉进去。当皇冠完全消失在泥潭中时，和煦光流站了起来，发出疯狂的笑声，但又像是发自真心的笑，或许是多年来终于又一次感到了快乐。她失去皇冠的那一刻，就像是她当年获得皇冠的那一刻一样。

随后，和煦光流疯癫地踉跄着进入了无尽之森。从此之后再也没有小马，看到过她的身影。
[en.@desc]
When the army entered the Canterlot Palace, Cozy Glow was no longer in the palace. She did not take the guards with her, nor did she take anything except her crown. Despite the order to search, she disappeared without a trace.

It is said that Cozy Glow escaped from Canterlot City, and the last time she was seen was in a small village near the Everfree Forest. The former Empress is now ragged and unkempt, but even when she sleeps in the bushes, she clings tightly to her crown. The crown was the only thing that made the witnesses not mistake her for an ordinary wandering pony.

At that time, the village had not received the national wanted notice for Cozy Glow. She came to the village to ask for directions, wanting to escape into the Everfree Forest along the road opened up by the caravan. At the entrance of the forest, she was tripped by a vine, and the crown fell to the ground and rolled into the nearby mud pit.
Cozy Glow lay there, not choosing to pick up her crown, but just staring calmly as the crown sank bit by bit into the mud pit. When the crown completely disappeared into the mud pit, Cozy Glow stood up, making a crazy laugh, but it seemed like a sincere laugh, perhaps she finally felt happy again after so many years. The moment she lost her crown was like the moment she got her crown years ago.

After that, Cozy Glow staggered into the Everfree Forest like a deranged pony. Since then, no pony has seen her again.
