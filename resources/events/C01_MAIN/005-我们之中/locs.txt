A Pair of Partners’ Encounter
[en.@desc]
Golden Hoof, a nervous unicorn, raced alongside his companion, Swift Feather, a vigilant pegasus. They weaved through dark alleys, hearts racing.
“Swift Feather, did you see that?” Golden Hoof gasped, his voice trembling. “That pony with the suspiciously large bag? He’s hiding something.”
Swift Feather glanced, her wings tense. “You’re right, Golden Hoof. Let’s report him. But first, let’s make sure we’re not being followed.”
They sought shelter in a decrepit building. Through its broken windows, Swift Feather watched the pony they trailed: his bag slung over his back and he glanced around nervously.
But Golden Hoof wasn’t looking out the window. He was staring at Swift Feather... “Swift Feather, I know we agreed that it’s your turn... but I’ve been having some financial difficulties lately, and I was the one who spotted him, so would you mind if I got the reward this time? Just this once. Next time, I promise it’ll be yours.”
Swift Feather’s wings stopped twitching, and her face darkened. This wasn’t the first time Golden Hoof had done this. Or the second. It was the third time. Swift Feather hadn’t paid her rent this month, and her landlord had been pestering her about it, but she knew she was no match for Golden Hoof’s silver tongue, so she had to swallow her pride and let him have his way again and again.
But this time, Swift Feather decided to act differently. “Oh for the love of Celestia, I’m sick of arguing with you. Golden Hoof, you know as well as I do that it’s the first pony to report who gets the reward, not the first pony to spot. I’m a pegasus, I can fly faster than you can run, so I can get there first. That’s the rule.”
With that, Swift Feather flew out, heading straight for the reporting station, leaving the poor Golden Hoof behind.
Their partnership ended that day, and Golden Hoof never spoke to Swift Feather again. A week later, Swift Feather received an envelope with a check. It read, “Reward: Reporting a pony with a suspiciously large bag.”
She paid her rent, and had some money left over. She thought of Golden Hoof, and felt a little guilty, so she decided to send him the rest of the money, along with an apology letter.
Golden Hoof had the most difficult month without the reward. His financial situation remained dire until he received the letter next month, with a check attached that read, “Reward: Reporting a pony who says ‘Oh for the love of Celestia’.”

金蹄，一只紧张的独角兽，和他的同伴，迅羽，一只敏锐的天马，快速地穿过昏暗的小巷，他们的心在胸膛里砰砰跳。
“迅羽，你看到了吗？”金蹄气喘吁吁地说，“那个背着一个可疑大包的小马？他在藏着什么东西。”
迅羽看了一眼，她的翅膀紧绷着。“你说得对，金蹄。我们去举报他。但是首先，我们要确定我们没有被跟踪。”
他们躲进了一座破败的建筑里。透过破碎的窗户，迅羽看着他们跟踪的小马：他背着包，紧张地环顾四周。
但是金蹄没有看窗外，而是盯着迅羽看... “迅羽，我知道我们说好了轮流的，但是我最近遇到了一些经济困难，而且我是第一个发现他的，所以能不能我这次拿到奖金？就这一次。下次，我保证是你的。”
迅羽的翅膀停止了抖动，她的脸色变得阴沉。这不是金蹄第一次这么做了。也不是第二次。是第三次。迅羽这个月还没有付房租，她的房东一直在催她，但是她知道她的口才不是金蹄的对手，所以她不得不忍气吞声，一次又一次地让他得逞。
但是这一次，迅羽决定做出不同的反应。“塞蕾丝蒂亚在上，我受够了和你争论了。金蹄，你我都知道，拿到奖金的是第一个举报的小马，而不是第一个发现的。我是一只天马，我飞得比你跑得更快，所以我可以第一个到。这就是规则。”
说完，迅羽飞了出去，直奔举报站，把可怜的金蹄留在了身后。
他们的合作就在那天结束了，金蹄再也没有和迅羽说过话。一周后，迅羽收到了一封信，里面有一张支票。上面写着：“奖金：举报一只背着可疑大包的小马。”
她付了房租，还剩下一些钱。她想起了金蹄，有点内疚，所以她决定把剩下的钱和一封道歉信一起寄给他。
金蹄在没有奖金的一个月里过得很艰难。他的经济状况一直很糟糕，直到下个月他收到了那封信，里面有一张支票，上面写着：“奖金：举报一只说‘塞蕾丝蒂亚在上’的小马。”
