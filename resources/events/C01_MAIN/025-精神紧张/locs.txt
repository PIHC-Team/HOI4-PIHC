[zh.@desc]
§b“在皮革包裹的办公室中，这里领头的和后面的也没什么分别，同样的疲倦辛苦。”§!

成为“友谊女皇”，和煦光流得到了前所未有的权力，前所未有的奴仆，前所未有的“朋友”，和前所未有的“责任”。来路不正的皇位容易滋生被害的妄想，随之而来的就是对一切的事无巨细的独断专行。
一如她那比她自己还要沉重的皇冠，她的权力也成为了她的枷锁。一如那彩票中奖后的随之而来的天价所得税单，这些权力的“赠品”就，彻底浇灭了和煦仅剩的欣喜。
即使是一天到晚埋头在公文中，即使是漠不关心到把所有的犯罪案件都批复“斩”，她案头的文件依然是只增不减，几乎淹没了她小小的身躯。这些公文犹如落水者面前带刺的救生圈，她无时无刻不在挣扎，但却紧紧抓住不肯松开。
也许当她有一天停下来的时候，可能会回想起那个风之魔盘旋的下午吧，胜利后的庆祝，或许是她最后的欢乐。而现在，她甚至没有时间去回想了。
[en.@desc]
§b“In leather-clad offices, here the first resemble the last, as they are equally tired, as the ones behind them.”§!

As the “Empress of Friendship”, Cozy Glow has gained unprecedented amount of power, unprecedented number of servants, unprecedented number of “friends”, and unprecedented “responsibilities”. The ill-gotten throne is easy to breed paranoia, and the subsequent result is the autocracy over every detail.
Just like her crown that is heavier than herself, her power has become the chain that binds her. Just like a lottery winner receiving an insanely high income tax bill, the “gifts” of these powers have completely extinguished the remaining joy in Cozy.
Even if she buries herself in documents all day long, even if she is indifferent enough to approve all criminal cases with a “beheading”, the files on her desk are still increasing, almost drowning her petite body. These documents are like lifebuoys with thorns in front of a drowning person, she struggles all the time, but she holds on tightly and refuses to let go.
Maybe when she stops one day, she might recall that afternoon when the Windigos were hovering, the celebration after the victory, perhaps her last joy. But now, she doesn’t even have time to think about it anymore.
