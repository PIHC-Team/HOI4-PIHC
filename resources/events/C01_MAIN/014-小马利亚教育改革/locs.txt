Equestria Empire Ministry of Education Announced a Reform
[en.@desc]
According to yesterday’s press conference, under the will of the Empress Cozy Glow, the Ministry of Education of the Equestria Empire has announced a reform of the education system.
The reform is currently in the planning stage, and the specific content about the reform has not been disclosed. However, the Ministry of Education has stated that the reform will be a comprehensive one, and will cover all aspects of the education system, including the curriculum, the teaching method, the examination system, and the management of the education system.
The public concerns about the reform mainly lies in the management part, more precisely, the difficulty that the current education system of Equestria is delegated to two different departments, the Equestria Empire Ministry of Education and the Equestria Education Association. The former is the official department of the empire, and the latter, though being a legacy organization from the old Equestria, still holds a great influence, or in fact, dominating influence, in the education domain.
Meanwhile, the reform on education also changes the policy about academic research resource distribution among colleges, which could also be a controversial topic between multiple interest groups: the Equestria Empire Ministry of Science and Magic, the Las Pegasus Lab, and all the magic schools in the empire, are all lurking to seize a piece of the pie.

根据昨天的新闻发布会，根据小马利亚女皇和煦光流的意志，小马利亚帝国教育部宣布对教育体系进行改革。
改革目前处于规划阶段，具体内容尚未公布。但教育部表示，改革将是一项全面的改革，将涵盖教育体系的所有方面，包括课程、教学方法、考试制度和教育体系的管理。
公众对改革的关注主要集中在管理部分，更确切地说，目前小马利亚的教育体系由两个不同的部门管理，分别是小马利亚帝国教育部和小马利亚教育协会。前者是帝国的官方部门，而后者虽然是来自旧小马利亚的遗留组织，但教育协会在教育领域仍然拥有巨大的影响力，或者说，主导性的影响力。
与此同时，教育改革还改变了学术研究资源在学院之间的分配政策，这也可能成为多个利益集团之间的争议话题：小马利亚帝国科学与魔法部、洛马基实验室和帝国内所有的魔法学校，都在争夺这块蛋糕。
