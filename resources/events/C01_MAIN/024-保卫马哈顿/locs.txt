[en.@desc]
Recently, the Starlight Rebels launched a war against Manehattan under the name of “ending the slave trade”, which caused serious concern of the Equestria Empire.
“Though the Empire has revoked the special license of Gladmane Group, and though there is indeed a slave trade in Manehattan, this cannot be a reason for the Starlight Rebels to invade.” said the spokespony of the Empire Ministry of Defence.
Considering the security of the cities in the eastern coastal area of Equestria outside Manehattan, Empress Cozy Glow has signed an order requiring Trixie, Duchess of Ponyville, to lead the army to stabilize the southern cities and stop the further actions of the Starlight Rebels.
Regarding whether the Empire has a plan to directly intervene, the spokespony of the Empire Ministry of Defence said: “The historical issues are complicated, and Manehattan and its surrounding areas have long enjoyed a high degree of autonomy...”
