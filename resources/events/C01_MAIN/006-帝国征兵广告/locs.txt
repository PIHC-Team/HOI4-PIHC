Equestria Empire Conscription Notice
[en.@desc]
Attention, loyal subjects of the Equestria Empire!
Join the ranks of the glorious Equestrian Armed Forces and experience the thrill of conscription under the wise and benevolent rule of Empress Cozy Glow! In our Empire, we believe that everypony has a duty to lend their hoof to the cause of eternal friendship and harmony, or else.
Enlist now and enjoy a life of adventure, camaraderie, and mandatory friendship lessons! Our conscription program offers exclusive benefits, including:
- Immerse yourself in the splendor of distant and exotic battlefronts! Witness the breathtaking sights of war-torn landscapes and engage in thrilling encounters with various adversaries!
- The opportunity to fight for the greater good of the Empire, with the promise of “protecting harmony”, or something else!
- Forge lifelong bonds with fellow soldiers, as you share your food, shelter, and other resources vital to your survival!
- Experience the pride of wielding traditional equipment, steeped in history and legacy! Revel in the challenges of mastering weaponry that possesses a rich history and unique personality!
- Marvel at the resourceful ingenuity of our logistics teams, as they ensure that every soldier receives precisely the right amount of ammunition and supplies required for their endeavors, never more, probably never less!
- Delve into the art of political subtleties, as our government deploys you to foreign lands with utmost discretion and minimal explanation!
Remember, conscription is not just a duty; it’s an adventure! Join the Equestrian Armed Forces today and help Empress Cozy Glow forge a brighter, friendlier future for all of Equestria. Together, we shall ensure that harmony reigns supreme, and everypony knows their place. Friendship is mandatory, but remember, it’s all for your own good!
