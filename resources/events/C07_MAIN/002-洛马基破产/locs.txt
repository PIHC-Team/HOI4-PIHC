洛马基市政府宣告破产！
[en.@]
Las Pegasus City Government Declares Bankruptcy!
[zh.@desc]
由于洛马基市内的供暖燃料不足，为保障居民供暖，洛马基市政府不得不从外地高价进口供暖燃料。市政府上调民用和工业暖气费用的提议遭到否决，小马利亚帝国财政部也拒绝为洛马基市的预算增长埋单。这最终导致了市政府的财政危机，今日，市政府宣告破产。
但幸运的是，总部位于洛马基市内的洛马基集团是一家富有社会责任感的企业。洛马基集团宣布，从今日起，洛马基集团旗下的洛马基市政有限公司将会接管市政府的所有职能，以保障市民的基本生活权益；与此同时，洛马基集团旗下的洛马基供暖有限公司将以略高于原暖气费，但低于政府上调后的价格，向市民提供供暖服务。对于市政府的债务，洛马基集团将全部承担，而此后，洛马基市便不再向小马利亚帝国中央负责，而成为小马利亚第一个由私企拥有的城市。
大部分洛马基居民对此表示支持，认为这是市政府的一次重大进步，也是洛马基市的一次新生。但也有部分居民认为，洛马基能源集团垄断供暖服务并且向外地出口是洛马基市内供暖燃料不足的根本原因，洛马基集团本就应该为市政府的破产负责。
[en.@desc]
Due to the shortage of heating fuel in Las Pegasus, the city government had to import heating fuel from other places at a high price to ensure the heating of residents. The proposal to raise the heating fees for civil and industrial use was rejected, and the Equestria Empire Ministry of Finance refused to pay for the budget growth of Las Pegasus city. This eventually led to the financial crisis of the city government, and today, the city government declared bankruptcy.
Fortunately, the Las Pegasus Group, headquartered in Las Pegasus, is a socially responsible enterprise. The Las Pegasus Group announced that from today, Las Pegasus Municipal Co., Ltd. under the Las Pegasus Group will take over all the functions of the city government to ensure the basic living rights of the citizens; at the same time, Las Pegasus Heating Co., Ltd. under the Las Pegasus Group will provide heating services to the citizens at a slightly higher price than the original heating fee, but lower than the price the government proposed. Las Pegasus Group will bear all the debts of the city government, and after that, Las Pegasus will no longer be responsible to the Equestria Empire, but will become the first city owned by a private enterprise in Equestria.
Most of the Las Pegasus residents expressed their support, believing that this is a major progress of the city government and a new beginning of Las Pegasus. However, some residents believe that the Las Pegasus Energy Group monopolizes the heating services and exports to other places, which is the root cause of the shortage of heating fuel in Las Pegasus, and Las Pegasus Group should be responsible for the bankruptcy of the city government.
