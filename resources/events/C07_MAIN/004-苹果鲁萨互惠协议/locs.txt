Las Pegasus has invited us to engage in economic cooperation. They will provide us with technological support, and we will provide them with agricultural resources. The cooperation will start with a reciprocity agreement, and we hope to join the Western Equestria Trade Alliance in the future. However, joining the trade alliance requires the consent of our ally, Rock Farm. Therefore, we will only discuss the reciprocity agreement with Las Pegasus for now.
It is worth noting that the two brothers of the Las Pegasus Consortium have a bad reputation and are known for their rich experience in breaking promises. We need to be careful not to be deceived by their sweet words.
