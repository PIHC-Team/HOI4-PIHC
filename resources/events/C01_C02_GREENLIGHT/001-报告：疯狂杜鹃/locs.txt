「绿灯」行动报告：疯狂杜鹃
[en.@]
Operation <=Greenlight=> Report: Rabid Rhododendrons
[zh.@desc]
疯狂杜鹃，一种生长于无尽之森的植物（动物？该对象似乎具有灵活移动能力）。其本体具有较弱的攻击性，可以缠绕或者使用尖刺攻击目标。疯狂杜鹃似乎具有视力，但我们尚未找到视觉器官。

然而，疯狂杜鹃的特殊之处在于，在狩猎目标的过程中，多只疯狂杜鹃会包围目标，并喷射一种特殊的气体。这种气体具有香气，可以使目标快速进入睡眠状态。

经考察，疯狂杜鹃具有无差别攻击性、培养成本高昂，不适宜用做武器。然而其产生的气体可以收集和分析，不排除将来可用作战争用途。
[en.@desc]
Rabid Rhododendrons, a plant (or animal? It seems to have flexible mobility) that grows in the Everfree Forest. It is aggressive but weak. It can entangle or attack targets with thorns. Rabid Rhododendrons seem to have vision, but we have not found visual organs yet.

However, the special feature of Rabid Rhododendrons is that, during the hunting process, multiple Rabid Rhododendrons will surround the target and spray a special gas. This gas has a fragrance that can quickly put the target into a sleep state.

After investigation, Rabid Rhododendrons have indiscriminate attack and high cultivation costs. Thus it is not suitable for use as a weapon. However, the gas it produces can be collected and analyzed, and it is not ruled out that it may be used for war purposes in the future.
