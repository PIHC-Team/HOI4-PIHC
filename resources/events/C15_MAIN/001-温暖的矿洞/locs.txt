【冰封北境考察第3周电报】
这是本周以来第四次路过一片无马开采的天然水晶矿洞了，证据充分表明冰封北境有着丰富的水晶资源。并且，这里的矿洞可以屏蔽风雪，矿洞内部还是比较温暖的，根据评估，在矿洞中建立居住点进行长期水晶开采是可行的。
唯一的问题是，遍布的水晶矿似乎对电报信号产生了一定的干扰，我们可能无法保证实时的高质量通信。接下来我们将只汇报重要的发现，更多不重要的发现将记录在考察日志中带回水晶帝国。

【Frozen North Expedition Report Telegram Week #3】
This is the fourth time this week that we have passed through a natural crystal cave without anypony mining. There is ample evidence that the Frozen North has abundant crystal resources. Moreover, the cave can shield the wind and snow, and the inside of the cave is still relatively warm. According to the evaluation, it is feasible to establish a living point in the cave for long-term crystal mining.
The only problem is that the scattered crystal mines seem to have some interference with the telegram signal, and we may not be able to guarantee real-time high-quality communication. Next, we will only report important discoveries, and more unimportant discoveries will be recorded in the expedition log and brought back to the Crystal Empire.
