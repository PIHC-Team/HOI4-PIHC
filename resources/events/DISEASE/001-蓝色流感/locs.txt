新型传染病：蓝色流感
[en.@]
New Disease: Blue Flu
[zh.@desc]
一种源头不明的新型传染病正在引发全国性的呼吸道疾病爆发。
小马利亚卫生组织（Equestria Health Organization, EHO）将该病命名为“蓝色流感”，因为感染者的皮肤会变成蓝色。该病高度传染性，但死亡率较低。该病的症状和后遗症仍在研究中。
虽然感染源头尚未确认，但据记载该病在终末之战之前就已出现过。EHO仍在努力确认病源，并研制疫苗。
EHO已向全体公民发出警告，要求避免与感染者接触，并向最近的EHO办事处报告任何疑似病例。
[en.@desc]
A new infectious disease of unknown origin is causing an outbreak of respiratory illness in the country. The Equestria Health Organization (EHO) has named the disease “Blue Flu” due to the blue color of the infected’s skin. The disease is highly contagious yet has a low mortality rate. The symptoms and sequelae of the disease are still being studied.
While the original source of infection has not been confirmed, it is documented that the disease has appeared once before the Ultimate Battle. EHO is still trying to identify the source of the disease and is working on a cure.
The EHO has issued a warning to all citizens to avoid contact with the infected and to report any suspected cases to the nearest EHO office.
