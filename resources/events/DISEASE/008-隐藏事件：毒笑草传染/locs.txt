[en.@]
Disease Alert: Poison Joke
[zh.@desc]
小马利亚卫生组织（Equestria Health Organization, EHO）提醒各地区的公民注意防范毒笑草，一种危险的蓝色植物引发的疾病。
请注意，毒笑草中毒后的症状完全随机，但只要不做出过激反应，感染后对健康本身没有影响，并且症状将在一段时间后自动回复。
因此，EHO建议所有小马远离蓝色植物，并在感染后在家中减少外出，直到症状消失。
[en.@desc]
The Equestria Health Organization (EHO) has issued a warning to all citizens to be on the lookout for Poison Joke, a dangerous blue plant caused disease.
Please note that the symptoms of Poison Joke are completely random, but as long as you don’t overreact, the infection has no effect on your health itself, and the symptoms will automatically disappear after a period of time.
Therefore, the EHO recommends that all ponies stay away from blue plants and reduce their outings at home after infection until the symptoms disappear.
