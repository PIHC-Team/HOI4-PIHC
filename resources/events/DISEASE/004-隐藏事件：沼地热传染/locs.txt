新型传染病：沼地热
[en.@]
New Disease: Swamp Fever
[zh.@desc]
一种源头不明、症状不明、传播途径不明的新型传染病正在引发局部恐慌。
小马利亚卫生组织（Equestria Health Organization, EHO）将该病命名为“沼地热”，一种此前在无尽之森附近的沼泽地带发现的疾病。
目前已知患者身上会出现橘色斑点，该病毒似乎并不会通过空气或接触传播，但疑似会通过食物和水传播。对于传播的细节，目前我们尚无法给出任何有效的结论，一个可能的猜测是该病毒通过沼地花粉和泡泡传播。该病毒的症状和后遗症仍在研究中。
EHO已向全体公民发出警告，建议大家不要前往无尽之森附近的沼泽地带，以免感染沼地热。
关于患者感染沼地热后会变为树的传言是不实的，EHO尚未观测到此现象，特此澄清，居民们无需恐慌。
[en.@desc]
A new disease with unknown origin, symptoms and transmission is causing local panic.
The Equestria Health Organization (EHO) has named the disease “Swamp Fever”, a disease previously found in the swampy areas near the Everfree Forest.
Currently, patients are known to develop orange spots on their bodies. The virus does not seem to spread through the air or contact, but is suspected to spread through food and water. We currently have no effective conclusions about the details of the spread, and a possible guess is that the virus is spread through swamp pollen and bubbles. The symptoms and sequelae of the virus are still under study.
The EHO has issued a warning to all citizens, advising everyone not to go to the swampy areas near the Everfree Forest to avoid infection with Swamp Fever.
The rumor that patients will turn into trees after being infected with Swamp Fever is false. The EHO has not observed this phenomenon, and residents do not need to panic.
