新元年庆祝活动
[en.@]
Jahr Null Celebration
[zh.@desc]
12月25日暖炉节当天，三巨头公开举办了盛大的宴会，宴会后，东道主和煦光流女皇宣读了三巨头共同签署的《伟大联合宣言》（全称《女皇和煦、女王邪茧与领主提雷克的伟大联合宣言》），并宣布以新节日“新元年”取代原有的暖炉节。
为彰显“新元年”的跨时代性和三巨头维护共同安全稳定的决心，三巨头联合举办了针对终末之战遗留战犯的公审活动，公审流程持续超过三个小时，取得以下成果：以暮光闪闪为首的六名小马战犯、以索拉克斯为首的若干幻形灵战犯，被处以钝器死刑；两名小马利亚前公主被宣判无期徒刑，将关押在塔尔塔洛斯监狱；六名长老被宣判无期徒刑，将关押在坎特洛特监狱。
“今天，标志着新元年的起点——新的纪元！今天，我们将彻底抛弃旧世界！历史，在今天终结；未来，从今天开始！”
新元年庆祝活动在热烈的呼声中结束。
[en.@desc]
On Hearth’s Warming Day, the “Big Three” held a grand banquet, and after the banquet, the host Empress Cozy Glow announced the signing of the “Great Joint Declaration” by the “Big Three” (a.k.a. “The Great Joint Declaration by Empress Cozy Glow, Queen Rebuild, and Lord Tirek”), and announced that the new festival “Jahr Null” would replace the original Hearth’s Warming Day.
To demonstrate the cross-era nature of “Jahr Null” and the determination of the “Big Three” to maintain common security and stability, they jointly held a public trial of the war criminals left over from the Ultimate Battle. The trial process lasted for more than three hours and achieved the following results: six pony war criminals led by Twilight Sparkle, several changeling war criminals led by Thorax, were sentenced to death by bludgeoning; two former princesses of Equestria were sentenced to life imprisonment and will be detained in Tartarus Prison; six elders were sentenced to life imprisonment and will be detained in Canterlot Prison.
“Today, marks the beginning of Jahr Null — Year Zero! We will do away with the old! Today, history ends, and the future begins!”
The Jahr Null Celebration ended in thunderous applause.
