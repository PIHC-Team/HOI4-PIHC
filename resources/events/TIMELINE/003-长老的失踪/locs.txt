Missing of the Pillars
[zh.@desc]
据小马利亚帝国警卫队坎特洛特市副队长称，六名关押于坎特洛特的罪犯——星璇、草甸清溪、闪电马格努斯、梦晶沙南布拉、雾鬃、石蹄，于昨日逃离了坎特洛特监狱，目前下落不明。小马利亚帝国警卫队提醒各位坎特洛特居民保持警惕，做好自身安全防护，并及时向帝国警卫队报告任何可疑行为的情报信息。同时，帝国警卫队也欢迎所有地区所有小马，包括小马之外的生物，积极提供信息，协助抓捕六名在逃罪犯。
[en.@desc]
According to the deputy captain of the Canterlot City Guard of the Equestria Empire, six prisoners detained in Canterlot: Starswirl, Meadowbrook, Magnus, Somnambula, Mistmane, and Rockhoof, escaped from Canterlot Prison yesterday and are currently missing. The Equestria Empire Guard reminds all Canterlot residents to remain vigilant, take precautions for their own safety, and report any suspicious behavior to the Empire Guard in a timely manner. At the same time, the Empire Guard also welcomes all ponies and creatures from all regions, including non-ponies, to actively provide information and assist in the capture of the six fugitives.
