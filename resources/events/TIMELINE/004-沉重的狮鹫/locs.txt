The Griffon Lies Heavy
[zh.@desc]
时隔多年，A.K.伊尔琳终于发布了无畏天马系列新作——《沉重的狮鹫》。
然而，一改往日的冒险风格，这本新书描述了一个独特的故事：无畏天马来到了一个幻想世界，与我们世界的历史完全不同。在那个幻想世界中，M6在终末之战中击败了三巨头。
“那是一个充满和谐的世界，所有小马都向往的世界...”伊尔琳这样在书中写到。
小马利亚帝国秩序部已经宣布将《沉重的狮鹫》列为禁书，停止出版印刷，所有持有《沉重的狮鹫》的小马应在7日内上缴。帝国秩序部已经发布了对A.K.伊尔琳的通缉令。
[en.@desc]
After years of waiting, A.K. Yearling has finally released a new book in the Daring Do series: The Griffon Lies Heavy.
However, in a departure from her usual adventure style, this new book describes a unique story: Daring Do comes to a fantasy world where history is completely different from our world. In that fantasy world, M6 defeated the Big Three in that Ultimate Battle.
“It is a world full of harmony, a world that all ponies yearn for...” Yearling wrote in the book.
The Ministry of Order has declared that The Griffon Lies Heavy is prohibited, and has banned its printing and publishing. All ponies who own The Griffon Lies Heavy should turn it in within 7 days. The Ministry of Order has also issued a warrant for the arrest of A.K. Yearling.
