多数小马对于和煦光流女皇的印象停留在她的电视形象上——宣示力量的双翼，展现权威的皇袍，尽显奢华的皇冠。然而，打入小马利亚帝国亲卫队的幻形灵情报工作者在与服饰女皇的仆从交谈后得知，晚上常常能看到女皇盯着一个盒式项链坠里面的相片。照片上一定是女皇很亲近的小马，不过我们的情报人员至今还没有机会一睹照片中小马的真面目。
[en.@desc]
The impression of Cozy Glow to most ponies is limited to her television image - the wings that declare power, the robe that displays authority, and the crown that exudes luxury. However, changeling intelligence operatives who infiltrated the Equestrian Royal Guard learned from the servants of the Empress that she often stares at a photo inside a cassette necklace at night. The photo must be of a pony that the Empress is very close to, but our operatives have not yet had the opportunity to see the true face of the pony in the photo.
