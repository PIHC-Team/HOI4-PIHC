坎特洛特反加税事件
[en.@]
Canterlot Anti-Tax Incident
[zh.@desc]
自7月12日起，大量小马聚集在坎特洛特，抗议小马利亚帝国的加税行为。尽管小马利亚帝国中央政府出动了大量警察和军队，多起流血事件发生，依然无法驱散聚集在坎特洛特的小马。7月14日，小马们试图涌入和破坏坎特洛特监狱。最终，小马们还是被驱散。
和煦光流女皇对此次事件表示极度愤怒，将展开迅速且深入的调查，将所有幕后推动者绳之以法。针对造成争议的提高税赋政策，帝国没有做出明确表态，但帝国中央电视台的报道似乎在试图暗示本次暴乱与税赋无关，有幻形灵混入挑拨离间，民众应该更加关心外敌而非内部税赋问题。
帝国中央的系列通告宣布这一事件已经结束，但有证据表明，随着居民直接暴动被镇压，“模仿者运动”在民间的支持度开始上升，小马们开始以模仿而不是反对和煦光流女皇的方式，通过阴阳怪气的赞美而非侮辱，表达着自己的不满。
[en.@desc]
Since July 12th, a large number of ponies have gathered in Canterlot to protest the tax increase by the Equestria Empire. Despite the large number of police and military forces dispatched by the central government of the Equestria Empire, many bloody incidents have occurred, and the ponies gathered in Canterlot have not been dispersed. On July 14th, the ponies tried to rush into and destroy Canterlot Prison. In the end, the ponies were still dispersed.
Empress Cozy Glow expressed extreme anger at this incident and will launch a rapid and in-depth investigation to bring all the masterminds to justice. Regarding the controversial policy of raising taxes, the Empire has not made a clear statement, but the reports of the Empire Central Television seem to be trying to imply that this riot is not related to taxes, and that the changelings have infiltrated and incited chaos, and the ponies should be more concerned about foreign enemies than internal tax issues.
A series of announcements from the Empire Central announced that the incident was over, but there is evidence that, as the direct riots of the residents were suppressed, the “imitator movement” began to rise in popularity among the people, and the ponies began to express their dissatisfaction through imitation rather than opposition to Empress Cozy Glow, through ambiguous praise rather than insults.
