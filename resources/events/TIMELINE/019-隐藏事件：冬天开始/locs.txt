小马谷的寒冬
[en.@]
Ponyville’s Harsh Winter
[zh.@desc]
根据气象学家的预测，受风之魔的影响，即使天气工厂和天气实验室已经将造雪产能降低为原来的30%，小马谷仍将面临一场前所未有的寒冬。
多亏了梅耶任镇长期间的小马谷农业改革，如今的小马谷以耐寒作物为主，但是这场寒冬仍将对小马谷的居民生活造成巨大的影响。
由于苹果树的数量较三年前大幅减少，苹果树的生长周期又较长，我们已经耗尽了所有储存的木材。这意味着这个冬天我们将会缺乏足够的木材来取暖。
因此，农业专家建议立即开始推行新的森林砍伐计划，同时种植更多速生林，以便在未来的寒冬中能够有足够的木材来取暖。
[en.@desc]
According to meteorologists’ forecast, due to the influence of the Windigos, even though the weather factory and the weather lab have reduced the snowmaking capacity to 30% of the original, Ponyville will still face an unprecedented harsh winter.
Thanks to the agricultural reform during Mayor Mare’s tenure, Ponyville is now mainly growing cold-resistant crops, but this harsh winter will still have a huge impact on the lives of Ponyville residents.
Due to the large reduction in the number of apple trees compared to three years ago, and the longer growth cycle of apple trees, we have exhausted all the wood in store. This means that this winter we will lack enough wood to keep warm.
Therefore, agricultural experts suggest that a new forest logging plan should be implemented immediately, and more fast-growing forests should be planted so that there will be enough wood to keep warm for future winters.
