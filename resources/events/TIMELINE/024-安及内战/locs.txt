在小马国西南边的沙漠中，一场冲突正在悄悄升级。
自安及进入城邦时代，安及同盟大会框架下，各个城邦之间相处较为融洽。豺狼、飞马与猫猫三个种族之间偶有摩擦，但基本保持克制。
在最近几年的时间内，随着洛马基和自由小马利亚相继盯上了安及地区富有的资源，商品和订单大量涌入安及，让城邦之间的矛盾逐渐紧张。
安及冲突的直接导火索是最近，豺狼族在洛马基集团的支持下开始愈发频繁的购置军事装备，并切断其他城邦的贸易路线。与之相邻的克骊欧佩特拉后嗣诸邦最先对此提出不满并在同盟大会上要求谴责。
随着豺狼拒绝接受会议结果退出同盟大会，原本就有独立意向的克骊欧佩特拉后嗣诸邦也退出同盟大会，同时谴责豺狼的侵略行为，以及同盟大会的不作为。安及同盟大会的框架逐渐崩溃。
嗅到战争的危险，生活在安及地区的猫猫族组成了单独的同盟宣布中立，而剩余留在同盟大会中的城邦则加入到对豺狼和克骊欧佩特拉后嗣的镇压行动中。
[en.@desc]
At the desert in the southwest of Equestria, a conflict is quietly escalating.
Since Anugypt entered the city-state era, under the framework of the Anugypt League Conference, the relationship between the city-states is relatively harmonious. Though there are occasional frictions between the Jackals, Pegasi and Cats, they are generally restrained.
In recent years, with the the wealth of Anugypt region being targeted by first Las Pegasus then the Free Equestria, a large number of goods and orders have poured into Anugypt, causing the contradictions between the city-states to gradually intensify.
The direct trigger of the Anugypt Conflict is that recently, the Jackals, with the support of Las Pegasus Group, have begun to purchase military equipment more frequently and cut off the trade routes of other city-states. The descendants of Cleopatrot, the neighboring city-states, were the first to express dissatisfaction with this and demanded condemnation at the Anugypt League Conference.
As the Jackals refused to accept the results of the meeting and withdrew from the Anugypt League Conference, the Cleopatrot descendants, who had always had independent intentions, also withdrew from the Anugypt League Conference, condemning the Jackals’ aggressive behavior and the inaction of the Anugypt League Conference. The framework of the Anugypt League Conference gradually collapsed.
Sensing the danger of war, the Cats living in the Anugypt region formed a separate alliance and declared neutrality, while the remaining city-states in the League Conference joined the suppression of the Jackals and the descendants of Cleopatrot.
