C for Cozy Glow
[zh.@desc]
11月5日，“模仿者”所宣称的起义日，“模仿者”预告将在这一天“杀死和煦光流”...在秩序部的协调下，小马利亚帝国警卫队在一个月前已经开始了戒严和宵禁，帝国国防部也抽调了部分军队驻扎在坎特洛特市中心和周边地区，自11月4日起，坎特洛特市中心进入全面封闭。
起义日终于到来，大量穿着和煦光流女皇装扮的“模仿者”出现在小马利亚帝国各个主要城市。坎特洛特市中心的警察和军队依照命令，对拒绝服从命令的“模仿者”使用武力...那是一个将永远被铭记的夜晚。
11月6日零点钟的钟声敲响，和煦光流亲自出面的电视讲话终结了“杀死和煦光流”的预言，在警察和军队的镇压下，各个城市的“模仿者”群众很快散去。
小马利亚帝国恢复了往日的平静，但在漩涡的中心——坎特洛特市中心广场上，警方还没能来得及清理昨晚留在广场上满地的“和煦光流”的尸体。从某种意义上来说，预言成功了——11月5日，“和煦光流”被杀死了，不是被起义军，而是被帝国警察和帝国军队。
[en.@desc]
On November 5th, the uprising day claimed by the “Imitator”, the “Imitator” announced that he would “kill Cozy Glow” on this day... Under the coordination of the Ministry of Order, the Royal Guard of the Equestria Empire has been on alert and curfew for a month, and the Ministry of Defence has also deployed some troops to station in the Canterlot city center and surrounding areas. Since November 4th, the Canterlot city center has been completely closed.
The uprising day finally arrived, a large number of “Imitators” dressed as Cozy Glow appeared in major cities of the Equestria Empire. The police and army in the Canterlot city center used force against the “Imitators” who refused to obey orders according to the orders... It was a night that will be remembered forever.
At midnight on November 6th, the bell rang, and Cozy Glow’s personal TV speech ended the prophecy of “killing Cozy Glow”. Under the suppression of the police and army, the “Imitators” in various cities quickly dispersed.
The Equestria Empire has returned to its former peace, but in the center of the vortex—the Canterlot city center square, the police have not been able to clean up the “Cozy Glow” bodies left on the square last night. In a sense, the prophecy was successful—on November 5th, “Cozy Glow” was killed, not by the rebels, but by the Imperial police and the Imperial army.
