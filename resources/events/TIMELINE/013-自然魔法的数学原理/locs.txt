《自然魔法的数学原理》
[en.@]
Magiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica
[zh.@desc]
近日，自由小马利亚出版的一本新书在学术界引起了不小的轰动。在自由小马利亚的一场学术研讨会上，对魔法本质的探讨引起了余晖烁烁的注意。随后她将这一问题交予来自另一个世界的人类暮光闪闪。经过一段时间的研究，结合对小马利亚魔法和人类世界科技的认知，人类暮光闪闪提出了一套新的系统性魔法理论，撰写成书：《自然魔法的数学原理》。
《原理》一书中称，法术包含充能与编织两个部分：魔法的充能决定了法术效果的强弱，例如悬浮一块石子还是搬起一座山，充能能力依赖于施法者的天赋和长时间的耐力训练；法术的编织则改变魔法的作用形式，例如创造一个茶杯还是一块点心，法术的编织依赖于想象力与对咒语的理解和学习。对于魔法的能量守恒问题和介质问题，书中则没有进行过多的阐述。无论如何，《原理》是划时代的，许多小马认为这将开辟一个全新的研究领域。
据称，暮光闪闪在一次使用魔法失误时被苹果嘉儿扔出的苹果砸中，灵光一现受到了启发。但也有批评者认为，暮光闪闪可能仅仅是剽窃了星璇长老的类似观点。对于此种争议，暮光闪闪回应：“如果我能看得更远一点的话，是因为我站在长老的马背上。”

Recently, a new book published by the Free Equestria has caused quite a stir in the academic world. At a symposium in the Free Equestria, the discussion of the nature of magic has attracted the attention of Sunset Shimmer, who then handed the question over to Twilight Sparkle from the human world. After a period of research, combined with the knowledge of the magic of Equestria and the technology of the human world, Twilight Sparkle proposed a new systematic theory of magic and wrote it into the book “Magiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica”.
The book states that a spell consists of two parts: charging and weaving. The charging of magic determines the strength of the spell’s effect, such as suspending a stone or lifting a mountain. The charging ability depends on the talent of the caster and long-term endurance training. The weaving of the spell changes the form of the magic, such as creating a teacup or a piece of cake. The weaving of the spell depends on imagination and the understanding and learning of spells. The book does not elaborate on the conservation of magic energy and the medium problem. Nevertheless, the “Principia” is epoch-making, and many ponies believe that it will open up a whole new field of research.
It is said that Twilight Sparkle was inspired by a mistake in using magic when she was hit by an apple thrown by Applejack. However, some critics believe that Twilight Sparkle may have simply plagiarized similar views of Starswirl. In response to this controversy, Twilight Sparkle said, “If I can see further, it is because I am standing on the shoulders of the Pillar.”
