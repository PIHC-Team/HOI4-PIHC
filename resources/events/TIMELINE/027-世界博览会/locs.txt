The Great Exhibition of Jahr Null

[zh.@desc]
新元年世界博览会今日开幕！提雷克领地的午夜城堡如期对外开放，展会将持续至10月11日。
展会将主要展示提雷克领地及其附属地区的文化与特产：包括半人马族群文化、石像鬼文化、狮鹫王国文化、龙族文化等。提雷克也宣布将在展会现场展示一些从未露面的珍宝。
除此之外，目前已经确定的一些代表性国际展品包括：洛马基财团研发的蒸汽机以及衍生产品、采石农场军工与矿业设备模型、克鲁格镇维可集团持有的前风暴帝国珍宝、钱多多农产品集团持有的苹果鲁萨原产魔虹苹果等。
[en.@desc]
The Great Exhibition of Jahr Null opens today! As scheduled, the Midnight Castle in Tirek’s realm is now open to the public, and the exhibition will last until October 11th.
The exhibition will mainly showcase the culture and specialties of Tirek’s realm and its affiliated regions: including the culture of the centaurs, gargoyles, griffons, and dragons. Tirek has also announced that some treasures that have never been seen before will be displayed at the exhibition.
Besides, some of the confirmed international exhibits include: the steam engine and its derivatives developed by the Lomax Consortium, the military and mining equipment models from the Quarry Farm, the treasures of the former Storm Empire held by the Veco Group in Krug Town, and the original rainbow apples from Appleloosa held by the Qian Duo Duo Agricultural Products Group.
