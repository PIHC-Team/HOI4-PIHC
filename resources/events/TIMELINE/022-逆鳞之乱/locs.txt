[zh.@desc]
由于我们尚不清楚的原因，麒麟国度爆发了一场剧烈的冲突，一部逆麟居民向占据主导地位的麒麟政府发起了叛乱。
外界对于麒麟国度的了解并不多，主流猜测是，内战的原因是麒麟与逆麟两个种族长久以来积压的矛盾终于爆发。但也有一些曾访问过麒麟国的学者称，麒麟与逆麟之间不存在生理学物种上的差异，他们之间的冲突更有可能是由于政治、经济、宗教等方面的其他原因。
麒麟国度位于麒麟索尔大陆，小马利亚大陆的东南侧，该国的主要生存物种是麒麟与逆麟。历史上麒麟与逆麟曾多次爆发冲突，但近几年来关系有所缓和。麒麟国度长期以来不与外界交流，直到小马利亚大陆上的战争爆发，麒麟国度才开始逐渐开放，但外界依然对其知之甚少。如今，小马利亚的各位领袖和政治学者都在密切关注麒麟内战的局势，并试图推断内战的原因，以及内战的结果对小马利亚大陆的影响。
然而，到目前为止，还没有国家明确表明将会介入这场内战，支持其中的某一方或者是调停双方的冲突。
[en.@desc]
Due to unknown reasons, the country of Kirin has erupted into a violent conflict, with a group of Nirik residents launching a rebellion against the dominant Kirin government.
There is not much known about the country of Kirin, and the mainstream speculation is that the cause of the civil war is the long-standing conflict between the Kirins and Niriks. However, some scholars who have visited the country of Kirin have claimed that there is no difference in the physiological species between the Kirins and Niriks, and that the conflict between them is more likely due to other reasons such as politics, economics, or religion.
The country of Kirin is located on the Kirinthal continent, to the southeast of the Equestria continent. The main living species in the country are the Kirins and Niriks. Historically, the Kirins and Niriks have had conflicts many times, but in recent years, the relationship has been somewhat relaxed. The country of Kirin has not communicated with the outside world for a long time, until the war on the Equestria continent broke out. Since then, the country of Kirin has gradually opened up, but the outside world still knows very little about it. Now, the leaders and political scholars of Equestria are closely watching the situation of the Kirin civil war, and trying to infer the cause of the civil war, as well as the impact of the civil war on the Equestria continent.
However, so far, no country has clearly stated that it will intervene in the civil war, support one side, or mediate the conflict between the two sides.
