坎特洛特爆炸案

Canterlot Explosion Incident
[zh.@desc]
“小马们生来而且始终是自由平等的”——匿名“模仿者运动”成员。
8月26日发生了一件震惊世界的事件：坎特洛特火车站遭到爆炸物袭击。与此同时，一盒录像带被寄往各路媒体，录像带中，一只装扮成和煦光流女皇的“模仿者”煽动民众于11月5日配合她的起义，并宣称将在11月5日“杀死和煦光流”。各路媒体所收到的录像带很快被小马利亚帝国官方政府没收，但录像带还是被《自由之镜》公之于众。
帝国秩序部连发数条规定，要求民众禁止谈论所谓的“11月5日起义”，禁止“模仿者”行为，并且规定所有小马有义务举报参与“模仿者运动”的小马。秩序部已将录像带中的匿名小马标记为最高级别通缉，将出动超出以往的警力寻找和逮捕目标。
[en.@desc]
“Everypony is born to be and should remain to be free and equal.” - Anonymous “Imitator Movement” member.
On August 26th, a shocking event occurred: Canterlot Train Station was attacked by explosives. At the same time, a videotape was sent to various media outlets. In the videotape, an “Imitator” dressed as Empress Cozy Glow incited the public to join her uprising on November 5th, and claimed that she would “kill Cozy Glow” on November 5th. The videotapes received by various media outlets were quickly confiscated by the official government of the Equestria Empire, but the videotapes were still made public by the “Mirror of Freedom”.
The Equestria Empire Ministry of Order issued several regulations, requiring the public to refrain from discussing the so-called “November 5th Uprising”, prohibiting “Imitator” behavior, and stipulating that all ponies are obliged to report ponies involved in the “Imitator Movement”. The Ministry of Order has marked the anonymous pony in the videotape as the highest level of wanted, and will mobilize more police force than ever before to find and arrest the target.
