雾轨从萼叶获得的情报表明，乘风正在两头下注：一方面，他在公开场合支持破空与现任层云堡政府；另一方面，他也帮着介绍了许多委员会成员前去参加闪尘的表演与晚宴...
对于一位商马来说，这种行为十分正常，毕竟他们唯一在乎的就是最大化收益最小化风险。但乘风的行为是否会代表更多群体的态度？我们那虚高的支持率是否有可能只是一群墙头草在填充？
值得密切关注乘风的后续动向，以及云中城商业委员会中的主流态度。同时也要注意反侦察，若乘风意识到层云堡当局正在监视竞争对手与他们的支持者，这种舆论影响可能是我们无法承受的。
[en.@desc]
The information obtained by Vapor Trail from the Sepal indicates that Wind Rider is betting on both sides: on the one hoof, he publicly supports Sky Stinger and the current government of Stratusburg; on the other hoof, he has also introduced many members of the Chamber to attend Lightning Dust’s performances and banquets...
For a businesspony, this behavior is quite normal, as they only care about maximizing profits and minimizing risks. But does Wind Rider’s behavior represent the attitude of more groups? Is it possible that our inflated approval rating is just a bunch of fence-sitters filling in the gaps?
It is worth closely monitoring Wind Rider’s subsequent actions and the mainstream attitude of the Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce. At the same time, we must also pay attention to counter-surveillance. If Wind Rider realizes that the Stratusburg authorities are monitoring competitors and their supporters, this kind of public opinion influence may be something we cannot afford.
