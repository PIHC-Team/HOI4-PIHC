淘汰者档案：云中城商业委员会
[en.@]
The Washouts Profile: Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce

[zh.@desc]
云中城商业委员会是一个历史非常复杂的团体，不同时期的商业委员会代表着完全不同的利益集团。早在皇家委员会刚刚接管大云中城地区的时候，云中城商业委员会就已经成立，那时委员会中的成员大多是来自坎特洛特的旧贵族，代表着奢侈品与时尚行业、高档餐饮业、以及新取得地位的女皇与帝国新军队的利益。随着滚雷等一批大云中城地区本地居民逐渐取得权力，商业委员会中也增添了更多云中城的新鲜面孔，开始偏好天气工厂等新兴轻工业。
羽蝶进入帝国担任首席经济顾问是商业委员会的一个重大转折 —— 滚雷失势后，羽蝶被准许在大云中城地区实践他的新经济理论，开启了长达一年的自由商业时代。在这一年中，云中城的经济迅速发展，但也因为缺乏监管而让闪尘代表的军火商、乘风代表的媒体财团等唯利是图的商马开始在云中城大肆横行。最终，呆蹄的当选终结了羽蝶前景广阔的实验，商业委员会从官方组织转变成了民间活动的商会。如今，云中城商业委员会几乎已经是乘风的私有商会，并尝试在层云堡、云中城等各个地区制造混乱，给自己旗下的企业创造趁虚而入的机会。
[en.@desc]
The Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce is an organization with a very complex history. The Chamber of Commerce in different periods represents completely different interest groups. The Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce was established about the time the Royal Commission took over the Greater Cloudsdale area. At that time, most of the members of the Chamber of Commerce were old nobles from Canterlot, representing the interests of the luxury and fashion industry, high-end catering industry, and the interests of the new Empress and the Empire’s new army. As local residents of the Greater Cloudsdale area like Rolling Thunder, gradually gained power, the Chamber of Commerce also added more fresh faces from Cloudsdale, beginning to favor emerging light industries such as the Weather Factory.
The appointment of Feather Flatterfly as the Empire’s Chief Economic Advisor was a major turning point for the Chamber of Commerce. After Rolling Thunder fell from power, Feather Flatterfly was allowed to practice his new economic theories in the Greater Cloudsdale area, starting a year-long era of free trade. During this year, Cloudsdale’s economy developed rapidly, but the lack of regulation allowed profit-seeking businesses like the arms dealers represented by Lightning Dust and the media conglomerates represented by Wind Rider to run rampant in Cloudsdale. Eventually, Derpy’s election ended Feather Flatterfly’s promising experiment. The Chamber of Commerce transformed from an official organization into a private business association. Today, the Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce is almost almost owned by Wind Rider and is trying to create chaos in various regions such as Stratusburg and Cloudsdale to create opportunities for its own enterprises.
