[zh.@desc]
当重组后的闪电天马掠过层云堡的天际线时，整座城市屏住了呼吸。黄蓝相间的身影撕裂云层，尾迹在夕阳下拖曳出流光的弧线 —— 仿佛谐律精华从未破碎，仿佛战争从未发生。
领队的飞火昂起头，翅膀的每一次拍打都精准如钟表。她身后，雾轨和破空以完美的编队紧随，气流在他们蹄下编织成无形的阶梯。
“就是现在！” 当编队继续向前飞行即将抵达坎特洛特时，飞火长嘶一声。三匹飞马同时加速，云层在激波中坍缩，蒸发，最终——

轰！
彩虹音爆在坎特洛特上空炸开，半径是云宝黛西当年纪录的三倍。七彩的涟漪扫过大地，风之魔的残影尖叫着消散，被奴役的小马们突然停下了工作。他们的鬃毛无风自动，眼中亮起陌生的金光。
传单如雪片般落下。
贵族用颤抖的蹄子拾起一张。
秘密警察的枪掉在了地上。
皇宫的露台上，和煦光流揉碎纸页，却听见自己的声音在不受控制地高喊：
“控制台是魔法！”

——接着，世界卡顿了。
天空像故障的屏幕般闪烁，飞火的翅膀突然定格在俯冲姿态，破空的笑容凝固成像素化的马赛克。天边传来雾轨轻轻的叹息声：“...连控制台都觉得这太荒谬了。”

[en.@desc]
As the reborn Wonderbolts streaked across Stratusburg’s skyline, the city held its breath. Their yellow-and-blue forms tore through the clouds, contrails painting the sunset — as if the Elements had never shattered, as if the war had never been.
Spitfire led with wings moving like clockwork. Behind her, Vapor Trail and Sky Stinger followed in flawless formation, their wake weaving an invisible stairway to the heavens.
“Now!” As they approached Canterlot, Spitfire shouted. The three Wonderbolts accelerated, the clouds collapsing and evaporating in their shockwave, and finally —

BOOM!
The sonic rainboom detonated over Canterlot, triple the radius of Rainbow Dash’s legendary blast. Its shockwave scoured the land, dissolving the Windigos’ remnants, freezing enslaved ponies mid-labor. Their manes lifted without wind, eyes glowing with unnatural light.
Pamphlets rained down.
Nobles picked one up with shaking hooves.
Secret police officers dropped their weapon.
On the palace balcony, Cozy Glow shredded a page — only to hear her own voice scream:
“CONSOLE IS MAGIC!”

— Then, the world glitched.
The sky flickered like a broken screen. Spitfire’s wings froze mid-dive. Sky Stinger’s grin pixelated into jagged cubes. A soft sigh echoed from the horizon, it was Vapor Trail, “... even the console found it too absurd.”
