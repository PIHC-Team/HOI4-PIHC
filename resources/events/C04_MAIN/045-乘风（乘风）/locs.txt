[zh.@desc]
在水晶工业彻底放开后不久，乘风和他的商业委员会下的注册商马们就很快垄断了廷马克图水晶的开采、加工与销售。云中城商业委员会摇身一变几乎成了廷马克图商业委员会。
但商马的欲望从不会停止与在一个行业实现垄断，商业委员会的成员们开始讲视角看向层云堡及其周边地区的各个行业：层云堡的餐饮生意、淘汰者的军火生意、彩虹工厂的“天马快乐水”、天气工厂的供暖行业... 他们全都要。
这便是乘风所索取的专营权的真正影响和最终结果，一个足以靠资金实力干预廷马克图地区，乃至整个层云堡的庞然巨物。除经济影响外，乘风还在积极寻求政治影响力，过分热情地尝试与层云堡政府合作、参与到政府的每一项行动中来...

[en.@desc]
Soon after the crystal industry was completely opened up, the registered merchants under Wind Rider and his Chamber of Commerce quickly monopolized the mining, processing, and sales of crystals in Timbucktu. The Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce has almost transformed into the Timbucktu Chamber of Commerce.
But the merchants’ desires never stop at monopolizing one industry, and the members of the Chamber of Commerce began to look at various industries in Cloudsdale and its surrounding areas: the catering business in Cloudsdale, the arms business of the Washouts, the “Pegasus Joy Juice” from the Rainbow Factory, and the heating industry of the Weather Factory... They want it all.
This is the true impact and final result of the monopoly rights that Wind Rider demanded, a behemoth powerful enough to intervene in the Timbucktu region and even the entire Stratusburg with just financial strength. Besides financial influence, Wind Rider is also actively seeking political influence, overly enthusiastically trying to cooperate with the Stratusburg government and participate in every action of the government...
