Stratusburg Shooting Incident
[zh.@desc]
层云堡枪击案让这个没什么存在感的小镇终于罕见地登上了一次新闻头条：昨夜，层云堡自治领领长在层云堡餐馆遭到不明身份小马的枪击，现已经被送往层云堡医院进行急救，暂未脱离生命危险。
帝国女皇和煦光流第一时间对此事表示了关切，强烈谴责了刺杀一名帝国自治领公职小马的行为，并表示将会排除秩序部与友谊部协助层云堡政府调查此案。担任代理领长破空，喷火的学徒，拒绝了帝国的此次协助，表示层云堡会独立调查此案。
[en.@desc]
The Stratusburg Shooting Incident finally thrust this otherwise unremarkable town into the headlines: last night, the governer of the Stratusburg Dominion was shot at the Stratusburg Diner by an unidentified assailant. She has since been rushed to Stratusburg Hospital for emergency treatment and remains in critical condition.
Empress Cozy Glow was quick to address the incident, vehemently condemning this “brazen attack on an imperial civil servant” and announcing plans to deploy Ministry of Order and Ministry of Friendship investigators to assist the local government. However, the acting governer Sky Stinger, Spitfire’s apprentice, declined the Empire’s assistance, asserting that Stratusburg would conduct its own investigation.
