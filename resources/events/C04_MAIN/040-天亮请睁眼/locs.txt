The Sun Rises
[zh.@desc]
今天是层云堡的大日子。才刚刚清晨，太阳的光芒甚至还没能浮现到云层之上时，在刚有微光的层云堡市政府会议室内就已经挤满了天马们：谐律主义学徒与层云堡餐饮集团创始者破空，层云堡政府顾问兼萼叶民兵领袖雾轨，云中城商业委员会主席乘风，还有一众前闪电天马队成员或学员们，齐聚一堂。
破空早已将演讲稿准备好，还特地检查了三四遍这次的演讲稿反面没有自己的涂鸦；雾轨则面无表情地坐在一旁，一会儿看着破空，一会儿又看看窗外的风景，没有小马知道她在想什么。乘风则是坐在桌边，手里拿着一杯星璇咖啡自顾自地喝着...
...
当第一缕阳光终于透过云层的缝隙从下方射来，将会议室的天花板染成橙红色时，破空已经第五次展开演讲稿，薄云纸边缘已被汗水洇出波浪纹。雾轨忽然起身走到窗边，云玻璃映出她瞳孔里跳动的微光 —— 东方云层开始渗出铁锈色。
市政府外的云层上，闪电天马队的一批年轻面孔正全副武装，穿着红蓝相间的制服随时准备起飞，但也因这漫长的等待而显得有些不安。乘风用汤匙拨弄着咖啡杯底部残留的咖啡渍，似乎想从中看出些什么，又放弃了，打了个哈欠。当乘风放下汤匙的时候，整个会议室里就只剩下了破空反复折叠演讲稿的声音。
...
当白得发亮的阳光终于彻底穿透了云层之时，会议室的大门才被打开，走进来的却并非喷火而是一位满脸惊恐的萼叶民兵。
“是帝国打过来了吗？” 乘风见状立即丢掉了咖啡杯上前询问，一副马上就要冲出会议室离开层云堡的样子；破空猛地抬起头丢掉了稿子，雾轨也猛地从窗边转身。
“是... 喷火领长... 在餐馆后面的苹果摊...” 萼叶民兵翅膀上融化的冰晶混着血迹滴落，“我们发现她倒在那里... 身上全都是血...”

太阳终于升到了云层之上，血红色的朝阳完整地出现在了窗外，朝阳如目光般直直地从盯着会议室。

[en.@desc]
Today was Stratusburg’s momentous day. In the dim pre-dawn light, the city hall meeting room already teemed with pegasi: Sky Stinger, Harmonism apprentice and founder of Stratusburg Diner; Vapor Trail, Stratusburg government consultant and leader of the Sepal Militia; Wind Rider, chairpony of the Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce; along with former Wonderbolt members and trainees.
Sky Stinger had prepared his speech, even triple-checked for absent-minded doodles on the reverse side of the speech draft. Vapor Trail sat impassively, her gaze alternating between Sky Stinger and the window’s vista, her thoughts a mystery to all. Wind Rider sat at the table, sipping his Starswirl coffee, lost in his own world, seemingly oblivious to the gathering tension in the room.
...
As the first rays of sunlight finally pierced through the gaps in the clouds from below, dyeing the meeting room’s ceiling in hues of orange and red, Sky Stinger had already unfolded his speech draft for the fifth time. The edges of the thin cloud-paper were warped from sweat. Vapor Trail abruptly rose and walked to the window, her reflection in the cloud-glass revealing faint flickers of light in her pupils — the eastern clouds now bled a rusty hue.
Outside the city hall, a group of young Wonderbolt recruits stood fully armored in their red-and-blue uniforms, ready to take flight, though the prolonged wait had visibly frayed their composure. Wind Rider stirred the coffee grounds at the bottom of his cup with a spoon, as if searching for some hidden meaning, before giving up with a yawn. When the spoon clinked against the porcelain, the only sound left in the room was the relentless crinkling of Sky Stinger’s speech draft.
...
By the time the blinding-white sunlight fully breached the cloud layer, the meeting room door finally swung open. But instead of Spitfire, an ashen-faced Sepal Militia member stumbled in.
“Has the Empire attacked?” Wind Rider blurted, tossing his coffee cup aside and surging forward, already poised to bolt from the room. Sky Stinger jerked his head up, speech forgotten, while Vapor Trail whipped around from the window.
“It’s... Governer Spitfire...” The militia member’s wings trembled, melted ice crystals mingling with blood dripping onto the floor. “We found her collapsed behind the apple pie stand behind the diner... She’s covered in blood when we found her...”

The sun now loomed fully above the clouds, its crimson dawn spilling mercilessly through the window. The light hung like a gaze, unblinking and accusatory, piercing straight into the heart of the room.
