淘汰者档案：火药桶
[en.@]
The Washouts Profile: Short Fuse

[zh.@desc]
不同于前面两位野心巨大的领导者，火药桶算是淘汰者中相对平庸的一个。他在闪电天马学院中能力并不突出，被开除的原因是无法控制自己的脾气。
火药桶关心的只有自己，他的生活大概只有飞行、饮食与发脾气。对于利益，他斤斤计较；对于朋友，他也不会有太多的忠诚；对于飞行技术，只能说他从未考虑过减肥。
在淘汰者组建初期，火药桶因为看到闪尘的表演大受欢迎、门票销售利润巨大而加入了淘汰者；随后又因为权力和财富跟从滚雷，成为了云中城安保部队的一员。在滚雷升任云中城大区区长时，他便接替了滚雷的位置，成为了云中城安保部队的新领导者。在任期间，他没有做过什么如滚雷一样的坏事，但也没有做过什么好事，只管拿着帝国的薪水，搜刮着云中城的财富。当滚雷被解职时，小跟班火药桶也跟着失去了一切。
虽说如今商业委员会与淘汰者是关系亲密，但假若商业委员会要在淘汰者中培养内线，那火药桶绝对是他们最好的选择。商业委员会与层云堡当局都相信，只要给的足够多，身为淘汰者成员的火药桶也并不介意为其他组织合作。毕竟，这在历史上差点发生过。
唯一需要注意的，大概就是和他讨价还价的时候，要小心他的脾气。尽管难以置信，但是他真的有在努力控制自己的脾气了。
[en.@desc]
Unlike the other two ambitious leaders, Short Fuse is relatively mediocre among the Washouts. He was expelled from the Wonderbolts Academy for his inability to control his temper.
Short Fuse only cares about himself. His life consists of three things only: flying, eating, and losing his temper. He is very concerned about his own interests, has little loyalty to his friends, and has never considered losing weight to improve his flying skills.
In the early days of the Washouts, Short Fuse joined the group because he saw Lightning Dust’s performance was very popular and the ticket sales were huge. Later, he followed Rolling Thunder for power and wealth, becoming a member of the Cloudsdale security forces. When Rolling Thunder was promoted to the District head of the Greater Cloudsdale area, Short Fuse took over her position and became the new leader of the Cloudsdale security forces. During his tenure, he did not do anything as bad as Rolling Thunder, but he did not do anything good either. He just took the Empire’s salary and plundered Cloudsdale’s wealth, as the job required. When Rolling Thunder was dismissed, Short Fuse lost everything as well.
Although the Chamber of Commerce and the Washouts have a close relationship now, if the Chamber of Commerce wants to cultivate an insider in the Washouts, Short Fuse is definitely their best choice. Both the Chamber of Commerce and our Stratusburg authorities believe that as long as we give him enough, Short Fuse, as a member of the Washouts, does not mind cooperating with any organization. After all, this has almost happened in history.
The only thing to be careful of, when bargaining with him, is his temper. You may find it hard to believe, but he is really trying to control his temper.
