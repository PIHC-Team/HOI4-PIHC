《云中城-层云堡贸易协定》
[en.@]
Cloudsdale-Stratusburg Trade Agreement

[zh.@desc]
云中城的代表再次来访，带回了呆蹄领主的旨意：她了解到层云堡居民许多是退役的闪电天马队成员或者闪电天马学院学员，于是提议云中城将闪电天马学院原址划归到层云堡管理，以此交换层云堡一年的马芬产量。如此，云中城不需要支付任何费用，而层云堡也可以获得闪电天马学院旧址。
自闪电天马队解散后，闪电天马学院也一直处于荒废状态... 直到呆蹄领主决定将这座空中小岛改造为马芬乐园。如今，闪电天马学院的多数飞行训练设施仍被保留，只是新增了一些马芬贴纸与玩偶，还额外开设了制作马芬的食堂...

[en.@desc]
The Cloudsdale representatives have returned, carrying the will of their Duchess Derpy Hooves: she has learned that many of the residents of Stratusburg are retired Wonderbolts or former students of the Wonderbolts Academy. Therefore, she proposed that Cloudsdale transfer the original site of the Wonderbolts Academy to Stratusburg’s management in exchange for a year’s worth of muffin production. In this way, Cloudsdale does not need to pay any fees, and Stratusburg can obtain the old site of the Wonderbolts Academy.
Since the disbandment of the Wonderbolts, the Wonderbolts Academy has been in a state of disrepair... Until recently, Duchess Derpy Hooves decided to transform this floating island into a muffin paradise. Today, most of the flying training facilities of the Wonderbolts Academy are still preserved, with the addition of some muffin stickers and dolls, and an additional cafeteria for making muffins...
