当乘风踏入喷火的办公室时，喷火已经泡好了冒着热气的星璇咖啡，等着他了。乘风做下，刚喝了一口咖啡，还没等他咽下，喷火就抢先开了口：“天气工厂，算了吧。我打算交给羽蝶了。”
乘风差点要讲咖啡喷出来，猛地坐直，被咖啡呛了几口，咳嗽了几声，才缓过来劲。趁此机会，喷火不给乘风回话的时间，赶紧继续说：“我不信任帝国的官员。但是帝国在层云堡不会作威作福太久了，所以我打算把天气工厂划给羽蝶，到时候我们就能找个借口收归层云堡政府了。今天上午我已经和羽蝶谈过了。这事和你没什么关系，我们也不希望到时候收归政府的时候，坐在桌子那头的是你。没有什么个马恩怨，只是生意，希望你能理解。”
乘风愣了几秒，刚想要张口，再次被喷火打断：“不过我邀请你来当然不是因为这个原因。而是彩虹工厂，我希望你能劝说一下劳雷特小姐...”

When the crystal industry’s profits had been nearly divided up, Wind Rider set his sights on the next opportunity — the newly established Weather Bureau and its subsidiaries: the Weather Factory and the New Weather Laboratory.
Upon entering Spitfire’s office, Wind Rider found her already waiting with steaming Starswirl coffee prepared. As he took his first sip, barely having a chance to swallow, Spitfire cut straight to the chase: “The Weather Factory, just drop it. I’m handing it over to Feather Flatterfly.”
Wind Rider nearly spat out his coffee, sitting bolt upright, coughing violently as the hot liquid went down wrong. Seizing the moment while he recovered, Spitfire pressed on without letting him respond: “I don’t trust Imperial officials. But the Empire won’t be throwing its weight around Stratusburg much longer. By assigning the factory to Feather Flatterfly now, we’ll have grounds to reclaim it for ourselves later. I’ve already discussed this with him this morning. This matter doesn’t concern you, and we don’t want you be the one sitting at the table when we reclaim the Weather Factory. It’s just business. I hope you understand.”
Wind Rider sat stunned for several seconds. Just as he opened his mouth to protest — “But that’s not why I called you here,” Spitfire interjected smoothly. “The Rainbow Factory... I need you to have a word with Miss Laurette about...”
