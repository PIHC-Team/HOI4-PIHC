[zh.@desc]
在水晶工业彻底放开后不久，乘风和他的商业委员会下的注册商马们就很快垄断了廷马克图水晶的开采、加工与销售。云中城商业委员会摇身一变几乎成了廷马克图商业委员会。
但商马的欲望从不会停止与在一个行业实现垄断，商业委员会的成员们开始讲视角看向层云堡及其周边地区的各个行业：层云堡的餐饮生意、淘汰者的军火生意、彩虹工厂的“天马快乐水”、天气工厂的供暖行业... 他们全都要。
这便是羽蝶的“自由贸易”所没能预料到的后果 —— 不加限制的竞争总是导致一个通吃的赢者。如今的乘风不仅几乎独享了羽蝶改革的全部收益，更是准备将天气工厂也兼并到商业委员会中。也难怪羽蝶的改革没能在帝国推行成功 —— 就帝国的那些盘根错节的利益关系来说，羽蝶的“自由贸易”不过是那些秘密警察和贵族官僚们敛财的新途径罢了。虽然和煦光流拒绝羽蝶的提议也许是出于私利，但这倒是阴差阳错避免了一场悲剧。

[en.@desc]
Soon after the crystal industry was completely opened up, the registered merchants under Wind Rider and his Chamber of Commerce quickly monopolized the mining, processing, and sales of crystals in Timbucktu. The Cloudsdale Chamber of Commerce has almost transformed into the Timbucktu Chamber of Commerce.
But the merchants’ desires never stop at monopolizing one industry, and the members of the Chamber of Commerce began to look at various industries in Cloudsdale and its surrounding areas: the catering business in Cloudsdale, the arms business of the Washouts, the “Pegasus Joy Juice” from the Rainbow Factory, and the heating industry of the Weather Factory... They want it all.
This is the result that Feather Flatterfly’s “free trade” failed to foresee — unrestricted competition always leads to a winner that takes all. Now, Wind Rider not only almost exclusively enjoys all the benefits of Feather Flatterfly’s reforms, but is also preparing to merge the Weather Factory into the Chamber of Commerce. It’s no wonder that Feather Flatterfly’s reforms failed to be implemented in the Empire — given the Empire’s tangled web of interests, Feather Flatterfly’s “free trade” was just a new way for those secret police and noble bureaucrats to make money. Although Warm Light Flow’s rejection of Feather Flatterfly’s proposal may have been out of self-interest, it inadvertently avoided a tragedy.
