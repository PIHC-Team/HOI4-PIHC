[zh.@desc]
在云中城采购商交涉的过程中，他们向喷火展示了一批保留下来的马芬样品：这些马芬看起来很正常，但是遭遇剧烈的温度变化或者快速移动时就会发生爆炸。云中城方面表示这些马芬是从层云堡进口的，但喷火对层云堡的几家餐厅都了如指掌，这绝对不是层云堡的产品。
喷火原以为对方会要求层云堡提供完整的出口记录表、马芬厂商名单以证明这些马芬的来源，但来自但是... 价格呢？云中城代表接到的任务是带回尽可能多的马芬，因此他们直接表示了信任（大概是受到了伟大的呆蹄领主的指示），并进一步提出了将层云堡的马芬进口正式化、规模化的提案。喷火有些惊讶，但随即想起了来自破空与雾轨的层云堡餐厅扩张计划，这似乎是一个不错的机会。

[en.@desc]
During the negotiation process with the buyers in Cloudsdale, they showed to Spitfire, a batch of reserved muffin samples: these muffins look normal, but they will explode when subjected to drastic temperature changes or rapid movement. Cloudsdale side claimed that these muffins were imported from Stratusburg, but Spitfire is very familiar with several restaurants in Stratusburg, and this is definitely not a product of Stratusburg.
Spitfire originally thought that the Cloudsdale representatives would ask Stratusburg to provide complete export records, a list of muffin manufacturers to prove the source of these muffins, but the representatives directly expressed their trust (presumably from the great Duchess Derpy’s instructions), and further proposed the proposal to formalize and scale the import of muffins from Stratusburg.
Spitfire was a little surprised, but then remembered the Stratusburg Diner Expansion Plan from Sky Stinger, which seems like a good opportunity now.
