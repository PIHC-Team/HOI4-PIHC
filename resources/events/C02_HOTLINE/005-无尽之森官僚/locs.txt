小马谷的官僚主义
[en.@]
Pontville’s Bureaucracy
[zh.@desc]
小马谷的官僚主义似乎存在抬头的趋势，相较于数年前梅耶任镇长期间，小马谷政府的公务员数量翻了近三倍。政府对此解释称，这是因为小马谷政府如今需要管理整个小马谷大区而不是仅仅是小马谷镇，因此自然需要更多的公务员来处理更多的事务。但如此庞大的政府机构是否真的能够更好地服务小马谷的居民，这一问题仍然存在争议。冗余的政府机构无疑在给小马谷的财政带来了巨大的压力，例如，在无尽之森边缘的一个小小路口，就雇佣了33名清洁工！
自从小马谷政府的官僚主义问题被曝光之后，小马谷的居民们纷纷表示对此感到不满。一位路过的商队小马在接受采访时表示：“我不明白为什么我们需要这么多的清洁工，他们的工资都是由我们辛苦经商的税金支付的，而我们却没有看到他们为我们做出什么贡献。”
[en.@desc]
There is a concerning trend of bureaucracy in Pontville, with the number of government officials nearly tripling compared to the time when Mayor May was in office a few years ago. The government explained that this is because the Pontville government now needs to manage the entire Pontville region rather than just the town of Pontville, so naturally more officials are needed to handle more affairs. However, there is still controversy over whether such a large government institution can really better serve the residents of Pontville. The redundant government institutions undoubtedly put huge pressure on Pontville’s finances, for example, 33 cleaners are employed at a small intersection on the edge of the Everfree Forest!
Since the bureaucracy problem of the Pontville government was exposed, the residents of Pontville have expressed dissatisfaction. A passing merchant pony said in an interview: “I don’t understand why we need so many cleaners. Their salaries are paid by the taxes we work hard to pay, and we don’t see what they contribute to us.”
