无尽之森事故
[en.@]
The Everfree Forest Incident
[zh.@desc]
近日，一只从小马谷前往坎特洛特的商队在无尽之森附近受困——他们的货车被无尽之森中生长的藤蔓缠住，无法前行。在附近小马发现后，小马谷政府立即组织了一支救援队伍，前往无尽之森救援。
最终，救援队伍成功将商队救出，然而商队的货物也因此损失惨重，预估损失高达数万金币。政府表示，将会对商队进行赔偿，同时也会对无尽之森的藤蔓进行清理工作，以避免类似事件再次发生。
据可靠消息称，早在事故发生前两个月，小马谷公爵就已经收到来自无尽之森附近的基层清洁工作者的警告，但是因为种种原因，小马谷市府并没有采取任何行动。
[en.@desc]
Recently, a caravan from Ponyville to Canterlot was stranded near the Everfree Forest — their cart was entangled by vines growing in the Everfree Forest and could not move forward. After being discovered by nearby ponies, the Ponyville government immediately organized a rescue team to go to the Everfree Forest for rescue.
In the end, the rescue team successfully rescued the caravan, but the caravan’s cargo also suffered heavy losses, with estimated losses of up to tens of thousands of bits. The government stated that it will compensate the caravan, and will also clean up the vines in the Everfree Forest to prevent similar incidents from happening again.
According to reliable sources, two months before the accident, the Duchess of Ponyville had already received warnings from cleaners near the Everfree Forest, but for various reasons, the Ponyville government did not take any action.
