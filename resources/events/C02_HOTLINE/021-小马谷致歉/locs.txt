小马谷爆炸案新闻发布会
[en.@]
Press Conference on Ponyville Explosion Case
[zh.@desc]
针对前几日的爆炸案，小马谷政府召开了正式新闻发布会。
首先，爆炸的起因是小马谷政府批准的一项科研项目，由蹄博士主持，但是由于实验失控，导致了爆炸。
公爵特丽克西首先发言，表示对此次事件的深切悼念，并且对受害者家属表示诚挚的慰问。她承认爆炸的责任在于小马谷政府未能尽到监管责任，对此深感愧疚。
随后，是蹄博士的发言，然而，在谈及爆炸原因的时候，蹄博士却似乎是走神了，他安静了足足一分钟没有进行发言，随后一句小声地“抱歉”就直接起身离开了会场。
作为爆炸的项目主持者，蹄博士的这种态度引起了在场媒体的强烈不满，但是由于小马谷政府的干预，这一不妥的发言并没有被媒体广泛报道。
[en.@desc]
To address the explosion case a few days ago, the Ponyville government held a formal press conference.
First of all, the cause of the explosion was a scientific research project approved by the Ponyville government, presided over by Dr. Hooves, but due to the out-of-control experiment, it led to the explosion.
The Duchess Trixies first spoke, expressing deep condolences for the event and sincere condolences to the families of the victims. She admitted that the responsibility for the explosion was due to the failure of the Ponyville government to fulfill its supervisory responsibilities, and felt guilty about it.
Next was Dr. Hooves’ speech, however, when it came to the cause of the explosion, Dr. Hooves seemed to be distracted, he was quiet for a full minute without speaking, and then a soft “sorry” and he left the venue directly.
As the project leader and the direct responsible pony for the explosion, Dr. Hooves’ attitude has caused strong dissatisfaction among the media present, but due to the intervention of the Ponyville government, this inappropriate speech has not been widely reported by the media.
