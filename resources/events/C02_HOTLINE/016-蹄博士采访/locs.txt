采访蹄博士
[en.@]
Interview with Dr. Hooves
[zh.@desc]
在近日对小马谷著名科学家蹄博士的采访中，虽然蹄博士对小马谷政府和公爵给出了高度的正面评价，但言语中似乎透露着政府对于科研支持不足的担忧。
蹄博士认为，现届政府对科研项目申请的批复速度过慢，导致了许多科研项目的进度受阻，甚至由于资金不足导致项目中断。他表示，希望政府能够加快批复速度，以便科研项目能够更快地进行。
蹄博士表示：“当然，政府在其他方面的工作还是做得很好的。我也能理解政府有太多事务要处理，只是希望政府能够在科研方面多加支持。”
采访一经播出，引起科学界的共鸣，许多科研工作者纷纷表示对蹄博士的言论表示赞同，并希望这一问题能引起不仅是小马谷政府，也是帝国政府的重视。
[en.@desc]
Recently, in an interview with Dr. Hooves, a famous scientist in Ponyville, despite giving high praise to the Ponyville government and the Duchess, there seems to be a concern about the lack of support for scientific research in the government.
Dr. Hooves believes that the current government’s approval speed for scientific research project applications is too slow, which has hindered the progress of many scientific research projects, and even caused project interruptions due to insufficient funds. He said that he hopes the government can speed up the approval process so that scientific research projects can proceed more quickly.
Dr. Hooves also said: “Of course, the government is doing well in other aspects. I can also understand that the government has too many affairs to deal with. I just hope that the government can provide more support in scientific research.”
The interview has resonated in the scientific community, and many researchers have expressed their agreement with Dr. Hooves’ remarks, hoping that this issue will be taken seriously not only by the Ponyville government, but also by the Empire government.
