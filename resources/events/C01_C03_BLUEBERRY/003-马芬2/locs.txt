Very early in the morning, the messenger of the Equestria Empire came to the house of Duchess Derpy, who was about to have breakfast.
“Duchess Derpy, good morning! The Equestria Empire has sent some gifts. Several members of the Unity Committee in Cloudsdale said that the Empire is very grateful for your contribution to the construction of Cloudsdale during your tenure as Duchess, and hope you will like these muffins.”

“Wow! Free muffins! Oh wait... what a pity, today is ‘Muffin Feedback Day’.”

“Muffin Feedback Day?” The messenger asked with some confusion.

“Yes, today is Muffin Feedback Day, so we have to send all the muffins back to their makers to commend them for their efforts in the muffin cause! Please inform the Unity Committee to make sure that the muffins are sent back to the factory so that the workhorses can all taste the delicious muffins! Okay?”

“But these were specially customized for you by the Unity Committee...” The messenger’s expression was a bit awkward.

“I really really want to eat them! But Muffin Feedback Day is a very important holiday, and we can’t go against its will! Send the muffins back!”
