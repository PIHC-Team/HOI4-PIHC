震惊！小马谷公爵领领主蹄博士，如今被成为一起谋杀指控的嫌疑犯！
帝国警方经过调查，已经掌握了蹄博士涉嫌毒杀一只云中城小马的证据。警方截获了蹄博士通过小马谷邮局发出的多封书信，并在受害者家中发现了带有致命毒药的马芬。
根据帝国秩序部已经掌握和公开的证据，我们推测，蹄博士层长期和受害者保持暧昧关系，在受害者离开小马谷前往云中城后，异地相隔让他们的关系逐渐减淡。期间，蹄博士多次单方面向受害者寄出表达爱意的信件，但都未得到积极回应，因此冲动产生了情杀的念头。

Astonishing News! The Duke of Ponyville, Dr. Hooves, is now implicated in a murder accusation!
According to the investigation of the imperial police, evidence shown that Dr. Hooves is responsible for poisoning a pony in Cloudsdale. The police intercepted several letters sent by Dr. Hooves through the Ponyville post office and found a muffin laced with a lethal poison in the victim’s home.
Based on the evidence that the EEMO has already obtained and made public, we speculate that Dr. Hooves had a long-standing ambiguous relationship with the victim. When the victim left Ponyville for Cloudsdale, their relationship quickly cooled down. During this time, Dr. Hooves unilaterally sent several letters expressing love to the victim, but did not receive a positive response, leading to impulsive thoughts of a crime of passion.
The central government of the Equestria Empire has stated that the Empire never overlooks the moral requirements of its Dukes and Duchesses and will not tolerate a murderer continuing to serve as a Duke of the Empire. Empress Cozy Glow has just signed and issued a dismissal order and arrest warrant. Currently, Dr. Hooves’ whereabouts are unknown. Meanwhile, the Equestria Unity Committee will act as the temporary ruler of Ponyville and Cloudsdale.
Please, vigilant citizens, pay close attention to your surroundings. If you see a blue telephone booth, please contact the imperial police immediately! The Equestria Empire appreciates your cooperation!
