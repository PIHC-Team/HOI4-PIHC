Morning, the messenger of the Equestria Empire came to the house of Duchess Derpy. They represented the Empire and brought gifts to Duchess Derpy — several boxes of muffins.
However, they were stopped at the door: “Duchess Derpy is having her breakfast. Come back after she finishes her breakfast to deliver the gifts.”

Two hours passed like this, and the messenger had become a little impatient. But there was still no movement in Duchess Derpy’s house, and the butler still refused to let the messenger in, so the messenger could only continue to wait.
Although at this time, the messenger was a little impatient and wanted to break in by virtue of the identity of the Empire’s messenger, he thought about it, muttered softly, “But her drugs are losing efficacy...” and then turned and left.

“What did you say, sir?”
“Oh, I said ‘butter cups are melting easily’, I’ll bring some new muffins in a while.”
