一日傍晚时分，一份小礼盒被送到了云中城领主和呆蹄的家中。盒子上贴着一张小纸条：
“恭喜您！您刚刚在我们公司举办的抽奖活动中中了一等奖！我们为您发放的奖品是：一盒巧克力马芬！请您尽快食用，以免影响口感！”
“请注意！该奖品为限量版，且为您专属定制，不可转让！否则我们将保留追究法律责任的权利！”

回到家中的呆蹄领主看到了这份礼物，打开盒子，起初十分惊喜：整整一盒子的马芬！但当她看到纸条上的字时，又仔细看了看盒子里面的马芬——没错，全是巧克力马芬。
她的健康医生刚刚建议她最近几个月少吃巧克力等甜食，多吃些水果口味的马芬。所以在那个深夜，与自己搏斗了十几次的呆蹄，最终还是选择将礼物扔进了垃圾桶。
“不可转让！真可惜！多么浪费！” 呆蹄领主叹了口气。

One evening, a small gift box was delivered to the home of the Duchess of Cloudsdale, Derpy. There was a small note on the box:
“Congratulations! You have just won the first prize in the lottery held by our company! The prize we have issued to you is: a box of chocolate muffins! Please consume it as soon as possible to avoid affecting the taste!”
“Please note! This prize is a limited edition and is exclusively customized for you, and cannot be transferred! Otherwise, we reserve the right to pursue legal responsibility!”

When Duchess Derpy returned home and saw this gift, she opened the box and was initially very surprised: a whole box of muffins! But when she saw the words on the note, she carefully looked at the muffins inside the box again — yes, they were all chocolate muffins.
Her health doctor had just advised her to eat less chocolate and more fruit-flavored muffins in the next few months. So on that late night, Duchess Derpy, who had struggled with herself back and forth more than a dozen times, finally chose to throw the gift into the trash can.
“Cannot be transferred! Awwwwwwwww! What a waste!” Duchess Derpy sighed.
