虽然我们敬爱的女王魔法能力强大，但在国家伟大领袖的安全问题上，我们不能有丝毫的大意。幻形灵中央情报局认为事态紧急，需要立即采取行动。
幻形灵帝国中央情报局提议：
1. 建立国家范围内的全面监视系统，确保未化茧幻形灵的思想纯洁性。监视的对象不仅包括公民，也包括幻形灵军队和政府高层：“没有谁可以逃脱怀疑”。
2. 女王近卫队应重新进行严格筛选排查，确保没有反叛分子混入。同时近卫队应加强对于女王的保护，以防止任何暗杀行动的发生。
3. 幻形灵中央情报局将在全国范围内发起对幻形灵内部不忠诚者的搜捕行动，以确保国家的安全。
由于上述行动成本较高，因此申请幻形灵帝国财政支持。出于对女王安全的考虑，我们请求女王尽快批准。

Evidence suggests that there still exists a very small number of unmetamorphosed changeling citizens who are dissatisfied with Queen Chrysalis, some of whom have infiltrated the upper echelons of changeling society, and there even exists assassination plans against Queen Chrysalis.
Although our beloved queen is powerful in magic, we cannot be complacent in the security of our great national leader. The Changeling Empire Central Intelligence Agency believes that the situation is urgent and requires immediate action.
CECIA proposes:
1. Establish a comprehensive surveillance system within the country to ensure the purity of thought of unmetamorphosed changelings. The surveillance targets not only citizens, but also the changeling army and the government’s upper echelons: “No one is above suspicion.”
2. The Queen’s Guard should undergo a strict re-screening to ensure that no rebels have infiltrated. At the same time, the Queen’s Guard should strengthen the protection of the queen to prevent any assassination attempts.
3. The Changeling Empire Central Intelligence Agency will launch a nationwide search for disloyal changelings to ensure the security of the country.
Due to the high cost of the above actions, we request financial support from the Changeling Empire. Out of concern for the queen’s safety, we request the queen’s approval as soon as possible.
