暗石——幻形灵帝国中央情报局报告
[en.@]
Dark Rock - Changeling Empire Central Intelligence Agency Report
[zh.@desc]
幻形灵帝国内遍布暗石矿——这种矿石具有特殊的魔法，能够削弱和屏蔽非幻形灵族的魔法。然而，由于其高昂的开采价格，和需求极高的激发与维护魔法量，其应用比较有限：目前只有邪茧女王的王座，以及幻形灵精锐部队有少量使用。
中央情报局提议与国防军、帝国战争科学研究院合作，研究暗石的应用：
1. 降低暗石的开采和后续使用成本，争取普及幻形灵军队中的暗石使用。
2. 暗石目前只能用于防御，应研究寻找暗石的攻击性应用。
3. 暗石目前只对非幻形灵有效，在对化茧幻形灵的镇压、以及对幻形灵的内部叛乱分子的抓捕中并无作用，应该考虑研究特化暗石，以应对这些情况。
根据当前的研究计划草稿，仅在试验阶段，预计需要使用的暗石数量就是当前全帝国开采量的3倍。因此，推进这一计划也有助于带动帝国采矿业的发展。
[en.@desc]
The Dark Rock is spread throughout the Changeling Empire. This ore has special magic that can weaken and shield the magic of non-changeling creatures. However, due to its high mining cost and the high demand for the magic to be activated and maintained, its application is relatively limited: currently only the throne of the Queen of the Chrysalis and a small number of elite changeling troops use it.
CECIA proposes to cooperate with the Defence Army and the Imperial War Science Research Institute to study the application of Dark Rock:
1. Reduce the cost of mining and subsequent use of Dark Rock, and strive to popularize the use of Dark Rock in the changeling army.
2. Dark Rock can only be used for defense at present, and research should be conducted to find its offensive applications.
3. Dark Rock is currently only effective against non-changeling creatures. It has no effect on suppressing the hatching of changelings and capturing internal rebel changelings. Specialized Dark Rock should be considered to deal with these situations.
According to the current draft of the research plan, only in the experimental stage, the expected amount of Dark Rock to be used is already three times the current total mining volume of the Empire. Therefore, promoting this plan will also help drive the development of the Empire’s mining industry.
