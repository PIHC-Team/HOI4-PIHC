在龙族的正式场合，余焰总是会佩戴一个号角徽章。这一徽章是卢瑟福公子赠送的，意在巩固牦牛与龙族的世代友好。但近日多次正式场合中，余焰并未佩戴这一徽章。情报局推测这可能是在表达对于今日牦牛氏族内部民族主义抬头，排斥龙族的若干事件的不满。稍加干预，我们也许可以恶化双方的关系。
[en.@desc]
During formal occasions of the Dragon Clan, Ember always wears a horn badge. This badge was given by Prince Rutherford, aiming to consolidate the generational friendship between the Yaks and the Dragons. However, in recent formal occasions, Ember did not wear this badge. CECIA speculates that this may be an expression of dissatisfaction with the recent rise of nationalism within the Yak tribe, which has led to the rejection of the Dragon Clan. With a little intervention, we may be able to worsen the relationship between the two sides.
