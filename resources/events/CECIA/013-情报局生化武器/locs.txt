Biological Weapons - Changeling Empire Central Intelligence Agency Report
[zh.@desc]
幻形灵中央情报局的触角遍布世界，其中自由小马利亚是我们重点关注的对象之一：自由小马利亚与另一个世界有所接触，一个被称为“人类”的生物所居住的世界。
据了解，“人类”这一生物在战争方面有着丰富的经验，他们的科技水平也远远超过了小马利亚大陆上的任何一个种族，而自由小马利亚正在计划将人类的战争科技引入到小马利亚大陆上。
在自由小马利亚的一份废稿中，我们的特工发现了一种名为“生化武器”的概念。“人类”这种生物认为，疾病，可以成为一种武器，可以用来对敌方的军队和平民进行无差别攻击。
即使幻形灵种族擅长战争和掠夺，但是我们此前并没有考虑过这种战争手段。自由小马利亚以“不符合道德”为由，拒绝了引入这一技术，但这不意味着幻形灵也同样拒绝。
情报局认为，这种新的战争概念值得我们深入研究。我们计划对小马利亚大陆流行的传染病进行深入研究：绿色流感、沼地热、可爱标志痘、天角流感等等。
我们将研究这些疾病的传播途径、潜伏期、症状、治疗方法、改造技术等等，以便我们在必要的时候，可以利用这些疾病对敌方进行打击。与此同时，我们也应该对幻形灵种族的抵抗力进行研究，以便我们在必要的时候，可以保护自己。
[en.@desc]
CECIA’s antennae is spread all over the world, and the ree Equestria is one of our key targets: the Free Equestria is in contact with another world, a world inhabited by a creature called “human”.
It is understood that the creature “human” has rich experience in war, and their technological level far exceeds any species on the continent of Equestria. The Free Equestria is planning to introduce human war technology to the continent of Equestria.
In a rejected draft from the Free Equestria, our agents discovered a concept called “biological weapons”. The creature “human” believes that diseases can be used as weapons to indiscriminately attack enemy armies and civilians.
As good as the Changelings are at war and plunder, we have not previously even considered this method of warfare. The Free Equestria rejected the introduction of this technology on the grounds of “immorality”, but that does not mean that the Changelings should also reject it.
CECIA believes that this new concept of war is worth further study. We plan to conduct in-depth research on the prevalent infectious diseases on the continent of Equestria: Green Flu, Swamp Fever, Cutie Pox, Alicorn Flu, etc.
We will study the transmission routes, incubation periods, symptoms, treatment methods, and modification techniques of these diseases, so that we can use these diseases to strike the enemy when necessary. At the same time, we should also study the resistance of the Changeling species, so that we can protect ourselves when necessary.
