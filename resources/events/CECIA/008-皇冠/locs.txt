§R「绝密」§!
自从邪茧女王宣布组建幻形灵中央情报局以来，情报局的发展迅速，也得到了来自邪茧女王的大力支持。如今，情报局的触角遍布世界，也拥有了局属武装力量，可以在全小马利亚范围内执行各类任务。
但邪茧女王没有意识到的是，情报局的监听的对象也包括她自己。“没有谁可以逃脱怀疑”——这是情报局的核心理念。收集邪茧女王有关情报的秘密小组直接汇报给情报局局长——欺诈者。该秘密小组仅由局长的亲信组成，外界几乎不知道这一秘密小组的存在。
比较值得担忧的是，最近的证据表明——我们敬爱的邪茧女王似乎正在变得软弱，她的决策正在变得越来越优柔寡断。秘密小组推测，这可能与“友谊”的侵蚀有关：早在终末之战时期，邪茧女王与和煦光流、提雷克的联盟就已经超越了单纯的“合作”关系，进入了不恰当的“友谊”范围。
秘密小组推测，邪茧女王正式出于对和煦光流的友谊，才迟迟没有批准幻形灵对小马种族全面战争的计划。
必要时，秘密小组相信应该对此采取行动，以确保幻形灵帝国和幻形灵种族的未来不受“友谊”的影响。
[en.@desc]
 §R<=TOP SECRET=>§! 
Since Queen Chrysalis announced the establishment of the Changeling Empire Central Intelligence Agency, the development of the agency has been rapid, and has also received strong support from Queen Chrysalis. Now, CECIA’s antennae is spread all over the world, and it also has its own armed forces, which can execute various tasks throughout the entire Equestria.
However, what Queen Chrysalis did not realize is that the agency’s monitoring targets also include herself. “No one is above suspicion” - this is the core concept of CECIA. The secret group that collects intelligence about Queen Chrysalis reports directly to the Director of the agency - Trickster. The secret group is composed only of the Director’s confidants, and the outside world is almost unaware of the existence of this secret group.
What is worrying is that recent evidence suggests that our beloved Queen Chrysalis seems to be weaker, and her decisions are becoming more and more indecisive. The secret group speculates that this may be related to the corruption by “friendship”: as early as the time of the Ultimate Battle, the alliance between Queen Chrysalis and Warm Light Flow and Trickster has already surpassed a simple “cooperation” relationship and entered an inappropriate “friendship” range.
The secret group speculates that Queen Chrysalis has been delaying the approval of the Changeling Empire’s plan for a full-scale war against the pony race out of her friendship with Cozy Glow.
When required, the secret group believes that action should be taken to ensure that the future of the Changeling Empire and the Changeling race is not affected by “friendship”.
