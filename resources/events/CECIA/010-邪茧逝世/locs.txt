A Sad Day for the Changeling Empire
[zh.@desc]
今日，幻形灵中央虫巢发布了一则简短而震惊世界的新闻——广受爱戴的幻形灵帝国女王，邪茧，因感染沼地热，在虫巢中逝世。
邪茧女王没有丈夫与后嗣，临终前，她紧急召见了幻形灵中央情报局局长欺诈者，将幻形灵帝国的统治重任委任于他。
邪茧女王逝世后，欺诈者进行了宣誓就职仪式，正式成为幻形灵帝国的统治者，承诺将会继续贯彻邪茧女王的意志，带领幻形灵族群走向更加美好的未来。今晚，他将在邪茧女王的葬礼上发表他的第一次演讲。
邪茧女王的突然逝世，在小马利亚大陆产生了广泛的担忧：欺诈者长久以来是邪茧女王的忠实追随者，极端程度相对于邪茧女王有过之而无不及。据了解，他在出任幻形灵中央情报局局长期间曾三次建议对小马的全面战争，都遭到邪茧女王的搁置。如今，似乎没有什么能够阻止他了。
[en.@desc]
Today, the Changeling Central Hive released a short but shocking news - the beloved Changeling Empire Queen, Chrysalis, died in the hive due to the infection of the swamp fever.
Queen Chrysalis had no husband nor heir. Before her death, she urgently summoned the Director of CECIA, Trickster, and entrusted him with the heavy responsibility of ruling the Changeling Empire.
After the death of Queen Chrysalis, Trickster held the inauguration ceremony and officially became the ruler of the Changeling Empire, promising to continue to implement the will of Queen Chrysalis and lead the Changeling race to a better future. Tonight, he will deliver his first speech at the funeral of Queen Chrysalis.
The sudden death of Queen Chrysalis has caused widespread concern in Equestria: Trickster has always been a loyal follower of Queen Chrysalis for a long time, and his radicalness is no less than that of Queen Chrysalis. It is understood that he has proposed three times for a full-scale war against ponies during his tenure as the Director of CECIA, all of which were shelved by Queen Chrysalis. Now, it seems that nothing can stop him anymore.
