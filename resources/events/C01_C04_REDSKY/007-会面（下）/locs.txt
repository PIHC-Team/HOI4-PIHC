喷火离开后，女皇房间的一扇暗门打开，花花短裤从阴影中走了出来，一边拍着蹄子一边称赞道：“多么精彩的表演啊，女皇陛下，您真是太厉害了！”
和煦光流也恢复到了原本傲慢的模样，“是花花短裤你的剧本写得好，我只是按照你的剧本演出而已。不过，你确定这样就能奏效么？”

入夜，层云堡对面的小餐馆里，庆祝的声音几乎要将屋顶掀翻。居民们本已经做好了最坏的打算——喷火可能没法活着回来，层云堡将与帝国军队爆发正面冲突。如今，不仅没有冲突，层云堡还从帝国那里得到了不少的好处，包括提升的7%补贴，以及战后的独立地位。这一切都是喷火的功劳，层云堡的居民们都在为喷火的胜利而庆祝。
流言沙沙则继续坐在角落里喝着酒，当有小马到他的桌前坐下的时候，他就会小声说几句：“你说，伟大的喷火究竟是怎么做到的呢？听喷火自己说，和煦光流态度180度大转弯，完全答应了她的条件，这怎么可能呢？我听到传闻说，和煦给了喷火一些个体的好处，有好几车金币呢，现在就停在市政厅后面。还有传言说是和煦给的是10%的补贴，而不是7%，剩下3%好像是喷火要求给自己的。你说，这是真的么？”
流言沙沙知道这里是层云堡最热闹的地方，一句话，就足够改变整座城镇的思想。

Spitfire left, and a secret door in the Empress’s room opened. Cozy Glow walked out of the shadow, clapping his hooves and praising, “What a wonderful performance, Empress, you are so amazing!”
Cozy Glow also returned to her original arrogant appearance, “It’s your script, Fancy Pants, I just performed according to your script. But, are you sure this will work?”

At night, the sound of celebration in the small diner opposite Stratusburg was almost enough to lift the roof. The residents had already prepared for the worst — Spitfire might not come back alive, and Stratusburg would have a direct conflict with the imperial army. Now, not only was there no conflict, but Stratusburg also received a lot of benefits from the Empire, including a 7% increase in subsidies and independent status after the war. All of this was thanks to Spitfire, and the residents of Stratusburg were celebrating Spitfire’s victory.
Rumor Rustle continued to sit in the corner drinking. When a pony sat down at his table, he would whisper, “I’m really curious, how did the great Spitfire do it? According to Spitfire’s own words, Cozy Glow’s attitude made a 180-degree turn and completely agreed to all her conditions. How is that possible? I heard rumors that Cozy Glow gave Spitfire some individual benefits, carts of gold coins, they are now parked behind the city hall. There are also rumors that Cozy Glow gave a 10% subsidy, not 7%, and the remaining 3% seems to be what Spitfire requested for herself. Do you think this is true?”
Rumor Rustle knew that this is the liveliest place in Stratusburg, and one sentence is enough to change the thoughts of the entire town.
