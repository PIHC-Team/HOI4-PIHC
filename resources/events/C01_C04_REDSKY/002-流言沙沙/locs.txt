黄昏，小马利亚帝国秩序部特工流言沙沙，走进了层云堡市政厅对面的餐馆。他点了一盘杯糕，一杯苹果汁，就在餐馆的角落里坐下来。
当太阳完全从地平线上消失时，餐馆内已经有不少小马，他们吃完了饭，在餐馆内聊天、看新闻或是打牌。又下肚了几杯苹果汁，流言沙沙混进了这些小马的谈话中。
装作醉醺醺的样子，他拖着长音说道：“...唔你们知道吗？我听说，嗝~ 和煦那个家伙，最近，最近又想来层云堡征兵了。” 群众中嘘声一片。
他紧接着说：“我就说，很搞笑吧？...诶！不过你别说，听说，听说这次征兵给的还挺多！我有个朋友，唔他拿，他拿，他拿到了整整一袋金币呢！” 说完，流言沙沙心中窃喜，这样应该就能引起一些小马的注意了。
流言沙沙知道这里是层云堡最热闹的地方，一句话，就足够改变整座城镇的思想。

第二天，小马利亚帝国的层云堡征兵处，访客数目依然为零。

At dusk, the special agent of the EEMO, Rumor Rustle, walked into the diner across from the town hall of Stratusburg. He ordered a plate of muffins and a glass of apple cider, then sat down in the corner of the diner.
When the sun completely disappeared from the horizon, there were already many ponies in the diner. They had finished their meals and were chatting, watching the news, or playing cards in the diner. After drinking a few more glasses of apple cider, Rumor Rustle mingled in the conversation of these ponies.
Pretending to be heavily drunk, he said with a long drawl, “... Eh, have you guys heard? I heard, burp~ that Cozy Glow, she recently, recently wants to recruit soldiers in Stratusburg again.” The crowd booed.
He continued, “Yeah! Isn’t it funny?... Hey! But anyway, don’t tell anyone, I heard, I heard that this time the recruitment is quite generous! I have a friend, uh, he got, he got, he got a whole bag of gold coins!” After speaking, Rumor Rustle was laughing inside, this should attract the attention of some ponies.
Rumor Rustle knew that this is the liveliest place in Stratusburg, and one sentence is enough to change the thoughts of the entire town.

The next day, the number of visitors to the Equestria Empire’s Stratusburg recruitment office remained zero.
