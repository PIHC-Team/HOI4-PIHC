Lost Outpost: Iron Wings
[zh.@desc]
角被安放在了钥匙孔内，队伍中的独角兽对着角注入魔法...

角开始发光，恢复魔力... 紧接着，角开始为大门充能。大门打开了，角也随之破裂。这个房间内有许多法器，以及许多蓝图，意外的保存完好。桌子上有一篇日志：
“这里实在是太冷了，天天都在刮暴风雪，我们不得不撤离。听说飓风司令已经打算找一片新的地方来定居，没有资源再支持哨站，我们的研究努力算是白费了。”

另一篇日志介绍了他们的研究努力：
“这个机器参考了鸟类展翅时的平飞姿态，运用魔法驱动，能以高速飞行，并装载武器，用以对空或对地攻击。因为其是能载小马飞行的机器，飓风司令亲自命名，将其称为‘飞机’。”

飞机的设计实在是新颖，与飞艇不同，它的速度更快，机动性更高。它的设计图纸肯定能促进我们航天方面的科技发展。
[en.@desc]
The horn is placed in the keyhole, and the unicorn in the team injects magic into the horn...

The horn begins to glow, restoring magic... Then, the horn begins to charge the gate. The gate opens, and the horn shatters. There are many magical instruments and blueprints in this room, all unexpectedly well-preserved. There is a log on the table:
“It’s too cold here, with blizzards every day. We have to evacuate. I heard that Commander Hurricane is planning to find a new place to settle down. There are no resources to support the outpost anymore, and our research efforts are in vain.”

Another log introduces their research efforts:
“This machine is designed based on the posture of birds when they spread their wings to glide. It is powered by magic, capable of high-speed flight, and can be armed for air-to-air or air-to-ground attacks. Because it is a machine that can carry ponies to fly, Commander Hurricane named it ‘airplane’.”

The design of the airplane is indeed novel. Unlike airships, it is faster and more maneuverable. Its blueprints will definitely promote our aerospace technology development.
