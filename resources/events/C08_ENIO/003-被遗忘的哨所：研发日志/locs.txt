Lost Outpost: Research Log
[zh.@desc]
万分惊险。那扇门一被打开，一道水流便冲了出来，门后面堵着的杂物“咚”地砸在地上，由此引发了连锁反应，整片地基如同地震般剧烈震动。

万幸我们加固的支撑结构足够稳固，不至于让哨所塌陷。从房内的废墟中，科考队找到了另一篇日志：
“...爆发冲突，许多士兵的翅膀受伤了，飓风司令派我们研发一种能让伤员重回天空的工具...”

余下部分因水浸而受损。日志中所记“研发成果”应在那扇上锁的门内，科考队正在清理房间，寻找钥匙。
[en.@desc]
It was a close call. As the door was opened, a stream of water rushed out, pushing the debris behind the door to the ground, triggering a chain reaction that caused the entire foundation to shake as if in an earthquake.

Fortunately, the support structure we reinforced was strong enough to prevent the outpost from collapsing. From the ruins inside the room, the research team found another log:
“... conflict broke out, many soldiers’ wings were injured, and Commander Hurricane sent us to develop a tool that would allow the wounded to return to the sky...”

The remaining part was damaged by water. The “research results” mentioned in the log should be inside the locked door, and the expedition team is cleaning the room to find the key.
