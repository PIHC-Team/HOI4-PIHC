[zh.@desc]
我们的科考队发现了一个哨所，它位于悬崖另一头的峭壁上，其外表看起来已经年久失修，确认不是商业联盟所建。

队伍中的天马事先观察了哨所外围，发现哨所的支撑结构不足以支持任意一只小马——准确来说，哨所根本没有支撑结构，而是几块石头，几根圆木搭的地基在苦苦支撑，似乎哨所下方从前并不是悬崖。

进行探索前，我们需要加固支撑结构，否则哨所会塌陷。
[en.@desc]
Our expedition team has discovered an outpost located on the cliff on the other side of the chasm. It looks like it has been abandoned for a long time and is not built by the Business Alliance.

Pegasi in our team observed the outpost from the outside and found that the support structure of the outpost is not enough to support any pony - to be precise, the outpost has no support structure at all apart from only a few stones and round logs. It seems that the ground below the outpost was not a cliff before.

Before we can explore the outpost, we need to reinforce the support structure, otherwise the outpost will collapse.
