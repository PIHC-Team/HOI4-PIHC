Liberation Front
[zh.@]
解放阵线
[en.@desc]
Whether called the Liberation Front or the Resistance, this organization established when Starlight Glimmer arrived at Our Town, has an ultimate goal of overthrowing the illegal empire of Cozy Glow, reclaiming all of Equestria, bringing down the “Big Three”, and restoring friendship and harmony to all ponies.
The Front is now united. Under Starlight’s leadership, the ponies within the Front are mostly able to reach a consensus and work together towards the goal of liberating Equestria. However, a significant number of ponies are still bickering over trivial matters such as resource allocation, putting the unity of the Front on the back burner.
