Cold Deck
[zh.@]
冷牌

[en.@desc]
When you shuffle poker cards, you may end up with bad luck. When you shuffle your government, you may end up with bad administration. But fear not, soon the shadowy dealer will take over the table.
[zh.@desc]
当你洗牌你的扑克时，你可能会遇到糟糕的运气。当你洗牌你的政府时，你可能会遇到低效的管理。但不要害怕，很快阴影中的荷官将会接管桌子。
