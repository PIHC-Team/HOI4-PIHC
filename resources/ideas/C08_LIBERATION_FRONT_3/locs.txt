Liberation Front
[zh.@]
解放阵线
[en.@desc]
Whether called the Liberation Front or the Resistance, this organization established when Starlight Glimmer arrived at Our Town, has an ultimate goal of overthrowing the illegal empire of Cozy Glow, reclaiming all of Equestria, bringing down the “Big Three”, and restoring friendship and harmony to all ponies.
The Front is currently in turmoil. Ponies are not thinking about how to unite and save Equestria, but are instead constantly preparing to cause trouble for the ponies they dislike. Every choice Starlight Glimmer makes affects the future of the Front, and she must be careful to unite every pony as much as possible while the situation is still salvageable.

§CUnite, ponies, for a brighter tomorrow...§!
