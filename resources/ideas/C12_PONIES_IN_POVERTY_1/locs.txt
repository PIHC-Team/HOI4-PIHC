Ponies in poverty
[zh.@]
困苦的小马们
[en.@desc]
For the ponies seeking refuge, filling their bellies is a daunting task. Despite the departure of the administrators sent by the committee, forced out by Pie familiy and desolate lands, the current administration has failed to fulfill its management duties adequately. Poverty and crime continue to thrive, leaving many ponies blind and desperate as they scour this land for valuable ores in exchange for meager sustenance, even if it’s not their forte.
