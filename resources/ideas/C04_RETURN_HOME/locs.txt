Returning Home
[zh.@]
归乡

[en.@desc]
Endless workponies stopped their crystal mining career and returned to their home in the Weather Factory. Most of them once stepped onto the train away from home, with dreams of getting rich and climbing the social ladder, but now they only carry fatigue and remorse on their way back. Among them are many young faces, who have never thought about what the Crystal Rushers should or shouldn’t have done — they only heard the legends of some of their peers discovering rich crystal mines during their excavations and regretted why themselves weren’t able to choose the right trail; some of the older faces usually smoke and play cards, happily winning a few silver coins on the train back, after all, those who have worked in the Weather Factory for a lifetime have seen everything.
[zh.@desc]
无数工马结束了他们在廷马克图的淘晶生涯，回到了天气工厂的老家。他们中的多数带着对暴富与阶层跃迁的憧憬踏上了离乡的火车，归乡时却只携带着疲惫与遗憾。他们之中不乏年轻的面孔，从未思考过淘晶者协会是否应该做或者没做什么，他们只听说了一些同龄小马在挖掘中发现了富水晶矿洞的传说，懊悔自己为什么没能选择正确的矿道；其中一些年老的面孔，则通常抽着烟打着牌，为在火车上赢得了几个银币而高兴不已，毕竟在天气工厂工作了一辈子的老工马什么都已经见过了。
