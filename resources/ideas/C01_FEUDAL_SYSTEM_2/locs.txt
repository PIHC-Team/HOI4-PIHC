Equestrian Feudal System
[zh.@]
小马利亚的分封制
[en.@desc]
After the Ultimate Battle, the young empress Cozy Glow took over the devastated Equestria without enough prestige. Cozy Glow was forced to implement the feudal system in Equestria. The duchies and autonomous regions with different minds greatly reduced the administrative efficiency of Equestria. Perhaps only a more powerful central imperial government can re-integrate Equestria and make all the ponies in the country obey the Canterlot’s rule.
