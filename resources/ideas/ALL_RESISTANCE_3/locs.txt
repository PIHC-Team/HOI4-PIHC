[en.@desc]
This country is a resistance force that denies the Canterlot Pact and opposes the rule of the Big Three, hoping to restore the world before the Ultimate Battle. Due to the the suppression of the Big Three and the forces that support the status quo, the country faces many challenges. Nevertheless, the country has passed its most difficult period, and the ideals and beliefs of pursuing freedom, harmony, equality or other goals have become the spiritual pillar of the resistance movement.
