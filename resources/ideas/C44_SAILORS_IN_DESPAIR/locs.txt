终末之战后，骏鹰与海马的分歧再次加大。谐律主义的失败让主张亲小马利亚的一派失势，许多民众都选择像过去那样回归海底，躲避地面上的威胁。与此同时，来自风暴岛的新思想和文化也在这片土地上传播，这让新一代愈发对传统感到不满。

[en.@desc]
After the Ultimate Battle, the discord between the Hippogriffs and Seaponies has increased again. The failure of Harmonism has marginalized the Equestria affiliates, and many pony choose to return to deep sea once again to avoid the threats on the ground. Meanwhile, new ideas and cultures from the Storm Island are spreading in this land, making the new generation more dissatisfied with the tradition.
