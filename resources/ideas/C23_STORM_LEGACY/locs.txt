Storm Empire’s Legacy

[zh.@desc]
尽管那个令无数生灵畏惧的帝国已经消失，但那些曾经为战争而制造的武器和那些部队得以保留。新政府在之前的时间里一直在接管那些缺少监管的军火库，收拢各地的散兵游勇。风暴大王为了征服而留下的武器装备现在为我们所用，而一支源于小马利亚和前风暴帝国部队为我们的安全提供了一定保障。
不过，我们的军队后勤与战斗力距离风暴帝国的巅峰时期仍然差的很远，捉襟见肘的经济也让我们很难发展军事科技和新理论。
[en.@desc]
Although the empire that once spread fear across the land has disappeared, the weapons created for war and the troops that they organized remain. The new government has been taking over unregulated armories and gathering scattered soldiers for some time. As the weapons left by the Storm King for conquest are now in our possession, a force composed of Equestria and former Storm Empire troops is providing us with some security.
However, the logistics and combat capabilities of our army are still far from the peak of the Storm Empire, and the struggling economy makes it difficult for us to develop military technology and new theories.
