森林之心
[en.@]
The Heart of the Forest

[zh.@desc]
作为世世代代生活在无尽森林中心位置的鹿族们，掌握着与生俱来的森林亲和性和自然魔法，只有他们才能驯服这个在许多小马眼中神秘而疯狂的森林，对于灌丛谷的对手而言，贸然踏入这里只会有一个后果。
[en.@desc]
As deer who have lived in the heart of the everfree forest for generations, they possess an innate affinity for the forest and natural magic. Only they can tame this mysterious and crazy forest in the eyes of many ponies. For the opponents of the Bush Valley, stepping into this place rashly will only have one consequence.
