War on Corruption
[zh.@desc]
有一个致命的问题困扰着麒麟族：原计划中只需三年即可完成的全国“去静默化”，为什么花费了接近五年？数据显示，“去静默化”工程中消耗的稚马之息数量远超过理论计算值。
考虑到稚马之息在外界市场高昂的价格、麒麟社群对稚马之息的严格管制以及一些流言碎语，我们很难不产生怀疑 —— 是否有麒麟在“去静默化”工程中私自售卖稚马之息牟利？
在民间，虽然没有太多麒麟信以为真，但几乎所有麒麟都多多少少听到过这种传言。而迄今为止，政府都从未对此展开过调查，难道不是很奇怪吗？
[en.@desc]
A fatal problem has plagued the Kirins: why did the national “de-Silencing” project, which was originally planned to be completed in three years, take nearly five years? Data shows that the amount of foal breath consumed in the “de-Silencing” project far exceeds the theoretical value.
Considering the high price of foal’s breath in the external market, the strict control of foal breath by the Kirin community, in conjunction with some rumors and gossip, it is hard not to be suspicious — are there Kirins who are privately selling foal’s breath for profit during the “de-Silencing” project?
Although not many Kirins actually believe it, almost all Kirins have heard such rumors more or less. So far, the government has never investigated this. Isn’t that suspicious?
