[zh.@desc]
虽然麒麟们并不想要卷入任何一场战争，但我们北边的邻居并非是同样的想法。终末之战后，龙族一直遭到半人马的政治恐吓与残酷压迫，而他们一刻也没有停止积蓄力量，想要推翻这对他们来说无法忍受的秩序。
半人马是龙族与麒麟的共同敌人，虽然我们无意与半人马发生冲突，但不代表我们不能在支持北方的同时，发展一下自己的经济。
[en.@desc]
Although the Kirins do not want to be involved in any war, our neighbors to the north do not share the same idea. After the Ultimate Battle, the dragons have been subjected to political intimidation and cruel oppression by the centaurs, and they have never stopped accumulating strength, wanting to overthrow the unbearable order for them.
The centaurs are the common enemy of the dragons and the Kirins. Although we have no intention of conflict with the centaurs, it does not mean that we cannot support the north while developing our own economy.
