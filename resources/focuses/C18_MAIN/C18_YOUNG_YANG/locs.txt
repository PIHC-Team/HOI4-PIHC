[zh.@desc]
麒麟刚刚从古老的村落迈入现代世界的门槛，像是初升的太阳般，充满了活力与希望。
财富，德行高尚者或是视之如土，但在动荡的时代中，那是普通者的生存之本。即使麒麟族的文化上轻视财富，在千年的轮回中也未能有财富的积累，但快速获取财富建立文明的能力，其实在一次次轮回中已经刻入了麒麟族流淌的每一滴血液中。
[en.@desc]
The Kirins have just stepped out of their ancient villages into the threshold of the modern world, like the rising sun, full of vitality and hope.
Wealth, those with noble virtues may regard it as dirt, but in turbulent times, it is the foundation of survival for ordinaries. Even though the Kirin tribe culturally despises wealth and has not been able to accumulate wealth in the cycle of thousands of years, the ability to quickly acquire wealth and establish civilization has actually been engraved in every drop of blood that flows through the Kirins.
