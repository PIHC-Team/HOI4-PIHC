抚平创伤
[en.@]
Heal the Wound
[zh.@desc]
这一次的逆麟之乱几乎抹平了我们的文明，战争的对象并非彼此，而是另一个世界。但我们活下来了，文明没有消亡，这一切是值得的。
现在，我们终于有机会抚平这一切的创伤，让我们的文明重新焕发生机，让火焰中的记忆在美好的未来中被逐渐淡忘。
[en.@desc]
The recent Nirik War almost wiped out our civilization, the war was not between us, but between two worlds. We survived, our civilization did not perish, and it was all worth it.
Now, we finally have the opportunity to heal all these wounds, to revitalize our civilization, and to gradually forget the memories in the flames in a beautiful future.
