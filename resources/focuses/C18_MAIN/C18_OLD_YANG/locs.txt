Old Yang
[zh.@desc]
在许多方面麒麟仍然是一个年轻懵懂的种族，但我们的文明已经有了数千年的历史。数千年的智慧积累，让我们对天命已经有了充分的认知。
命者，立之于己，而受之于天。麒麟不仅仅需要谋求自身的生存，更要为整个世界的未来负责，如正午的太阳般，照耀世界。

[en.@desc]
In many ways, the Kirins are still a young and ignorant species, but our civilization has a history of thousands of years. Thousands of years of accumulated wisdom have given us a full understanding of destiny.
Destiny is established by oneself but received from the heavens. The Kirins not only need to seek our own survival, but also take responsibility for the future of the entire world. Like the sun at noon, shining on the world.
