Kirin Mining
[zh.@desc]
狭关蕴藏着丰富的高质量水晶矿藏，但曾经因为地质结构脆弱，曾发生地震和矿难，导致政府不得不关闭矿山。
随着时代进步，我们的建筑技术也在进步。经过评估，我们已经有把握重新启动狭关采矿业，为麒麟社群的发展提供更多的资源。

[en.@desc]
Narrrow Gate is home to rich, high-quality crystal deposits. However, due to the fragile geological structure, earthquakes and mining accidents have occurred in the past, leading to the government closing the mine.
As time progresses, so does our construction technology. After evaluation, we are confident in restarting the Narrow Gate mining industry to provide more resources for the Kirin commune.
