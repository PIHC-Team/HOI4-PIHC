转移支付试点项目
[en.@]
Transfer Payment Pilot Project
[zh.@desc]
东部的农业是西部贸易发展的牺牲品，东部的贫困是西部繁荣的代价。转移支付试点项目是为了解决这个问题而制定的。
虽然额外提高的税收会导致西部的不满，但当他们清楚这是为了东部的兄弟姐妹时，他们会理解的。

[en.@desc]
The agriculture in the east is the sacrifice of the trade development in the west, and the poverty in the east is the cost of the prosperity in the west. The Transfer Payment Pilot Project is designed to address this issue.
Though the additional tax increase will lead to dissatisfaction in the west, when they understand that this is for their brothers and sisters in the east, they will understand.
