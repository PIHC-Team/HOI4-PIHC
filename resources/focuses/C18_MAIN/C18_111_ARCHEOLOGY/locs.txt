以史为鉴
[en.@]
Learn from History
[zh.@desc]
“以史为鉴”在麒麟族内并不仅仅只是一句古谚语，而是麒麟社会制度实打实的一部分：不仅仅是经验，还有技术。
在千百年的轮回中，麒麟们学到的最重要的知识，就是如何从毁灭的文明中快速重建，在灰烬中提取旧日的知识，在尘土中种下新生的希望。
无数的文明在同一片土地上诞生有消失，其中许多曾经到达过我们现在都不曾触及的高度。千百年的智慧，那些现在看来像是魔法一般的先进科技，并未再火焰中失传，而是在灰烬之下沉睡，等待着被考古学家唤醒的那一天。
[en.@desc]
“Learn from History” is not just an ancient proverb among the Kirins, but a real part of our social system: not just experience, but technology.
The most important knowledge the Kirins have learned over the millennia of cycles is how to quickly rebuild from the ruins of a civilization, to extract the knowledge of the past from the ashes, and to plant the seeds of hope in the dust.
Countless civilizations have risen and fallen on the same land, many of them reaching heights we have not yet touched. The wisdom of millennia, the advanced technology that now seems like magic, has not been lost in the flames, but sleeps beneath the ashes, waiting for the day when archaeologists will awaken it.
