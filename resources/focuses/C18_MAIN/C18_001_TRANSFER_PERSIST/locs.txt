转移支付推广
[en.@]
Transfer Payment Promotion
[zh.@desc]
东部的农业是西部贸易发展的牺牲品，东部的贫困是西部繁荣的代价。转移支付是为了解决这个问题而制定的。
虽然额外提高的税收会导致西部的不满，但当他们清楚这是为了东部的兄弟姐妹时，他们会理解的。
如今试点已经取得了成功，我们可以持续推广这一政策。

[en.@desc]
The agriculture in the east is the sacrifice of the trade development in the west, and the poverty in the east is the cost of the prosperity in the west. The Transfer Payment is designed to address this issue.
Though the additional tax increase will lead to dissatisfaction in the west, when they understand that this is for their brothers and sisters in the east, they will understand.
As the pilot has been successful, we can continue to promote this policy.
