雨晴选择了“静默”，秋烨解除了“静默”，但即使方法不同，目的却是一样的：避免逆麟之乱的发生。
逆麟来自于愤怒，但却不仅仅是一种个体现象：愤怒可以传染，每一只对生活抱有不满的麒麟都是潜在的易感染者。
因此，就像我们要引入友谊的理念一样，我们要从社会的角度根除戾气的来源：让每一只麒麟都享受到开放与发展的成果，让每一只麒麟都能幸福生活和全面发展。

Rain Shine implemented the “Silence”, Autumn Blaze lifted the “Silence”, but even though the methods are different, the purpose is the same: to prevent the Nirik Rebellion.
Niriks come from anger, but it is not just an individual phenomenon: anger can be contagious, and every Kirin who is dissatisfied with his or her life is a potential carrier.
Therefore, just as we want to introduce the concept of friendship, we want to eradicate the source of hostility from a social perspective: let every Kirin enjoy the fruits of openness and development, and let every Kirin live a happy and comprehensive life.
