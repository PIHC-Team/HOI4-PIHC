[en.@desc]
Gen, unwavering like a mountain. Although development can solve many problems, it cannot solve all problems, especially the problems right in front of us.
Due to difference in the time of opening up, the western coastal areas including the Peaks of Peril began their exchanges with the outside world earliest, while in the eastern forests, there are still quite a few Kirins living in poverty, isolated from the world.
If the Kirins in the eastern forests look towards the prosperity and debauchery on the Peaks of Peril in the distance, how would they feel? Merely promising them the future is not enough, they need the present.
If development is not fast enough to quell their complaints, then the west should not be stingy with the §Crelief§!.
