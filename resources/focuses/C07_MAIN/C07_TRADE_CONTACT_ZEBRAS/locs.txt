Approach Zebras
[zh.@desc]
斑马，虽然从任何一个角度上看，都是选择盟友的一个糟糕选择——他们未开化、思想野蛮、行动随意，而且还与强大的幻形灵帝国为敌。但是，有一句古老的谚语，大概吧：“永远做房间里最聪明的小马。” 和一群愚蠢的家伙做盟友，大概可以从他们身上捞到不少好处。
[en.@desc]
Zebras, although a terrible choice for an ally from any angle — they are uncivilized, barbaric, and unpredictable, and they are also enemies of the powerful Changeling Empire. But there is an ancient saying, probably: “Always be the smartest pony in the room.” It is probably possible to get a lot of benefits from these stupid allies.
