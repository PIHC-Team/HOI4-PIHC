Rainbow’s End
[zh.@desc]

[en.@desc]
Zebras seem to be unaware of how lucky they are. At the Rainbow’s End, there is a portal that can transport our army to the edge of Equestria. All we need is a request to cross the forest, a false pretense of passage.
