防暴警察
[en.@]
The Riot Police
[zh.@desc]
独立的目标离不开军事实力的支持，但大力发展军备必然会引起坎特洛特的注意，这是一个需要谨慎权衡的问题。不过，一个很简单的思路是，只需要不把军队称为“军队”，而是称为“防暴警察”，一切问题就迎刃而解了。考虑到特丽克西曾经长期兼任小马谷安保队长，将其仅仅看做是回馈安保部队的结党营私，也合理了许多。只是腐败而已，绝对不是谋反。
[en.@desc]
The goal of independence cannot be achieved without military strength, but the development of military power will inevitably attract the attention of Canterlot, which is a problem that needs to be carefully weighed. However, a very simple idea is to stop calling the army the “army” and call it the “riot police”, and all problems will be solved. Considering that Trixie once served as the captain of the Ponyville Security Team for quite a long time, it is reasonable to regard it as a poltical payoff to the police forces. It’s just corruption, definitely not treason.
