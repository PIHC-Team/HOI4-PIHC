[en.@desc]
Dr. Hooves’s Lab is a renowned research institution in Equestria and even the whole world. But this institution is only supported by the power of Dr. Hooves, a single pony.
Therefore, if we expand the scale of this institution and build a new research center in Stallioncon Valley, it will greatly help to improve the status of Ponyville in the field of scientific research.
