Dodge College
[zh.@desc]
比经济更加长久的是工业，比工业更加长久的是教育。小马谷政府计划在道奇路口地区建设一座新的综合高等学院，在其中教授魔法、科学、军事、艺术等多种学科。
这座学院将会是新时代小马谷最大的高等学府之一，唯一的难题是——获得教育协会的认证。
[en.@desc]
What lasts longer than economics is industry, and what lasts longer than industry is education. The government of Ponyville plans to build a new comprehensive college in the Dodge Junction area, teaching a variety of subjects such as magic, science, military, and art.
This college will be one of the largest institutions of higher learning in the new era of Ponyville, with the only obstacle being accreditation from the EEA.
