第一个五月计划
[en.@]
The First Five-month Plan
[zh.@desc]
在过去的两年多里，虽然特丽克西一直在关注并帮助着小马谷的民生改善、经济恢复、工业发展，但频繁的职务调动使得小马谷始终缺乏一个长期稳定的执政主体。如今，特丽克西终于有了自己的政府，和相对自主的领地管辖权，她也准备好开始实施自己的第一个五月计划：在未来五个月内，将小马谷的经济发展重心从农业转向工业和魔法，为小马谷的未来发展打下坚实的基础。当第一个五月计划评估成功后，我们就将顺势继续展开第二个、第三个... 以至于第十个、第一百个、第一千个... 当有小马问及她的创意来自哪里时，特丽克西总是会回答：“一位称职的公爵懂得如何听取别的小马的建议。”
[en.@desc]
In the past two years, although Trixie has been paying attention to and helping the ponies’ livelihood improvement, economic recovery and industrial development of Ponyville, frequent job transfers have made Ponyville always lack a long-term and stable ruling body. Now, Trixie finally has her own government and relatively autonomous jurisdiction over the territory. She is also ready to start her first five-month plan: in the next five months, she will shift the focus of Ponyville’s economic development from agriculture to industry and magic, laying a solid foundation for Ponyville’s future development. When the first five-month plan is evaluated as a success, we will continue to launch the second, the third... to the tenth, the hundredth, the thousandth... When asked where her ideas came from, Trixie always replied, “A competent Duchess knows how to listen to advice from another pony.”
