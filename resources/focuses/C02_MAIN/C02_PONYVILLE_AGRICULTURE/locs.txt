第二次小马谷农业改革
[en.@]
The Second Ponyville Agricultural Revolution
[zh.@desc]
在1000年春，为了应对当年的寒冬，小马谷在梅耶镇长的领导下开展了一次农业改革：砍伐耐寒性较差的苹果树等树木，种植耐寒性较强的果树和作物，以应对逐渐寒冷的气候。
梅耶的改革思路是正确的，然而由于苹果家族的反对、“幽灵列车”事故，以及随之而来的梅耶下台，改革也就不了了之。
如今，小马谷再次面临着远超过了1000年春的寒冷，或许，是时候再次进行农业改革了。
[en.@desc]
In the spring of 1000, in order to cope with the cold winter of that year, Ponyville carried out an agricultural reform under the leadership of Mayor Mare: cutting down non-cold-resistant apple trees and other trees, planting cold-resistant fruit trees and crops to cope with the gradually colder climate.
Mayor Mare’s reform was generally a correct idea, but due to the opposition from the Apple family, the “Ghost Train” incident, and the subsequent fall of Mayor Mare, the reform was aborted.
Now, Ponyville is once again facing a cold far beyond that of the spring of 1000. Perhaps it is time to carry out another agricultural reform.
