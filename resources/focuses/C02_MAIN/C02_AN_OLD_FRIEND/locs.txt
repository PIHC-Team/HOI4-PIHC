星光熠熠... 她现在怎么样了呢？
特丽克西还记得遇见星光熠熠的那一天，两颗孤独的灵魂在小马谷的水疗馆里相遇，自此便紧密相连。和星光熠熠的友谊也帮助了特丽克西摆脱了在镇民眼中的糟糕印象，可以说，没有星光熠熠，就没有现在特丽克西的一切。
但两位曾经的挚友已经长久没有任何联系。特丽克西对于这段友谊依旧充满忧虑：如果她和星光的联系被和煦光流发现，后果将难以想象。除此之外，特丽克西更不知道如何面对星光熠熠的质问。特丽克西对和煦的投降，将会是她与星光友谊之间不可忽视的一抹污渍。
但在内心深处，特丽克西仍心存一丝侥幸，她相信星光熠熠会给这段友谊一次机会，只要自己能迈出那一步——但犹豫再三，特丽克西还是没有行动，出于恐惧？还是出于愧疚？
月光下，她坐在桌前，看着自己的那枚月亮形胸针，迟迟难以入眠。

Starlight Glimmer... I wonder how she’s doing?
Trixie still remembers the day she met Starlight Glimmer, two lonely souls met at the Ponyville Day Spa, and they have been inseparable ever since. Her friendship with Starlight Glimmer also helped Trixie get rid of the bad impression in the eyes of the townsponies. It can be said that without Starlight Glimmer, there would be nothing of Trixie now.
But the two old friends have not been in contact for a long time. Trixie is still full of worries about this friendship: if her contact with Starlight is discovered by Cozy Glow, the consequences will be unimaginable. In addition, Trixie does not know how to face Starlight Glimmer’s questioning. Trixie’s surrender to Cozy will be an undeniable stain on her friendship with Starlight.
But deep down, Trixie still has a glimmer of hope, she believes that Starlight Glimmer will give this friendship a chance, as long as she can take that step - but after much hesitation, Trixie still did not act. Out of fear? Or out of guilt?
Under the moonlight, she sat at the table, looking at her moon-shaped brooch, and she couldn’t fall asleep.
