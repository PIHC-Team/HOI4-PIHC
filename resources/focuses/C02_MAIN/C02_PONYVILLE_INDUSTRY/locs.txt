马鞍湖重工业园区
[en.@]
Saddle Lake Heavy Industrial Park
[zh.@desc]
小马谷的建筑已经越来越多，但是小马谷却一直是一个农业为主的地区。重工业园区一直没有建立，而我们已经开始用完土地资源了。
如果我们在旁边的马鞍湖建立一个工业园区，将会为小马谷带来更多的就业机会，同时也能够为小马谷的经济发展提供更多的动力。
[en.@desc]
The buildings in Ponyville are increasing, but Ponyville has always been an agricultural area. A heavy industrial park has not been established, and we have begun to use up land resources.
If we establish an industrial park in the nearby Saddle Lake, it will bring more employment opportunities to Ponyville, and also provide more impetus for the economic development of Ponyville.
