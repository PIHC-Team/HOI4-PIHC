麻瓜的春天
[en.@]
Muggles’ Spring
[zh.@desc]
当魔法教育普及，小马谷的无数陆马、和那些失去魔力的独角兽们才终于意识到，魔法无处不在。
并不是只有用独角释放咒语才能称之为魔法，飞马的翅膀、陆马的力量、古董与神器、森林里的怪物、会爆炸的杯糕都是魔法。
在经过恰当的学习后，即使是麻瓜也可以使用魔法让生活更加美好。
[en.@desc]
When magic education is popularized, countless earth ponies in Ponyville and unicorns who have lost their magic finally realize that magic is everywhere.
It is not only the unicorns who can release spells that can be called magic, but also the wings of the pegasi, the strength of the earth ponies, antiques and artifacts, monsters in the forest, and exploding cupcakes are all magic.
After proper learning, even Muggles can make life better with magic.
