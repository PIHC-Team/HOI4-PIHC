蜂熊灾
[en.@]
The Swarm of Bugbears
[zh.@desc]
幻形灵帝国曾多次谴责小马利亚帝国对于塔尔塔洛斯与德伦加尔的疏于管理，导致塔尔塔洛斯附近野生生物群系躁动，引发不小的麻烦。如今，幻形灵帝国再次遭受蜂熊灾，这提供了一个绝好的借口。
[en.@desc]
Changeling Empire has repeatedly condemned the Equestria Empire for its lax management of Tartarus and Drungar, which has led to the restlessness of the nearby wild creatures and caused many troubles. Now, the Changeling Empire is once again suffering from the swarm of Bugbears, which provides a perfect excuse.
