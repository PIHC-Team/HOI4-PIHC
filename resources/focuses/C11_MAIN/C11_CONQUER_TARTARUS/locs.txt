对塔尔塔洛斯的征服
[en.@]
Conquer Tartarus
[zh.@desc]
塔尔塔洛斯是小马利亚最核心的监狱，里面关押着各种危险的生物，以及此前的战犯——两位公主。依据《坎特洛特协定》，塔尔塔洛斯属于小马利亚帝国的管辖范围，也被视作是幻形灵帝国和小马利亚帝国的缓冲区。但很显然，小马利亚帝国并没有心思抑或是能力去维护这个监狱。接下来幻形灵帝国想要逐渐向中央大陆扩张，塔尔塔洛斯就是一扇至关重要的大门，而且，监狱中的生物也将成为幻形灵帝国爱意供给的潜在来源。而对我们占领塔尔塔洛斯唯一的阻碍，便是现在脆弱而可笑的和平——我们并不想在这个时候引起小马利亚帝国的过分关注，静待时机，是我们的最佳选择。
[en.@desc]
Tartarus is the most essential prison in the Equestria world, where all kinds of dangerous creatures and two former war criminals — the princesses are imprisoned. According to the Canterlot Pact, Tartarus is under the jurisdiction of the Equestria Empire, and is also considered as a buffer zone between the Equestria Empire and the Changeling Empire. However, it is obvious that the Equestria Empire does not have the intention or ability to maintain this prison. Next, the Changeling Empire wants to gradually expand to the central Equestria continent, and Tartarus is a very important gate. The creatures in the prison will also become a potential source of love for the Changeling Empire. The only obstacle to our occupation of Tartarus is the current fragile and ridiculous peace — we do not want to attract too much attention from the Equestria Empire at this time, and waiting for the opportunity is our best choice.
