[en.@desc]
We have successfully captured Tartarus. However, no matter whether we continue to advance eastward or stay to manage Tartarus, we seem to have encountered some difficulties — just as the Equestria Empire once encountered: various strange creatures such as Thestrals and Bugbears seem to be unwilling to submit to the control of the Changelings.
Well, if they are only unwilling, the armed forces of the Changelings have ways to make them willing. But what is more difficult is that these barbaric creatures seem to be unable to understand our requirements.
Among all the creatures near Tartarus, only Thestrals have the highest sociality and organization. Therefore, we need the support of Thestrals to control this area. It is known that Thestrals unconditionally obey the Nightmare Moon — and at this moment, if nothing unexpected happens, Princess Luna should be imprisoned in the Tartarus prison. So...
