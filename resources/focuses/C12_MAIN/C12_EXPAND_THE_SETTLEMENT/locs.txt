扩建南部定居点
[en.@]
Expand Southern Settlement
[zh.@desc]
我们的南部地区紧邻神秘莫测的独眼巨人领，这里的土地跟绿色山脉类似，同样被茂密的植被覆盖，然而，这里更加原始和未被涉足，苍天巨树星罗棋布，无论是采石农场还是洛克维尔等矿业城镇都与这些地方截然不同。虽然目前只有少数小马定居在那里，但我们应该着力推动开发工程，鼓励他们开垦边界并尝试开采橡胶和矿藏，长远来看会为我们带来大量有价值的回报
[en.@desc]
Our southern region borders the mysterious and inscrutable Domain of the One-Eyed Giant. Here, the land is cloaked in dense vegetation, and majestic, towering trees are scattered like stars across the sky—entirely unlike the rock farm or mining towns such as Rockville. Although only a few ponies have settled there so far, we should actively promote development projects, encouraging them to cultivate the frontier and explore the extraction of rubber and mineral deposits, which in the long run will yield a wealth of valuable returns.
