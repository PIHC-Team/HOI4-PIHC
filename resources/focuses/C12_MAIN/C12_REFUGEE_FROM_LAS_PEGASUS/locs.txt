[en.@desc]
After the Last Roundup, many impoverished ponies flocked to the Western Equestria region of the farm, but they didn’t stay there. Instead, they chose to brave the dangers and continue onwards to Las Pegasus for survival. At that time, the quarry farm was overwhelmed, while Rockmont flourished.

Now, Las Pegasus has hit a development bottleneck. If we can open our arms to the ponies of Las Pegasus, at least some of them would be willing to give us a second chance.
