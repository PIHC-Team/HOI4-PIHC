[zh.@desc]
石青派仁慈的接受了小马们的反复请求，成为小马利亚的护国公，这一全新的词汇代表她和派家族仍然只是大洛克维尔的普通公民，只不过被迫终身掌管洛克维尔旗下的土地，并且享有永恒的立法权、行政权和军事权，以免其落入任何居心不良的家伙蹄中。只有在她的庇护下， 小马们才能安居乐业，重建自己的家园。为了扫清帝国势力的残余，让每个小马都能安然入眠，石青派将继续带领军队奋战在一线，所以请不要忘记，在每天的晚饭前进行集体祷告来感谢石青派为所有小马的无私奉献。
[en.@desc]
Limestone Pie kindly accepted the repeated pleas of the ponies and became the Protector of Equestria. This brand-new title signified that she and the Pie family remained merely ordinary citizens of Greater Rockville, albeit forced to manage Rockville’s lands for life and endowed with eternal legislative, executive, and military powers—to ensure these powers never fall into the hooves of any ill-intentioned individuals. Only under her protection can the ponies live in peace and rebuild their homes. In order to sweep away the remnants of imperial forces and allow every pony to sleep soundly, Limestone Pie will continue to lead the troops on the frontlines. So please, do not forget to join in collective prayer before dinner each day to thank Limestone Pie for her selfless dedication to all ponies.
