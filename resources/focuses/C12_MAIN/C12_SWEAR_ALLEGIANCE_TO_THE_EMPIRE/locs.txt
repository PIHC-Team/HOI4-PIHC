Swear of Allegiance

[zh.@desc]
为了防止帝国在派家族做好准备之前先行一步，我们不得不发布一个声明替帝国撇清干系：父母的死与帝国无关，妹妹们则是自作自受...

在被撕碎的信纸已经快要填满垃圾桶时，石灰派才终于成功——在没有中途因控制不住脾气而字迹扭曲的情况下，完成了声明的撰写，将自己又杀死了一点。那些密密麻麻而又整齐冷静的文字之下，那张没有一句真心话的纸彻底遮蔽了石青派本就已像石头一样坚固而封闭的内心。无论是帝国、和煦、公众、家族还是石青派自己，都将不再有机会看透。
[en.@desc]
To prevent the Empire from taking the initiative before the Pie family is ready, we had to issue a statement to absolve the Empire of any responsibility: our parents’ death had nothing to do with the Empire, and our sisters brought it upon themselves...

After the shredded paper had almost filled the trash can, Limestone Pie finally completed the task of writing the statement without losing her temper and distorting her writing. She managed to kill another bit of herself from the inside. Beneath the neat and orderly words, the paper that contained not a single word of truth completely obscured the already rock-like heart of Limestone Pie. No pony anymore, not the Empire, the Empress, the public, the Pie family, or Limestone Pie herself, would ever have the chance to see through it again.
