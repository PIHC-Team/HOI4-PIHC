[en.@desc]
Before we figure out how to install engines on these heavy war wagons, they can only be pulled by the strongest of earth ponies. However, on the battlefield, these heavy war wagons can provide cover for our soldiers and, in some urgent moments, can also effectively transport wounded and supplies.
