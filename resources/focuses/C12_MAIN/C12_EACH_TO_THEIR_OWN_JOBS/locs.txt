Jobs for All
[zh.@desc]
在各地区政府部门的调剂和安排下，我们会尽量确保每个小马都能找到自己的工作，这不仅是为了降低失业率，也是为了每个小马都能积极摆脱过去黑暗的日子，与采石农场一同迎来新的春天。
[en.@desc]
With the reorganization and arrangements made by government departments in various regions, we will strive to ensure that every pony can find employment. This is not only to reduce unemployment rates but also to enable every pony to actively move away from the dark days of the past and welcome a new spring together with the rock farm.
