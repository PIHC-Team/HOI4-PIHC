[zh.@desc]
野牛部落一直坐落在采石农场的东面，这里生物稀少，却蕴含着丰富的资源，同时，这里易守难攻，我们的部队可以从这里直接发起进攻小马镇公爵领的南部，并绕开帝国在我们北方重兵布置的防线。然而，虽然野牛和小马在过去友好相处，但是终末之战后它们躲在领地并不肯待见任何小马。我将派遣部队和我的外交官前去说服野牛放弃争端，他们别无选择，必须同意加入我们的家族联盟中来。
[en.@desc]
The buffalo tribe has always resided to the east of the rock farm. This area, though sparsely populated, is rich in resources and strategically advantageous, being easy to defend and hard to attack. Our forces can launch direct offensives into the southern regions of the Ponyville Duchy from here, effectively bypassing the heavily fortified imperial defenses to our north. However, despite past harmonious relations between the buffalo and ponies, they have become reclusive since the Last Roundup, unwilling to engage with any ponies. I will dispatch troops and diplomats to persuade the buffalo to set aside disputes. They have no choice but to agree to join our family alliance.
