洛马基的“无偿”援助
[en.@]
“Free” Sponsorship of Las Pegasus
[zh.@desc]
洛马基在听说了我们土地上发现许多矿藏的信息后，立刻向我们伸出了援助之蹄。只需要跟他们签一笔奇怪的协议，许多的物资都被冠以无偿的名号。虽然我们与他们并不熟悉，也不清楚他们的真实目的，但这些物资可以迅速改善我们的工业。
[en.@desc]
Upon hearing about the discovery of numerous mineral deposits on our land, Las Pegasus immediately extended a helping hoof to us. They offered assistance in the form of a peculiar agreement, with many supplies labeled as donations. While we were not familiar with them and unsure of their true intentions, these supplies could swiftly improve our industrial capabilities.
