控制镜像水潭
[en.@]
Control the Mirror Pool

[zh.@desc]
坊间一直有传闻，镜像水潭中还留存着我妹妹萍琪派的某个倒影，甚至有小马声称曾在附近看见过她。不论传言是否属实，她肯定曾经在那里做过什么，这给我带来了一丝希望。不管这个镜像水潭的所有者是那个该死的混沌龙马还是其他什么东西，我都要得到它。
[en.@desc]
Rumors have long circulated that some reflection of my sister Pinkie Pie remains in the Mirror Pool, and some ponies even claim to have seen her nearby. Whether or not the rumors are true, she must once have done something there, and that gives me a glimmer of hope. No matter who owns the Mirror Pool—that damned draconequus or anything else—I will have it.
