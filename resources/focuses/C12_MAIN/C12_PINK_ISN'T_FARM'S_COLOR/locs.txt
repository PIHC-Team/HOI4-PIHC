本无欢笑
[en.@]
Pink Isn’t Your Color
[zh.@desc]
采石农场的日出日落依旧，只是我们终究还是失去了她。
那颗牵挂了数年的心，最终还是在现实面前粉碎，如今的采石农场无法愤怒，也无法悲伤。
但正如敲碎的石头会显出其中的金刚石，当采石农场的沉默被敲碎的那天，锋利的金刚石将会让锤子付出代价。
[en.@desc]
Rock Farm’s sunrise and sunset are still the same, but we have lost her after all.
The heart that has been concerned for years is finally shattered in front of reality. The Rock Farm can neither be angry nor sad.
But as the broken stone will reveal the diamond, when the silence of the Rock Farm is broken, the sharp diamond will make the hammer pay the price.
