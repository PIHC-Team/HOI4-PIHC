开发鸣雷矿脉
[en.@]
Search in Big Thunder Mine

[zh.@desc]
过去的鸣雷矿脉因为雷暴天气频繁被认为不适合小马居住，现在经过地理勘探发现，是其地下蕴含着的大量金属所导致的，我们应该准备开采这些丰富的资源。
[en.@desc]
The past reluctance to inhabit the Big Thunder Mine due to frequent thunderstorms has been reevaluated. Recent geological surveys have revealed a significant presence of metals underground. It’s time to prepare for the extraction of these abundant resources.
