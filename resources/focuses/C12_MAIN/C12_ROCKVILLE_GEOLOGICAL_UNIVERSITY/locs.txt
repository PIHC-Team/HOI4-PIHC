洛克维尔地质大学
[en.@]
Rockville Geological University

[zh.@desc]
在派家族的号召下，整个小马利亚最出色的地理学家来到采石农场的洛克维尔，我们有了足够的经济实力去创办一所世界顶尖的地质大学和学院，这将把我们对我们蹄下土地的了解推向新的高峰，也终于让我们在某一领域的研究能处于领先地位。
[en.@desc]
Under the call of the Pie family, the finest geographers of all Equestria came to Rockville, the quarry farm. With sufficient economic strength, we established a world-class geological university and research center. This endeavor would elevate our understanding of the land beneath our hooves to new heights and finally place us at the forefront of research in a certain field.
