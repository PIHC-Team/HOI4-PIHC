翻新采石火车站
[en.@]
Renovate Train Stations

[zh.@desc]
采石农场的火车站建立之初并没有考虑到日后庞大的运货量需求，与周围几个城镇也没有很好的相连，我们应该着手翻新这些火车站和铁轨，确保它们不会影响我们的货物运输。
[en.@desc]
At the inception of the rock farm’s railway stations, the colossal demand for freight transport in the future and their connection with the surrounding towns were not adequately considered. It is imperative that we undertake the renovation of these railway stations and tracks to ensure they do not impede our freight transportation operations.
