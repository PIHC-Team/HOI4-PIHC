木质化石展览
[en.@]
Wooden Fossil Exhibition
[zh.@desc]
在石灰派和其未婚夫泥枝的努力下，一个有关木质化石的展览会将举办，旨在提高采石农场的凝聚力和小马们的地理素养...如果小马们感兴趣的话。
[en.@desc]
Under the efforts of Maud Pie and her fiancé Mudbriar, an exhibition featuring petrified wood would be organized, aimed at fostering cohesion within the quarry farm community and enhancing the geographical literacy of the ponies. Whether the ponies would show interest remained to be seen.
