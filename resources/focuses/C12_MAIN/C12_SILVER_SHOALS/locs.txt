Feeding everypony has long been an issue we cannot overlook. In the past, especially before the Great New Year, most ponies experienced hunger. Now that we have a robust industry, we must prevent such situations from recurring, particularly during times of war. Silver Shoals has always been regarded as an excellent grain-producing area, and we can leverage this advantage.
