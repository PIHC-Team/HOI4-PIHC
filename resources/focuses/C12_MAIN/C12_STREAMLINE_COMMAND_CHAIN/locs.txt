精简指挥链
[en.@]
Streamline Command Chain
[zh.@desc]
在对许多战斗的复盘后，我们发现我们的指挥链存在许多不足，军官与连队的沟通在瞬息万变的战场上总是容易断开，这使得许多士兵无法及时接受到命令，从而错过战机。我们需要继续改良我们的部队结构，使得命令下达更加便捷。
[en.@desc]
After reviewing numerous battles, we discovered many deficiencies in our chain of command. Communication between officers and companies is frequently disrupted on the rapidly changing battlefield, causing many soldiers to miss orders in time and thereby forgoing critical opportunities. We need to continue refining our unit structure to ensure that orders are delivered more efficiently.
