[zh.@]
穿越麦金托什山
[en.@]
Cross the Macintosh Hills

[zh.@desc]
我们领土最东边的麦金托什山脉绵延不断、难以逾越，阻碍着我们与苹果鲁萨和野牛部落这些友好地区的联系，一条山路和补给站应该被打通来让我们同时与山脉的另一侧便捷的交流。
[en.@desc]
Our territory extends all the way to the eastern edge of the Macintosh Mountains, which stretch endlessly and are difficult to traverse, hindering our communication with friendly regions such as Appleloosa and the Buffalo Tribe. A mountain pass and supply station should be established to facilitate convenient communication with the other side of the mountains
