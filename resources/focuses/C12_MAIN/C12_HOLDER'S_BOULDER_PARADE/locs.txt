奠基顽石巡游
[en.@]
Holder’s Boulder Parade
[zh.@desc]
木质化石展取得了非常不错的效果，为了进一步加强岩石农场本地与自治领其他地区的联系，我们应该组织一场有关奠基顽石的游行来让它成为整个自治领的精神象征。
[en.@desc]
The Wooden Fossil Exhibition has achieved excellent results. In order to further strengthen the connection between the local rock farm and other areas of the autonomy, we should organize a parade related to Holder’s Boulder to make it the spiritual symbol of the entire autonomy.
