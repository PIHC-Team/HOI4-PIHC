[en.@]
In the Name of Laughter

[zh.@desc]
当你躲在战壕，身上流淌着滴落的酸雨，望向嘶嚎着的炮火，当你在街头小巷，只身把残缺的遗体拖回，你是否会感到害怕？曾有一个年长的雌驹说过这样一个故事，当你对你的恐惧大笑，它们将无法再伤到你
[en.@desc]
When you take shelter in the trenches, your body streaked with dripping acid rain, and you gaze at the howling barrage, when you drag mutilated remains through the narrow streets all alone—do you feel fear? An old mare once told a story: when you laugh at your fear, it can no longer hurt you.
