[zh.@desc]
派家族与帝国早已势不两立，即便选择性的忽视新元年之前的种种恶行，帝国最近对火岩派和云母的所作所为也足以让派家族当中最愿意与之妥协的小马感到绝望。回顾往昔，一路走来的努力让石灰派拥有了足够的底气，当时机成熟，派家族将毫无犹豫的会加入反抗可西光辉暴政的浪潮。
[en.@desc]
The Pie family had long been at odds with the Empire. Even if one were to selectively overlook all of the misdeeds before the New Era, the Empire’s recent actions toward Igneous Rock and Cloudy Quartz have left even the most conciliatory ponies in the Pie family utterly desperate. Reflecting on their past struggles, Maud Pie has gained enough confidence that when the time is right, the Pie family will unhesitatingly join the rising tide of resistance against Cozy Glow’s tyranny.
