The Breezie Village has always been situated to our south, bordering the Leota Forest. For a long time, as an ancient tradition of the pony tribes, few ponies ventured there to disturb these beautiful and fragile beings. However, to gain more leverage against the Empire, I must take control of that area; only roaring factories can truly harness the potential of that land.
