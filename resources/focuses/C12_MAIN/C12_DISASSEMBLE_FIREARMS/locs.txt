拆解洛马基火器
[en.@]
Disassemble Firearms
[zh.@desc]
我们的邻国洛马基拥有着整块大陆上最先进的科技，我们应当想方设法走私一些他们使用的火枪，通过拆解它们的设计来更好的改进我们的制式装备。
[en.@desc]
Our neighboring country, Las Pegasus, boasts the most advanced technology on the continent. We should endeavor to smuggle some of the firearms they use, dismantle their designs, and improve our standard equipment accordingly.
