清除帝国官僚
[en.@]
Purge the Imperial Bureaucracy

[zh.@desc]
所有曾经为帝国官僚机构服务过的小马都要被给予严厉的处罚，他们作为女皇的走狗，与其同罪，需要为这十年来给小马利亚造成的一切混乱和伤痕负责，特别是石青派心中的。无数亲自签署的批准令像千斤石般堆在了临时军管机构的木桌上，而石青派的新晋支持者则会毫不留情的在当地执行她的任何指示。
[en.@desc]
All ponies who once served in the imperial bureaucracy must be subjected to severe punishment. As the Empress’s lackeys, they are guilty by association and must be held accountable for the chaos and scars inflicted upon Equestria over the past decade—especially those seared into Limestone Pie’s heart. Countless signed orders of approval are now piled like boulders atop the wooden desks of the temporary military administration, and Limestone Pie’s newly risen supporters carry out her every command in the region without hesitation or mercy.
