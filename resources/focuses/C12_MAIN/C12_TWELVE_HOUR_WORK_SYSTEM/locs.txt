十二小时工作制
[en.@]
Twelve Hour Work System

[zh.@desc]
通过最新有关劳动准则的法律颁布，所有劳动小马的每日工作时长被要求从八小时延长到十二小时以上来珍惜备战的每一天，任何不服从的小马将受到严厉惩罚，只有靠这一特别举措才能保证未来战争的胜利。
[en.@desc]
With the recent promulgation of labor standard laws, all working ponies are now required to extend their daily working hours from eight to over twelve hours in order to make the most of each day spent preparing for war. Only through this measure can victory in future wars be guaranteed
