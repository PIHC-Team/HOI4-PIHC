优胜劣汰
[en.@]
Natural Selection

[zh.@desc]
高额的死亡率已经无法满足采石农场对精英士兵的需求。科学家将筛选优秀精英的基因，创造小马胚胎并从小培养。他们一辈子只需要做两件事：服从指令和效忠于派家族。
这远远突破了小马社会的伦理，但石青派并不在乎。
[en.@desc]
The high mortality rate can no longer meet the rock farm’s demand for elite soldiers. Scientists will select the genes of outstanding elites, create pony embryos, and raise them from a young age. They will only need to do two things in their lives: obey orders and be loyal to the Pie family.
This far exceeds pony ethics, but Limestone Pie doesn’t care.
