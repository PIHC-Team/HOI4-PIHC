Enhance Equipment Compatibility

[zh.@desc]
科学家将对铸岩者的装备添加一定的辅助动力装置，从而可以适配独角兽和天马等种族，对士兵原本的身体需求也有所降低，这让军队可以更广泛的补充铸岩者的兵源，填补越来越大的缺口。
[en.@desc]
Scientists will add some auxiliary power devices to the equipment of the Rockorged Warriors, making it adaptable for unicorns and pegasi. This also reduces the physical demands on soldiers, allowing the army to recruit a wider range of ponies to replenish the ever-growing gaps in the Rockorged Warriors’ ranks.
