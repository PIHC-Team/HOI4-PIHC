The rock farm has now become a leading exporter of mineral resources. Our ores are loaded onto trains, shipped across Equestria and even to other parts of the world, gaining increasing recognition. However, we are also facing growing competitive pressure. To maintain our market share, we should intensify our mining efforts and implement various export policies.
