Same as Before
[zh.@desc]
采石农场的生活似乎没有什么变化，偏远的地区让我们逃过了委员会的税赋以及和煦的胡乱指挥。在采石农场，你只能从中得到石头，因此也没有什么可以被抢走。
因此采石农场自然地被划为自治领。除了向帝国提供的矿石价格比以前更低了一点，我们的工作更辛苦了一点，其他的则依然如故。
唯一的区别，只能是石头家族可爱的妹妹，我们已经许久没有见到了。
[en.@desc]
Life in the Rock Farm seems to have changed little, the remote location has spared us the taxes of the Committee and the warmongering of Cozy Glow. In the Rock Farm, you can only get rocks, so there is nothing to be taken away.
So the Rock Farm was naturally designated as an autonomous territory, a dominion. Apart from the lowered price of the ore we provide to the Empire, and the harder work, everything else remains the same as before.
The only difference, is that we haven’t seen the lovely sister of the Rock family for a long time.
