拼凑出的资金
[en.@]
Patchwork Wealth
[zh.@desc]
虽然苹果家族如今已经称不上富裕，但在得知我们的请求后，仍然拼凑出一笔资金来帮助我们实施工业化。这笔钱要远远少于洛马基能提供的，但我们会最大化利用它们来报答苹果家族。
[en.@desc]
Though the Apple family isn’t as affluent as they once were, upon learning of our request, they still managed to scrape together some funds to assist us in our industrialization efforts. The amount they provided falls far short of what Las Pegasus could offer, but we will make the most of it to repay the Apple family.
