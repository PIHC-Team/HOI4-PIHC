Play  Strength of Pie Family

[zh.@desc]
派家族的小马对岩石和这片土地都有着与生俱来的专精和了解，但对于采石农场的其他绝大多数小马而言并非如此，派家族将整理传承下来的知识，编入采石农场的基础教育大纲来进行普及，旨在提高小马们对矿石的理解和认知。
[en.@desc]
The Pie family ponies possessed an innate expertise and understanding of rocks and the land, but this wasn’t the case for most other ponies on the quarry farm. Recognizing this, the Pie family integrated their inherited knowledge into the basic education curriculum of the rock farm, aiming to enhance the understanding and cognition of minerals among the ponies.
