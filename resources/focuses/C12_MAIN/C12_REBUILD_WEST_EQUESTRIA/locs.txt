重建西小马国
[en.@]
Rebuild Western Equestria

[zh.@desc]
作为采石农场与洛马基重要的交通要道，西小马利亚地区具有着极其重要的地位，但在过去，这里只是从北方前来避难的小马的中转站，犯罪和贫困一直困扰着这片土地。我们应该邀请洛马基与我们共同开发这块土地。随着双方合作的不断深入，这块土地也会随之变得繁荣昌盛。更重要的是让其在我们的完全掌控之下。
[en.@desc]
As a vital thoroughfare between the rock farm and Las Pegasus, the Western Equestria region holds an extremely important position. However, in the past, it served merely as a transit point for ponies seeking refuge from the north, plagued by crime and poverty. We should invite Las Pegasus to join us in developing this land. As cooperation between the two sides deepens, this land will also become prosperous. More importantly, it will be under our complete control.
