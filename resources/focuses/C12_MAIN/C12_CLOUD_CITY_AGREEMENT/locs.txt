云中城协议

Cloud City Agreement

[zh.@desc]
无论是派家族还是自治领，陆马种族都占以绝大多数，从来没有接触过天空，我们的陆马学员们总是不能很好的掌握飞行技巧，训练受伤率居高不下。
如果我们可以前往小马利亚最大的飞马聚集区云中城，与他们据说好吃懒做的总督签订互助条约，那么我们可以以合理的代价组建一支天马雇佣军。
[en.@desc]
Whether it’s the Pie family or the autonomous territory, the Earth pony race makes up the vast majority, having never had contact with the sky. Our Earth pony students have always struggled to master flying skills, resulting in high rates of injury during training.
 If we could travel to Cloud City, the largest gathering place of pegasi in Equestria, and sign a mutual assistance treaty with their reputedly indolent governor, we could establish a pegasus mercenary force at a reasonable cost.
