Steam Power Improve
[zh.@desc]
虽然在洛马基等科技发达地区已经正在有被更先进动力取代的趋势，但蒸汽动力引擎的制造和使用仍然被越来越多采石农场的小马所采纳，军事方面也该如此，通过推广这种神奇的动力装置来取代陆马自己的蹄子
[en.@desc]
Although there was a trend in technologically advanced areas like Las Pegasus towards more advanced power sources, steam engine manufacturing and usage were still being adopted by an increasing number of rock farm. This trend extended to military applications as well, with efforts being made to replace traditional hoof power with these miraculous steam-powered devices.
