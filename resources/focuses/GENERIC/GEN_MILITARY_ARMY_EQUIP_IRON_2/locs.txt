[zh.@desc]
或许，时代并没有变。
曾经，炮响意味着生日派对的开始。现在，炮响预告着凯旋派对的开始。
曾经，清理用完的彩纸碎屑是一种折磨。现在，清理瓦片和尸体也是一种折磨。
曾经，请派对策划大师来设计派对是一种奢侈，不用那么麻烦，只要炮一响，氛围就有了。现在，请战争策划大师来设计战略也是一种奢侈，不用那么麻烦，只要炮一响，优势就有了。
所以你看其实没什么太大区别对吧？
[en.@desc]
Maybe the time hasn’t changed afterall.
Once, the sound of confetti cannons meant the beginning of a birthday party. Now, the sound of cannons heralds the beginning of a victory party.
Once, cleaning up the used confetti scraps was a torture. Now, cleaning up the tiles and bodies is also a torture.
Once, hiring a party planner to design a party was a luxury, but it’s not that troublesome, as long as the cannon goes off, the atmosphere is there. Now, hiring a war planner to design a strategy is also a luxury, but it’s not that troublesome, as long as the cannon goes off, the advantage is there.
So you see, there’s actually not much difference, right?
