Air Theory
[zh.@desc]
在小马利亚世界，空军士兵是一项危险的工作，因为你需要时时刻刻提防着来自各个方向的威胁，不止是空中的敌军，还有糟糕的天气、飞鸟、来自地面的投掷物、彩虹激光、带着螺旋桨的乌龟、不遵守物理定律的粉色陆马还有各种其他花里胡哨的东西。
[en.@desc]
In the world of Equestria, being an air force soldier is a dangerous job, as you need to be on guard against threats from all directions, not just enemy aircraft, but also bad weather, birds, projectiles from the ground, rainbow lasers, turtles with propellers, pink land ponies that don’t obey the laws of physics, and all sorts of other fancy things.
