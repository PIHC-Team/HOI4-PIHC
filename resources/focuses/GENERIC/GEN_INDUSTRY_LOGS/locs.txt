一棵一年的树通常很小，很脆弱，树干细小，分支有限。
一棵十年的树经历了显著的生长，树干更粗更结实，提供更好的结构支持。
一棵二十年的树已经成熟并且根深蒂固，树干粗壮，分支众多，叶子繁茂。
一棵三十年的树已经老而睿智，树干巨大，分支繁茂，叶子茂盛，提供了充足的阴凉和庇护。
一棵四十年的树可能是热情而温暖的，因为它已经被砍伐并变成了柴火。
[en.@desc]
A year 1 tree is typically small and fragile, with a thin trunk and a limited number of branches.
A year 10 tree has experienced significant growth. The trunk is thicker and stronger, providing better structural support.
A year 20 tree is mature and well-established. The trunk is thick and sturdy, and the branches are numerous and full of leaves.
A year 30 tree is old and wise. The trunk is massive and the branches are heavy with foliage, providing ample shade and shelter.
A year 40 tree is probably enthusiastic and warm, as it has been cut down and turned into firewood.
