从前，有一只蚂蚁和一只蚱蜢住在一片草地上。蚂蚁每天都很勤奋地工作，收集煤炭，为即将到来的冬天储存煤炭。蚱蜢却整天唱歌，享受阳光，没有考虑未来。
当冬天来临时，草地变得寒冷刺骨。蚂蚁准备好了它的煤炭储备，用来保持温暖和舒适。相比之下，蚱蜢发现自己在冰冷的天气中发抖和冻结。
绝望和寒冷，蚱蜢走向蚂蚁，寻求温暖和煤炭。蚂蚁提醒蚱蜢，它应该为冬天做好准备，而不是浪费时间。蚱蜢后悔自己的懒惰，学到了一个关于努力工作和未来规划的重要教训。
[en.@desc]
Once upon a time, there was an ant and a grasshopper who lived in a meadow. The ant worked diligently every day, collecting coal and storing it for the approaching winter. The grasshopper, however, spent its days singing and enjoying the sunshine, not thinking about the future.
When winter arrived, the meadow turned bitterly cold. The ant was prepared with its stockpile of coal, which it used to keep warm and comfortable. In contrast, the grasshopper found itself shivering and freezing in the icy cold.
Desperate and cold, the grasshopper approached the ant, seeking warmth and coal. The ant reminded the grasshopper that it should have prepared for the winter instead of wasting its time. The grasshopper regretted its idleness and learned a valuable lesson about the importance of hard work and planning for the future.
