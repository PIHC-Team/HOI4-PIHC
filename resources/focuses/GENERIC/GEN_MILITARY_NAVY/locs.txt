海军理论
[en.@]
Navy Theory
[zh.@desc]
长久以来小马利亚的生物们都忽视了海洋的重要性，海军一直是被忽视的军事分支。然而，随着风暴王国的崩溃，海盗的崛起，以及南露娜洋周围发生的一切，海军变得越来越重要。
[en.@desc]
For long Equestria creatures have overlooked the importance of the sea, and the navy has been a neglected branch of the military. However, with the collapse of the Storm Kingdom, the rise of the Pirates, and whatever is happening around the South Luna Ocean, the navy is becoming more and more important.
