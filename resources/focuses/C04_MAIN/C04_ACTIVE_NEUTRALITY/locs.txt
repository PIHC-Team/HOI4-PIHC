[zh.@desc]
随着层云堡餐馆的整整日上、闪电天马学院回归、谐律主义教育的成功... 层云堡的一切似乎都在向着积极的方向发展。另一方面，帝国正被东方的威胁搞得焦头烂额，第二次起义的阴影恐吓着帝国让其无暇西顾。
正是在这样的形势下，破空认为，层云堡是时候迎来一个新的发展阶段了：层云堡将要从一个不起眼的小地方，变成影响整个帝国的重要力量。我们将要公开地与帝国划清界限，打出谐律的旗号，号召所有自治领加入“积极的中立”俱乐部，最终拨乱反正，实现谐律的全面复兴。

[en.@desc]
With the prosperity of the Stratusburg Diner, the return of the Wonderbolts Academy, and the success of Harmonism education... everything in Stratusburg seems to be moving in a positive direction. On the other hoof, the Empire is distracted by the threat from the east, and the shadow of a second Uprising is haunting the Empire, leaving it with no time to look west.
In this situation, Sky Stinger believes that Stratusburg is ready for a new stage of development: Stratusburg is about to transform from an insignificant place into an important force that affects the entire Empire. We will openly draw a line with the Empire, raise the banner of Harmonism, call on all autonomous territories to join the “Active Neutrality” club, and finally set things right and achieve the full revival of Harmonism.
