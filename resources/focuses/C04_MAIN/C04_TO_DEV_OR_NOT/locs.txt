To Develop or Not, That is the Question
[zh.@desc]
发展层云堡的经济，新增采矿业等收入来源、提升居民的收入水平和生活质量，总不会有任何坏处吧？
然而，喷火清楚，层云堡能存活至今的最重要原因不是军事强大，而是经济落后：帝国从征服层云堡获取的预计收益甚至不够弥补镇压的开支。一旦层云堡经济发展，帝国入侵的利益增加，层云堡的生存反而会更加困难。

[en.@desc]
Develop the economy of Stratusburg, add new sources of income such as mining, improve the income level and quality of life of residents, and there will be no harm, right?
However, it is clear to Spitfire, that the most important reason why Stratusburg can survive to this day is not military strength, but economic backwardness: the estimated income that the Empire can obtain from conquering Stratusburg is not enough to make up for the cost of suppression. Once the economy of Stratusburg develops, the benefits of the Empire’s invasion will increase, and the survival of Stratusburg will become more difficult.
