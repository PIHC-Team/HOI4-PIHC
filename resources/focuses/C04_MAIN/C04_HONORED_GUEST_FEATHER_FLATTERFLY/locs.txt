初次投资的大获成功让羽蝶更加坚定了将层云堡当做新的改革试验场的决心。回到坎特洛特的羽蝶很快得到了帝国内改革派的支持，花花短裤更是亲自在女皇面前为羽蝶背书。羽蝶带着一纸帝国公文回到层云堡时，这位曾经的云中城自由贸易试验区项目的总指挥，如今成为了层云堡气象局长... 只不过，即使喷火清楚羽蝶纯粹出于好心，她还是无法容忍一位帝国指派的官员重新接管天气工厂：上次是自发的投资合作，这次是帝国的命令，这是两回事。可以说，一纸女皇亲笔签名的公文不仅没能为羽蝶推行改革带来方便，反而成为其就任气象局长最大的障碍。

[en.@desc]
The huge success of the first investment made Feather Flatterfly more determined to use Cloudsdale as a new experimental field for his reform. After returning to Canterlot, Feather Flatterfly quickly gained the support of the reformists within the Empire, and Fancy Pants even endorsed Feather Flatterfly in front of the Empress. When Feather Flatterfly returned to Cloudsdale with an imperial document, the former commander of the Cloudsdale FTZ project had now become the director of the Equestria Meteorological Bureau... However, even though Spitfire knew that Feather Flatterfly was purely well-intentioned, she could not tolerate an imperial official taking over the weather factory again. Previous cooperation was a spontaneous investment, this one is an imperial order, which makes it completely different. It turned out that the imperial document signed by the Empress not only did not make it easier for Feather Flatterfly to implement reforms, but instead became the biggest obstacle to his appointment as the director of the Meteorological Bureau.
