Rebuild Wonderbolts

[zh.@desc]
时隔四年多，退役的闪电天马队员和学员们再次踏入了曾经的学院。感慨中，也有些许的不安。
当然，层云堡如今还隶属于帝国，公然重建“闪电天马队”这一前政权的空军部队必然会招致帝国的强势回应，所以目前为止，新队伍的名字还是“马芬天马队”。所有的徽章都是双层的 —— 外面一层是马芬图案，而里面一层则是闪电天马队的原本徽章。待到时机成熟，层云堡只需要撕下外面的徽章...

[en.@desc]
After more than four years, the retired Wonderbolts and students once again entered the former academy. There is a sense of nostalgia, but also a little unease.
Of course, Stratusburg is still part of the Empire. A blatant reconstruction of the “Wonderbolts” — the air force of the former regime — will inevitably provoke a strong response from the Empire. Therefore, the new Wonderbolts is named “Muffinbolts” for now. All the badges are double-layered — the outer layer is a muffin pattern, while the inner layer is the original Wonderbolts badge. When the time is right, Stratusburg only needs to tear off the outer badge, and all will be revealed...
