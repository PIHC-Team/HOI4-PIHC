The Release Party

[zh.@desc]
由于层云堡警方掌握的证据只是闪尘和滚雷非法持有少量武器，因此便没有理由长时间关押他们。在他们被释放的那天，“淘汰者”们当即在警察局门前的云朵上做起了杂技表演，庆祝他们的领导者回归。
虽然他们的嚣张与吵闹引来了不少的居民投诉，但层云堡警方也只能无可奈何地看着闪尘带着他们扬长而去 — 即使所有小马都清楚他们还是会再次回来的。

但奇怪的是，迎接的队伍里似乎却并没有火药桶的身影。
[en.@desc]
As the only evidence the Stratusburg police had against Lightning Dust and Rolling Thunder was the possession of a small amount of illegal weapons, there was no reason to keep them in custody for long. On the day of their release, the Washouts immediately began performing acrobatics on the clouds in front of the police station to celebrate the return of their leader.
Although their antics and noise drew many complaints from the residents, the Stratusburg police could only watch helplessly as Lightning Dust and her followers flew away — even though everypony knew they would probably come back here again.

But strangely, there was no sign of Short Fuse among the welcoming crowd.
