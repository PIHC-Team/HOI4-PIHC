Sire’s Hollow Preservation Plan

[en.@desc]
Though Firelight has agreed to share governance of Sire’s Hollow with Stellar Flare, that does not mean he agrees with her policies during her reign.
Sire’s Hollow is a beautiful little town, and that beauty should be preserved and maintained, not destroyed by Stellar Flare’s ambitions.
Desire brews danger. Thus, within the framework of peaceful negotiation, Firelight will do his best to remove that danger.
