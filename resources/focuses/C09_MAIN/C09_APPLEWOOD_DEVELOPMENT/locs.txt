The City of the Future
[zh.@desc]
苹果坞是一个绝妙的地区：
这里此前有着城市一座城市，因此建筑地基、排水设施等已经完全建设完毕；
这里已经被废弃，因此拆迁将无需征求任何居民的同意；
这里的地理位置优越，位于小马利亚和幻形灵尼亚大陆的必经之处，毗邻南露娜洋、洛马基和彩虹尽头。
在这里，我们将施展我们的宏伟计划，建设一座未来之城，一座整个小马利亚世界为之侧目的城市。
[en.@desc]
Applewood is a perfect area:
It has been a city, so the infrastructure like building foundations and drainage facilities are already built;
It has been abandoned, so the demolition will not require any consent from residents;
It has a superior geographical location, located at the intersection of the Equestria continent and the Changelinia continent, adjacent to the South Luna Ocean, Las Pegaus and Rainbow’s End.
Here, we will carry out our grand plan to build a city of the future, a city that will attract the attention of the entire Equestria world.
