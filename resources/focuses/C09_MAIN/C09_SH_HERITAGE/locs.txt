名流谷文化遗产
[en.@]
Sire’s Hollow Heritage
[zh.@desc]
即使在这样一个动乱的年代，我们也不能忽视我们珍贵的历史文化遗产。
要铭记我们的传统的重要性，其中凝聚了几千年来的痛苦与欢愉。当我们回望过去，便听到城镇缔造者的真言，那真言教会了我们，怎么活下去。
[en.@desc]
Even in such turbulent times, we must not ignore our precious historical and cultural heritage.
Remember the importance of our traditions. They hold the pain and joy of thousands of years. When we connect to the past, the townfounders speak, and they tell us how to suervive.
