接触云中城

Approach to Cloudsdale
[zh.@desc]
在其他地方，当你想要推销一支股票，你需要进行市场研究，了解目标受众的投资偏好和风险承受能力；你需要定制推销策略，强调该支股票的特点和潜在回报，以吸引潜在投资者的兴趣；你需要准备相关的市场材料和演示文稿，以清晰、简洁地传达关键信息，并与潜在投资者进行有效的沟通和互动。
但在云中城，你只需要把这支股票改名为“马芬股票”即可。
[en.@desc]
In other places, when you want to promote a stock, you need to conduct market research to understand the investment preferences and risk tolerance of the target audience; you need to customize the promotion strategy, emphasize the characteristics and potential returns of the stock to attract the interest of potential investors; you need to prepare relevant market materials and presentation slides to clearly and concisely convey key information, and effectively communicate and interact with potential investors.
But in Cloudsdale, all you need to do is rename the stock to “Muffin Stock”.
