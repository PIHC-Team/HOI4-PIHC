[en.@desc]
Though Firelight has read many ancient war books, it is clear that those things are no longer suitable for the present. He learned from his daughter that developing the army and producing equipment requires military factories, but he cannot let those stinking, noisy factories destroy the tranquility of the Sire’s Hollow.
Meanwhile, Seaddle, although there has never been a military factory here before, has a dock — and therefore has a heavy industry base, which is a good choice.
