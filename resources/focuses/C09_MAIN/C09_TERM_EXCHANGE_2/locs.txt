自从恒星耀斑和火光和解后，在名流谷联合会的框架下，由发展委员会和保护协会规律地交替领导着，名流谷的居民们在这里享受着现代与传统交织的平静生活。
与恒星耀斑的宏伟安排不同，火光更在乎如何维护名流谷的传统和平静生活——火光更喜欢名流谷现在的样子。无论是对帝国、洛马基、还是幻形灵，火光都更喜欢“合作”的概念，而不是“竞争”。

After Stella Flare and Firelight reconciled, the leadership of Sire’s Hollow alternates between the Development Committee and the Preservation Society regularly. The residents of Sire’s Hollow enjoy a peaceful life that intertwines modernity and tradition.
Unlike Stella Flare’s grand arrangements, Firelight cares more about maintaining the tradition and peaceful life of Sire’s Hollow - Firelight prefers Sire’s Hollow as it is now. Whether it is with the Empire, Las Pegasus, or the Changelings, Firelight prefers the concept of “cooperation” rather than “competition”.
