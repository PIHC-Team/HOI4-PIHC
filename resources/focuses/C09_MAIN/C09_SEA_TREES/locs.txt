舰队橡树林
[en.@]
Fleet Oak Forest
[zh.@desc]
当我们已经建立了一支初步的海军填补了空白之后，接下来就是考虑长远的海军发展规划了。
我们计划在苹果坞种植90,000棵橡树，这将为我们提供足够的木材来建造一支强大的海军。
当树木生长足够后，我们将会通知委员会，以便他们能够开始建造船只。
[en.@desc]
After we have established a preliminary navy to fill the gap, the next step is to consider the long-term navy development plan.
We plan to plant 90,000 oak trees in Applewood, which will provide us with enough wood to build a powerful navy.
When the trees grow enough, we will notify the committee so that they can begin building ships.
