[zh.@desc]
自从恒星耀斑和火光和解后，在名流谷联合会的框架下，由发展委员会和保护协会规律地交替领导着，名流谷的居民们在这里享受着现代与传统交织的平静生活。
在火光的任期内，凭借着传统和保守的主张，名流谷龟缩着躲过了终末之战和随后的诸多动乱。如今，恒星耀斑将要接过火光的职位，她的任期将会是名流谷的发展和开放时期。在联合发展委员会恒星耀斑的领导下，名流谷的目标是走向全小马利亚，成为新兴的经济中心。
[en.@desc]
After Stella Flare and Firelight reconciled, the leadership of Sire’s Hollow alternates between the Development Committee and the Preservation Society regularly. The residents of Sire’s Hollow enjoy a peaceful life that intertwines modernity and tradition.
During Firelight’s term, Sire’s Hollow survived the Ultimate Battle and the subsequent turmoil by hiding behind tradition and conservatism. Now, Stella Flare is about to take over Firelight’s position, and her term will be a period of development and openness for Sire’s Hollow. Under Stella Flare’s leadership, the goal of Sire’s Hollow is to become a new economic center in all of Equestria.
