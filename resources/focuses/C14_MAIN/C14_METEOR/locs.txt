However, the Empire’s behavior this time has violated this tacit understanding, and we must take action: Cozy Glow? Who does she think she is? She doesn’t really think that she can do whatever she wants with the title of Empress, does she? We once got rid of the mayor sent by the Royal Commission, and now we can get rid of the Empire’s special action team as well.
