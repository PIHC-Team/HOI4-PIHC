Armed Protest

[en.@desc]
Empire seems to think we won’t act, but they’re wrong. In fact, if we take up arms, the Empire is not willing to send troops across the Endless Forest to punish us - and they might not even win. But on the other hand, this also means that we must be prepared for public opinion, and our defiance of the Empire must find a reasonable excuse. Well, let’s start with hindering the Empire’s special action team.
