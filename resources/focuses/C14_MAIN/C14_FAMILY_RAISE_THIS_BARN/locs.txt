搭起谷仓
[en.@]
Raise This Barn
[zh.@desc]
为了延续苹果家族杰出成员苹果嘉儿留下的传统，也为了等待她有朝一日回到家族，上次家族聚会中的谷仓搭建活动将成为每次家族聚会的惯例活动。
[en.@desc]
To carry on the tradition left by Applejack, an outstanding member of the Apple family, and to await the day she may return, barn raising will become a custom at every family reunion.
