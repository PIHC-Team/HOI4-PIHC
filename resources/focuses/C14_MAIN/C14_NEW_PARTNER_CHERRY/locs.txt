Unite Cherry Family

“起初他们针对苹果家族，我没有站出来，因为我不是苹果。然后他们针对梨家族，我没有站出来，因为我不是梨。然后他们针对种子家族，我没有站出来，因为我不是种子。然后他们针对樱桃家族，那时已经没有小马站出来了为我说话了。” —— 马丁·樱桃，樱桃家族诗歌作者
[en.@desc]
“THEY CAME FIRST for the Apple Family, and I didn’t speak up because I wasn’t an Apple. THEN THEY CAME for the Pear Family, and I didn’t speak up because I wasn’t a Pear. THEN THEY CAME for the Seed Family, and I didn’t speak up because I wasn’t a Seed. THEN THEY CAME for the Cherry Family, and by that time no one was left to speak up.” — Martin Cherry, Cherry Family poet
