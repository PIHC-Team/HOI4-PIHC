在苹果家族的惯例中，苹果家族聚会是苹果小马们一辈子都难得见到的家族团圆盛会。虽然不久之前，家族刚刚在苹果农场举办过一次大型家族聚会，但... 众所周知，这是一个艰难的时代。为了保住苹果家族的势力，在这个混乱的时代稳定家族的团结和信心，我们认为有必要再次举办一次家族聚会。布雷本将以“表哥”的身份邀请全小马利亚的苹果家族，共同商讨如何应对这个时代的挑战。
[en.@desc]
In Apple Family tradition, the Apple Family Reunion is a rare event that Apple ponies can hardly see in their lifetime. Although the family has just held a large family reunion at the Apple Farm not long ago, ... we all know that this is a difficult time. In order to maintain the power of the Apple Family, and to stabilize the unity and confidence of the family in this chaotic era, we believe it is necessary to hold another family reunion. Braeburn, as the “Cousin”, will invite the entire Apple Family all over Equestria to discuss how to deal with the challenges of this era together.
