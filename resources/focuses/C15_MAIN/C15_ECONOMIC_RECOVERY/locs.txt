The Return of Love
[zh.@desc]
战争摧毁了水晶帝国的经济核心——你以为说的是水晶出口贸易么？不，是爱意，随着水晶帝国的爱意被大量贡奉给幻形灵，水晶帝国的小马们在变得冷漠。
通过在水晶小马中宣扬爱的力量，水晶帝国将重新获得活力，让小马们在爱意的包围下重新拥有活力，精神满满投入到水晶矿开采和出口中，或者其他什么工作中去。毕竟，水晶出口贸易还是很重要的。
[en.@desc]
The war has destroyed the economic core of the Crystal Empire - you think it’s the crystal export? No, it’s love. As the love of the Crystal Empire is devoted to the changelings, the ponies of the Crystal Empire are becoming indifferent.
By promoting the power of love in the Crystal Ponies, the Crystal Empire will regain its energy, allowing ponies to regain vitality in the embrace of love, and devote themselves to the mining and export of crystal mines, or whatever. After all, crystal export trade is still important.
