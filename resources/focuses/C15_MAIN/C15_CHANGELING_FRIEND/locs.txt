向幻形灵妥协
[en.@]
Compromise with the Changelings
[zh.@desc]
幻形灵帝国和《协定》的影响力依旧在，我们应该向幻形灵做出让步，换取幻形灵对水晶帝国发展的支持。毕竟，一个更加繁荣、产出更多爱意的水晶帝国符合幻形灵帝国的利益。
[en.@desc]
The influence of the Changeling Empire and the Pact is still huge. We should make concessions to the Changelings in exchange for their support for the development of the Crystal Empire. After all, a more prosperous and more loving Crystal Empire is in the interest of the Changeling Empire.
