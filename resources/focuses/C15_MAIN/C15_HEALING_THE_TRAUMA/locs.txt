Healing the Truama
[zh.@desc]
水晶帝国输掉了战争，成为坎特洛特和幻形灵的殖民地，一个沉睡千年的国度刚刚被唤醒便被逼迫着跪地，水晶小马们被夺走了他们最珍视的骄傲。
战争的创伤还未过去，接踵而至的是水晶小马们无处发泄的迷茫，愤怒、悲伤、绝望、无感，这些情绪在他们心中翻腾，他们不再知道该如何看待自己的国家。
在这种时刻，一位君主，便是这个国家的最后一道防线：即使和其他普通小马一样无助，风雪之心公主必须要表现得庄严、坚定，而又亲切。
一位年纪还尚未成熟的女儿，如今必须要成为所有普通水晶小马们母亲般的形象，在一切都改变的时代里，让水晶小马们看到一盏不变的明灯。
[en.@desc]
The Crystal Empire lost the war, becoming the colony of Canterlot and the Changelings. A kingdom that has just been sleeping for a thousand years is forced to kneel just after being awakened, and the Crystal Ponies have been deprived of their most cherished pride.
The trauma of war has not yet passed, and the subsequent confusion of the Crystal Ponies is nowhere to be vented. Anger, sadness, despair, and numbness are boiling in their hearts, and they no longer know how to look at their country.
At this moment, a monarch is the last line of defense for this country: even if she is as helpless just as other ordinary ponies, Princess Flurry Heart must appear dignified, firm, and yet kind.
A daughter who has not yet matured must now become the image of a mother for all ordinary Crystal Ponies, and let them see an unchanging beacon in an era of change.
