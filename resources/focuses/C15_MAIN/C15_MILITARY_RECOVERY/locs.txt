Revive the Army
[zh.@desc]
尚武精神是水晶帝国的高贵传统，但如今，水晶帝国被各种条约所束缚，无法再发展军事。很显然，想要复兴水晶帝国，就必须重振军队。

[en.@desc]
Martialism is the noble tradition of the Crystal Empire, but now, the Crystal Empire is bound by various treaties and cannot develop its military. Obviously, in order to revive the Crystal Empire, it is necessary to revive the army.
However, the Changelings will not peacefully watch the Crystal Empire rebuild its army, so they will definitely try to obstruct it.
