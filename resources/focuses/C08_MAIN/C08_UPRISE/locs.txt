[zh.@desc]
1001年冬，距终末之战已经过去了两年，星光与无序终于有了足够的力量向帝国发起挑战。然而，彼时的和煦光流也已经逐渐站稳脚跟。在战争中，抵抗组织成功建立了几片稳固的根据地，但却未能如愿向坎特洛特进军。与此同时，帝国管辖范围内的各地与星光呼应的抵抗组织也被尽数剿灭，星光和无序的联合抵抗组织也被步步紧逼的帝国精锐压缩。
[en.@desc]
In the winter of 1001, two years after the Ultimate Battle, Starlight and Discord finally had enough strength to challenge the Empire. However, Cozy Glow had gradually stabilized the situation. In the war, the resistance organization successfully established several solid bases, but failed to march towards Canterlot as they wished. At the same time, the resistance organizations in various places under the jurisdiction of the Empire that collaborated with Starlight were also wiped out, and the joint resistance organization of Starlight and Discord was pushed back by the elite forces of the Empire.
