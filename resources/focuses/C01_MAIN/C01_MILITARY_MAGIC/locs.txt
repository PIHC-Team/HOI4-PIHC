Witchcraft Reserves
[zh.@desc]
为即将到来的战争准备好足够的巫术武器，但要小心，因为这些神秘的工具是有代价的，据说那些使用它们的小马必须付出比他们的理智更多的代价。武器只负责施放书中的法术，而施法者们则负责献祭他们的灵魂。
[en.@desc]
Prepare enough witchcraft weapons for the upcoming war, but beware, for these mystical tools come at a price, as it is whispered that those who wield them must trade more than just bits and pieces of their sanity. The weapons are only responsible for casting spells in the books, but it is the caster who must sacrifice their soul.
