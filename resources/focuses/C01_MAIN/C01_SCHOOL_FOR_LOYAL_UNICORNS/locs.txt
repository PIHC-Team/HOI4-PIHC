忠诚独角兽学院
[en.@]
School for Loyal Unicorns
[zh.@desc]
天才独角兽学院是前小马利亚公主，战犯塞拉斯蒂娅所设立的学校，专门招收和培养魔法能力强的天才独角兽，受到战争的影响而关停。
如今时代已经不同了，相比于魔法天赋，我们更应该注重培养忠诚的公民。因此，我们应该重新命名并重新开放学校，让更多帝国公民有机会接受更好的教育。
[en.@desc]
The School for Gifted Unicorns was established by the former Princess of Equestria, the war criminal Celestia, to recruit and train talented unicorns with strong magical abilities, and was closed due to the impact of the war.
Now the times have changed, and we should pay more attention to cultivating loyal citizens rather than magical talents. Therefore, we should rename and reopen the school and give more citizens of the Empire the opportunity to receive better education.
