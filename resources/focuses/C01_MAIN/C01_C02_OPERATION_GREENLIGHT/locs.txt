[zh.@desc]
如果说坎特洛特是小马利亚“权力”的中心，马哈顿、洛马基与名流谷可能是“金钱”的中心，那么小马谷，则绝对是“民众”的中心。
小马谷，一个传统农业发达而又逐渐走向原始工业化的地区，有着小马利亚最为庞大的马口。几乎所有的小马都或多或少有着小马谷的血统，或在小马谷有着自己的利益。
然而，就是这样一个重要的地区，却也因为其“民众”的属性而尚未成为帝国的囊中之物——我们虽拥有者名义的管辖权，但当初为了牵制特丽克西公爵而建立的“民主”制度如今正在成真，并在将我们自己一并摧毁。
帝国、公爵、民众，这三者之间的关系已经变得如此复杂，以至于我们无法再像过去那样简单地用“武力”来解决问题。也许如今帝国获胜的唯一方法，就是让公爵与民众，同归于尽。
[en.@desc]
If Canterlot is the center of “power” in Equestria, if Manehattan, Las Pegasus and Sire’s Hollow are the center of “money”, then Ponyville is definitely the center of “ponies”.
Ponyville, a region with developed traditional agriculture and gradually moving towards primitive industrialization, has the largest population in Equestria. Almost all ponies either have some Ponyville bloodline, or have their own interests in Ponyville.
However, it is such an important region that has not yet become the Empire’s possession due to its “ponies” specialties. Although we have the nominal reign, the “democracy” system we established once to restrain Duchess Trixie is now becoming more and more true, and is destroying us at the same time.
The three-way relationship between the Empire, the Duke/Duchess, and the Ponies has become so complicated that we can no longer solve problems as simply as we did in the past with “force”. Perhaps the only way for the Empire to win now is to let the Duke/Duchess and the Ponies perish together.
