驹歇尔计划
[en.@]
The Mareshall Plan
[zh.@desc]
相比于帝国的直辖领土，应该先在各个分封领土进行经济发展，强化其对中央的忠诚度，并计划通过贸易和产业转移反哺帝国中央。
[en.@desc]
Instead of directly developing the imperial territories, we should first develop the various fiefdoms, strengthen their loyalty to the central government, and plan to feed back the central government through trade and industrial transfer.
