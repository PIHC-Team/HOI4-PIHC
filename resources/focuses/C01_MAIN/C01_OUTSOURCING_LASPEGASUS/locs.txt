Order at Las Pegasus

[en.@desc]
Las Pegasus, as the most developed area in the central continent of Equestria, has the longest trade tradition. The industrial capacity of the Las Pegasus Consortium can fully support the construction of the Empire’s facilities. However, Las Pegasus has already monopolized too much. Handing over this major project to Las Pegasus may help to boost the arrogance of the Consortium.
