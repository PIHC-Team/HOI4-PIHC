天才独角兽学院
[en.@]
School for Gifted Unicorns
[zh.@desc]
天才独角兽学院是前小马利亚公主，战犯塞拉斯蒂娅所设立的学校，专门招收和培养魔法能力强的天才独角兽，受到战争的影响而关停。
如今，我们有必要辩证地看待这个学校，塞拉斯蒂娅的错误不代表学校的错误，学校的存在客观上有利于小马利亚的发展。因此我们应该重新开放学校，让更多的帝国公民有机会接受更好的教育。
[en.@desc]
The School for Gifted Unicorns was established by the former Princess of Equestria, the war criminal Celestia, to recruit and train talented unicorns with strong magical abilities, and was closed due to the impact of the war.
Now, it is necessary for us to look at this school dialectically. Celestia’s mistakes do not represent the mistakes of the school. The existence of the school is objectively beneficial to the development of Equestria. Therefore, we should reopen the school and give more citizens of the Empire the opportunity to receive better education.
