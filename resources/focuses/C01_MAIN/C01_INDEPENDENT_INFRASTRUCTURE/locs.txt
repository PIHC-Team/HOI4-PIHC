自主基础设施建设
[en.@]
Independent Infrastucture
[zh.@desc]
虽然小马利亚帝国的条件较为困难，但是基础设施建设的重大工作还是应该自主完成，不能过度依赖其他外部的帮助。独立的建设可能是更加缓慢的，但也许是必要的。
[en.@desc]
Although the conditions for the Equestria Empire are relatively difficult, the major work of infrastructure construction should be independently completed, and cannot be overly dependent on other external assistance. Independent construction may be slower, but it may be necessary.
