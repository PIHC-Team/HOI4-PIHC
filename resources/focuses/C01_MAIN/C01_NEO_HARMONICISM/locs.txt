新谐律主义理念
[en.@]
Neo-harmonism
[zh.@desc]
目前主流理论认为六个谐律元素是：魔法、诚实、慷慨、忠诚、欢乐和善良。
但是根据一份旧时代的文献资料（考试问卷），友谊女皇曾经提出六个谐律元素应该是五件高领毛衣和一个奶酪刨丝器。
虽然这一答案在当时被认为错误，但鉴于友谊女皇的成功和批卷老师暮光闪闪的失败，现在我们有必要对女皇当时这一充满创意的猜想重新进行严谨的理论考据。
[en.@desc]
The current mainstream theory holds that the six elements of harmony are: magic, honesty, generosity, loyalty, laughter, and kindness.
However, according to an old document (an exam paper), the Empress of Friendship once proposed that the six elements of harmony should be five turtlenecks and a cheese grater.
Although this answer was considered incorrect at the time, given the success of the Empress and the failure of the grader Twilight Sparkle, it is now necessary to re-examine the Empress’s creative conjecture with rigorous theoretical evidence.
