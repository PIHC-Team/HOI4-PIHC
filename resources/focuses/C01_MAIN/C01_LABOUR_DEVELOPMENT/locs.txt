工业的发展，离不开劳动力。帝国通过宣传活动，提倡小马们为国家多多创造和培养新的劳动力。同时，帝国也会在政策上给予优惠，例如下调允许参与生产活动的最低年龄，让年轻的小马们可以更快如愿加入他们梦寐以求的辛勤劳作中去。
[en.@desc]
The development of industry cannot be separated from labor. The Empire advocates that ponies create and cultivate new labor force for the country through propaganda activities. At the same time, the Empire will also give preferential policies, such as lowering the minimum age allowed to participate in production activities, so that young ponies can work hard as soon as possible, which is definitely what they have been dreaming of.
