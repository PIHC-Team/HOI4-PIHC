外包基础设施建设
[en.@]
Outsourcing Infrastructure
[zh.@desc]
由于本土缺乏条件，最好还是将建设工作外包给独立领地。外包的工作往往完成较快，质量也较高，但是我们将为此支付一笔不菲的费用。
[en.@desc]
Due to the lack of local conditions, it is best to outsource the construction work to an independent territory. Outsourced work is often completed faster and of higher quality, but we will pay a hefty fee for this.
