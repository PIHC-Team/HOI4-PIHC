Operation <=Blueberry=> 
[zh.@desc]
呆蹄是一个不错的领主：她很听话，从来不会与女皇作对；她很愚蠢，从来不会想到反抗；她很善良，从来不会伤害任何小马；她很单纯，从来不会怀疑我们讲给她的一切经过精心包装的谎言。
然而，这种愚蠢的盟友似乎也并未给帝国带来太多好处。呆蹄与剪剪蜗蜗那些蠢货不同。虽然剪剪蜗蜗愚蠢而又忠诚，但不要误以为这适用于一切小马，愚蠢从不只是忠诚的代名词。
也许只需要无序或星光的一车马芬，就足以让她叛变帝国；也许只需要一段耳语，云中城的野心家就可以假借她的名义将帝国推入深渊。
更重要的是，帝国为何能够允许一位发自真心受到民众爱戴，对其赞美甚至超越对女皇的崇拜的领主存在？
[en.@desc]
Derpy is quite a satisfying Duchess: she is obedient and never opposes the Empress; she is stupid and never thinks of rebellion; she is kind and never hurts any pony; she is simple and never doubts the carefully packaged lies we tell her.
However, this foolish ally does not seem to have brought much benefit to the Empire. Derpy is very different from those stupid but loyal Snips and Snails. Although Snips and Snails are stupid and loyal, do not think that this applies to all ponies, foolishness is never just a synonym for loyalty.
Perhaps all it takes, is a cart full of muffins from Discord or Starlight, to make her betray the Empire; perhaps all it takes is a whisper, for the ambitious usurper in Cloudsdale to push the Empire into the abyss in her name.
More importantly, why can the Empire allow a Duchess who is sincerely loved by the ponies, and whose praise even surpasses that of the Empress, to exist?
