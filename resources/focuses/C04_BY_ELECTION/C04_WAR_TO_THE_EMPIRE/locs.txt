[en.@desc]
The situation is different from the past, we need an excuse to convince Stratusburg to fight against the Empire... But meanwhile, what we can achieve is no longer that conservative plan we used to have: Sky Stinger firmly believes that the true mastermind behind Spitfire’s assassination is Canterlot. If we can find solid evidence linking Canterlot to the assassination, we can ignite the anger of all Stratusburg and Cloudsdale, not to mention those lords and governors of other dominions and duchies who will have to be very careful not to become the next Spitfire — this will be the first domino to the downfall of an Empire.
