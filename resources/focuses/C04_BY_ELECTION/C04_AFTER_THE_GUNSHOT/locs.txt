After the Gunshot
[zh.@desc]
突如其来的枪声打断了层云堡的“重返天空”计划，在失去喷火领导的一片狼藉中，只剩下破空与雾轨的茫然无措。
那些刚刚重新穿上制服的闪电天马们不得不再次脱下他们，那些试图将水晶工业扩展到帝国辖区的富商也不得不收起他们的野心，层云堡的反对势力们正在暗中窃喜，而那些层云堡居民们则只能够祈祷与等待。
谁能知道将来会发生什么呢？

[en.@desc]
The sudden gunshot interrupted the “Return to the Sky” plan in Stratusburg, leaving only Sky Stinger and Vapor Trail in a state of confusion after losing Spitfire’s leadership.
The Wonderbolts, who had just put on their uniforms again, had to take them off once more. The wealthy merchants, who tried to expand the crystal industry into the Empire’s territory, had to put away their ambitions. The opposition forces in Stratusburg were secretly rejoicing, while the residents of Stratusburg were only praying and waiting.
Who could know what would happen in the future?
