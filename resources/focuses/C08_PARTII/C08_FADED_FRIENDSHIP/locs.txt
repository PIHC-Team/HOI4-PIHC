Twilight Sparkle and her friends vanished from Equestria's sight. There were no records, no messages, and no pony knew where they had been taken. In three years, three princesses had disappeared, leaving only Princess Cadance under house arrest in the Crystal Empire. After the so-called “Jahr Null”, the fate of Equestria plunged into darkness... Yet life had to continue, and the truth had to be found.
