穿越迷雾
[en.@]
Through the Fog
[zh.@desc]
在迷雾中，一直困在原地纠结方向只会导致迷失，最终招致不测。

想从迷雾中脱身，抵抗军必须从处决中的停滞抽身，坚定地向着前方再次出发。
[en.@desc]
When you are in a fog, staying in place and hesitating about the direction will only lead to getting lost and eventually to disaster.

To escape the fog, the Resistance must break free from the current stalemate, be prepared once more, and steadily move forward.
