消逝的十年
[en.@]
Decade of Nonexistence
[zh.@desc]
§g“邪恶者的记忆将得到祝福，但正义者的名字必要朽烂。”§!
很快，什么都不会剩下。那曾经的十数年，就像是从未存在过一样。所有的记忆都将变得模糊，然后再变得空白。
没有小马会关心，甚至没有小马会记得：没有什么自由的水晶帝国，没有什么爱意的公主，没有什么和平的时代，一切回到了千年前它们应该的样子。
[en.@desc]
§g“The memory of the wicked is a blessing, but the name of the righteous will rot.”§!
Soon, nothing will be left. The decades that were, will be as if they never were. All memories will become hazy, then blank.
No pony will care, and no pony will remember: no free Crystal Empire, no loving princess, no peaceful era, all will return to how they should have been a thousand years ago.
