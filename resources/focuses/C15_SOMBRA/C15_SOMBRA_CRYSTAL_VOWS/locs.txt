效忠的誓言
[en.@]
Oath of Allegiance
[zh.@desc]
§g“我是世界的黑暗。我的追随者，将永远不会走在光明之中，而将拥有生命的黑暗。”§!
暗影的子嗣如预言所说，为暗影重新带回了属于它们的‘光明’，也给水晶帝国带来了彻底的新生。无论是趋向黑暗，还是在那窒息的头盔之下如落水者紧抓绳索般试图抓住一丝丝的光明，那么都值得向他效忠。
[en.@desc]
§g“I am the darkness of the world. Whoever follows me will never walk in light, but will have the darkness of life.”§!
The descendant of shadows, as the prophecy told, brought back to the shadows the ‘light’ that belonged to them, and brought a complete rebirth to the Crystal Empire.
Whether you choose to pursue darkness or try to grasp a strand of light under that suffocating helmet as a drowner holding on to a straw, it is worth pledging allegiance to him.
