噤声的恐惧
[en.@]
Silence of Fear
[zh.@desc]
§g“静默地等待着吧，等待着国王的救赎。”§!
寂静，彻底的寂静，没有什么恐惧比得上持续的寂静。当你张开嘴，却无法发出声音；当你想要大声呼喊，却只能无声挣扎；当你想要表达自己的愤怒，却甚至不敢以凶狠的眼神回应。那么，你还有什么理由不去听从我的命令呢？
[en.@desc]
§g“It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the King.”§!
Silence, complete silence, nothing is more fearful than continuous silence. When you open your mouth, but cannot make a sound; when you want to shout out loud, but can only silently struggle; when you want to express your anger, but dare not even respond with a fierce look. So, what reason do you have not to obey my orders?
