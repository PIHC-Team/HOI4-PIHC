谐律的末日
[en.@]
Doom of Harmonism
[zh.@desc]
§g“臣民们，我藉我们的王森布拉的名劝你们，你们要说一样的话，毫无分裂，在意念和意见上同心合意。”§!
比和谐更好的是一致，比一致更好的是统一，比统一更好的是唯一。
当你物理消灭所有的不和谐，你得到的，无论是什么，难道不能称之为和谐吗？而要做到这一点，第一步，就是先消灭那些喊着“和谐”的疯子。
[en.@desc]
§g“I appeal to you, subjects, by the name of our King Soombra, that all of you agree, and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be united in the same mind and the same judgment.”§!
What’s better than harmony is concord, what’s better than concord is unity, and what’s better than unity is the one and only one.
When you physically eliminate all disharmony, what you get, no matter what it is, can’t it be called harmony? And to do this, the first step is to eliminate those madponies who shout for “harmony”.
