暗影的信徒
[en.@]
Followers of Shadow
[zh.@desc]
§g“让顺从与忠诚永不离开你；将它们系在你的颈项上，刻在你心中。这样你必在王与影魔的眼前蒙恩得福。”§!
积极的情感总是容易战胜消极的情感，就像爱总是强于仇恨，友谊总是强于纷争。即使是热爱恐惧的黑晶王森布拉也不得不承认这一点。
当恐惧者见到安全，当黑暗中摸索者见到光明，他们总会不顾一切地冲向那虚假的希望，像是飞蛾扑火一般。
因此，暗影的队伍需要更多积极的情感 — 比如信仰总是强于怀疑，忠诚总是强于背叛，遵从总是强于反抗。
[en.@desc]
§g“Let obedient and faithfulness never leave you; bind them around your neck, write them on the tablet of your heart. Then you will win favor and a good name in the sight of King and Umbrums.”§!
Positive emotions always prevail over negative emotions, just as love is always stronger than hatred, and friendship is always stronger than conflict. Even King Sombra, who has a deep passion for fear, has to admit this.
When the fearful see safety, when the ones groping in the dark see the light, they will always rush towards that false hope, like moths to a flame.
Therefore, the followers of shadows need more positive emotions — such as faith is always stronger than doubt, loyalty is always stronger than betrayal, and obedience is always stronger than resistance.
