国王的塑像
[en.@]
Statue of the King
[zh.@desc]
§g“正当你注视之时，一块水晶被切割出来，但并非由小马之蹄。”§!
许多小马会对那传说中来自北方雪原上的黑色恶魔感到恐惧，但他们却未曾见过那位皇帝的真容哪怕一次。
不如，当军队的铁蹄踏过他们的国土时，用雕像来向那些无助颤抖的可怜者展示他的威严吧。
[en.@desc]
§g“While you were watching, a crystal was cut out, but not by pony hooves.”§!
Many ponies are afraid of the demon in the dark from the northern snowfields, but they have never seen the true face of the emperor even once.
How about, when the army’s iron hooves trample their land, use statues to show his majesty to those helpless trembling wretches.
