旧日的覆灭
[en.@]
Fall of the Olds
[zh.@desc]
§g“但要将你的财宝积攒在黑暗中，因为你的财宝在哪里，你的心也在哪里。”§!
让旧世界坍塌，让光芒熄灭，让我们适应黑暗。
正如那黑暗中的视线，我们首先习惯，那么在旧日覆灭黑暗笼罩一切只是，我们就将是黑夜中的第一批猎手，将那些无法适应黑暗的瞎子们送去见塞拉斯蒂娅。
[en.@desc]
§g“But store up for yourselves treasures in darkness, for where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.”§!
Let the old world crumble, let the light be extinguished, and let us adapt to the darkness.
Just as we first get used to the sight in the dark, we will be the first hunters in the night when the old days are over and the darkness covers everything, sending those blind ponies who cannot adapt to the darkness to meet Celestia.
