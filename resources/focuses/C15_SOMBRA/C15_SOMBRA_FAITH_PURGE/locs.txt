影魔的盛宴
[en.@]
Umbrums’ Feast
[zh.@desc]
§g“暗影亲近于伤心者，以他们破碎的灵魂为食。”§!
正如那些纯净的灵魂对恶魔最有价值一样，最坚定的反抗者对影魔最有价值。
影魔以绝望为食：对于奴隶，或是无辜者，他们只能产出少量普通的绝望，而真正美味的绝望，来自于击碎那些曾经热诚地拥抱希望的心。
[en.@desc]
§g“The shadows are close to the brokenhearted and feast on those who are crushed in spirit.”§!
As the purest souls are the most valuable to demons, the most resolute resisters are the most valuable to umbrums.
Sombra feeds on despair: slaves or innocents can only produce a small amount of ordinary despair, while the truly delicious despair comes from shattering the hearts that once embraced hope fervently.
