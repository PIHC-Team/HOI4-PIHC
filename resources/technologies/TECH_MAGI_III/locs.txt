魔法子充能技术
[en.@]
Magictron Recharge Technology

[zh.@desc]
在掌握了过充能法器理论和魔法子理论后，一个批量生产过充能法器的可行方案便浮现了：大量招募独角兽或其他具有魔法的生物，工业流水线式从他们身上提取魔法子并加压注入到任意法器中（当然，需要首先测定法器的魔法子容）。这种技术被称为魔法子充能技术。
[en.@desc]
After mastering the theory of overcharging artifacts and the theory of magictrons, a feasible solution for mass production of overcharged artifacts emerged: recruit a large number of unicorns or other magical creatures, and extract magictrons from them in an industrial assembly line. Then pressurize the extracted magictrons into arbitrary artifacts (of course, the magictron capacity of the artifact needs to be measured first). This technology is called Magictron Recharge Technology.
