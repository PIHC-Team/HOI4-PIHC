Enchanted Wooden Melee:
Yes, wood. It may seem like an odd choice for a weapon, as wood is generally better suited for building birdhouses or starting a campfire. But fear not! This wooden melee is enchanted by friendship. You mean magical spell? Nah, just friendship. Friendship is magic remember? If that doesn’t convince you that this wooden melee is way better than any other wooden melee, then I don’t know what to say. It’s a wood, what more do you want?
§g“If you’re still not convinced, try sing a song. That should do the trick. Everypony knows that it is impolite to cut off a song, unless you’re a really bad singer, then you’re on your own.”§!

Enchanted Steel Melee:
Behold, the enchanted steel melee! Bashing your enemies over the head with a solid chunk of steel is not usually seen as a friendly gesture. But you are blessed with the enchantment anyway, because everypony knows, violence is only acceptable when it’s wrapped in a pretty ribbon of harmony. So it’s totally justified, I guess.
§g“They often say that the pen is mightier than the sword. But meanwhile, they also say that don’t bring a magic quill to an enchanted steel melee fight. Quite confusing to me.”§!
