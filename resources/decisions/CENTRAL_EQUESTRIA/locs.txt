小马国的遗产

[en.@desc]
The day will come,
when the fire of friendship is reborn,
when the trees are full of birds,
when the meadowlands vibrant with flowers,
when what divides us will no longer separate us,
when what belongs together grows together.

Soon, we’ll be one nation again.

那一天将会到来，

当树上再次充满了鸟儿，
当草地上再次充满了鲜花，
当分隔我们的东西不再分隔我们，
当本属于一起的再次聚在一起。

很快，我们将再次成为同一个国家。
