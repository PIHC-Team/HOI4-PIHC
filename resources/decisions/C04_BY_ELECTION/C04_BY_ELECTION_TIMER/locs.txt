[en.@desc]
At almost the same time as receiving the letter from Stratusburg, Spitfire had already delivered her candidacy speech in the Unicorn Mountain Range — she and her infamous “Washouts” are even too afraid to deliver their speech in Stratusburg, but instead in the unmanaged territory of Cloudsdale. She publicly declared that she had no ambition or politcal background, and would neither collude with the Empire nor the Free Stratusburg Movement. She said she only wanted to pursue “the extreme of Pegasi”, but she could not explain why she received support from the Empire.
As for Feather Flatterfly and Miss Laurette, they clearly expressed their unwillingness to run. Feather Flatterfly obviously did not want to complicate matters with his nomination as an Imperial official, his work at the Meteorological Bureau, and his economic reform at this time. Meanwhile, Miss Laurette probably did not want to get involved in political conflicts, trying to avoid the spotlight.
As for the other candidates... well, no one really cares about their opinions anyway.
