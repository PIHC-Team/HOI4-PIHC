[en.@desc]
Empress Cozy Glow often emphasizes that the territories should consciously and actively contribute to the Empire’s central government.
Duchess Derpy Hooves must have understood this, so she offered many muffins to the Empress. Afterall this is what she thinks is the best.
“No, Derpy, I don’t want these, can you produce some weapons?”
“Okay!”
So in the next tribute, Duchess Derpy Hooves offered a batch of muffins that were hard enough to break teeth.
