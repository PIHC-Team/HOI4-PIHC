Compulsory Collection
[zh.@]
强制征收

[en.@desc]
In order to ensure that every resident can enjoy a warm winter, it is necessary for the government to intervene with tangible means, forcibly collect a portion of heating materials, and distribute them to residents who cannot obtain enough heating materials on their own.
[zh.@desc]
为了保证每一位居民都能享受到温暖的冬季，政府有必要用有形的大蹄进行干预，强制征收一部分供暖材料，分发给那些无法自行获得足够供暖材料的居民。
