马芬轮盘赌：§Y反转协议§!

[en.@desc]
If the next opened box is good, you will be punished for opening instead. If the next opened box is bad, you will be rewarded for opening instead.

§YThis does not change the next box itself, so it does not affect shuffling or divination results.§!

§YOnly effective in this game.§!
§YIf you drop the next box, the effect will extend to the box after that.§!
