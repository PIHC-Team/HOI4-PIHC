The Duchy of Ponyville is a nominally democratic region. Although the Duchess is appointed by the Empress Cozy Glow, the title of “Duchess” is not permanent in the Equestria Empire, due to deep historical and political reasons. The Duchess of Ponyville is elected by the citizens of the region every four years (but of course, no pony has ever promised that the election is fair).
