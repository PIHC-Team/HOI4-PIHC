The Great Exhibition of Jahr Null
[zh.@]
新元年世界博览会

[en.@desc]
The Great Exhibition of Jahr Null is hosted by the Tirek’s Realm, showcasing the culture and specialties of various species such as centaurs and gargoyles, as well as some treasures that have never been seen before.
Countries and regions from the international community are also welcome to participate and showcase their own culture and specialties. The exhibition will last until October 11th.
