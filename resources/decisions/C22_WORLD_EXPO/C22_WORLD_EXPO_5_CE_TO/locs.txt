> Enter the Changeling Empire Venue
[zh.@]
> 前往幻形灵帝国会场

[zh.@desc]
如果看到展厅内出现蜂熊、木狼、鸡蛇兽等奇怪的生物，不要惊慌，这些都是幻形灵帝国展厅的一部分，它们都是员工。
但幻形灵展厅的员工不会变成斑马、紫色或粉色的独角兽、龙马，如果看到这些生物，离开展厅前，请把门票上虚线处撕下来，交给半人马守卫。

[en.@desc]
If you see strange creatures such as bugbears, timberwolves, cockatrices, etc. in the exhibition hall, do not panic, they are all part of the Changeling Empire venue, they are all employees.
But notice our employees will not turn into zebras, purple or pink unicorns, draconequus, if you see these creatures, before leaving the venue, tear off the dashed line on the ticket and hand it to the centaur guards.
