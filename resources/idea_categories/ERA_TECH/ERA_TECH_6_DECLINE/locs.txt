[zh.@]
堕落之途
[en.@]
Road to Degradation
[zh.@desc]
我们曾经拥有辉煌的文明，但是那些东西并没有拯救我们，科学也许可以战胜敌人，但它无法战胜时间。时间是恐怖的，我们会逐渐遗忘曾经所知道的一切。
[en.@desc]
We once had a glorious civilization, but those things did not save us. Science may defeat our enemy, but it cannot defeat time. Time is invincible, and we will gradually forget everything we once knew.
