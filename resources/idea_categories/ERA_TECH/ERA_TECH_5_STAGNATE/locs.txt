永恒停滞
[en.@]
Eternal Stagnation
[zh.@desc]
这里不再有新的艺术，不再有新的文学，不再有新的科学。当我们无所不能时，我们将不再需要科学。美丑之分将不复存在。不再有好奇心，不再有对生活过程的享受。所有竞争的快乐都将被摧毁。
[en.@desc]
There will be no new art, no new literature, no new science. When we are omnipotent we shall have no more need of science. There will be no distinction between beauty and ugliness. There will be no curiosity, no enjoyment of the process of life. All competing pleasures will be destroyed.
