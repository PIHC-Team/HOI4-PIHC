明日颂诗
[en.@]
Ode to Tomorrow
[zh.@desc]
我们站在一场技术革命的边缘，这场革命将从根本上变革我们生活、工作和相互关系的方式。它的范围、规模和复杂性，将超出这片土地上所有生灵此前的经验！
[en.@desc]
We stand on the brink of a technological revolution that will fundamentally alter the way we live, work, and relate to one another. In its scale, scope, and complexity, the transformation will be unlike anything creatures of this land have experienced before!
