[en.PIHC_HUNGER_TOOLTIP]
The country’s food supply level is calculated based on the ratio of §YFarms§! to the population.
Currently, there are [?ROOT.VAR_NUM_FARMS_OWNED|Y0] §YFarms§! in our country.
The required number of farms for each food supply level is as follows:
§YAbundant Food§!: at least [?ROOT.VAR_NUM_FARMS_REQUIRED_2|Y0] Farms are required based on our country’s population.
§YNormal Supply§!: at least [?ROOT.VAR_NUM_FARMS_REQUIRED_3|Y0] Farms are required based on our country’s population.
§YFood Shortage§!: at least [?ROOT.VAR_NUM_FARMS_REQUIRED_4|Y0] Farms are required based on our country’s population.
The level of supply will be checked monthly, and if the conditions are not met, the supply level will be lowered by one level.
