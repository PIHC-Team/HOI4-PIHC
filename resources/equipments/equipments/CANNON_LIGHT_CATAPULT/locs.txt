Catapult (Light Cannon)

[en.@desc]
The Catapult, a magnificent contraption capable of launching projectiles with high kinetic energy concentrated inside it. These types of projectiles are typically naturally occurring mineralogical body composed primarily of chemical compounds and element fragments exhibiting cohesive rigidity as a result of electromagnetic forces between constituent particles on the atomic and molecular scale, generally forming a conglomerate solid of appreciable size capable of withstanding terrestrial compressional and tensile stresses indigenous to its ambient lithospheric environment. 
§g“Or the unprofessionals may call them, ‘rocks’.”§!
[zh.@desc]
投石机是一种宏伟的装置，能够发射内部集中有高动能的投射物。这种类型的投射物通常是天然存在的矿物体，主要由化合物和元素碎片组成，由于原子和分子尺度上的组成粒子之间的电磁力而表现出内聚刚性，通常形成具有可观尺寸的砾岩固体，能够承受陆地压缩和周围岩石圈环境固有的拉应力。
§g“或者一些非专业者可能会称它们为‘石头’。”§!
