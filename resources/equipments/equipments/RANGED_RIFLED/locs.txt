Rifled Musket (Infantry)

[en.@desc]
The rifled musket is a weapon of precision and sophistication. Equipped with spiral grooves inside the barrel, it spins the bullet with such finesse that it almost whispers sweet nothings to its target before sending it to an early retirement. With this weapon in your arsenal, you can finally say goodbye to the days of firing in the general direction of your enemies and hello to the age of shooting like a true professional (or at least pretending to be one).
§g“Sometimes, a spinning bullet is the spinning diplomatic wheel of a country, unofficially of course.”§!
