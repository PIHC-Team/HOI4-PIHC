Bow (Infantry)

[en.@desc]
While it may be a tad outdated and require a hoof-ton of training to even hit the broad side of a barn, the bow still holds a certain charm. It’s perfect for those who enjoy the challenge of trying to take down an armored tank with a feathered stick and a flimsy string. Aim carefully, lest you accidentally hit a passing bird and become the first pony to be sued by an avian lawyer.
§g“With a bow in hoof, you’ll have plenty of time to contemplate the mysteries of friendship and the meaning of life, while your arrow gracefully dances past your intended target.”§!
