With a flick of the hoof, the Firelance spews forth a torrent of searing flames, engulfing enemy positions in a blaze of chaos and confusion. Those unfortunate souls who stand before its fiery wrath shall bear witness to the incineration of their once proud fortifications, their dreams reduced to naught but smoldering ashes. The flame of unyielding enmity shall consume thee, leaving behind naught but a charred reminder of thy former glory. Verily, this instrument of destruction spares no mercy, for it is a testament to the fiery fury that shall befall all who dare to challenge the might.
