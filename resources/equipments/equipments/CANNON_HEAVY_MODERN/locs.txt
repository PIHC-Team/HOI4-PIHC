While some are still trying to argue that a steam engine could be invented without knowledge from another world, it is generally believed that the internal-combustion engine would not be the first choice of creatures of this magical land. Nevertheless, the species responsible for the creation of this engine remains a certain consistency: they understand that the best use of their energy is on war machines, unlike those innocent and naive Equestrian creatures who are still trying to use it to power their homes, or produce barrels of apple cider.
