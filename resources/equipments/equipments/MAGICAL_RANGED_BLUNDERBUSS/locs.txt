Now I get it! The role of magic in a blunderbuss is to make it less loud. You see, the sound energy of enchanting a spell is usually high-frequency, where as the firing sound of a blunderbuss carries most its energy in low-frequency. So, if you enchant a blunderbuss with a spell, the firing sound and the spell sound combined will be reduced by a lot on the whole. That’s why it’s called an enchanted blunderbuss. Yep, that’s it.
