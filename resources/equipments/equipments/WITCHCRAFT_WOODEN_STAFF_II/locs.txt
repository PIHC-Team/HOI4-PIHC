High Rank Wooden Staff (Witchcraft)

[en.@desc]
The high-rank wooden staff, is a humble and affordable option for any aspiring pony wizard. Such a staff could potentially be a relic from a time when magic as well as the staff itself was still in its “beta testing” phase, or probably a family treasure passed down from generation to generation among third-class sorcerers, or perhaps it was just a random stick you found on the ground with fewer worms than the low-rank ones. Sure, it may not possess all the fancy enchantments and flashy effects of its professional counterparts, but hey, it gets the job done... most of the time.
§g“Some say a wooden staff is like an overnight cupcake, promising sweetness and delight, but occasionally leaving you with a toothache. Emm... non-metaphorically.”§!
