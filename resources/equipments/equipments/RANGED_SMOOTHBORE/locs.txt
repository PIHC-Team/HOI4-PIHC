Smoothbore Musket (Infantry)

[en.@desc]
With its impressively overlong barrel and cumbersome reload time, it’s the perfect choice for those who like to have a calm and constructive conversation first, and then take their sweet time to reload and aim, while their enemies have already retired and opened a tavern at their hometown. Remember, slow and steady wins the race, unless the race is against a draconequus on roller skates, which would be a bad choice to challenge for a duel in the first place.
§g“When it comes to hitting your mark, the smoothbore musket is as accurate as a politician’s promise.”§!
[zh.@desc]
它的枪管超长，装弹时间也超长，非常适合那些有条不紊的小马使用：先和对手进行平静而有建设性的谈话，然后慢慢享受装弹和瞄准的时间，此时对手已经从军队退休并且回老家经营起了属于他们自己的酒馆。记住，慢而稳才能赢得比赛，除非比赛是和一只穿着轮滑鞋的龙马，这也许本来就是一个糟糕的决斗目标。
