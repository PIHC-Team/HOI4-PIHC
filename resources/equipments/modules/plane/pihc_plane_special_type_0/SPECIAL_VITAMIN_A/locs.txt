维生素A

[en.@]
Vitamin A

[zh.@desc]
“维生素A在视觉中扮演着重要的角色。为了看到光的全谱，你的眼睛需要产生视网膜正常工作所需的某些色素。维生素A缺乏会停止这些色素的产生，导致夜盲症。” —— 小马利亚卫生组织（Equestria Health Organization, EHO）

[en.@desc]
“Vitamin A plays an important role in your vision. To see the full spectrum of light, your eye needs to produce certain pigments for your retina to work properly. Vitamin A deficiency stops the production of these pigments, leading to night blindness.” — Equestria Health Organization (EHO)
