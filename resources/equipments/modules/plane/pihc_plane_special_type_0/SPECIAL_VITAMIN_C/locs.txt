[en.@]
Vitamin C

[zh.@desc]
“坏血症是由严重的维生素C缺乏引起的疾病。不吃足够的水果和蔬菜是这种疾病的主要原因。如果不及时治疗，坏血症会导致牙龈出血、牙齿松动和皮肤下出血。” —— 小马利亚卫生组织（Equestria Health Organization, EHO）

[en.@desc]
“Scurvy is a disease caused by a serious vitamin C deficiency. Not eating enough fruits and vegetables is the main cause of the disease. Left untreated, scurvy can lead to bleeding gums, loosened teeth and bleeding under your skin.” — Equestria Health Organization (EHO)
