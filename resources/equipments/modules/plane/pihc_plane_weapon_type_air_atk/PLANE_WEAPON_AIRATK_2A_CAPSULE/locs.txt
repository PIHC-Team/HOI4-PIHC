[zh.@]
单兵射击舱

[en.@]
Individual Shooting Capsule

[zh.@desc]
通过在飞行器上加装多间独立的小屋，每个小屋内配备一名士兵和射击窗，我们可以在空中让陆军士兵进行射击。
最重要的是，在独立的小屋内，我们可以在墙壁上贴满隔音材料，这样小屋之间的射击声音就不会互相干扰，可以保证每个士兵都能专心射击。
(绝密信息：隔音材料的真正作用不是防止隔壁士兵射击的声音被听到，而是假如将来我们让法师在飞行器上射击时，敌军不能偷听我们的施法咒语。)

[en.@desc]
By installing multiple independent cabins on the aircraft, each cabin is equipped with a soldier and a shooting window, allowing the land army soldiers to shoot in the air.
Most importantly, we can cover the walls with soundproof materials in the independent cabins, so that the shooting sounds between the cabins will not interfere with each other, ensuring that each soldier can focus on shooting.
(Top Secret: The true function of the soundproof materials is not to prevent the sound of the shooting from being heard by the soldiers in the next cabin, but to prevent the enemy from eavesdropping on our spellcasting incantations when we have mages shooting on the aircraft in the future.)
