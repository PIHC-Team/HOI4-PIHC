轻型对地火器

[en.@]
Light Air-to-Ground Firearm

[zh.@desc]
为了对空、对侧翼而发明的的单兵射击舱并不适合对地攻击，否则就需要把射击窗口开在地板上，像是公共厕所的设计一样...
所以，另一种更好的方式是直接在飞行器上捆绑枪口向下轻型火器，由飞行器驾驶员控制瞄准和开火。
“但是只要让飞行器上安装的火器枪口朝前或者朝向侧翼不是也就可以对空了吗？为什么我们不这样做呢？”
“额... 这一定是因为... 空气动力学的原因吧...”
[en.@desc]
The individual shooting capsule invented for air-to-air and side-wing attacks is not suitable for ground attacks. Otherwise, the shooting window needs to be opened on the floor, just like the design of a public toilet...
Therefore, another better way is to directly bind the downward light firearm to the aircraft, and the aircraft driver controls the aiming and firing.
“But as long as the firearm mounted on the aircraft is facing forward or towards the side wing, can’t it also attack the air? Why don’t we do this?”
“Uh... this must be because... of aerodynamics...”
