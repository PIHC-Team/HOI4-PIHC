诅咒装甲 (空)

[en.@]
Cursed Armor (Air)

[zh.@desc]
慎戒汝之欲望，汝等渴望永恒存在而忘于祈求有机之躯，故化为被诅咒之不死者，缚于诅咒装甲之中。
[en.@desc]
Take heed of thine desires, forsooth, ye who yearned for eternal existence yet neglected to beseech for an organic vessel, henceforth transformed into the accursed undead, bound within the cursed armor.
