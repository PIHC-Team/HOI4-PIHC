炸弹

[en.@]
Bomb


[zh.@desc]
这种传统的卡通动画中常常出现的经典炸弹形象，或许并不适合真正的战争。由于飞行的高度很难控制，往往需要投弹员目测高度，计算下落时间，然后手动剪断引线点燃后投掷。这种方式非常低效，杀伤力也很随机。
但这种炸弹有一个其他轰炸武器无法替代的优势：卡通动画中，当敌军接到从空中掉下来的炸弹时，往往会盯着炸弹愣几秒，然后盯着画面愣几秒（给观众反应时间），然后进行一段加速度为零的起跑动作。因此，这种炸弹往往命中率很高。
[en.@desc]
This classic bomb image that often appears in traditional cartoon animations may not be suitable for real wars. Due to the difficulty of controlling the flight height, the bomb dropper often needs to visually estimate the height, calculate the falling time, and then manually cut the fuse to ignite and throw. This method is very inefficient, and the lethality is also very random.
However, this bomb has an advantage that other bombing weapons cannot replace: in cartoon animations, when the enemy receives a bomb falling from the sky, they often stare at the bomb for a few seconds, then stare at the screen for a few seconds (giving the audience time to react), and then perform a zero-acceleration runaway action. Therefore, this bomb often has a very high hit rate.
