[zh.@desc]
轮子看上去可能很复杂，其实它们的工作原理很简单：
圆形的部分围绕着中心旋转，当轮子的边缘与地面接触时，产生的压力会推动整个世界向后滚动远离你，给你一种你在前进的感觉。
[en.@desc]
Wheels migght look complicated, but the way they work is actually simple:
The circle bit revolves around the center, and then as the wheel skin here pushes against the ground. The pressure rolls the entire world back and away from you, giving you the impression that you’re moving forward.
