传动杆

[en.@]
Transmission Rod

[zh.@desc]
传动杆可以链接多辆车辆，使其动力保持均匀，行军速度提高且更加稳定，但这也让车队在遭遇侧翼袭击时更加被动。此时，请卸下传动杆——这样你就可以握着它们攻击了，像球棒一样。
[en.@desc]
The rod can link multiple vehicles to keep their power balanced, increase the marching speed and make it more stable. But it also makes the convoy more passive when encountering side flank attacks.
At this time, please remove the transmission rod — so you can hold them to attack, like a baseball bat.
