Track

[zh.@desc]
当你到别家做客的时候，不想脱鞋，但是又不想弄脏对方的地板，这时候你就可以用一个袋子把鞋套住。履带就是这样一种类似地设计。多么有礼貌有文明的发明啊！
[en.@desc]
When you visit some house, you don’t want to take off your shoes, but you don’t want to dirty the floor. At this time, you can use a bag to cover your shoes. The track is such a similar design. What a polite and civilized invention!
