鬼火

[en.@]
Ignis Fatuus

[zh.@desc]
慎戒汝之漫游，汝等在最黑暗之夜中寻觅道路，跟随难以捉摸之鬼火指引，却最终迷失于与恶作剧的萤火虫永无止境之舞蹈中，直至那炮火的终末方可解脱。
[en.@desc]
Take heed of thine wanderlust, forsooth, ye who sought to find the path in the darkest night under the guidance of the elusive Ignis Fatuus, only to end up lost in a never-ending dance with a mischievous firefly, until the armageddon of the artillery to finally break free.
