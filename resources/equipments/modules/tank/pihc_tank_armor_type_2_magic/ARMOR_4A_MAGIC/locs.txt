Magic Force Field

[zh.@desc]
魔法力场像空气一样透明，但却比最坚硬的水晶还稳固。但是，当你向还在使用钢铁甚至木头装甲的敌军炫耀你的新玩具的时候，记得提前充满能量，否则空气装甲显得没那么有创意。
[en.@desc]
Magic force field is as transparent as air, but more solid than the hardest crystal. However, before showing off your new toy to the enemy still using steel or even wooden armor, remember to charge it up, as air armor is not very creative.
