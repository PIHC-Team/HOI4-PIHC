幽冥

[en.@]
Spectral

[zh.@desc]
慎戒汝之狂妄，汝等自毂构筑不可侵犯之铠甲、能量护盾以至于魔法力场，并自诩安稳无恙，必将受其幽冥同族之重击，其灵魂将永远铭刻于其战争机械舱体之上。
[en.@desc]
Take heed of thine hubris, forsooth, ye who doth construct an impervious armor, an energy shield, or even a magic force field, and believeth thyself to be secure, shall be smitten by their spectral foes, and their souls shall be eternally inscripted to the hull of their war machine.
