晶体矩阵

[en.@]
Crystalline Matrix

[zh.@desc]
自动驾驶是一把双刃剑：一方面，晶体的光学检测可以保证我们的自动驾驶坦克绝对不会撞到墙上；但是另一方面，当敌军突然冲到在我们的坦克前，我们也没法碾过他们，而只能等待安全驾驶系统礼貌地停车拉刹，然后开炮。
[en.@desc]
Autopilot is a double-edged sword: on one hoof, the optical detection of the crystalline can ensure that our autopilot tank will never run into a wall; on the other hoof, when the enemy suddenly rushes to our tank, we can’t run over them, and can only wait for the safe driving system to politely stop and pull the hoofbrake, and then open fire.
