黄铜装甲

[en.@]
Brass Armor

[zh.@desc]
黄铜是一种相对柔软而易于塑形的合金，所以当然最适合担任坦克的防御性装甲：轻便，防热，防腐蚀，在被敌方炮弹击中后方便破拆逃生。
[en.@desc]
Brass is a relatively soft and malleable alloy, so it is of course the best choice for tank armor: lightweight, heat-resistant, corrosion-resistant, and easy to break and escape after being hit by an enemy shell.
