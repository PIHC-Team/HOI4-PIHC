尖锥魅影

[en.@]
Phantom Wedge

[zh.@desc]
总有一些聪明的小马认为他们可以在坦克碾压过来的时候趴在地上，从履带或车轮中间穿过从而躲过一劫，这下他们会学到一些新东西，只不过新知识可能没有机会用上了。
[en.@desc]
There are always some clever ponies who think they can lie on the ground when the tank rolls over, pass through the tracks or wheels, and escape. Now they will learn something new, it’s just that they no longer have the opportunity to put the new knowledge into practice.
