[zh.@NEWS_DESC]
特丽克西回到马车，关上门。昏暗的灯光下，一把铳枪已经指向了她的后脑勺。
“星光，我知道这一天最终一定会到来。” 特丽克西没有回头，声音低沉。

“特丽克西，我还是无法相信你就这样... 加入了他们。” 星光的声音从背后传来，带着一丝哀伤。
“接受现实吧！星光！我们已经输了。” 特丽克西的声音冷漠而坚定。

“如果坚持不投降就永远不会输，而你却选择直接向和煦光流投降。” 星光的声音中带着一丝悲伤。她不愿意相信特丽克西已经放弃了，她不愿意相信特丽克西已经背叛了她。
“我为和煦光流工作，只是为了借此保护小马谷，使其免于和煦的暴政。我理解你想要复仇的心情，但是 —— 现在你们才是造成痛苦和混乱的祸首，看看天上的风之魔吧！” 和煦光流曾经说，正是星光熠熠的“反叛”，抵抗军与帝国的冲突，才是风之魔肆虐不止的原因。虽然亲眼见证过终末之战前风之魔肆虐的特丽克西从未相信过和煦光流的无耻谎言，但此时看着马车窗外盘旋的风之魔，特丽克西产生了动摇。

“什么？你不会真的相信那些鬼话吧！你更愿意相信他们的虚假宣传，而不是我？” 星光愤怒地质问特丽克西。
“如果你希望我相信你，你也应该相信我绝对不会背叛我们的友谊。我以朋友的身份恳求你 —— 放下过去吧！” 特丽克西终于转过身来，双眼通红，带着哭腔，近乎是对着星光咆哮着。

“不要放弃希望，特丽克西。不论你现在在帮他们做什么，现在停止还来得及。”
“我已经无法回头了。”

沉默过后，星光颤抖着再次将铳枪枪口对准特丽克西，握紧了扳机，但却不忍心开枪。
“求你再想想，特丽克西，我真的不想伤害你。” 星光哀求着，而特丽克西则看着星光，表情没有一丝变化。
“如果你迫不得已，那么，开枪吧。” 特丽克西闭上眼。

特丽克西的独角开始发光，蓝色的强光照亮了整个马车，球状的眼泪悬浮在空中，已分不清来自哪一方。星光以为特丽克西要发射激光攻击，将扳机攥得更紧，却始终无法下定决心。
于是，那蓝色的魔法控制住了星光持枪的蹄子 —— 扣下了扳机。
“不！特丽克西！不要！”

[en.@NEWS_DESC]
Trixie returned to her wagon and closed the door. In the dim light, a hoofgun was already pointed at the back of her head.
“Starlight, I know this day would eventually come.” Trixie did not turn around, her voice was low.

“Trixie, I still can’t believe you just... joined them.” Starlight’s voice came from behind, tinged with sadness.
“Face it! Starlight! We’ve lost.” Trixie’s voice was cold and firm.

“Not if you don’t give up and surrender to Cozy Glow.” Starlight’s voice was tinged with sadness. She could not believe that Trixie had given up, she could not believe that Trixie had betrayed her.
“I work for Cozy Glow only to protect Ponyville from her tyranny. I understand you want your revenge, but — you are the ones causing pain and destruction now, just see the Windigos!” Cozy Glow once said that it was Starlight Glimmer’s “rebellion” and the conflict between the Resistance and the Empire that caused the Windigos to continue to ravage. Although Trixie, who had witnessed the Windigos ravaging before the Ultimate Battle, never believed Cozy Glow’s shameless lies, she was shaken by the sight of the Windigos circling outside the wagon.

“What? you can’t really believe that! You would rather trust their fake propaganda rather than me?” Starlight angrily questioned Trixie.
“In that case, you should also trust me that I would never betray our friendship. I’m begging you as a friend — let it go!” Trixie finally turned around, her eyes red, her voice choked, almost roaring at Starlight.

“There’s still hope, Trixie. Whatever you’ve been doing for them, it has to be stopped.”
“I don’t know how.”

After a moment of silence, Starlight trembled and pointed her hoofgun at Trixie again, gripping the trigger tighter, but couldn’t bear to fire.
“Trixie, please! I don’t want to hurt you.” Starlight pleaded, while Trixie was staring at Starlight, without any change in expression.
“If you have to, then, do it.” Trixie closed her eyes.

Trixie’s horn began to glow, the blue light illuminating the entire wagon, spherical tears floating in the air, unable to tell which side they came from. Starlight thought Trixie was about to shoot a laser, so she gripped the trigger tighter, but could not make up her mind.
Then, the blue magic took control of Starlight’s hoof holding the hoofgun — and pulled the trigger.
“No! Trixie! Don’t!”
