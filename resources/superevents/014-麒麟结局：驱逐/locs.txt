通道缓缓开启，无数麒麟已经站在了通道的那一端。逆麟战犯们将得到仁慈的审判，他们将在最后时刻与他们的亲朋好友团聚。
临走之前，他们有机会与这个世界和解：他们有的见到了自己逝去已久的亲朋好友，有的见到了自己从未存在过的孩子，有的见到了自己。
有些幸运者，或者应该说是不幸者，他们在战争中杀死了自己，因而获得了背负着罪孽在这个世界上继续生存下去的机会。他们将带着文明的记忆，成为行走的纪念碑。
我们为他们送去了稚马之息、食物、加热器、药品与毛毯，祝福他们在最后的时刻能够忘却冰雪的寒冷，忘却仇恨的痛苦，忘却自己的罪孽，获得片刻的宁静。
逆麟的火光将整个通道照亮，融化了通道那头的积雪，最后一位逆麟——秋烨’，也向着通道走去。临走前，她回过头，看向秋烨*。
她点了点头，因为她知道如果自己站在通道的这一侧，也会做出同样的选择。
“愿我们在下一个轮回再见。”
[en.@NEWS_DESC]
The tunnel slowly opens, and countless Kirins are already standing at the other end of the tunnel. The Nirik war criminals will receive a merciful judgment, and they will reunite with their relatives and friends at the last moment.
Just before leaving, they have the opportunity to reconcile with this world: some saw their long-lost relatives and friends, some saw children who had never existed, and some saw themselves.
Some lucky ones, or perhaps unlucky ones, killed themselves in the war, and thus gained the opportunity to continue living in this world with their sins. They will carry the memory of civilization and become walking monuments.
We sent them foals’ breath, food, heaters, medicines, and blankets, wishing them to forget the cold of ice and snow, the pain of hatred, the sins of themselves, and to gain a moment of peace in the last moment.
The flame of Niriks lit up the entire tunnel, melting the snow at the other end of the tunnel, as the last Nirik - Autumn Blaze’, walked towards the tunnel. Before leaving, she turned back and looked at Autumn Blaze*.
She nodded, because she knew that if she stood on this side of the tunnel, she would make the same choice.
“May we meet again in the next cycle.”
