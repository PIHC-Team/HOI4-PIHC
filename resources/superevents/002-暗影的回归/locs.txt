[zh.@NEWS_DESC]
小马利亚是一片充满魔法的土地，所以生活在其中的生物们已经见识过了一切。如今，一位已经逝去数年的角色再次出现在世界面前，也并不会引起太多惊讶。当然，这也绝对不是什么好消息，也不是平平淡淡的新闻。
黑晶王森布拉，曾经的水晶帝国皇帝，曾在千年前统治着水晶帝国，被封印而消失千年。数年前，解除封印回归的黑晶王尝试重新夺取水晶帝国，甚至小马利亚王国，但没能成功，失败的他也被友谊的魔法撕成了碎片。
或许是水晶的碎片有着什么隐藏的繁殖能力，又或者一切都在森布拉的计划之中，又或者背后有着更加黑暗的力量，总之，黑晶王再次回到了忠诚于他的水晶帝国。
朝代更迭的速度超乎想象，一夜之间，水晶帝国的旗帜变成了黑色，水晶小马们的眼睛变成了红色，皇宫中长满了黑色的水晶。《协定》体系要开始适应谈判桌上的新面孔了。
[en.@NEWS_DESC]
Equestria is a land full of magic, so the creatures living in it have seen everything. Now, the reappearance of a character who has been dead for years will not cause much surprise. Of course, this is definitely not good news, nor is it ordinary news.
King Sombra, the former emperor of the Crystal Empire, once ruled the Crystal Empire a thousand years ago and was sealed and disappeared for a thousand years. Several years ago, King Sombra was released from the seal, and tried to retake the Crystal Empire and even the Equestria Empire. However, he failed and was torn to pieces by the magic of friendship.
Perhaps the crystal fragments have some hidden breeding ability, or everything is in Sombra’s plan, or there are even darker forces behind it. In any case, King Sombra has returned to his loyal Crystal Empire.
The speed of dynastic change is beyond imagination. Overnight, the flag of the Crystal Empire turned black, the eyes of the crystal ponies turned red, and the palace was filled with black crystals. The “PACT” system will have to adapt to the new face at the negotiating table.
