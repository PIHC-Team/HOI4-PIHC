迹象表明，《协定》体系三大核心国之一的幻形灵帝国，似乎并未从《协定》体系中获取足够多的好处来支付它战争所消耗的成本。自去年，甚至可以说更早些开始，广泛的饥荒就开始困扰着这个刚刚成为世界霸主不久的国家。
在帝国成立的那一天，邪茧女王曾向子民承诺不再饥荒，让他们相信通过高效的“掠夺”，总是可以得到比“分享”多得多的爱意。很显然，邪茧女王并没能成功证明自己的观点，穷兵黩武的军事化幻形灵帝国在水晶帝国、斑马族、塔尔塔洛斯、驯鹿族等各个方向都遭遇不顺，而帝国内部的饥荒问题也愈演愈烈。曾经被胡萝卜加大棒威胁着臣服女王的幻形灵们，开始了窃窃私语。
有一部分幻形灵还记着那个“分享”的时代，虽说“分享”得到的爱意不多，但也足以让幻形灵不再感到饥饿。他们重新捡起了索拉克斯时期的教诲，甚至有不少违抗禁令偷偷变回化茧状态，并将监狱中的奥瑟蕾丝视作精神导师，试图与邪茧女王对抗。还有一些更加务实的幻形灵相信，无论是“分享”还是“掠夺”都无所谓，回到索拉克斯时代，或是更早的虫巢时代也未尝不可，因为最终的目的只有一个：让所有幻形灵不再饥饿——而无法实现当初承诺的邪茧，则应付出失信的代价。当然，也不是所有幻形灵都想要回到过去，也有许多幻形灵在“向前看”。在虫巢高层，有许多邪茧女王的信徒正在为她的“掠夺”辩护，但面对无数的质疑，也开始女王是否已经失去了当年的铁腕与锐气——一个铁血的统治者怎么能允许异见者如此肆无忌惮？他们相信“掠夺”，只是不认为邪茧女王还是那个能够带领幻形灵走向辉煌的最佳选择，新的时代也许需要新的领袖。
因此，各方势力在暗中酝酿风暴，直到严重饥荒导致的化茧派暴动。暴动的幻形灵攻入了粮仓，也攻入了监狱，释放了奥瑟蕾丝；被派去镇压的军队也开始分裂，不少幻形灵军队选择了独走；而镇压不力使得虫巢内部也更加怀疑邪茧女王的决策，“掠夺派”内部的矛盾也公开爆发。幻形灵帝国的内战，似乎已经不可避免。
我们将会看到《协定》体系的霸主从她的王座上跌落吗？又或者，这场风暴将会让整个《协定》体系陷入风雨飘摇之中？
[en.@NEWS_DESC]
Signs indicate that the Changeling Empire, one of the three core countries of the PACT system, seems to have NOT gained enough benefits from the PACT system to pay for the costs of its war. Since last year, or even earlier, widespread famine has plagued this country, which has just become the world hegemon.
On the day the empire was established, Queen Chrysalis promised her subjects that there would be no more famine, convincing them that through efficient “plundering”, they could always get much more love than “sharing”. Obviously, Queen Chrysalis failed to prove her point. The militarily aggressive Changeling Empire has encountered difficulties in all directions, such as the Crystal Empire, the Zebra Tribe, Tartarus, and the Reindeers. Meanwhile, the internal famine problem has become more and more serious. The changelings who were once threatened by the carrot and stick to submit to the queen began to whisper.
Some of the changelings still remember the period of “sharing”. Though there was not much love gained through “sharing”, it was enough to keep the changelings from feeling hungry. They picked up the teachings of the Thorax period again, and many of them secretly turned back into the metamorphosed state, viewing Ocellus in the prison as their spiritual mentor to resist Queen Chrysalis. There are also more pragmatic changelings who believe that it doesn’t matter whether it is “sharing” or “plundering”, it is possible to return to the Thorax period or even the earlier times. To them there is only one ultimate goal: to keep all changelings from feeling hungry — and if Queen Chrysalis cannot fulfill her original promise, she should pay the price for her breach of trust. Of course, not all changelings want to go back to the past, many are “looking forward”. In the upper echelons of the hive, many of Queen Chrysalis’s followers are defending her “plundering”, but in the face of countless questions, they are also beginning to wonder whether the queen has lost the iron fist and sharpness she had in the past — how can a bloodthirsty ruler allow dissenters to be so unbridled? They believe in “plundering”, but do not believe that Queen Chrysalis is still the best choice to lead the changelings to glory, and the new era may require a new leader.
Therefore, the storm is brewing in secret, until the famine-induced metamorphosis riots. The rioting changelings broke into the granary and the prison, releasing Ocellus; the troops sent to suppress the riots also began to split, many changeling troops chose to go their own way; the failure to suppress the riots made the hive more suspicious of Queen Chrysalis’s decisions, and the contradictions within the “plundering” faction also erupted. The civil war in the Changeling Empire seems inevitable.
Will we see the hegemon of the PACT system fall from her throne? Or will this storm plunge the entire PACT system into turmoil with it?
