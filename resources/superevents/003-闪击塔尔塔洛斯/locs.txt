突发新闻！
幻形灵帝国的一支军队刚刚跨越中立区界限占领了塔尔塔洛斯监狱，导致幻形灵帝国与小马利亚帝国的关系紧张。小马利亚帝国已发出正式抗议，谴责这次违反《协定》的行为。截至目前，邪茧女王并未做出任何回应。
塔尔塔洛斯是小马利亚帝国下属的监狱，其中关押着一些重要的政治犯、以及野生怪物。由于小马利亚帝国长期缺乏对监狱的维护，塔尔塔洛斯附近地区的野生生物泛滥成灾，这引起了邻国幻形灵帝国的不满。塔尔塔洛斯边境的紧张局势从几个周前开始，幻形灵帝国东部受到严重的蜂熊灾影响。幻形灵帝国声称是小马利亚帝国对塔尔塔洛斯监狱管理的不作为导致蜂熊泛滥，并在未经小马利亚帝国的同意前提下派出军队“协助治理蜂熊”。随后幻形灵帝国又声称夜骐、塔佐蠕虫等生物同样造成了灾害，如果不彻底解决塔尔塔洛斯问题，幻形灵帝国东部边境就不得安宁。而在此次突袭塔尔塔洛斯之前，小马利亚帝国从未对此事发表过任何回应。

[en.@NEWS_DESC]
BREAKING NEWS!
A troop of the Changeling Empire has just crossed the Neutral Zone border and occupied Tartarus, causing greate tension between the Changeling Empire and Equestria Empire. Equestria Empire has launched a formal protest, condemning this open violation of the PACT. Until now, Queen Chrysalis has made no comment on the matter.
Tartarus is a prison under the Equestria Empire, which imprisoned some important political prisoners and wild monsters. Due to the Equestria Empire’s long-term lack of maintenance of the prison, wild creatures in the area around Tartarus proliferated, causing dissatisfaction in the neighboring country of the Changeling Empire. The tense situation at the Tartarus border began several weeks ago, when the eastern part of the Changeling Empire was seriously affected by a bugbear disaster. The Changeling Empire claimed that the Equestria Empire’s mismanagement of the Tartarus prison led to the proliferation of bugbears, and sent troops to “assist in the management of bugbears” without the consent of the Equestria Empire. The Changeling Empire then claimed that creatures such as the thestrals and the Tatzlwurms also caused disasters, and that if the Tartarus problem was not completely resolved, the eastern border of the Changeling Empire would not be peaceful. The Equestria Empire has never made any response to this matter before the attack on Tartarus.
