[zh.@NEWS_DESC]
自从乐善集团的恶行被电台公之于众，小马利亚各地区就紧紧盯着马哈顿和其他东小马利亚城市。聚光灯下，紧张的氛围笼罩着整个东小马利亚：没有哪座乐善集团管理下的城市是干净的，所有城市都有着自己的腐败和黑暗。在帝国发布声明撇清干系之后，城内的富商们也终于确认——犯下的罪孽终将付出代价，鲜血与泪水换来的片刻财富终将回归于土地上的民众。
终于，星光抵抗军，或者被帝国称之为“星光叛军”的势力，在周边地区势力甚至是长久以来一直作对帝国政府的默许下，击溃了盘踞东小马利亚的乐善集团。在抵抗军领袖星光熠熠的号召下，马哈顿的民众们终于挣脱了乐善集团的枷锁，解放了他们自己。值得庆幸的是，乐善集团在东小马利亚所掠夺的财富大部分还没来得及挥霍或是带走，黑市上残留的物品和集团没能带走的工业机器，将成为这些沿海城市从战争废墟上快速恢复的基石。
敬自由，敬新生！
[en.@NEWS_DESC]
Ever since the atrocities of the Gladmane Group were exposed by the radio, all regions of Equestria have been closely watching Manehattan and other cities in eastern Equestria. Under the spotlight, a tense atmosphere envelops the entire eastern Equestria: no city under the management of the Gladmane Group is clean, and all cities have their own corruption and darkness. After the Empire issued a statement to disassociate itself from the matter, the rich merchants in the city finally confirmed that the sins they committed must eventually be paid for, and the momentary wealth obtained through blood and tears will eventually return to the ponies on the land.
Finally, the Starlight Resistance, or the force that the Empire calls the "Starlight Rebels" has defeated the Gladmane Group that has been entrenched in eastern Equestria for a long time. This behavior has even been tacitly approved by local forces and even the Empire that has always been against the resistance. At the call of the leader of the resistance, Starlight Glimmer, the ponies of Manehattan finally broke free from the shackles of the Gladmane Group and liberated themselves. Fortunately, most of the wealth plundered by the Gladmane Group in eastern Equestria has not yet been squandered or taken away. The items left on the black market and the industrial machinery that the group did not take away will become the cornerstone for these coastal cities to quickly recover from the ruins of war.
To freedom, to rebirth!
