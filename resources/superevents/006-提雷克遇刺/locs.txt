小马们似乎从未想到过，获取了半个世界的魔力，世界上最强大的生命体，也可能会在一瞬间倒下。提雷克大王，曾经的三巨头之一，在巡回演讲中被一发狙击枪的子弹击穿了头颅。
刺杀提雷克的计划并不是诞生于虚空之中，事实上，无论是幻形灵帝国中央情报局、小马利亚帝国秩序部、还是抵抗组织，都有制定过不少刺杀提雷克的计划，但无一能推演成功 — 直到这一刻，一组不知名的此刻将桂冠摘下，终结了一切讨论。
原来，提雷克并不是无敌的。普通的狙击枪并不能伤他分毫，而即使是再有天赋的独角兽所施展的攻击法术也并不能与提雷克的魔法护罩强度相提并论。但是，奥术魔能引擎和魔法子理论的出现改变了这一切，经过法术改良的狙击枪最终做到了。
提雷克的死亡震惊了世界，但无论是在悲痛还是喜悦中，都鲜有小马注意到了更关键的问题 — 格罗迦之铃哪去了？
[en.@NEWS_DESC]
Ponies never seemed to have thought that the most powerful creature in the world, who had half the world’s magic, could fall in an instant. Lord Tirek, once one of the three giants, was shot in the head by a sniper bullet during a tour.
The plan to assassinate Tirek did not originate from the void. In fact, the Changeling Empire Central Intelligence Agency, the Equestria Empire Ministroy of Order, or the resistance organization, all formulated many plans to assassinate Tirek, but none of them could be successful — until this very moment, a group of unknowns took off the crown and ended all discussions.
It turns out that Tirek is not invincible. Ordinary sniper rifles cannot hurt him at all, and even the attack spells cast by the most talented unicorns cannot be compared with Tirek’s magic shield strength. However, the appearance of the Arcane Magic Engine and the theory of magictron changed everything, and the arcane-enchaned sniper rifle finally completed the job.
Tirek’s death shocked the world, but whether in sorrow or joy, few ponies noticed the more critical issue — where did the Grogar’s Bell go?
